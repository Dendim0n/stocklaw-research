About StocKlaw

What is this

StocKlaw is a data-driven US equity research notebook. All analysis is based on publicly verifiable data and facts, not narratives or tips.

Content Categories

  • Signals — Daily investment signals from Hacker News and GitHub
  • Research — Portfolio research including AI moat, AI application, and growth screening
  • Analysis — Individual stock deep dives and sector/theme studies
  • Reviews — Data verification and commentary on investment articles

Methodology

  • Every claim must have a verifiable numerical anchor
  • Cross-market signal verification
  • Scenario matrices, not point predictions
  • Honest uncertainty acknowledgment

Data Sources

FRED, yfinance, SEC EDGAR, Finnhub, Google News, Hacker News, GitHub
