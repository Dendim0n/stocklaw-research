## Core Data

- US tech giants invested $560B in AI, earned back $35B = 16:1 ratio
- US debt $39T, annual interest $1.23T exceeds defense budget
- Predicts global financial crisis within 12-18 months

## Verification

The 16:1 ratio needs context - it only counts direct AI revenue, excluding efficiency gains on existing businesses. Debt figures are directionally accurate. The 12-18 month window is almost certainly too precise.

## Score: 6.5/10

Impactful data points, but conclusions lean doomsday. The 16:1 ratio would look much better including indirect efficiency gains.
