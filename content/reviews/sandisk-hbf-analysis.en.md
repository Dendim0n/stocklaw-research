The numbers are accurate. The driver is real earnings: Q3 FY2026 revenue grew +251% YoY.

### HBF Technology: Real, But Misunderstood

**HBF (High Bandwidth Flash) is a genuine technology direction:**

- Aug 2025: SanDisk and SK Hynix agreed to co-develop and standardize HBF
- Built on NAND Flash with HBM-like stacking (CBA technology)
- Official data: within 2.2% of unlimited-capacity HBM performance in large-capacity scenarios
- Samples expected H2 2026, AI inference device sampling in early 2027
- Small-scale commercial deployment 2027-2028, widespread adoption post-2030

**But the article confuses critical concepts:**

HBF and HBM are **complementary**, not substitutes:

| Dimension | HBM | HBF |
|-----------|-----|-----|
| Underlying tech | DRAM | NAND Flash |
| Latency | Nanoseconds | Microseconds |
| Capacity | GB-scale | TB-scale |
| Use case | AI training | AI inference storage layer |
| Maturity | Mass production for years | Sample stage |

The claim "SanDisk = Micron + SK Hynix + Samsung" is incorrect. These three are core HBM suppliers with 95%+ market share. SanDisk only has the NAND line — an order of magnitude difference in scale.
