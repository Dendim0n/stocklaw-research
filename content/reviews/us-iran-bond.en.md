## Data Verification

All key data points verified through FRED:

- 10Y yield: article 4.441% vs FRED 4.47% ✓
- Real rate (DFII10): article 2.09% vs FRED 2.07% ✓
- Inflation expectation (T10YIE): article 2.39% vs FRED 2.39% ✓
- Fed Funds rate: article 3.5%-3.75% vs FRED 3.63% ✓

Decomposition confirmed: of the 41bp yield rise, real rates contributed 31bp (76%), inflation expectations only 10bp (24%). **Real rates are indeed the dominant driver.**

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
1. Real rate vs inflation expectation decomposition is genuinely valuable — most retail investors only look at nominal yields
2. 2022 analogy with Shopify is a classic case of fundamentals improving while valuation multiples compress
3. Option skew visualization effectively shows stock-bond sentiment divergence

**Weaknesses:**
1. "Stocks aren't crashing = risk is low" is survivorship bias — the market hadn't crashed yet, not the same as risk being absent
2. S&P at 7610 with elevated PE means the valuation sensitivity to rates is real
3. "Fed won't hike aggressively" is premature — if inflation rebounds from tariffs or geopolitical shocks, the Fed could turn hawkish

## Score: 8/10

Quality macro analysis with strong data backing. Core weakness: using current market price to prove risk is overstated is circular reasoning.
