**Key Error:** Article claims S&P 500 inclusion requires 50% public float. **Actual threshold is 10%** (S&P Global methodology). Doesn't invalidate core logic but overstates delay.

## Core Logic

Combined valuation: $3.6T (roughly France's GDP). At standard 15% float, this requires $548B in public market capital — exceeding the total of all US IPOs over the past decade. These companies will likely IPO at 3-8% float. When lockup expires and float reaches S&P 500 inclusion threshold, ~$20T in passive funds must sell existing holdings (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA) to buy new entrants.
