## Core Framework

Harry Browne's Permanent Portfolio: four 25% allocations matching four economic scenarios. Historically, max drawdown was only -4.9%, with ~10% annualized returns (US version).

## Verification

- Mathematically sound: low correlation between four assets
- 2022 broke the bond hedge assumption (TLT -30%+ in stock-bond double kill)
- Gold currently elevated (2000→3500+), far above rebalancing threshold
- Forward 10% annualized probability: ~60-70% given current valuations

## Score: 7.5/10

Classic framework, but needs adjustment for current environment (duration risk, gold overvaluation).
