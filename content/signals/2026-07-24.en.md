## 3. Signal Analysis

### Signal 1: AI Infrastructure Bubble Risk Rising — Watch for Compute Overinvestment

**Logic chain**: HN lead story exposes AI companies hiding massive debt off-balance-sheet → real capex far exceeds reported figures → AI compute demand may be overestimated → if debt issues surface, compute orders could contract → bearish for AI hardware chain (NVDA, AVGO, AMD, SMCI) and AI cloud providers.

**Scenario analysis**: Currently a risk signal, not confirmed fact. But if AI company debt rating downgrades or financing condition tightening emerges in coming months, NVDA's valuation logic needs reassessment. Open-source model efficiency gains (Echo) are also weakening the "must buy more GPUs" narrative.

### Signal 2: AI Geopolitical Risk Escalation — Semiconductors Under Dual Pressure

**Logic chain**: US mulls restricting Chinese open-source AI model access + DARPA AI F-16 test flight success → tech cold war expanding from hardware (chip bans) to software (model restrictions) → near-term positive for US closed-source AI (MSFT/GOOGL), but bearish for semiconductor China exports (NVDA/AMD) → simultaneously bullish for defense AI (PLTR, LMT, RTX).

**Key tension**: Semiconductor companies face a tug-of-war between "growing US domestic AI demand" and "shrinking China market revenue." Watch NVDA's next quarterly earnings for China revenue share changes.

### Signal 3: AI Agent Infrastructure Explosion — Enterprise AI Adoption Accelerating

**Logic chain**: GitHub trending dominated by AI agent toolchain (OmniRoute, pi, code-review-graph, Kimi Code) → agent infrastructure moving from "toys" to "engineering-grade" → enterprise AI deployment barriers lowering → bullish for cloud platforms (MSFT Azure, AMZN AWS, GOOGL GCP) and enterprise SaaS (Salesforce, ServiceNow).

**Testable hypothesis**: If large enterprises announce AI transformation cases based on agent architecture within 3-6 months, cloud providers' AI revenue growth will exceed expectations.
