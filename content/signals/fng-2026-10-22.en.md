## Divergences: plausible explanations and counter-evidence

**Divergence 1: the index is near a one-year high while participation sits at one-year lows.**
The S&P at 7,757.17 is in the 94.8th percentile, just -0.79% below the sample high of 7,818.93, while strength is in the 6.0th percentile and breadth in the 7.2nd.
- Explanation A (mega-caps carrying the index): a handful of large caps hold the index up while the median stock is weak. **Counter-evidence:** over the last ten sessions RSP is **+2.87%**, clearly ahead of SPY at -0.53%, and on 10-21 QQQ (+1.05%) led RSP (+0.36%) — the recent two-week pattern is "tech drawdown, equal-weight repair", the opposite of mega-cap dominance. If mega-caps were carrying the tape, RSP would be persistently behind.
- Explanation B (level versus change): strength and breadth are **level** measures built from weeks of accumulated new highs/lows and a width reading; a higher index print does not imply more new highs. The concentrated September-October drawdown in semis and AI infrastructure (SOXX -11.8% in the week to 10-16, per media tallies) created many new lows and depresses the strength reading. **Counter-evidence:** breadth raw rose from 167.11 to 387.20 (+131.7%) in ten sessions; the repair is not slow, and if it continues the percentile will rise quickly, which would falsify the "structural deterioration" framing.
- **How this report handles it:** no "narrow rally = danger" conclusion and no "breadth repair = bottom" conclusion. Index level and participation are currently facts pointing in different directions, presented side by side.

**Divergence 2: momentum scores 47 (Neutral) while the S&P sits in the 94.8th percentile.**
- Explanation: the sub-indicator measures the **size of the deviation** from the 125-day MA, not the level's percentile. A +2.68% cushion sits mid-range for the past year, hence Neutral. **Counter-evidence / forward risk:** the 125-day MA itself is in the 99.6th percentile, i.e. rising fast — unless the index accelerates, the cushion narrows mechanically and the score falls. A flat index is enough to make momentum deteriorate.

**Divergence 3: junk bond and safe-haven raw percentiles do not match their scores.**
Junk bond raw at 1.2121 sits in the **7.6th** percentile (close to a one-year low) yet scores Neutral 49.8; safe-haven raw at 0.1789 sits in the **29.9th** percentile yet scores 20.0 (Extreme Fear).
- Explanation: CNN does not publish its normalisation (possibly rate-of-change, rolling windows, or a non-linear map), so **raw percentiles cannot be used to infer scores**. The same mismatch appeared last run (junk raw in the 14.3rd percentile while scoring 44.2).
- **Counter-evidence and open item:** if CNN's junk reading is a high-yield minus investment-grade yield spread (lower = stronger demand), the 7.6th percentile should map to a high score, not 49.8 — a contradiction. This report makes **no directional use** of it and only records the mismatch pending clarification of the definition.

**Divergence 4: VIX is Neutral while its 50-day average sits in the 4.4th percentile.**
VIX at 16.68 (42.6th percentile) is **+6.05%** above its 50-day average of 15.73, and that average is itself near a one-year low. In other words absolute volatility is not high, but it is in the process of retreating from a spike relative to a low recent baseline. That matches the path from the 10-16 peak of 19.99 to 16.68 on 10-21, with the five-day VIX average at 17.78 versus a 20-day average of 16.14.
- **Counter-evidence:** only if VIX falls below 15 with the 50-day average continuing to decline does the Neutral reading mean volatility risk has genuinely faded. Today the correct statement is "retreat from the panic peak", not "volatility risk resolved".

## Data limitations

1. **The daily change must be given as a range, not a point.** The `previous_close` basis of **+4.17** and the historical-series basis of **+1.80** differ by 2.37, and that gap comes from CNN revising the 10-20 record (prior live snapshot 27.2 → this run's series value 29.57). The reason is undisclosed; both bases agree in direction (up), so only the range and the two basis values are reported. A similar revision occurred last run (10-19 cut from 23.1071 to 20.8044, -2.30), confirming that the field is "the mean of the prior snapshot's sub-scores" while the history is recomputed retroactively.
2. **Today's 31.37 is provisional.** It may be revised tomorrow (precedents: 10-16 revised +4.13, 10-19 -2.30, 10-20 +2.37), so all magnitudes here are described as snapshot values.
3. **Credit spreads are missing this run.** FRED is under site-wide maintenance (HTTP 200 with an HTML maintenance body), so HY OAS, IG OAS, T10Y2Y and all DGS series were unobtainable. The last confirmed values, 3.29% / 0.87% on 10-19, come from the prior run. No substitute source was used to fabricate a same-day spread, and no spread was back-solved from HYG prices.
4. **Rates now come from the official Treasury curve.** `crisis_signals.py`'s `spread_10y2y` is not same-day as its printed `t10y`/`t2y`, and with FRED failing all of its rate fields are N/A; this report computes yields from the same-day treasury.gov CSV, including 10Y-30Y.
5. **Sub-indicators are sampled at different times:** momentum 16:31 ET, Put/Call 16:23 ET, VIX 16:15 ET, junk bond 16:10 ET, breadth/strength 18:06 ET, and **safe-haven demand 15:59 ET (one minute before the close)**. The composite stitches together different observation windows, so its "daily change" mixes them; safe-haven demand is especially exposed to pre-close quotes.
6. **Internal contradictions in CNN's payload remain unverified:** several sub-indicators' per-point `rating` conflicts with their own `rating` — breadth points labelled "extreme greed" while the sub-indicator is 18 (Extreme Fear); junk bond points "extreme fear" while the sub-indicator is 49.8 (Neutral); momentum points "extreme greed" while it is 47 (Neutral); VIX points "extreme fear" while it is 50 (Neutral); safe-haven points "extreme fear" and it is 20 (Extreme Fear, consistent). CNN does not explain the difference, and **no judgement here relies on per-point ratings**.
7. **Raw units are unverified:** the units of breadth 387.20, strength -7.6507, CNN junk 1.2121 and safe-haven 0.1789 cannot be confirmed from the payload; only relative position and percentile are used.
