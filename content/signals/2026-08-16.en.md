## Signal Analysis

### Signal 1: Edge AI Inference Tipping Point — Cloud Compute Monopoly Breaking?

**Logic chain:** needle (14MB model on phones/wearables) + unsloth (local LLM training) → AI inference migrating from cloud to edge → If edge devices can run capable enough models, cloud inference demand growth may slow → But training-side demand remains unchanged, and may even increase as edge model deployment scales → Short-term positive for edge chips (AVGO/QCOM), medium-term cloud inference growth needs re-evaluation (NVDA short-term unaffected, but long-term inference revenue growth uncertainty exists).

**Related tickers:** AVGO, QCOM (edge chip beneficiaries), NVDA (training-side fine short-term, long-term inference logic needs monitoring), AAPL (biggest beneficiary of on-device AI capability strengthening)

### Signal 2: NVIDIA Extending from Hardware to AI Infrastructure Software Stack

**Logic chain:** NVIDIA releases Switchyard (cross-model LLM router) → NVIDIA no longer just selling GPUs, now building AI middleware layer → Increases customer stickiness, locks in ecosystem → Upgrades from "selling shovels" to "selling shovels + mine management systems" → Long-term, increases NVDA's software recurring revenue mix, reduces hardware cyclicality → But also means NVIDIA now competes with Cloudflare, LangChain and potential partners at the routing layer.

**Related tickers:** NVDA (direct beneficiary), NET/Cloudflare (computer project creates competitive dynamic worth watching)

### Signal 3: GLP-1 Indication Expansion + Consumer Credit Stress Rising

**Logic chain:** Semaglutide linked to lower dementia risk (331-point HN post) → GLP-1 drugs expanding from weight loss → cardiovascular → dementia prevention, indication ceiling keeps rising → NVO/LLY long-term revenue space expanding → Meanwhile credit card debt hits $1.26T record → Consumer discretionary spending capacity under pressure → But GLP-1 has strong "necessity + out-of-pocket" characteristics, likely more resilient than discretionary spending in economic downturn.

**Related tickers:** NVO, LLY (GLP-1 beneficiaries), XLY (consumer discretionary ETF, bearish signal from credit card debt)
