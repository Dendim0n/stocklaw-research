**Related names**: AMZN (strengthens cloud data layer, long-term beneficiary); MDB, SNOW (rising competition, valuation narrative pressure); likewise other independent database names.

### Signal 2: Chinese open-source models ship in lockstep; global inference costs keep falling
**Logic chain**: GLM-5.3-Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next top HN the same day; China's model iteration accelerates with open weights + low-cost inference → global unit inference costs keep falling and model-layer monetization gets squeezed → beneficiaries shift to application and compute layers where usage expands.
**Related names**: NVDA (expansion in total inference demand vs. narrowing China exposure under export controls — a two-way tug); BABA (Qwen ecosystem monetization); Chinese AI application layer.

**Logic chain**: Sustained AI datacenter power demand → first startup commercially produces HALEU, moving fuel supply from government programs to the market → nuclear/SMR narrative advances from "story" to "supply-chain landing."
**Related names**: Uranium and fuel chain (CCJ, LEU-related), SMR/advanced reactor concepts (OKLO, SMR-related); note HALEU volume is only an early milestone — far from scaled output, so theme volatility will outrun fundamental realization.
