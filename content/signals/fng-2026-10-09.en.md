## Indicator Divergence: Possible Explanations and Counter-evidence

**Core divergence: Fear sentiment (38.11) coexists with Greed in credit/market indicators.**

1. **Stock breadth improving vs. stock strength still deteriorating**
   - Breadth (up/down volume ratio 151.62→167.11) improved, indicating expanding trading volume on advancing stocks.
   - But strength (RSI -8.88→-8.76) remains in extreme fear, meaning rallies are oversold bounces, not trend reversals.
   - Counter-evidence: If breadth sustains above 160 into next week and strength turns positive, current Fear has technical basis; if breadth retreats while strength worsens, the rally is unsustainable.

2. **VIX Neutral vs. FNG Fear**
   - VIX=15.41 is inherently moderate (historical median ~20), scored Neutral.
   - But FNG composite 38.11 is dragged by stock strength/breadth/PCR.
   - Explanation: Options market does not price significant volatility risk, but trend-following indicators (RSI, breadth) show weakness. VIX "calmness" may be a lagging indicator — it measures implied vol, not directional pressure.

3. **Junk Bond Extreme Greed vs. Fear sentiment**
   - HY OAS 1.14%→1.16% still at low levels; credit markets deem default risk controllable.
   - Divergence from FNG Fear: credit markets look at fundamentals/default probability, while FNG sub-indicators reflect short-term trading sentiment.
   - Counter-evidence: If 10Y-2Y spread widens further or HY OAS spikes to 3%+, credit market optimism may reverse.

4. **Safe haven demand declining (4.71→2.94)**
   - Capital flowing from Treasuries/gold into risk assets.
   - Consistent with FNG still in Fear — safe-haven decline may be post-panic rebound capital entering, not a trend reversal.
