## Possible Explanations for the Divergences, and Counter-Evidence

**Divergence 1: the composite is in extreme fear while the index-level decline remains mild.** On 10-16 the SPX fell only 1.09% and closed **+1.58%** above its 125-day moving average (independently computed 125-day MA 7,540.12; that deviation is the **lowest of the 127 computable observations since 2026-04-17**, against a window median of +5.69% and a high of +9.38%). Explanation: CNN's sub-scores are normalized against each indicator's own historical distribution and are highly sensitive to regime shifts — a VIX close of 19.99 sits only at the 81.7th percentile of its own one-year sample (`^VIX` one-year range 13.47–31.05) yet is normalized to a **score of 3.97**; the momentum sub-indicator measures not the index level but the cushion above its 125-day average, and the thinnest cushion in a year earns 23.6. **Counter-evidence (against dismissing this as a false alarm):** breadth remains at the 5.2nd percentile and strength at the 4.0th percentile of their own samples, so participation itself is very weak; the 5-day average put/call ratio rose to 0.82 (84th percentile), so demand for protection is genuinely rising. Extreme fear here looks more like a price on **a vanishing trend cushion plus rising hedging costs** than on realized losses.

**Divergence 2: CNN's junk-bond metric (1.2306%, score 42.6 / Fear) and FRED HY OAS (3.23%, 10-15) point to different states.** The two differ in constituents, convention and source and are **neither interchangeable nor subtractable**. Facts: the CNN-basis spread has widened for 5 consecutive records (1.1458% → 1.2306%, +8.5bp cumulative), the same direction as equities but tiny; FRED HY OAS instead tightened from 3.25% on 10-14 to 3.23% on 10-15. Explanation: credit is not currently confirming the equity market's emotional collapse; Junk Bond Demand scores only 42.6 because it is measured **relative to its own history**, not because the absolute level has deteriorated. **Counter-evidence:** if the CNN-basis spread breaks above its sample median of 1.3045%, or FRED HY OAS breaks above 3.50%, the "credit is not confirming" judgment fails.

**Divergence 3: the breadth reading has risen for 8 consecutive records while the composite printed a new low.** The reading climbed from 141.70 on 10-05 to 312.08 (+120%), against a sample median of 1,027.07. Explanation: this is a repair from an extremely low base and remains deeply depressed. **Counter-evidence:** if the repair stalls and the reading falls back below 200, breadth has weakened a second time, resonating with a broadening index decline.

**Divergence 4 (what we do not infer):** on 10-16 the Russell 2000 fell 1.66%, the SPX 1.09% and the Nasdaq 100 2.51% — small caps outperformed and large-cap tech led the decline. That comes from index prices themselves and **cannot** be reverse-engineered from CNN's breadth or strength readings. Likewise, rising put/call and VIX show only that protection costs more and **cannot** be used to identify "smart money" direction.

## Data Limitations

1. **Two day-change bases exist** (-13.60 snapshot / -12.40 series); the 1.20 gap comes from CNN's post-hoc revision of the 10-15 record (prior live value 31.54 vs. this run's series value 30.34). We adopt the snapshot basis because it matches this run's `previous_close` field of 31.5429 to the digit and is reproducible from the seven sub-scores; the conflict is shown in full above and is not resolved.
2. **The composite timestamp is not unique:** 10-16 appears twice in the series (00:00 UTC and 22:13:24 UTC) with identical values (17.9383), so there is no conflict and the latest timestamp is kept by rule. The series has 252 entries and 251 unique daily records after deduplication.
3. **Sub-indicator sampling times are not uniform:** Momentum 16:42 ET, Breadth/Strength 18:13 ET, Put/Call 17:22 ET, Junk Bond 16:10 ET, VIX 16:15 ET, and **Safe Haven 15:59 ET (one minute before the close)**. The composite combines sub-indicators sampled at different times, so the -13.60 day change mixes observation windows; Safe Haven in particular warrants caution.
4. **CNN's internal contradiction is unverified:** for Momentum, Breadth and Junk Bond the per-point ratings conflict with the indicators' own ratings (see the note under the sub-indicator table); this run does not use them interpretively.
5. **CNN's normalization method is not exposed in the payload:** each indicator's historical normalization window is longer than the 251 daily records in this series, so we report scores and ratings only, using raw values' percentiles within their own series as a secondary reference. Divergences between percentile and score (e.g. VIX at the 81.7th percentile scoring 3.97) reflect convention differences, are **not treated as data errors, and are not used to reverse-engineer CNN's algorithm**.
6. **Units unverified:** the units of the breadth and strength raw values cannot be confirmed from the payload or CNN's public documentation, and the local script's comment is inconsistent with the magnitude of the readings; they are marked as unverified and used only for relative position.
7. **Credit and leverage data age differently:** FRED HY/IG OAS through 10-15 (10-16 unpublished); the DGS series through 10-15, with only `T10Y2Y` available for 10-16; margin debt for September (October data due the third week of November); ETF flows as of the 9/30 month-end value plus the week ending 10-09. Each is labelled with its own as-of date; **no cross-period splicing** was performed.
8. **Script constants are unusable:** the margin debt ($1.304T, 2026-04), IPO (~73 deals) and ETF ($856B) values hard-coded in `crisis_signals.py` disagree with the primary sources fetched this run. This report substitutes FINRA / Renaissance / ETFGI primary data with explicit as-of dates.
9. **What we do not infer:** record margin debt or ETF inflows do not by themselves establish a crisis; put/call or VIX do not identify "smart money" direction; breadth/strength are not used to infer large- vs. small-cap moves. This output contains **no** uncalibrated "red-light count" or crisis probability.
