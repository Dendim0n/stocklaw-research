### 3. Signal Analysis

**Signal 1: Robot Industry Capital Shift → Watch Tesla (TSLA), Rockwell Automation (ROK)**
- Hyundai's $325M acquisition of Boston Dynamics and SoftBank's exit signal that industrial capital is replacing VC money in robotics. This means the industry is moving from "proof of concept" to "scale deployment." Tesla Optimus and Figure AI valuations may need re-evaluation through an industrial application lens. Rockwell Automation and similar industrial automation firms could also benefit.

**Signal 2: AI Inference Cost Optimization Demand Explodes → Watch NVIDIA (NVDA), AMD, Google (GOOGL)**
- headroom (+4,000 stars/day) and LMCache (+876/week) surging simultaneously show that inference efficiency is developers' most urgent pain point. Low-cost inference tools won't reduce GPU demand — they'll lower the barrier to AI adoption and expand total compute consumption. But they may pressure NVIDIA's premium pricing power over the long term.

**Signal 3: EU AI Regulation Tightening → Watch Microsoft (MSFT), Google (GOOGL), Meta (META)**
- Norway restricting school AI combined with Google's antitrust behavior shows escalating regulatory pressure on tech giants in Europe. Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, and Meta AI expansion in European markets may face headwinds. Watch Q2 earnings for European revenue growth changes.
