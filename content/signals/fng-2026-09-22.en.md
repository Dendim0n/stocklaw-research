## Divergence Analysis and Counter-Evidence

**Core divergence: Fear sentiment vs. Normal credit / safe-haven flows**

1. **Fear may be driven by short-term technicals and options pricing** -- VIX, PCR, and stock breadth may have weakened in mid-September due to post-earnings profit-taking or macro expectation shifts, but credit spreads (2.68% HY OAS) and equity-bond spreads (3.29) did not follow.

2. **Counter-evidence:** If credit markets were also deteriorating (HY OAS breaking 3%→5%), current fear could be a precursor to larger drawdown. Currently at 2.68%, still in healthy range.

3. **Safe haven demand score 57 (Greed)** indicates equity-bond spread still wide -- stocks still offer yield advantage vs. bonds, no panic rotation into fixed income. This contradicts the "fear" composite conclusion.

**Confirmation needed:** Whether VIX is rising in tandem, PCR turning bearish, and stock breadth (advancing vs. declining issues) continues to deteriorate. Without these confirmations, composite score of 28.7 alone does not indicate systemic risk rise.
