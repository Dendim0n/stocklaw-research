## 4. Indicator Divergences and Alternative Explanations

**Divergence 1: VIX Neutral vs. Price Breadth Extreme Fear**

VIX at 50 (neutral) while price breadth at 0.2 (Extreme Fear) indicates extreme market internal divergence—few weighted stocks prop up the index,多数 stocks weak. Possible explanations:
- **Interpretation**: Large-cap concentration (Mag 7 effect); VIX measures index-level implied volatility, not breadth
- **Counter-evidence**: If VIX were genuinely low, both SP500 and SP125 momentum should improve. Both are 28.2 (Fear), supporting the breadth deterioration thesis

**Divergence 2: Current F&G 43.14 vs. Historical Series 28.09 (Oct 1)**

- **Interpretation**: Sub-indicator data updated Oct 2–5 (esp. VIX or PCR improvement), but historical series not yet written with latest trading day
- **Counter-evidence**: If no new data was written post-Oct 1, then 43.14 may include non-trading-day (weekend) cached values—requires verification

**Divergence 3: Safe Haven Demand Fear vs. Normalizing Curve**

Safe haven demand at 35.8 (Fear) shows funds still flowing into Treasuries; yet 10Y-2Y at +47bp (normal-flat), not reflecting deepening inversion from flight-to-quality. This may indicate safe-haven flows concentrated in short-end Treasuries, with long-end rates not declining correspondingly.
