### 3. Signal Analysis

**Signal 1: AI agent ecosystem enters a "skills-library standardization" phase — enterprise adoption accelerates**
- Evidence: concentrated momentum in agent-skills repos (archify +18k stars/week, scientific-agent-skills 39k stars, dense updates to Claude/Cursor official plugin directories).
- Logic chain: agent skill standardization → lower enterprise AI integration cost and adoption barrier → faster AI monetization for software vendors, rising AI infrastructure and observability demand.
- Related tickers: Microsoft (Copilot/enterprise AI), Salesforce/ServiceNow (Agentforce-style products), Datadog/Snowflake (AI observability & data infra); long-term positive for application-layer software.

**Signal 2: Inference "free-ification" and maturing open-source models push pricing power further down the stack**
- Evidence: high traction for freellmapi (7.4B tokens/month free) and free-claude-code (1.3B+ tokens) aggregation services.
- Logic chain: open-source models approaching closed-source quality + sharply falling marginal inference cost → token price war → margin pressure at the model/API layer; compute demand still grows but pricing power shifts to infrastructure and upper-layer applications.
- Related tickers: modestly bearish — pure model/API layer pricing pressure; supportive of inference volume growth (long-run compute consumption still rises; NVDA thesis intact but elasticity dulls); watch cloud vendors and the open-source camp (Meta, etc.).

**Signal 3: Europe regulation (encryption backdoors) + AI supply-chain security — cybersecurity and compliance spend rising**
- Evidence: EU ProtectEU reviving encryption backdoors (347-pt thread) + in-depth HuggingFace incident postmortem.
- Logic chain: regulation and security incidents resonating → enterprises raise cybersecurity/compliance budgets and AI supply-chain audit demand → security software benefits; meanwhile backdoors impose compliance and trust costs on big-tech privacy products.
- Related tickers: PANW/CRWD/FTNT/ZS and peers; big tech (AAPL/GOOGL/META) faces rising compliance costs — net-negative for risk appetite.

> Note: Europe drought (248 pts) is a separate macro risk worth tracking — hydropower decline → energy and agricultural prices → inflation expectations; if it persists it can transmit into commodity and rates pricing.
