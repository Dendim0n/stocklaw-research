## Signal Analysis

**Signal 1: AI Supply Bottleneck — From GPUs to Data Centers Under Pressure**

Kimi K3 subscription halt + OpenAI context window reduction + data center NIMBY movement — three independent signals converging on one conclusion: AI infrastructure supply cannot keep up with demand. Logic chain: overwhelming demand → compute shortage → GPU/data center capex must continue accelerating → NVDA, AVGO, ANET benefit. However, data center construction headwinds could push cloud pricing higher medium-term, favoring incumbents with existing capacity (MSFT Azure, AMZN AWS, GOOGL GCP).

**Signal 2: AI Coding Tools Enter a New Arms Race Phase**

Codex nearing 100K stars, Claude Code rewriting in Rust, OpenInterpreter at 66K stars, kimi-cli and jcode rising fast — AI coding agents are becoming the new platform-level entry point. Logic chain: coding tools are the high ground for developer traffic → whoever controls the coding entry point controls the AI development ecosystem → MSFT/GitHub (Copilot + Codex) and Anthropic (Claude Code) in direct competition → bullish for cloud infrastructure and API consumption.

**Signal 3: AI Trading Democratization — What Vibe-Trading's 25K Stars Mean**

An AI quant trading project gaining 5K+ stars in one week signals real and massive retail demand for AI-assisted trading. Logic chain: AI lowers quant barriers → more retail traders use AI trading tools → retail brokers (HOOD, FUTU) may benefit from higher trading volumes, but also face disintermediation risk from open-source tools → traditional active fund alpha continues to compress.
