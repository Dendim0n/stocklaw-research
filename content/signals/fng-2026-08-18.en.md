## Structural Contradiction Analysis

**Core Contradiction: Junk Bond Extreme Greed (99) vs Stock Price Strength Fear (27.4)**

This is the most significant structural divergence currently. The 72-point extreme gap implies:

- **Fixed income market**: Credit spreads compressed to the extreme; investors chasing yield at any cost — extremely high risk appetite
- **Equity market**: Very few stocks making new highs; market breadth narrowing; concentration risk elevated
- **Historical parallel**: This combination has historically appeared near market tops — credit markets not pricing risk while equity internals already deteriorating

**Secondary contradictions:**
- Composite index declining 5 days (66.63 → 59.97) while junk bond spreads continue to tighten → credit market more optimistic than equity market
- Safe haven demand dropping from 5.20 to 3.74 → bonds beginning to outperform stocks, suggesting defensive rotation may be underway
