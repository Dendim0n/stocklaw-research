## Divergences: possible explanations and counter-evidence

**Divergence 1: sentiment at the 8.8th percentile of the year while the index sits at the 82.5th percentile.**

The S&P closed at 7,663.43 versus a sample high of 7,818.93, i.e. **-1.99%** from the high. Explanation: four of the seven inputs (breadth, strength, VIX, Put/Call) are participation/volatility measures that stay dull at low levels in a mega-cap-led tape — which is exactly why the composite has not touched 50 in 38 sessions. Counter-evidence: a true "few stocks holding the index up while the rest bleed" regime would show equal weight persistently underperforming. The opposite is true: over ten sessions **RSP +3.62%, QQQ -5.77%, IWM -0.32%**. The more accurate description is that **sentiment gauges are dull at low levels while internal structure is improving** — these are not contradictory.

**Divergence 2: breadth and strength improving while the index craters.**

This is the cleanest signal of the run: CNN breadth +8.85% and strength +0.679 against RSP +0.73%, DJI +0.02%, IWM +0.07%. Explanation: **the damage is concentrated in high-weight, high-multiple AI names** (press reports: Nvidia -4.4%, Lam Research -11.8%, CoreWeave -10.1%, Meta -7%). Counter-evidence: if this were a single-name accident, the sector would disperse; instead the entire AI capex chain sold off together (semicap, compute leasing, mega-cap platforms), **which supports a narrative repricing rather than an isolated miss**.

**Divergence 3: CNN junk bond demand deteriorating while FRED high-yield spreads tighten.**

The CNN raw value rose 1.21209 → 1.25278 (score 49.8 → 32.2, inverted), while FRED `BAMLH0A0HYM2` **tightened** from 3.33% on 10-20 to 3.27% on 10-21. Explanation: these are not the same measure — the CNN item reflects **tradable-ETF relative demand** (HYG +0.16% versus IEF +1.08% that day; HYG/IEF -0.69% on the day, -2.39% over ten sessions), whereas FRED is the **cash-bond option-adjusted spread**, published a day in arrears. Counter-evidence: genuine credit deterioration would push HY OAS above 3.5% within one to three sessions and IG OAS above 1.10%. **Neither has happened.**

**Divergence 4 (unexplained): gold fell 2.63% on a risk-off day.**

GLD -2.63% while 10Y nominal yields fell 17bp, the dollar was flat (UUP 0.00%) and geopolitical risk escalated (Brent +3.85%). The textbook framework says all three are gold-positive. Candidate explanations include position liquidation/margin calls, profit-taking after a long run, or the market reading escalation as a dollar-liquidity squeeze rather than a safe-haven bid. **No verifiable flow data is available on this machine to support any of them**, so this is logged as an unexplained divergence rather than turned into a conclusion. It also flags that today's "safe haven" was **bond-market-specific**, not cross-asset.

**Divergence 5: an -11.56 sentiment collapse against a -1.21% index move.**

The momentum cushion compressed only 1.30 percentage points yet the score fell 24.4. The market did not change by 24 points; the normalisation window amplified a low-volatility input. **Practical implication: treating the FNG daily change as a second derivative of sentiment systematically overstates it.** Level (8.8th percentile) and structure (are breadth and strength still improving) are the more reliable reads.

## What would escalate — and what would ease

**Escalation triggers (any one raises the risk tier):**

1. **Credit confirmation:** HY OAS above **3.60%** for two consecutive sessions (now 3.27%) and IG OAS above **1.10%** (now 0.88%). This is the only evidence that would reclassify the episode from valuation event to credit event.
2. **Breadth collapse:** breadth raw below **300**, or RSP turning negative while IWM breaks its recent low — the damage spreading from heavyweights to the whole market.
3. **Bonds losing their haven role:** equities falling while the **10Y rises more than 10bp** (currently the opposite). That is the stagflation / term-premium-loss shape.
4. **A higher volatility centre:** VIX above **22** with its 50-day MA (15.8) accelerating upward; the MA is rising now, but slowly.
5. **Leverage not clearing:** November's FINRA October margin debt setting another record while the index is more than 5% off its high — leverage and price deteriorating together is the classic tail-risk precursor.

**Easing triggers (any one signals sentiment repair, but structure still needs confirmation):**

1. The composite back above **>25** (out of Extreme Fear) **with breadth and strength still rising** — a lift driven only by the VIX item is algorithmic noise and does not count.
2. The momentum cushion back above **2%** while QQQ stops underperforming RSP (the ten-session gap is RSP +3.62% versus QQQ -5.77%).
3. VIX back below **17** and Put/Call below **0.75** (now 0.817, 81.3rd percentile).
4. **Brent back below $105** — today stacked AI capex fears on top of geopolitics; if oil does not retreat, the inflation channel keeps pressuring the long end independently of the AI story.
5. The 10-22 HY OAS print confirming **3.2%–3.3%**, i.e. credit not responding at all.

**Where this puts us:** sentiment is at Extreme Fear, price is only 2% off its high, and credit has not moved. That combination looks more like a partial unwind of a crowded trade than the start of a systemic event. But "not a crisis" is not the same as "buyable" — breadth in the 8th percentile, leverage at records and a deeper long-end inversion all argue against raising risk budgets here.

## Limitations

1. **The historical series was revised, so there are two daily changes.** CNN revised the 10-21 composite from **31.3714 to 27.2264 (-4.145)** while the `previous_close` field still carries the pre-revision 31.3714. Hence **-11.56 (previous_close basis)** and **-7.41 (revised-series basis)**. **Both point the same way but differ by 4.15, and this report does not declare a single "correct" decline.** This is the third consecutive run with a revised historical value; CNN gives no reason.
2. **Last run's VIX sub-score of 50.0 is likely a default value, and part of this run's decline is restoration.** The basis: in the prior snapshot `vix` and `vix_50` both printed exactly 50.0; this run both print 17.6923, explained by the VIX/50MA ratio of 1.2031. **This is an inference, not a CNN-confirmed fact**, so the -32.31 should not be read entirely as deterioration.
3. **The API's per-point `rating` field conflicts with the score-based rating and affects chart colouring.** For example the 7,663.43 momentum point is tagged "extreme greed" and the 421.44 breadth point likewise, while both scores rate Extreme Fear. The sub-trend chart colours points by that per-point field, so **its Momentum and Breadth panels show red dots that contradict the table's ratings**. Ratings in this report bind to score only (as required); the per-point field's algorithm is **unverified**.
4. **Two raw units are unobtainable.** Stock price strength reads **-6.9716** (negative) while the script's own comment calls it "percentage near 52-week highs" — the two are incompatible; junk bond demand's **1.25278** is not on the scale of a spread in percent. Both are treated as "CNN methodology undisclosed" and are not used in any cross-indicator conversion.
5. **Credit data lag one session.** Today's equity and rate prints are closes, but HY/IG OAS only run through 10-21. **Every cross-date comparison is labelled in the text**; the 10-21 spread is not presented as 10-22 evidence.
6. **No new ETF flow, IPO or margin debt figures this run.** ICI returns 403; `crisis_signals.py`'s margin debt ($1.304T, Apr 2026) and IPO count (~73) are **hardcoded constants, not live data**; FINRA October margin debt is unpublished.
7. **News items are second-hand.** The references to roughly $825bn of Meta AI-related commitments, Tesla trimming capex and Nvidia's CEO calling financing "scarce" come from press reports (AP/WSJ/BI/NBC); **the underlying 8-K/10-Q was not checked here**, and one reported comparison base looks like a typo and is not cited.
8. **Charts were proof-read for readable text only.** The trend chart's date (2026-10-22) and end reading (20, integerised), the radar centre (20 / Extreme Fear) and the sub-trend grid's seven terminal readings all match the JSON; the PNG filenames carry the run date 2026-10-23 rather than the data date, per the established naming rule.
