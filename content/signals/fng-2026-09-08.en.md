- **Stock Strength: 12.6 (Extreme Fear)** ← Equity investors are panicking
- **Junk Bond Demand: 76.2 (Extreme Greed)** ← Bond investors are greedy
- **Interpretation:** The equity and bond markets are completely split. The bond market sees minimal credit risk (HY OAS only 2.65%), while the equity market is pricing in serious downside risk.
