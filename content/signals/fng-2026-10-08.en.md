## Indicator Divergence: Possible Explanations and Counter-evidence

**Core Divergence: Index Momentum Greed vs. Stock Breadth/Strength Extreme Fear**

This combination (strong index but weak stock participation) has appeared repeatedly in history, typically pointing to:

1. **Narrow-led rally** — A few mega-cap stocks (e.g., Magnificent 7) pulling SP500/SP1250 momentum higher, while most constituents are flat or declining. Strength -8.877 and breadth 151.6 confirm "index good, stocks bad" structure.
2. **Options market divergence** — P/C 0.761 leans fear (puts outnumber calls), suggesting derivatives participants still hedge tail risk, contrasting with index greed.
3. **VIX neutrality is the buffer** — VIX=15.08 sits in low territory, not rising despite breadth deterioration. If VIX rose alongside worsening breadth, that would be a healthier correction signal. Currently VIX flat means vol hasn't priced breadth risk yet.

**Counter-evidence:** Junk bond demand extreme greed (93.2) and safe haven demand greed (70.8) indicate **credit markets and capital flows are not following stock breadth weaker**. If breadth problems were becoming systemic, credit spreads should widen and capital should flow into Treasuries/gold. Current credit calm means institutional capital still longs risk assets.
