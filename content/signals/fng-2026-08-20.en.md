## Structural Contradiction Analysis

**Core divergence: Junk bond extreme greed (92.8) vs stock price strength fear (26.2)**

The extreme ~66.6-pt gap has persisted for a third day:

- **Fixed income**: Spreads compressed to the limit; investors are chasing credit risk for yield
- **Equities**: Only 44% of stocks sit above their 50-day MA; breadth is weak despite the modest uptick
- **Historical lesson**: Credit markets staying bullish while internals of the equity market weaken is a pattern often seen near tops — credit optimism tends to be a lagging signal

**Secondary divergences:**
- Composite reclaimed the greed zone, yet momentum (44.8) and price strength (26.2) remain in fear → the index is being propped up by large caps while breadth lags; the quality of the bounce is unverified
- Junk bond demand (92.8) stays pinned at highs vs weak price strength → the disconnect has not narrowed for three days
- Safe haven demand (68) recovered from 2.79% to 3.65%, pausing the defensive rotation → supportive for equities near-term, but also signals risk appetite re-heating
