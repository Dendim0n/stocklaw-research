This combination is the classic "too much money, too few targets" — liquidity is flooding risk assets, but leadership is highly concentrated. Historically, similar divergences have appeared multiple times in late-cycle environments.

**2. Momentum Greed vs Breadth Fear — Index Illusion**
Market Momentum 74.6 vs Stock Price Strength 28.6. The S&P 500 is well above its moving average, but fewer than 1% of stocks are making new highs. The index is being carried by a handful of mega-caps — surface prosperity masking deteriorating individual stock participation.

Safe Haven Demand 78.8 (near-zero demand for safe havens) vs VIX 50.0 (Neutral). If safe haven demand is truly this low, VIX should be lower (more greedy). VIX sitting at neutral suggests the options market is still pricing in some tail risk — a subtle cognitive gap with the spot market's extreme optimism.

### 3. Margin Debt

The current $1.502T absolute level and 4.63% GDP ratio are both all-time highs. Leverage can climb for extended periods, but it is a critical accumulator of systemic fragility.
