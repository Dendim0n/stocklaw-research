## 📊 Signal Analysis

### Signal 1: The "Debt Inflection" Risk in AI Capex
**Chain:** BIS report → AI financing shifting from cash flows to debt → rising rate sensitivity → capex sustainability questioned
BIS warns from a central bank perspective about AI industry financing structure changes. If rates stay elevated, debt-dependent AI infrastructure investment faces cost pressure. Hyperscaler AI capex (MSFT, GOOG, AMZN) continues accelerating, but payback timelines are being scrutinized more carefully by markets.
**Related tickers:** NVDA (direct capex beneficiary with cyclical risk), MSFT/GOOG/AMZN (cloud AI ROI), SMH (semiconductors broadly)

### Signal 2: AI Agent Infrastructure Arms Race
**Chain:** CubeSandbox + orca + herdr + OmniRoute → agent runtime infrastructure exploding → shifting demand patterns for compute and cloud services
Multiple agent infrastructure projects crossing 10K stars in a single week (CubeSandbox, orca, herdr, OmniRoute all above 10K). This is not coincidence — agents moving from "toys" to "production" requires sandboxes, orchestration, and routing infrastructure. Bullish for cloud infra and dev tools, but also means lower barriers to entry in the agent application layer.
**Related tickers:** NET (edge compute), MDB (data infrastructure), MSFT/AMZN/GOOG (cloud platforms), DDOG (observability)

### Signal 3: Edge AI + Model Miniaturization Accelerating
**Chain:** Bonsai 27B on phone + edge inference maturing → cloud inference demand potentially diverted → edge chips benefit
A 27B model running on a phone means model compression and quantization are crossing critical thresholds. If on-device AI becomes the dominant inference method, it directly challenges cloud AI inference economics. Apple and Qualcomm's NPU strategies may prove more valuable than the market currently prices in.
**Related tickers:** QCOM, ARM (edge chips), AAPL (on-device AI ecosystem), NVDA (near-term unaffected but long-term landscape shifts)
