## Structural Contradictions

**Core divergence: Junk bond demand extreme greed (95.8) vs stock price strength fear (29.4)**

A 66.4-point gap, held for the 5th straight day:

- **Fixed income**: spreads compressed to the limit (HY OAS 2.75% at absolute lows), investors chasing credit risk for yield — extreme risk appetite
- **Equities**: index bounced back into the greed zone, but price strength (29.4) and momentum (39) remain in fear — breadth (57.6) is repairing, strength has not caught up
- **Historical lesson**: credit staying bullish while equity internals are weak is a classic top-region pattern — credit optimism is often a lagging signal; watch for a turn that could amplify equity drawdowns

**Secondary divergences:**
- Momentum reading (39, fear) vs actual index position (+6% above 125-day MA) → CNN's segmented weighting skews conservative; read the level alongside the score, avoid over-interpreting the single point
- Strength (29.4) vs breadth (57.6) divergence is converging: strength up 4 days, breadth up 2 days, internals repairing — but strength still in fear; the bounce needs breadth→strength transmission to confirm
- Safe haven demand (71) back to greed, VIX back to 15 → risk-on + low vol combo helps a short-term bounce but also re-accumulates sentiment; the top-range battle is not over
