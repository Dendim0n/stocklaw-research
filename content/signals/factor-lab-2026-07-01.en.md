### Key Findings

**1. Momentum Factor Absolute Dominance**
- ICIR 2.70, 100% positive IC rate, zero exceptions over 39 weeks
- "Stronger gets stronger" self-reinforcing loop still running
- But Q5 vs Q1 spread at 8% is at extreme levels — crowding risk is high

**2. Value Factor "Dead Cat Bounce"**
- EP and FCF ICs are negative (-0.06 and -0.06), yet Q1 (highest value) outperforms Q5
- Interpretation: value stocks declining less than growth stocks in an overall down market = "defensive value"
- This is NOT a value regime rotation — it's mean reversion in overvalued growth dragging relative returns

**3. Volatility Factor Directional Anomaly**
- High-vol Q5 returns 8.15% vs low-vol Q1 at 0.54%
- Contradicts traditional low-volatility premium
- Market is "buying volatility" rather than "selling vol" — classic late-cycle risk-seeking
- This pattern reverses violently in drawdowns

**4. Mild Small-Cap Premium**
- Size ICIR 1.25, small-caps generating positive alpha
- But modest (-2.06% spread), suggesting liquidity conditions still manageable
- Watch for deterioration if FNG continues declining

### Strategy Implications

- **Momentum crowding extreme** — any reversal could trigger outsized drawdown
- **Value + Low Vol defense** — relatively favored in current environment
- **High-vol trading reversal risk** — current "buy vol" positioning unwinds fast when VIX spikes
- **Monitor small-cap liquidity** — may be first to suffer if sentiment deteriorates further
