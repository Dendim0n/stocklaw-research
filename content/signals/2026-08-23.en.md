Logic chain: tools like NVIDIA Switchyard and llmfit let app developers cheaply switch/route across models → model supply becomes substitutable and inference costs keep falling → pure model-API pricing pressure, value migrates to the inference and distribution layers.
Related: NVIDIA (inference layer) and pure software model vendors facing API pricing pressure; lower app-layer costs are a net tailwind for downstream application margins.
