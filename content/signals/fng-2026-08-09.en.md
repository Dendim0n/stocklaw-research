| **Middle Ground** | VIX(50), Safe Haven(64.6) | 50-65 |

**Three extreme greed (≥80) vs two fear (≤40), a 65-point spread.**

This is a textbook late-cycle bull market divergence:
- **Liquidity/sentiment extremely optimistic**: tight spreads, call-heavy options, momentum chasing
- **Fundamentals/breadth clearly lagging**: few new highs, narrow advance

Historical pattern: When momentum indicators ≥80 and breadth ≤40, the rally is typically driven by a handful of mega-cap stocks (concentration risk). Market fragility increases. Any catalyst (rate shock, geopolitical event, earnings miss) could trigger a concentration reversal.

- **<20 (Extreme Fear)**: 0 indicators

- **Lowest**: Stock Price Strength 32.2 — severe stock-level divergence
