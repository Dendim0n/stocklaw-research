🔥 **Key Headlines This Hour**

**Goldman Sachs Lifts AMD Target to $640 (+42%)**
Goldman Sachs raised its AMD price target from $450 to $640, a more than 40% increase, signaling continued conviction in the AI chip thesis. AMD has already seen significant gains recently; this move may further catalyze sentiment.

**SK Hynix Officially Launches US IPO Roadshow, ADRs Oversubscribed**
SK Hynix kicked off its US IPO marketing roadshow on Monday, offering ~17.79 million common shares as ADRs, already oversubscribed. Subscription closes at 4:00 PM New York time on July 8. The offering rides the wave of elevated investor enthusiasm for memory chip names.

**HKEX USD Gold Futures Hit All-Time High Volume**
Hong Kong Exchange USD gold futures recorded 6,676 contracts traded during the day session, a new all-time high, far surpassing the previous record of 3,039 contracts set in November 2022. Bid-ask spreads on the active month narrowed to 1-2 ticks, signaling dramatically improved liquidity.

**Hapag-Lloyd Immediately Resumes Red Sea Transit**
German shipping giant Hapag-Lloyd announced its SE3 service will immediately switch to sailing through the Red Sea instead of rounding the Cape of Good Hope. This is a significant signal following the easing of Red Sea tensions, with potential downstream benefits for shipping costs.

**Ukrainian Drones Reach Omsk Industrial Hub in Russia**
The local governor confirmed that multiple Ukrainian drones successfully reached the northern industrial center of Omsk, marking a geographic expansion of the conflict.

**Yen Intervention Debate Intensifies: SocGen Cites Growth Needed, Ex-Official Calls for 130**
USD/JPY rose back above 162. Societe Generale noted that intervention can only buy time; sustained yen recovery requires improved growth prospects (Japan still holds ~$1.3 trillion in FX reserves). A former head of Japan's FX policy argued the yen's fair value is around 130.

**Memory Chip Price Hikes Fully Hit Consumer Market**
1TB SSDs have surged from ¥500 to ¥1,000 at retail. Dealers are reportedly "panicking and refusing to stockpile." Memory chip price increases have fully transmitted from upstream to the consumer end.

**JPMorgan: Luxury Sector Q2 Data Signals Industry-Wide Improvement**
US credit card data shows resilient consumer spending, with Q2 trends flat to improving versus Q1.
