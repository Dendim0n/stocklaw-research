🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

The 7% crude oil crash is the clearest signal yet that the Middle East storm is calming — the US-Iran ceasefire is moving from paper to reality. But shedding nearly two months of geopolitical premium in a single day suggests panic unwinding may be overdone.

Gold holding a 1% gain amid the oil rout tells us the market is not truly risk-on — the safe-haven bid persists. This week's FOMC decision is the real main event.

Netanyahu and Zelenskyy converging on Washington simultaneously means both the Middle East and Eastern European fronts are seeking Trump's definitive stance. White House diplomatic intensity will be extreme this week.

Warsh's "policy black box" approach has Wall Street deeply uncomfortable — the absence of forward guidance amplifies the potential for outsized moves after the rate decision.

BofA's August-October seasonal warning deserves attention, but historical patterns are probabilities, not certainties. The real variables: can the US-Iran ceasefire hold, and will the Fed actually turn hawkish.
