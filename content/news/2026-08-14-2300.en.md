🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. US Consumer Sentiment Falls for First Time in Three Months**
University of Michigan preliminary August consumer sentiment index dropped to 51, down from 55.2 in July — an approximately 8% decline. Consumers' expectations for business conditions deteriorated notably, with inflation concerns rising. Republican respondents saw the largest confidence drop.

**2. USD Heads for Sixth Weekly Decline as Rate Hike Expectations Fade**
The dollar index (DXY) fell to its lowest since May. Weaker-than-expected July retail sales data prompted traders to further scale back bets on rate hikes this year. Short-term Treasury yields fell, with the 2Y yield moving lower.

**3. UAE Mubadala Major Portfolio Reshuffle**
SEC filings reveal Mubadala Investment Company cleared its positions in Apple (AAPL), Broadcom (AVGO), and Salesforce (CRM). The sovereign wealth fund increased Micron (MU) by 3% to 45,451 shares, Palantir (PLTR) by 40.4% to 95,656 shares, and added to CVS Health. It reduced Gilead Sciences (GILD) by 19.9% and still holds 65,326 NVDA shares.

**4. Central Huijin and China Securities Finance Exit Moutai's Top 10 Shareholders**
Kweichow Moutai's 2026 interim report shows that by end of Q2, both Central Huijin and China Securities Finance exited the top 10 shareholder list. In Q1, they held 0.83% and 0.32% respectively.

**5. Storage Sector Rallies Against the Trend**
Sandisk (SNDK) surged over 9%, Seagate (STX) +7.2%, Western Digital (WDC) +4.5%, Micron (MU) and SK Hynix both up over 3.5%. SK Hynix's H1 equipment investment increased over 70% YoY, ramping up AI storage capacity.

**6. Broadcom Drops 5% — Worst S&P 500 Performer**
Broadcom (AVGO) fell 5% after Mubadala disclosed clearing its position, making it the biggest decler in the S&P 500. All three major US indices turned negative.

**7. Geopolitical Developments**
Russia struck Ukrainian ports of Odesa and Pivdennyi. US Senator Warren demanded Treasury Secretary Bessent explain the basis for yen intervention.
