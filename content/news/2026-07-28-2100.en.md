🧭 **Analysis**

Gold dropped over $45 in a single session, approaching the 4000 level. The primary driver: Trump's positive signals on Iran negotiations are unwinding safe-haven demand, while the "we control the strait" posture temporarily eases supply disruption fears.

Crude fell nearly 1.4% in tandem, with WTI testing the $80 mark. Diplomatic glimmers on the Strait of Hormuz are squeezing out the geopolitical premium.

The Strait of Hormuz transit game is entering a new phase. The Oman co-management proposal and Trump's unilateral control doctrine represent opposing paths — failure of either could reignite risk premiums.

Tonight's home price data, if stronger than expected, could reinforce the narrative that the Fed need not rush to cut rates, adding further pressure on gold.
