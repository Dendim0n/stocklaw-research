## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Trump Threatens Iran with "Strong Force"**

Trump issued multiple hawkish statements: the US controls Iran's funds with "complete control," Iran's inflation is 300%, its currency is worthless, and Iranians aren't paying soldiers' salaries. He stated that "if I hadn't done that, they would have nuclear weapons by now" and that the US could use "strong force" against Iran. Geopolitical tensions are clearly escalating.

**2. Atlanta Fed's Venable: Inflation Too High, Outlook Depends on Middle East Conflict**

Atlanta Fed First Vice President Venable said inflation remains too high, with Southeast contacts conveying the same message. The core question is whether price pressures will ease in coming months, which "depends largely on the outcome of the Middle East conflict." Labor market is overall stable, but stagnant labor supply poses an "unusual situation," creating "delicate risk" between inflation and employment mandates.

**3. US July Existing Home Sales Hit 3-Month Low**

Existing home sales annualized at 4.06 million, down 1.7% m/m (vs. -1.0% expected), a three-month low. Median price $434,100, up 2.0% y/y. High home prices and mortgage rates continue to suppress housing demand.

**4. Intel Stock Offering: ~33% of Orders Unfilled**

Market sources indicate approximately 33% of subscription orders for Intel's stock offering went unfilled, reflecting persistent weak market confidence in Intel and tightening financing conditions.

**5. Russian Forces Sink Ukrainian Patrol Boat in Black Sea**

Russia's Ministry of Defense reported sinking a patrol boat south of Odesa, which was escorting a cargo ship transporting weapons for Ukrainian forces. The Russia-Ukraine conflict continues to escalate.

**6. US July NFIB Small Business Confidence Rises to 99.8**

Up from 97.4, a significant recovery. Small business optimism is improving, presenting a contrast with the weak housing data.

**7. Russia Releases Former Marine Robert Gilman**

US officials confirmed Russia released former Marine Robert Gilman.
