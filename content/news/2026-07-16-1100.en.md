## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**🇰🇷 KOSPI Crashes 7%+, Circuit Breaker Triggered**

South Korean equities suffered a triple whammy on Thursday: chip stocks collapsed across the board, leveraged ETFs detonated in a cascade of forced selling, and the Bank of Korea unexpectedly hiked rates by 25bp to 2.75%—its first increase in over three years. Leveraged products tied to Samsung and SK Hynix became ticking time bombs. Korean mortgage rates are now approaching 8%. BOK Governor Shin Hyun-song pledged continued action until inflation stabilizes and downplayed the potential inflows from an MSCI status upgrade.

**🚀 SpaceX Briefly Falls Below IPO Price, $1T+ in Market Cap Erased**

SpaceX dipped below its IPO price intraday Wednesday, capping a 40% collapse from its all-time high with over $1 trillion in market value destroyed. Musk's personal stake has been severely compressed.

**🇺🇸 US Inflation Cools More Than Expected—Again**

Following Tuesday's CPI drop to 3.5% (vs. 3.8% expected), June PPI came in at 5.5% YoY (vs. 6.2% expected) with core PPI at 4.7% (vs. 5.2%). The across-the-board downside reduces rate hike urgency, though Fed Chair Warsh stated he is "not satisfied with any inflation metric."

**🛡️ Trump Seeks Expanded Military Operations Against Iran**

Reports indicate Trump favors broader military action, with the Pentagon assessing options including seizing Kharg Island and bombing nuclear tunnels. The US-Iran ceasefire has already collapsed, the Strait of Hormuz remains disrupted, and oil traders warn the global market is approaching "empty tank" territory.

**🗣️ Buffett: Market Is "Like a Casino," Warsh a Good Fed Choice**

Buffett once again blasted speculative culture, saying too much capital chases short-term thrills. He revealed personally approving the Alphabet purchase and called Warsh a "good choice" for Fed Chair.

## 🧭 Strategic Assessment

The KOSPI crash is the biggest risk event in Asia-Pacific today—rate hikes, leveraged ETF unwinds, and chip valuation compression are forming a negative feedback loop. Watch for contagion into US semiconductor names tonight.

With both CPI and PPI coming in below expectations, the probability of a July Fed hike continues to decline. However, Warsh's hawkish rhetoric signals rate cuts remain distant. The market is settling into a new equilibrium: hikes on pause but rates staying elevated.

SpaceX breaking below its IPO price is not an isolated event—high-valuation growth stocks face sustained repricing pressure in the current rate environment. This may be another signal of the tech bubble deflating.

The Strait of Hormuz remains the largest tail risk. If the US military meaningfully escalates, oil could spike 10-15% within hours.

Tonight's US retail sales and initial jobless claims are the key variables. Consensus expects retail sales MoM to plunge from 0.9% to 0.2%. A downside miss would reinforce the economic slowdown narrative.

Gold is holding above the 4000 level but lacks upside momentum, caught between cooling inflation and geopolitical risk. Expect 4000-4100 range-bound trading in the near term.
