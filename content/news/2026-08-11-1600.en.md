🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Consecutive Commercial Vessel Attacks in Mandeb Strait, Oil Surges**
Houthi forces attacked a commercial vessel with ballistic missiles in the Mandeb Strait, killing 3 foreigners. A Saudi vessel was also attacked in the Mandeb Strait with at least 3 dead. Red Sea shipping safety continues to deteriorate. Brent and WTI both rose over 2% to their highest levels since July 31. European energy stocks followed higher at open.

**2. Trump Cites Three "Strategies" on Iran**
In an interview, Trump stated the US has three strategies on Iran: 1) monitor its deteriorating situation, 2) launch devastating strikes, 3) economic pressure. Iranian FM Araghchi responded with defiant rhetoric, saying "no one expected Iran could force the enemy to ask for negotiations." Progress on restoring normal Hormuz Strait shipping through US-Iran negotiations has slowed.

**3. Iran Oil Minister: 95M m³ Gas Production to Resume by End-September**
Iran's oil ministry website Shana reported that Oil Minister Paknejad stated gas production cycles will resume by end-September, signaling an energy supply expectation.

**4. A-Shares Fade After Early Rally; Robot Concept Pops in Afternoon**
Shanghai Composite opened low then rose before fading in the afternoon, closing -0.82%. ChiNext briefly gained over 1% before paring. Main net fund outflow from Shanghai and Shenzhen markets reached 39.872 billion yuan. HK non-ferrous metals weakened: Lingbao Gold -9%+, Zijin Mining -6%+.

**5. Tesla Recalls 20,349 US Vehicles**
Tesla is recalling 20,349 US vehicles due to excessively bright low-beam headlights that may reduce visibility for oncoming drivers. Rivian simultaneously recalled 3,829 vehicles (turn signal malfunction).

**6. Romania to Shut Down Second Nuclear Reactor**
Due to low Danube River water levels, Romania will shut down its second nuclear reactor within 48 hours, adding further pressure on European energy supply.

**7. Thailand Plans to Pressure US with Reduced Military Cooperation for Tariff Relief**
Thailand plans to warn the Trump administration that high US tariffs may force Thailand to reduce military cooperation, including the "Cobra Gold" exercise — Southeast Asia's largest annual multinational joint military drill.
