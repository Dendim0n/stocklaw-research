🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**Huge bearish bet emerges in gold options market** — While the market remains in bullish euphoria, a rare outsized bearish options position has appeared. With Jackson Hole approaching, Wall Street is bracing for possible market swings from Fed Chair Warsh, and concerns are rising over a pullback after gold's surge toward 4,700.

**Tesla raises US Cybertruck prices** — Tesla increased the US price of the Cybertruck Dual Motor AWD to $74,990 and the Cybertruck Premium AWD to $84,990, drawing attention in the US equity space this hour.

**RBA minutes lean hawkish, Aussie near 11-week highs** — The RBA's August policy minutes showed "several" members saw a case for a rate hike given upside inflation risks, with the market having underestimated the tightening bias. The Aussie hovered just below 11-week highs on Tuesday, as officials said they would ensure liquidity while keeping the cash rate near target.

**JPMorgan eases collateral rules on newly listed stocks, Anthropic in focus** — JPMorgan is relaxing its margin-loan policy for employees and early investors of recently listed companies; it typically rejects stock of companies listed within the past 135 days. Insiders expect the bank may apply a similar approach when Anthropic goes public.

**Iran faces domestic fuel crunch, long queues at Tehran gas stations** — According to the Financial Times, long lines have formed at gas stations around Tehran, with a 20-liter-per-vehicle limit forcing drivers to keep returning. This comes after Hormuz Strait transit volumes hit recent lows, underscoring strain on Iran's domestic energy supply.

**US-Canada trade talks collapse, Mexico gains leverage** — After the breakdown of US-Canada trade negotiations, markets bet Washington will avoid opening another trade front, giving Mexico room to press for better terms and adding a new variable to the trade landscape.

**India's central bank selling dollars to support the rupee** — Reports indicate the RBI is selling USD to prop up the Indian rupee.
