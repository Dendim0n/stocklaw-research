## 🧭 Strategic Assessment

The US-Iran conflict has escalated from strikes on military facilities to infrastructure (bridges), signaling a strategic shift from "punishment" to "paralysis" of Iran's force projection capability.

Iran has yet to launch substantive retaliation but rhetoric is escalating — the market is pricing in "retaliation" uncertainty, with gold breaking $4,000 being the most direct reflection.

The A-share and HK tech selloff, compounded by external geopolitical risks, creates a "double whammy" — but the surge in broad-market ETF volumes suggests policy-floor capital is stepping in.

Crude oil's relatively muted response (+0.29%) to the Hormuz crisis indicates the market still views a full strait blockade as a low-probability event.

Japan's verbal intervention proved ineffective again, with the most accurate yen forecaster now targeting 170 — the BOJ's policy dilemma is deepening.
