🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Hormuz Strait Closed, but Saudi Arabia and UAE Secretly Shipping Oil**
Despite the Hormuz Strait remaining closed to navigation, the UAE and Saudi Arabia are accelerating crude oil exports via alternative routes. Satellite imagery shows approximately 150 large vessels gathered in the Gulf of Oman, ready to transport Saudi crude. The UAE is also actively bypassing the strait. This development explains why the strait's closure has had less impact on oil prices than expected.

**2. VIX Hits Yearly Low; Wall Street Warns of Autumn Selloff Risk**
The S&P 500 has surged year-to-date with continuous capital inflows, but historically seasonal selloff periods, ongoing geopolitical conflicts, and emerging signs of consumer weakness are prompting Wall Street strategists to warn of heightened autumn selloff risk. The fear index is at its lowest level of the year, suggesting market optimism may be excessive.

**3. Trump Claims Secret Communication with Iran's IRGC; Iran Denies**
Trump stated that the US has secret communication channels with Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) and demanded Iran "raise the white flag and surrender." The IRGC immediately dismissed the claim as "delusion." Iran's Supreme Leader stated the same day: "We will remain vigilant and actively defend Iran's interests." Middle East tensions remain highly elevated.

**4. French 30-Year Bond Yield Rises to 18-Year High**
France's 30-year bond yield rose to 4.86%, the highest level in 18 years. European bond market stress continues to spread, warranting attention to spillover effects on global risk appetite.
