🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Iranian FM Statements on Hormuz Strait Situation**

Iran's Foreign Minister stated that the US must meet Iran's conditions to restore shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. He also indicated that Iran has not yet decided to resume negotiations with the US, and that exchanges with Qatar and Pakistan are information-sharing rather than negotiations. Iran's talks with Oman are conducted independently, focusing on maritime routes in the Hormuz Strait.

**2. Trump's Naval Deployment Demands Peak**

According to Politico, Trump's demands on the Navy reached their peak this month, putting an already fiscally and logistically strained service at risk of further degradation. The Lincoln aircraft carrier deployment has set a record, operating for nine consecutive months without rotation.

**3. Modi Calls for Energy Self-Sufficiency**

Indian Prime Minister Modi stated that some countries' weaponization of resources and strategic sea routes poses a risk to India's economic security, calling for greater energy self-sufficiency to reduce vulnerability to overseas supplies.

**4. South Korean President Lee Proposes "Peaceful Coexistence Blueprint" for North Korea**

Lee Jae-myung proposed an "inclusive and stable peaceful coexistence blueprint" for North Korea, stating he would initiate a process to transform the unstable armistice regime on the Korean Peninsula into a peace regime.

**5. China Strongly Condemns Japanese Officials' Yasukuni Shrine Visit**

China's Foreign Ministry spokesperson strongly condemned Japanese officials' visits to the Yasukuni Shrine, which houses Class A war criminals, and has lodged solemn representations and strong protests with Japan.
