But the current context differs — data (initial claims 196K, Philly Fed 37.8) show no recession. **Assessment: 5% likely holds short-term, but a break could trigger negative feedback from algorithmic selling.** Continuous pressure on equities; gold faces tug-of-war between nominal rate headwind and safe-haven demand. Strategy: avoid long-duration bonds, favor short-duration defense; in equities, prefer earnings-certified tech leaders, avoid high-duration valuation names.
