🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Houthi Fourth Wave Attack on Mocha Port, Hodeida Governor Survives Assassination**

Yemen's military spokesman said Houthi attacks on Mocha port killed seven. According to Sky News, the fourth wave of Houthi missiles and drones targeted Mocha port and surrounding government military positions. Meanwhile, Yemeni government media reported that the governor of Hodeida province survived a Houthi missile attack on his residence in Al-Khawkh. Houthi offensive intensity continues to escalate.

**2. Syria and Russia Reach Memorandum on Restructuring Russian Coastal Presence**

According to Syrian state news agency SANA, Syria's foreign ministry stated that Damascus and Moscow have reached a memorandum of understanding to resolve the future of Russian bases at Tartus and Hmeimim, and will begin restructuring Russia's coastal presence in Syria under the framework of the memo. The agreement follows 18 months of intensive negotiations, marking a new phase for Russian military presence in Syria.

**3. Hamas Senior Official Reaffirms Commitment to Cairo Roadmap**

Hamas senior official Basem Naim stated that Hamas remains committed to the roadmap agreed with mediators and the peace committee representatives in Cairo 10 days ago, and expects mediators and the US to pressure Israeli PM Netanyahu to comply. Previously, Netanyahu said of the US 15-point Gaza plan that "some ideas we can accept, some we cannot."

**4. Turkey: Black Sea Transit Continues Smoothly**

Two Turkish government officials stated that despite heightened security concerns, vessels continue to pass smoothly through Turkish straits to the Black Sea. Turkey had previously restricted commercial vessel traffic to the Black Sea following attacks on ships.
