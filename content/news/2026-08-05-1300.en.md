🔥 **Top Stories This Hour**

**1. Iran-Oman Hormuz Talks Hit a Snag: US Threats Delay Agreement**

After last hour's report of "good progress" and a planned Wednesday announcement, the situation shifted. An Iranian official told Al Jazeera that US military threats are the main reason for delaying a Hormuz Strait deal with Oman: "Iran will not reach any agreement under the shadow of threats." However, Iran's Press TV simultaneously reported that talks have entered a "new phase" and the sides "could reach consensus" aimed at establishing an intermediate passage. President Trump warned in an interview that if Iran walks away from negotiations again, it will face "very severe strikes." Geopolitical uncertainty rebounded, pushing gold above $4,140 intraday.

**2. RBI Holds Repo Rate at 5.25% for Fourth Straight Meeting**

The Monetary Policy Committee voted unanimously to keep the repo rate at 5.25% with a neutral stance, matching expectations. Governor said headline inflation has slightly exceeded target mainly due to fuel and food prices, but core inflation is expected at a moderate 4.3%. The RBI raised FY2027 real GDP growth forecast from 6.6% to 6.7%, viewing growth risks as balanced. The central bank is watching whether energy price increases driven by the Iran conflict will broaden into wider inflation and needs clearer data before acting.

**3. Russia Strikes Logistics Hubs in and Around Kyiv**

Russia's Defense Ministry said it destroyed logistics hubs in and around Kyiv, claiming the facilities were used for military purposes. Several warehouses caught fire in the Kyiv region after the attack. The Russia-Ukraine conflict continues to escalate, adding another layer to global geopolitical risk.

**4. Indonesia Q2 GDP Grows 5.29%, Beating Expectations**

Indonesia's statistics bureau reported Q2 GDP growth of 5.29% year-over-year, with H1 growth at 5.45%. Strong household consumption and investment supported the economy against a complex global environment, and the rupiah gained on the news.

**5. Dalio Warns of AI Bubble: Echoes of 1929 and 2000**

Bridgewater founder Ray Dalio wrote about the "paper wealth" trap, pointing to the core contradiction of the AI bubble: valuations have created massive paper wealth without corresponding real cash flows. When the market reverses, a rush to liquidate will cause asset crashes while debt remains, warning of echoes of the 1929 and 2000 bubbles.

**6. Warsh's "Fed Revolution" Faces Reality Test**

Just two months into his tenure, Fed Chair Warsh's pledge for "institutional change" has been overshadowed by post-July meeting communication turbulence. The more pressing task: rebuilding market trust in his inflation-fighting credentials. Treasury Secretary Bessent recently endorsed Warsh's approach of weakening forward guidance as "desensitization therapy," but market skepticism persists.

**7. JPMorgan Cuts McDonald's Price Target to $280**

JPMorgan lowered McDonald's (MCD.N) price target from $305 to $280.

**8. ByteDance Launches Native Full-Duplex Audio-Video LLM "SeedRealtime"**

ByteDance officially unveiled SeedRealtime, a native full-duplex audio-video large model, intensifying competition in the AI real-time interaction space.
