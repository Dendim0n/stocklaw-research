🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**Hormuz Strait Crisis Continues to Escalate**
Iran's parliament has formally introduced the Strategic Action for Security and Sustainable Process in the Strait of Hormuz and Persian Gulf bill. India's Ministry of External Affairs summoned the Iranian ambassador after an Indian national was killed in a tanker attack in the Strait of Hormuz. German Foreign Minister Wadhful met with Bahrain's foreign minister, strongly condemning Iran's recent attacks on its neighbors and insisting the strait must remain open without tolls or transit fees. Brazil's President Lula criticized Trump's proposed 20% transit fee on Hormuz vessels as piracy — at roughly $30 million per supertanker passage.

**Super Inflation Day Tonight**
US June CPI drops at 20:30 CST — the last major inflation print before the July FOMC meeting. Falling energy prices are expected to drag headline CPI lower, but core inflation remains sticky with healthcare and insurance services still elevated. Also on deck: 18:00 NFIB Small Business Optimism Index, 20:15 ADP weekly employment change. New Fed Chair Warsh makes his congressional testimony debut tonight — markets will scrutinize his views on inflation and rate trajectory.

**Ericsson Warns of AI Hardware Cost Pressure**
Ericsson's CFO stated that AI infrastructure buildout will intensify price pressure on chips, ASICs, memory and other components in H2 2026, with restructuring including layoffs through the rest of the year.

**De Beers Suspends South Africa's Largest Diamond Mine**
Diamond giant De Beers announced a two-year production halt at the Venetia mine in Limpopo, South Africa to cut costs, with other operations maintaining current output.

🧭 **Analysis**

Gold breaks above 4030 to fresh session highs, silver rallies in tandem — precious metals seeing strong safe-haven buying driven by the dual catalyst of the Hormuz crisis escalation and tonight's CPI.

The Hormuz Strait situation has moved from diplomatic friction to legislative action, with Iran's parliament formally introducing a bill. Combined with Trump's transit fee proposal drawing rebukes from multiple nations, geopolitical risk premium continues to flow into oil and gold prices.

Japan government bond yields fell across the curve, with the 20Y plunging 12.5bp — a rare move signaling a clear flight to safe-haven assets.
