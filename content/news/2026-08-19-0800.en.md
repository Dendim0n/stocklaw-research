## 🔥 Key Overnight News

1. **Middle East escalation** — UAE said its air defenses detected two ballistic missiles fired from Iran (one landed in its territorial waters), then announced suspension of all trade, commercial and financial transactions with Iran; Tehran's foreign ministry dismissed the reports as "lacking basis."
2. **US-Iran standoff unresolved** — Trump denied any ongoing negotiations with Iran, saying the naval blockade remains fully in effect and the Strait of Hormuz is open and functioning, calling it "new US territory" to Tehran's outrage; Iran's FM said it "will not accept a ceasefire, only the end of the war" and will not reopen Hormuz until conditions are met.
3. **Surging bond yields pressure AI tech** — A global bond selloff pushed the US 10Y yield to 4.75% (highest since early 2025) with multi-year highs in long-dated yields elsewhere; higher-rate concerns weighed on valuations, and JPMorgan warned "AI concentration risk" has spread from equities into fixed income.
4. **Memory/semiconductor rout** — Micron, Sandisk, Western Digital and SK Hynix fell 7-9%, with the SOX down as much as 5%; Baidu plunged 10% after earnings and Meta hit a two-week low amid the 29-state lawsuit.
5. **OpenAI/Anthropic** — OpenAI posted Q2 revenue of $6.7B (+18% QoQ) but wider losses, disappointing some shareholders; Anthropic is expected to launch its IPO process within weeks, with a pre-IPO credit facility possibly exceeding $10B, and plans super-voting shares to protect founders.
6. **US-Canada tariff impasse** — A plan to exempt certain Canadian goods from a 50% tariff has been submitted for Trump's approval ahead of a midnight deadline; sources put the odds of a deal at "50/50 or lower," with Trump reportedly weighing a delay.
7. **Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant attack** — A drone struck a commuter bus stop serving the plant, injuring 16, which the IAEA called the "most serious event" at the site; in the Russia-Ukraine war, Ukraine launched a massive 620-drone attack on the Moscow region.

## 🧭 Situation Assessment

The overnight theme was a tug-of-war between rates and risk-off: rising bond yields crushed tech valuations, Middle East tensions underpinned oil, and gold pulled back from highs.

The US 10Y yield above 4.75% is the biggest swing factor right now — the high-rate environment is a stress test for richly valued AI stocks.

The collective selloff in memory names deserves attention; crowding in the AI hardware chain is being repriced after earnings season.

A US-Iran deadlock supports the oil price floor, but the market has partly priced in the headlines — expect more volatility than a one-way move.

The midnight US-Canada tariff deadline is the first event risk today; tariffs landing would dent risk appetite.

Tonight's EIA crude inventory (prior build of 17,423K barrels) plus Lagarde's remarks raise the risk of oil and rates moving in tandem.
