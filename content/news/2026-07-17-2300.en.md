🔥 **Key Headlines This Hour**

**US-Iran Conflict Escalates to Civilian Infrastructure**

US military destroyed an IRGC maritime surveillance tower at Kalantari Port on July 16, used for decades to track and target commercial vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz. Iran retaliated with strikes on water and power facilities in the Gulf region. The conflict has expanded from military targets to critical civilian infrastructure. IRGC responded to questions about further action: "Please be patient."

**Strait of Hormuz Transits Plummet**

According to Iran's Tasnim News Agency, confirmed vessel transits through the Strait of Hormuz have dropped to just 8 ships, with 7 passing through Iranian waters and zero through Omani lanes. Iran claims it maintains firm control of the strait.

**Fed's Hammack: Core PCE Nowcast at 3.3%**

Fed official Hammack stated persistent high inflation remains the top concern, with the current core PCE nowcast showing 3.3%. Labor market is near full employment with no conflict between the dual mandate. Business leaders cited energy costs, supply chain disruptions, insurance pressures, and AI data center construction as key factors.

**US Consumer Confidence Hits 5-Month High**

University of Michigan consumer sentiment rose to a five-month high in July, but the improvement may be temporary given renewed Middle East conflict pushing up gasoline prices.

**Suspected Pirate Hijacking off Yemen**

A transport vessel named "Asana" was suspected of being hijacked by Somali pirates while transiting the Gulf of Aden. Yemen's coast guard is tracking the incident.
