## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Kuwait Military Announces Interception of Iranian Missiles and Drones**
Kuwait's Armed Forces General Staff issued a statement that air defense units are engaging hostile drone attacks in response to Iranian aggression. The military stated that any explosions heard are from air defense systems intercepting incoming threats. This marks a major signal of Kuwait being directly drawn into the Iran conflict, with spillover risk escalating sharply across the Gulf.

**2. Iranian Parliament Speaker: Iran Has Won the War, Must Expand Supreme Leader's Authority**
Parliament Speaker Kalibaf stated in a parliamentary address that Iran has won the war, but must consolidate and solidify the victory, and the nation must maintain hope and confidence for the future. The remarks suggest Iran's internal power structure may be accelerating consolidation, with post-war order arrangements now on the agenda.

**3. Explosions Near Tanker in Omani Waters**
The UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) received a report of an incident 21 nautical miles northeast of Khasab, Oman. A tanker captain reported seeing widespread splashes and explosions near the vessel, with no ship damage reported so far. The location is near the Strait of Hormuz, creating a compounding effect with Iran's announcement yesterday suspending passage through the strait.

**4. Hungary Shuts Down Nuclear Reactor Due to Low Water Levels**
Due to low water levels, Hungary has closed a nuclear reactor, reducing the country's nuclear power plant output to below one-quarter of rated capacity. European energy security faces another hidden risk, as summer heat and drought impacts on nuclear output warrant continued monitoring.

**5. Chinese Gold Jewelry Prices Cut by ~12 Yuan/Gram**
Major domestic gold jewelry brands cut prices by approximately 12 yuan/gram compared to yesterday, with most brands quoting 1,226 yuan/gram. Gold's retreat from highs is beginning to transmit to the consumer end, though absolute prices remain at historical highs.
