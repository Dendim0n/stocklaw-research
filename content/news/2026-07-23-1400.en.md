🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🛢️ Middle East Conflict Escalates Sharply**

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed an attack on a US base in Jordan, destroying a THAAD radar system, a Patriot system, and a C-RAM radar, with the base's fuel depot set ablaze. Separately, Iranian officials reported two search-and-rescue vessels damaged by US attacks near Hormuzgan Province. On the US side, B-1 strategic bombers deployed from UK bases have joined operations against Iran, marking a significant escalation in conflict intensity. WTI crude surged over 2% on the news.

**🇷🇺 High-Level US-Russia Diplomatic Contact**

Russian Foreign Minister Lavrov held a call with US Secretary of State Rubio, discussing the Ukraine conflict and normalization of diplomatic missions. Russia confirmed readiness to resolve the Ukraine conflict through political and diplomatic means — the highest-level bilateral engagement in recent weeks.

**📉 Hyundai Motor Q2 Profit Misses Estimates**

Hyundai's Q2 operating profit fell short of analyst expectations as global retail sales declined, with US policy uncertainty and intensifying competition weakening demand. The company warned its 2026 global sales guidance may be revised slightly downward.

**💻 STMicroelectronics: AI Drives Q3 Revenue Growth**

STMicro expects Q3 revenue of approximately $3.7 billion, up 6.7% YoY, driven by AI data center demand. Shares rose in pre-market trading.

**🏦 ECB Rate Decision Preview**

Markets widely expect a "hawkish pause" — the ECB is set to hold rates steady, but inflation rebound concerns may force policymakers to leave the door open for a September hike.

🧭 **Market Outlook**

The Middle East situation is this hour's core variable. Iran's direct attack claim on a US base signals escalation from proxy-level to direct military confrontation — WTI crude was the first to react.

If Iran's claims are verified (THAAD + Patriot systems simultaneously damaged), this represents a major setback for US air defense — the scale of US retaliation will determine oil's next direction.

Gold found support near 4120 but lacks rebound momentum. Multiple institutions — BofA, UBS, ING — are unanimously bearish in the near term, viewing the bounce as unsustainable.

Tonight's ECB decision is the day's biggest macro event. A hawkish pause is largely priced in; the key lies in whether Lagarde's press conference language hints at a September hike.

Tesla's mixed earnings — revenue beat but negative FCF and profit decline — plus Musk's merger hints warrant close attention; post-market volatility could be significant.

Sustained US-Iran escalation would materially deteriorate global risk appetite, increasing capital outflow pressure on emerging markets.
