🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Fed's Hammack Strikes Hawkish Tone**
Fed's Hammack stated he is "not confident inflation will continue to improve," calling for faster reduction to 2%. He also flagged concerns about private credit and potential AI bubble. While acknowledging recent inflation reports were encouraging, overall tone was cautiously hawkish.

**5. CPC Issues El Niño Watch**
The US Climate Prediction Center (CPC) indicated El Niño is strengthening, with probability of a strong El Niño event exceeding 90% for the 2026-27 Northern Hemisphere fall-winter season.

**6. Russian Seaborne Oil Product Exports Plunge**
Industry data shows Russian seaborne oil product exports fell 33% in July compared to June.

**7. Chile-Argentina Mining Agreement**
Chile's mining minister stated the agreement with Argentina could bring $207 billion in investment and add 540,000 tons of annual copper production.
