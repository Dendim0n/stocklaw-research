🔥 **Key Headlines This Hour**

🔴 **Trump Calls for Immediate Israel-Iran Ceasefire** — U.S. President Trump posted on social media explicitly demanding that Israel and Iran "must stop firing immediately." This is the first time Washington has publicly pressured both sides to cease fire, a significant signaling shift.

🏭 **Alibaba Establishes Token Foundry Division** — Alibaba announced the merger of its Tongyi Large Model Division and Future Life Laboratory into a new Token Foundry division, with CEO Wu Yongming directly overseeing the move — a major AI organizational restructuring.

📡 **Latvia Reports Russian Electronic Warfare Incursion** — Latvian military stated sensors detected Russia using electronic warfare tactics, causing drones to enter Latvian airspace. NATO aircraft continue border patrols.

⚠️ **UAE Opens First Post-War Crude Tender** — With the Strait of Hormuz nearly blockaded, the UAE has begun supplying crude to Asian buyers via alternative routes, marginally easing global supply tightness. Analysts warn the current calm in oil markets may be a false signal, with sharp correction potential if sentiment reverses.

🧭 **Analysis**

Trump's public ceasefire call signals a shift from behind-the-scenes pressure to direct diplomatic mediation on the Middle East — the Israel-Iran conflict may enter a new phase of diplomatic maneuvering.

However, the EU simultaneously escalated sanctions on Iran, revealing a push-pull split within the Western bloc — Washington wants to cool things down, Europe is tightening the screws.

Oil has traded at elevated levels near $95 for four months in an unusually stable pattern, with geopolitical risk and price action severely diverging. A sentiment reversal could trigger violent correction.

The Xi-Kim summit combined with Latvia's electronic warfare report underscores that great-power competition is extending from economic arenas to military security frontlines.

Alibaba's sweeping AI restructuring signals a new round of organizational consolidation in China's large model competition.
