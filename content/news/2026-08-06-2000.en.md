🔥 **Key News This Hour**

1. **Warsh Maintains "Minimalist" Communication, Keeps September Hike Option Open** — The Financial Times reports Fed Chair Warsh acknowledges communication missteps but refuses "spoon-fed" forward guidance. Sources say Warsh is prepared to raise rates at the September meeting if upcoming inflation data comes in strong and market expectations of rising borrowing costs intensify, signaling a data-dependent hawkish tilt.

2. **Alphabet (GOOG) Launches $1 Billion Investment-Grade Bond Offering** — Google's parent company initiated a 10-tranche USD investment-grade bond issuance, reflecting the trend of large tech firms leveraging high credit ratings for low-cost financing.

3. **UK CMA Clears Paramount-Warner Bros Merger** — The UK Competition and Markets Authority decided not to intervene in the Paramount-Warner Bros Discovery (WBD) merger, based on legally binding commitments from Paramount. Paramount also stated the deal has not triggered any antitrust concerns in any market.

4. **Unitree Technology Strategic Placement: DeepSeek, Tencent Among Investors** — Unitree set its IPO price at 150.80 yuan/share, with DeepSeek and Tencent's investment arm among strategic placement recipients, highlighting strategic synergy between AI and robotics supply chains.

6. **ConocoPhillips (COP) Q3 Production Guidance** — Expects Q3 production of 2.29-2.32 million BOE/day vs. market estimate of 2.32 million, with the midpoint slightly below expectations.

7. **Multiple A-Share Companies Clarify NVIDIA Partnership Rumors** — Zhiwei Intelligent, Tongyu Communications, and Jin'an Guoji all issued statements clarifying that their NVIDIA-related collaborations have been exaggerated by the market, indicating that AI-concept speculation has drawn regulatory attention.
