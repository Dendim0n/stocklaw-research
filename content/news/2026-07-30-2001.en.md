🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**BoE Holds Steady, Internal Divisions Deepen**
The Bank of England voted 6-3 to hold rates at 3.75%, with the hawkish camp growing to three members. The central bank also warned that inflation is expected to remain above the 2% target until end-2027, and that quantitative tightening has had a larger-than-expected impact on borrowing costs.

**Geopolitical Tensions Escalate Sharply**
Russia launched nationwide airstrikes on Ukraine, with missiles striking Zelenskyy's hometown of Kryvyi Rih. Poland scrambled fighter jets for defense, and NATO has activated both air and ground defense responses, stating it will take all necessary measures to defend NATO territory.

**Israel-Gaza Ceasefire Talks Signal Breakthrough**
According to Israel's Channel 12, both Israeli and Gazan officials are hinting at progress in negotiations, with a possible announcement on consensus for the second phase of the ceasefire agreement coming soon.

**ICE to Acquire MarketAxess**
Intercontinental Exchange will acquire bond trading platform MarketAxess for $167 per share in cash, aiming to build a premier fixed-income marketplace.

**German July CPI Due**
Germany's preliminary July CPI month-on-month reading was due at 20:00 CST, with markets closely watching the inflation trajectory of the eurozone's largest economy.

🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

Gold spiked to 4100 before pulling back, reflecting a brief safe-haven bid on the BoE decision that was quickly digested. However, geopolitical risks — the Russia-Ukraine escalation and NATO's involvement — provide a floor for gold prices. Short-term outlook remains range-bound at elevated levels.

Crude oil's 1.6% decline reflects multiple headwinds: US-Iran diplomatic channels remain open, marginally easing Middle East supply disruption risks; meanwhile, progress in Israel-Gaza ceasefire talks further erodes the geopolitical premium.

The BoE's hold with growing internal division, combined with an inflation forecast extending to end-2027, signals that a high-rate environment will persist longer than markets had anticipated, weighing on risk asset valuations.

The violation of Polish airspace marks a new spillover risk from the Russia-Ukraine conflict. NATO's substantive military response escalation warrants close monitoring, as further expansion could impact European assets and the euro.

The ICE-MarketAxess deal is a significant signal of fixed-income market infrastructure consolidation, reflecting traditional exchange groups' strategic positioning in fixed-income amid heightened rate volatility.
