🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**① Iran FM: US-Iran MOU Will Not Be Signed Tomorrow (21:00)**
Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Baghaei stated clearly that the signing of the US-Iran MOU will not happen tomorrow. This comes just after Trump declared the deal could be reached "this weekend or next Monday," marking a clear divergence between the two sides on the signing timeline.

**② Iran FM: Signing Date "Won't Be Tomorrow" (20:54-20:57)**
In a series of statements, the Iranian FM spokesperson elaborated: the MOU focuses on ending the current phase of war, not nuclear issues; signing within days remains possible, but due to American hesitation, any comments on dates must be approached with caution. The tone has notably cooled from "possible within days" to "must be cautious."

**③ Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Plant Restores Power After Attack (21:32-21:38)**
Russian-controlled Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant reported Ukrainian forces attacked its transport workshop, damaging three vehicles (two completely burned). However, under an IAEA-brokered local ceasefire, backup power was restored and reconnected to the grid. IAEA confirmed normal operations and radiation at natural background levels, but warned external power supply challenges persist.

**④ Multiple Iranian Banks Hit by Technical Outages (21:03)**
Major Iranian banks including Bank Melli, Bank of Industry and Mine, and Bank of Commerce experienced technical failures, with customers reporting service disruptions. Repair efforts are underway.

**⑤ Hezbollah-Israel Clashes Continue in Southern Lebanon (21:43-21:58)**
Hezbollah reported firing rockets at Israeli military positions in southern Lebanon's Majdal Zun and downing an Israeli Hermes 450 drone over Kardali using a missile. This follows the IDF's strikes on 70+ Hezbollah targets in southern Lebanon earlier in the day.
