**⚔️ Kremlin: Ukraine Conflict Now a "Full-Scale War"**

In a barrage of statements, the Kremlin declared for the first time that the Russia-Ukraine conflict is no longer a "special military operation" but a "full-scale war" involving Russia against Ukraine, multiple European nations, and the US. Peskov added that Zelensky could still end the conflict by withdrawing from Donbas and recognizing the "already established facts on the ground." Since US-Iran hostilities reignited this week, Hormuz Strait transits dropped from 30 vessels on Wednesday to just 22 on Thursday. Monitoring today shows virtually no vessel activity, with traffic flows significantly reduced. Shipowners are pulling back, driving up shipping costs and placing material strain on global energy supply chains.
