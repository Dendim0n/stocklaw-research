🧭 **Situation Assessment**

Iran's president simultaneously signaled "returning frozen funds" and "not surrendering uranium enrichment" — a classic dual track of concession and firmness, suggesting Tehran wants progress but insists on protecting core interests.

Hormuz traffic hitting zero is a more concrete data point than simply "closed." Roughly 20% of global oil transit flows through this strait; duration of the blockade directly determines the oil price ceiling.

The Kremlin's vague accusation of "one party breaching the agreement," released on the same day as the Swiss talks, is conspicuously timed — possibly aimed at adding pressure to the US-Iran negotiations.

The Supreme Leader advisor's warning was notably blunt — "energy flows will halt" — effectively signaling that the strait won't reopen unless the MOU is honored.
