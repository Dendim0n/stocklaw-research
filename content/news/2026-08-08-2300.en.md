🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Major De-escalation Signals from Iran**

US Vice President Vance stated that Iran has notified the US it will not charge transit fees for the Strait of Hormuz, despite internal regime figures suggesting otherwise. Vance also claimed Iran's asymmetric military capabilities have been significantly degraded.

Iranian President Pezeshkian told media: now is the best time for a deal, as Iran is "strong and united, seen as a victor in the war."

Oman also confirmed that ongoing negotiations regarding Strait of Hormuz arrangements are positive and constructive.

**2. Ship Attacked in Gulf of Oman**

The UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) received a report that a vessel was struck by an unidentified projectile and caught fire 18 nautical miles east of Khasab, Oman. The fire was extinguished. Crew reported safe, no environmental impact.

**3. Yemeni Forces Launch Operations Against Houthis**

A spokesperson for Yemeni armed forces announced operations against Houthi forces and associated "militias."

**4. Libyan Pipeline Resumes Operation**

Libya's Waha Oil Company: The Zakuit-Sidra pipeline experienced a leak, which has been repaired and operations resumed.

**5. Bulgaria Border Explosion**

Romania's Ministry of Defense stated that regarding the explosion in Bulgaria near the Romanian border, Romanian radar systems detected no aircraft crossing Romanian airspace into Bulgaria.
