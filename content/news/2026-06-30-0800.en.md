## 🔥 Key Overnight News

**🏛️ Fed Independence Vindicated by Supreme Court**
The US Supreme Court ruled 5-4 to block Trump's removal of Fed Governor Cook, reinforcing central bank independence. Analysis suggests this ruling blocks the path for Trump to influence interest rate policy through personnel changes, with current Fed Chair Wathen emerging as the unexpected institutional winner. Trump vowed immediate action regarding Cook's eligibility.

**🕊️ US-Iran Doha Talks Signal Confusion**
Trump announced that US and Iran would hold talks in Doha today, but Iran's deputy foreign minister immediately denied "nothing is scheduled." The two sides can't even agree on whether a meeting will take place, with mediators reportedly setting up emergency communication channels as a precaution.

**⛽ Trump Pressures Retailers to Cut Prices**
Trump posted that gas retailers must lower prices immediately, targeting $2.50/gallon. He noted crude oil has fallen to $68/barrel, making current gas prices unjustifiably high.

**📊 South Korea Industrial Data Misses Wildly**
South Korea May industrial production fell -3.0% month-on-month (vs +0.5% expected), with year-on-year at -0.9% (vs +3.6% expected), potentially reflecting a cyclical slowdown in semiconductor exports.

**🇮🇱 Russia-Ukraine Conflict Continues**
Russian forces claimed control of Bohodarivka in eastern Ukraine; Dnipro city struck, killing 4 and injuring 10. Peace talks remain stalled with no substantive progress.

**⚠️ Morgan Stanley Warns of Semiconductor Peak**
MS strategists flagged that semiconductor earnings upgrade breadth has hit historical extremes, with large-scale capital outflows from cloud service providers — a clear leading signal for the sector's imminent period of underperformance.
