## 2. What is actually new

**① Gold breaks 3,900 and closes at a 13-month low; miners fall harder | 01:00-07:18**
Spot gold closed 11-12 at **3,877.66 (-2.76%)**, a third straight decline, which the Jin10 morning wrap called the lowest level in more than a year ([07:03](https://xnews.jin10.com/details/234981)). I checked it independently: **GLD closed at 354.89 on 11-12, the lowest close in its 300 daily bars back to 5 September 2025; the last close at or below that level was 354.79 on 2 October 2025**, and it sits **-28.44%** below the 52-week high of 495.90 set on 29 January 2026. This is not a pullback from a high; it is a fresh low inside a ten-month downtrend. Same-session reads: Shanghai gold's night session fell **-2.87% to 836 yuan/gram** and Shanghai silver -3.43% to 12,066 yuan/kg ([02:31](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113023124299800)); Gold T+D fell -2.46% to 834.5 yuan/gram. **The counter-evidence sits in the ETF**: SPDR Gold Trust holdings fell only **0.38 tonnes** to 1,040.703 tonnes ([07:18](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113071816317800)), i.e. -0.04% - gold fell 2.76% and the largest gold ETF barely moved, which says the selling came from futures and leveraged longs, not from ETF redemptions.

**② The denominator: three Fed speakers hawkish on the same day, a soft 30-year auction | 00:05-04:51**
Goolsbee (2027 voter) called inflation at a "dangerous moment", said the hot inflation report is "not a one-off", and that the Fed has not done its job on inflation ([02:56](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113025631215800)); Daly said further tightening "may be necessary" and that inflation is seeping into services ([04:51](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113045104887800)); Schmid said the Fed has not yet beaten inflation. **③ Houthis claim strikes on Saudi's East-West pipeline and Yanbu; oil jumps while EIA reports a large build | 00:57-03:45**

## 3. Judgement and counter-evidence

**A | Gold: the second leg of the denominator trade - it is right, but this is no place to chase.**
View: downside continues short term; do not catch the knife and do not chase it short. Evidence: 2-year 4.86% (+13bp), 10-year 5.10%, dollar index 103.174 - the drop has a complete denominator explanation, with no need for a "safe-haven demand collapsed" narrative; GLD is -28.44% from its 29 January high and at a 13-month low, which makes this a ten-month trend rather than a one-off shock. **Counter-evidence at the same timestamp**: SPDR holdings fell just 0.38 tonnes (-0.04%), essentially no response to a 2.76% decline; a genuine allocation exit would show multi-tonne outflows. That makes the current selling look like futures and leveraged longs, and the rebound would be fast once rates stop rising.
Affected: gold, silver (50.14), A-share and Hong Kong gold miners (Zijin Gold International fell 10.35% on 11-12), gold ETFs, long-duration growth stocks.
Invalidation: if the 2-year falls below 4.70% while gold stays under 3,850, the driver has shifted from the denominator to positioning, and that needs WGC quarterly demand and ETF flow data, which I did not obtain this run; if gold recovers 3,987.93 (the 11-11 close) while the 10-year drops below 5.00%, this section is void.

**B | Nvidia: for the first time the market is pricing an idiosyncratic discount rather than a cycle discount - but the counter-evidence is also live.**
View: idiosyncratic risk is priced, systemic risk is not; neither chase the drop nor buy it. Evidence: the -13.08% open was panic pricing, the -4.34% close, the 11.69% recovery off the low and the 168.70 after-hours print show the selling was absorbed; over the same session Broadcom +5.54%, AMD +5.86% and the Nasdaq 100 only -0.30%, so money rotated inside AI rather than leaving it; the forward P/E of about 12x is the lowest since 2012 ([05:48](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113054859653800), media figure, EPS side not independently verified). **Three pieces of counter-evidence**: Super Micro -11.30%, the Golden Dragon index -3.1% and Alibaba -7.05% show this is not "the whole AI chain benefits"; the DOJ antitrust probe and the congressional testimony keep the legal process on the calendar; and if the new management tightens the $164.5bn of financial commitments, the damage lands on neoclouds and cloud customers - assets that did not fall by anything like the appropriate amount on 11-12.
Affected: Nvidia, Nasdaq 100 weights, GPU rental and neoclouds, A-share CPO/optical modules.
Invalidation: **the Q3 FY27 report after the close on 11-17** is the dividing line - if guidance is cut and Hon Hai's language on AI servers weakens with it, this shifts from key-man risk to capex-cycle risk and the whole chain has to be re-rated; if the stock reclaims 173.73 (the 11-11 close) before the report, the discount was a one-off shock.

**C | Oil: an interruption-risk premium, not a shortage - the inventory data already refuted shortage.**
View: do not chase it long, do not short it either; both sides are betting on one-sided statements. Evidence: Brent's +3.30% exceeds WTI's +2.55% and the spread is $7.08, so the premium sits in waterborne crude, which is the signature of shipping and loading risk; yet EIA showed crude +4.657m barrels, gasoline +1.799m, distillates +4.071m and record output of 14.013m b/d, so the physical picture is unambiguously loose. **Sell-side does not support spot either**: Goldman's base case for 2027 Brent is **$88** ([00:53](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113005302432800)), more than $8 below the current 95.82. The attack itself rests on **one-sided claims** from the Houthis and Iran's military; Saudi Arabia has not responded, and neither shipowners nor a third party have confirmed the operational status of pump station 7 or Yanbu.
Affected: front-month crude, product cracks, tankers and war-risk insurance, energy equities, inflation expectations (transmission lags; this is not evidence of an inflation peak or trough).
Invalidation: if Saudi Arabia or a third party confirms damage and loadings are actually interrupted, "interruption risk" upgrades to "supply risk" and this section is void; if the strikes go unconfirmed and WTI falls back below 87, the move was a one-off pulse. Today's 21:30 US October PPI and 23:30 EIA natural gas storage are the nearest tests.
