🧭 **Analysis**

The reported use of white phosphorus by Israel in Lebanon, if confirmed, would severely undermine the prospects for ceasefire implementation, sharply raising the risk of further escalation.

The UK's sanctions on a sitting Israeli minister set a precedent among Western allies, potentially prompting other nations to follow suit and deepening Israel's diplomatic isolation.

The simultaneous travel of US and Iran's negotiating teams to Switzerland signals both sides remain committed to advancing the ceasefire deal, though Israel's military escalation casts a shadow over the talks.

Gold weakness is primarily driven by a stronger dollar — Fed hawkishness combined with improved US-Iran negotiation expectations suppresses safe-haven demand, but further deterioration in the Middle East could reverse this dynamic.
