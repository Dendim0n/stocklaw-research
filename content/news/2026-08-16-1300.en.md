🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Duan Yongping's Q2 13F: Trimmed NVIDIA & Google, Initiated Alibaba Position**

H&H International Investment, managed by renowned value investor Duan Yongping, reported Q2 end portfolio value of ~$19.1B, down from ~$20B in Q1. Key moves: reduced NVIDIA and Google holdings, opened a new position in Alibaba. As a closely watched value investor, this rebalancing reflects growing caution on AI mega-cap valuations and renewed interest in Chinese ADRs.

**2. Iran Claims 3 Pilots Detained by Qatar; Qatar Denies**

Iran's military stated that 3 pilots were captured by Qatari forces during a March strike on a US base in Qatar and remain detained. Qatar's foreign ministry dismissed the claims as "misleading statements." Middle East tensions add another layer of complexity; Hormuz Strait security remains a key variable for oil prices.

**3. FT Poll: Majority of US Voters Say They're Worse Off Under Trump**

A Financial Times poll shows most US voters believe their living conditions have deteriorated under Trump, with rising dissatisfaction over his handling of the economy and cost of living. This puts pressure on Republican midterm prospects and may influence future fiscal and tax policy direction.

**4. Colombia Requests US Tariff Suspension Due to Earthquake**

Colombian President De la Espriella asked Trump to temporarily suspend tariffs on Colombian goods, as the country deals with a deadly earthquake that has killed nearly 300 people. The flexibility of tariff policy is worth watching.

**5. China's State Council Info Office to Hold Press Conference Aug 17**

The State Council Information Office will hold a press conference at 3 PM on Sunday, August 17, to release July 2026 national economic data. Expected releases include industrial production, retail sales, and fixed asset investment — a key macro event early next week.

**6. SpaceX Completes Falcon 9 Dual Coastal Launches**

SpaceX completed back-to-back Falcon 9 launches from Florida and California. Combined with last hour's revelation of NVIDIA's $21B SpaceX stake, the commercial space sector continues to attract capital and attention.

**7. Israeli Defense Minister Issues Hardline Statement**

Israel's Defense Minister stated "we will not leave any unresolved issues on any battlefield" and pledged full protection of soldiers and citizens. Middle East tensions show no signs of easing.
