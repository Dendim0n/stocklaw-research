🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Iranian Parliament Passes Anti-Infiltration Law**
Iran's parliament passed an anti-infiltration law aimed at countering foreign intelligence agencies and governments. Parliament speaker for national security and foreign policy committee spokesperson Kashkavi introduced the bill. This marks a step to tighten internal security amid regional tensions.

**2. Iranian Army Commander Claims US Forces Expelled from Strait of Hormuz**
Army Commander Hatami stated that US forces have been expelled and are no longer permitted to enter the Persian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, and Strait of Hormuz. Hatami emphasized that US military bases in the region cannot return to their previous state. The rhetoric is extremely hardline — if Iran acts on this, it would directly threaten the world's most critical oil shipping route.

**3. Israel Q2 GDP Surges 15.4%**
Israel's Central Bureau of Statistics reported Q2 GDP grew at an annualized 15.4%, beating expectations and recovering rapidly from the contraction caused by the Iran military operation. The rebound pace significantly exceeds forecasts.

**4. Netanyahu to Meet US Envoy Kushner Tomorrow**
Prime Minister Netanyahu will meet US envoy Kushner and senior peace committee officials tomorrow (August 17) in Israel. The meeting likely covers Gaza ceasefire and regional security issues.

**5. Israeli Airstrikes on Khan Younis, Southern Gaza**
Israel's Channel 12 reported that Israeli fighter jets conducted airstrikes on the Khan Younis area in southern Gaza. Military operations are ongoing.
