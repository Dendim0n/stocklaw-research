## 🔥 Key Developments This Hour

**🕊️ Major US-Iran De-escalation Signals**

- **US pauses strikes on Iran**: A Pentagon source told CNN that operations against Iran are now in a "pause" status. After 13 consecutive nights of strikes, US Central Command announced no new strikes on Friday or Saturday.
- **NYT reveals inside story**: President Trump shelved plans for a major escalation of military strikes against Iran, citing concerns that intensifying conflict would dangerously deplete the Pentagon's already-reduced stockpile of Patriot interceptor missiles and other air defense munitions in the Middle East.
- **Iran-Oman Hormuz talks progress**: Iran's Foreign Ministry stated that multiple rounds of deputy foreign minister-level talks were held in Tehran on July 24-25. Both sides conducted productive technical consultations on ensuring safe passage through the Strait of Hormuz, while emphasizing the need to respect the sovereign rights of both coastal states. The spokesperson added that navigation conditions in the strait remain unchanged.

**🛢️ Oil Tumbles on De-escalation**

- WTI crude closed Friday down 2.05% at $90.89, hitting an intraday low of $88.11. Easing tensions around the Strait of Hormuz is the primary driver.
- JP Morgan previously estimated that each month of continued Iran conflict could add $7-8 to oil prices. The current de-escalation means this risk premium is unwinding.

**🐻 "Big Short" Burry Adds to Semiconductor Shorts**

- Michael Burry revealed in a Substack post that he further shorted Micron Technology (MU) at $933.86, while continuing to short NVIDIA (NVDA) and other semiconductor companies.

**🤖 AI Sector Buzz**

- Samsung Chairman Lee Jae-yong met with OpenAI's Sam Altman in San Francisco to discuss AI and semiconductor collaboration.
- NVIDIA and Seoul National University to establish the "NVIDIA AI Technology Center (NVAITC)" focusing on foundation model research.
- Elon Musk told The Economist that China is highly likely to become the AI leader, and US bans on Chinese models won't stop it.
- UK AI Minister: chips and drones will be central to the new PM Burnham's "re-industrialization" strategy.
- 25 tech giants jointly urged Trump not to impose blanket restrictions on open-weight AI models.

**🌪️ Typhoon Hongxia Hits Guangdong**

- Typhoon Hongxia (12th of the season) made landfall in Huidong County, Huizhou, Guangdong. Provincial disaster relief response upgraded to Level II.
- China's NDRC allocated 100 million yuan in central budget investment for emergency post-disaster recovery.
- Hong Kong Observatory lowered signal from Typhoon 8 to Strong Wind Signal 3.
