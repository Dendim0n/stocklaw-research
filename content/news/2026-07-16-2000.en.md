**Middle East: Ceasefire Collapses, Tensions Escalate Further**

The ceasefire "may be over" (Trump's words). JPMorgan indices show Middle East sovereign bond spreads surging to their highest since October 2022. **Iraq: Basra Drone Incident Clarified + Export Surge**

Iraq's SOMO stated the drone incident at the Basra oil terminal was not a direct attack on the terminal or any vessels. IEA Chief Birol warned that despite declining oil reserves, the global economy has yet to overcome risks.

**Russia Responds: US Actions Aim to Control Energy Markets**

Russian security officials explicitly stated that US military actions against Iran and Venezuela are fundamentally about controlling global energy markets — a characterization worth noting.
