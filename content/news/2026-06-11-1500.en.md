## 🔥 Key Developments This Hour

**1. US May CPI: 4.2% YoY, Highest Since April 2023 ⚠️**

A major Russian refining facility was knocked offline by a Ukrainian drone attack. Combined with Ukrainian strikes on Kherson bridges, both sides are escalating attacks on each other's energy and transport infrastructure. Global refining capacity faces dual-side contraction risk.

**3. Markets Reprice for Long US-Iran "War of Attrition"**

With diplomatic efforts stalling, investors are abandoning hopes for a swift resolution. Pricing logic has shifted from short-term shock to prolonged conflict — this explains gold's resilience without dramatic spikes, and why oil keeps falling despite escalation headlines.

**4. Qatar Negotiators Leave Tehran After Overnight Talks With Iran**

Qatar-mediated diplomacy continues with marathon overnight sessions. This remains the only active diplomatic channel; its outcome will determine whether conflict escalates or de-escalates.

**5. Kpler: ~96M Barrels of Non-Iranian Crude Transited Hormuz Since Early May**

Shipping intelligence shows Strait throughput remains substantial, with total exports exceeding 100 million barrels. This explains oil's counterintuitive decline — actual supply disruption is limited despite the rhetoric.

A 25bp hike is widely expected. Jefferies believes this may be the only hike in the cycle, while Berenberg's chief economist warns tightening during wartime is pouring oil on fire. Tonight's updated projections may signal stagflationary pressure.
