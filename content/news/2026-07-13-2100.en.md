🧭 **Market Assessment**

The Strait of Hormuz is the absolute centerpiece this hour. Trump's "dominate the strait" rhetoric and Iran's "no retreat" response create a hard-versus-hard dynamic, with little hope of near-term de-escalation.

WTI crude's 3.18% surge directly reflects the geopolitical risk premium, but the pullback from the 74.94 high suggests the market is still assessing whether the conflict will actually disrupt physical supply.

Gold's 1.28% decline is notable — safe-haven flows appear to be choosing "USD + crude" over gold, as geopolitical conflict pushes inflation expectations higher, paradoxically weighing on bullion.

The Middle East powder keg combined with Europe's gas scramble keeps the energy sector as the volatility epicenter short-term, but geopolitical events are fast-moving — chasing rallies carries significant risk.

Intel's €5B European manufacturing bet extends its Fab strategy; limited near-term catalyst, but the medium-to-long-term execution will be the real test.
