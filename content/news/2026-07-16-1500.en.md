🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🌏 Asia-Pacific Markets Rout**
Nikkei 225 closed down 2.79%, Korea's KOSPI crashed over 6%, and China's three major A-share indices all fell more than 2%, with ChiNext down 3.2%. The securities sector weakened in the afternoon, with Huaan Securities hitting limit down. Zhongji Innolight turnover reached 30 billion yuan, down 5.58%.

**🇺🇸 Trump Tariffs Return + National Address Tonight**
The Trump administration announced a 25% additional tariff on select Brazilian imports effective July 22, citing policies that harm US business competitiveness. Tonight, Trump will deliver a national address again focusing on 2020 election disputes. Critics worry he is reinforcing distrust in the US electoral system ahead of the midterms.

**🏦 Fed Chair Warsh Hearings: Admits White House Contact, Stresses Independence**
Fed Chair Warsh testified for a second consecutive day before Congress, acknowledging regular communication with the Treasury Secretary and White House officials but insisting rate decisions remain independent. Former NY Fed chief economist warns: Warsh is trapped in a "hawkish persona," and the Fed may be forced to hike.

**🇺🇦 Russia-Ukraine Escalation: Drone Strikes + EU Sanctions Stalemate**
Ukraine struck two Russian "shadow fleet" oil tankers in the Black Sea using naval drones. Russia warned that any multinational forces deployed to Ukraine would be considered legitimate military targets. The EU's 21st sanctions package against Russia remains deadlocked.

**🏆 TSMC: AI Demand Strong Through 2030, Packaging Capacity Tight**
TSMC's chairman stated AI will impact all industries, with very strong demand through 2030. A14 process node chips expected to begin mass production in 2028. However, packaging capacity is tight and the company is working to close the gap between advanced packaging demand and capacity. No firm timeline yet for new US investments.

**🚀 SpaceX Briefly Falls Below IPO Price, Down 40%**
SpaceX has crashed 40% from its peak, with over $1 trillion in market value evaporated, severely eroding Musk's holdings.

**💰 Wall Street Trading Revenue Boom**
Top five banks could rake in $180 billion in annual trading revenue, with equity trading as the biggest engine. Unlike post-GFC trading booms, this one is happening near all-time highs.

**🇯🇵 Japan Pushes Trillion-Yen Pension Fund Back Home**
Japan's Finance Minister reiterated plans to encourage GPIF to increase domestic asset holdings and explore venture capital. Options markets suggest the yen could slide toward 165.

**🏅 Fidelity Plans Return to Gold**
After the recent pullback, Fidelity International plans to rebuild its previously trimmed gold positions, maintaining conviction in gold's long-term thesis and expecting a bull market return in 2027.
