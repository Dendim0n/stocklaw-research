🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**Iran Crisis: US Strikes Abadan, IAEA Probes Nuclear Plant Attack**

Outskirts of Abadan city in Iran's Khuzestan province were hit by missile strikes, with Iran attributing the attack to the US military. The IAEA is investigating a reported overnight attack on the construction site of Iran's Darkhovin nuclear plant, which remains in early construction with no nuclear material present during the agency's last visit. Iran's military reports shooting down a "Lucas" enemy drone in the south.

US Energy Secretary Wright stated unequivocally that military operations against Iran will continue until Trump achieves his objectives — preventing Iran from acquiring nuclear weapons and degrading its military capabilities.

Israel's Defense Minister warned that any Iranian attack would be met with strikes on their missile platforms. Aqaba port authority confirmed operations were unaffected after intercepting missiles.

**Trump on North Sea Oil: UK to Fully Develop**

Trump issued a series of statements claiming incoming UK Prime Minister Burnham will fully open North Sea oil resources, calling it "among the finest oil anywhere on Earth, with centuries of production remaining." He criticized the UK for paying Norway billions annually for oil while Norway only exploits "less valuable areas." Trump asserted this would transform the UK from "a poor, disaster-ridden nation into one of the wealthiest countries in the world."

Expectations of full North Sea development combined with Iran supply disruption risk drove WTI crude up 3.6% in a single session.

**Japan Political Risk: Kochi Cabinet Approval Below 50% for First Time**

A Mainichi Shimbun poll shows Prime Minister Kochi Hayaka's cabinet approval plunging from 51% in June to 41%, with disapproval rising 9pp to 44%. This marks the first sub-50% reading since she took office. The Nikkei 225 crashed 4.45% on Thursday, with political uncertainty as the primary driver.

**China's "National Team" Continues Market Support**

China Guoxin has deployed over RMB 50 billion through special stock buyback relending facilities and matching funds to stabilize markets. China Chengtong and affiliated platforms have purchased nearly RMB 10 billion in Chinese equities and pledged to continue large-scale buying using proprietary capital and relending facilities.
