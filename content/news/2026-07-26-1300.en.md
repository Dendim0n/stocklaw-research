## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**Typhoon "Hongxia" Hits Guangdong — Emergency Level II**
Typhoon Hongxia (No. 12) made landfall near Huidong County, Huizhou, as a severe typhoon (14-level, 45 m/s). Guangdong upgraded its disaster relief response to Level II. The NDRC allocated ¥100M in central budget funds for post-disaster recovery. Hong Kong Observatory lowered the signal to T3. For the first time, China deployed swarm drones for full-process 3D observation of typhoon landfall.
