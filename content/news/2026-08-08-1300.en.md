🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. BHP Port Hedland Terminal Workers Begin 24-Hour Strike**
BHP terminal workers at Port Hedland in Western Australia launched a 24-hour strike on Saturday morning, halting bulk carrier loading from Aug 8-9. Port Hedland is the world's largest iron ore export port; the short-term shipping disruption could provide support for iron ore prices.

**2. Russian Ilysky Refinery Catches Fire from Drone Debris**
Russia's operations command reported that the Ilysky refinery caught fire after drone debris fell on the facility. This is the latest in a series of energy infrastructure strikes in the Russia-Ukraine conflict, sustaining geopolitical risk premiums.

**3. Musk Refuses Ukraine Starlink Access for Strikes on Russian Territory**
According to U.S. sources, SpaceX founder Elon Musk explicitly refused to allow Ukraine's military to use the Starlink satellite internet system for strikes against Russian territory. This decision further underscores the critical role of private tech companies in geopolitical conflicts.

**4. Typhoon "Baihai" Disrupts Fujian Coastal Shipping**
Typhoon "Baihai" has forced the suspension of 40 passenger and ferry routes along Fujian's coast, with 76 vessels halted, 115 maritime construction projects suspended, and 290 construction vessels evacuated. Short-term disruption to domestic supply chains and port operations expected.

**5. Salesforce Lays Off 74 at San Francisco HQ**
Filings indicate Salesforce will cut 74 positions at its San Francisco headquarters. While modest in scale, it continues the broader tech industry layoff trend.
