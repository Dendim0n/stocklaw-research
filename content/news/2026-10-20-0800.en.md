## 3. Judgements and counter-evidence

**Judgement 1: today's +2.22% in crude is an expiry-week front-month squeeze, not a Q4 supply-demand repricing; using 103.66 as an oil benchmark overstates Q4 prices by roughly $5.**

Evidence: in the same settlement batch November rose 2.22% while December fell 0.50%, and the spread widened from 2.37 to 5.12; the platform series (second-month magnitude) was essentially flat (97.936 to 97.917); the Jin10 calendar pins the rollover at 02:30 on 10-21. **Counter-evidence and failure conditions**: Iran said at 03:21 it has not yet linked Suez, warned about undersea cables at 03:05, Dammam airport stopped operating at 02:37, Israel's "overthrow the regime" order at 01:20 and the US defense secretary's visit to Israel at 06:34 all leave upside optionality in the risk premium; if December trades above 100 with diesel cracks widening, it is genuine supply pricing rather than a technical squeeze. The medium-term offsets are shadow-fleet legalisation (dozens to hundreds of tankers) and G7 reserve releases, while "no Russian diesel released yet" at 04:01 means diesel tightness persists. Verification points: API tonight at 04:30, rollover at 02:30 on 10-21, EIA at 22:30 on 10-21.

**Judgement 2: the US move is a memory-led oversold repair plus event flow, not a broad recovery in risk appetite; with the 10Y at 5.15% and a 17.2% October hike probability, its durability depends on memory pricing, not on macro.**

Evidence: the SOX +1.61% ended eight straight declines but is still 22.78% below its 52-week high; the Nasdaq-100 was up 2% intraday and closed +1.17%; the Dow fell 0.07% and the S&P sits 1.96% below its high; leadership was concentrated in memory and cloud (SK Hynix +4.6%, Micron +2.97%, Amazon +4.98%), not broad; the 10Y rose 6bp the same day. **Counter-evidence**: if the SOX reclaims 11,500 and the 10Y falls below 5.05%, the "rates compress multiples" narrative weakens and the bounce can be upgraded to a trend; if Micron's 10-21 technology briefing delivers above-consensus HBM pricing, memory can trade on its own cycle. Failure condition: a SOX drop back below 11,000 that breaks the 10-16 low makes today noise.

**Judgement 3: accelerating private AI financing coexists with public-market valuation anxiety - near term it is an order-book positive for compute, memory and power, medium term it is a valuation-anchor risk for high-multiple names.**

Evidence: OpenAI raising about $30 billion at a possible valuation above $1.4 trillion, timed before Anthropic's mid-November IPO; the $45 billion Nscale-Anthropic cloud deal; both point to compute and power orders through 2027. **Counter-evidence**: both reports come from unnamed sources and neither amount nor valuation has been confirmed by the companies; "locking in money before the IPO" shows the same investors choosing between two issuers - competition for capital, not new demand; yesterday's Barclays private-credit review shows the credit end is already under scrutiny. Verification: the Anthropic IPO range, Micron's 10-21 briefing, and capex guidance from Meta and Tesla this week.
