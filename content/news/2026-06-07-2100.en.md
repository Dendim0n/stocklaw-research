🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🔴 OPEC+ formally confirms July production increase of 188k bpd**

Seven OPEC+ countries will raise oil production quotas by 188,000 barrels per day in July. This upgrades from prior "reported plans" to an official statement. Despite US-Iran war hindering several members' capacity to boost output, this marks the fourth increase in just a few months — supply-side pressure continues to mount.

**🔴 Israel strikes Beirut southern suburbs with 3 missiles**

Israeli Air Force fired 3 missiles hitting two apartments in Beirut's Dahieh district. This follows the Israeli military's announcement of strikes against "terrorist headquarters" in Beirut, in response to Hezbollah firing on Israeli territory. The conflict has escalated from ground reconnaissance to airstrikes — a significant deterioration.

**🟡 US drafts IAEA resolution: Iran's nuclear safeguards cannot be unilaterally modified**

A US-drafted IAEA Board of Governors resolution emphasizes that Iran's NPT safeguards agreement cannot be unilaterally modified or suspended, and demands Iran provide accurate information on nuclear materials and facilities without delay. The US-Iran confrontation extends into nuclear diplomacy.

**🟡 US Central Command: 132 merchant ships rerouted, 6 disabled**

As of June 7, US Central Command has rerouted 132 merchant vessels and disabled 6. Maritime security in the Middle East remains under severe pressure, posing potential risks to global supply chains.

**🟢 Huang Renxun and SK Group chairman to announce partnership Monday**

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang and SK Group Chairman Choi Tae-won will announce a cooperation plan on Monday. Following Huang's recent comments that memory shortages will persist for years, this partnership may involve HBM capacity expansion — a critical AI supply chain development.
