🔥 **Key News This Hour**

🔴 **SpaceX to acquire Cursor maker Anysphere for $60B** — SpaceX announced it will acquire Anysphere, developer of the popular AI coding agent Cursor, for $60 billion, expanding its footprint in enterprise AI. The deal sets a new record for AI sector acquisitions.

🔴 **EU Parliament approves US-EU trade deal** — The European Parliament voted to approve EU-US trade deal legislation, which will now go to the EU Council for final approval. Germany's chemical industry association (VCI) welcomed the move. Trump had previously pressured France over digital regulation.

🔴 **DeepSeek first funding round confirmed** — DeepSeek's first funding round has been finalized. Founder Liang Wenfeng personally invested approximately 20 billion yuan as the largest single investor; Tencent contributed roughly 10 billion yuan; CATL's ecosystem also participated.

🔴 **US-Iran to sign interim peace deal Friday** — The US and Iran are preparing to formally sign an interim peace deal in Switzerland on Friday, with both sides claiming victory. The G7 plans to assist with mine-sweeping in the Strait of Hormuz, but shipping giants remain skeptical about how quickly navigation can resume.

🟡 **BofA survey: 40% of investors expect Fed rate hike within 12 months** — Bank of America's June global fund manager survey found 40% of investors expect the Fed to raise rates at least once in the next 12 months, up sharply from 16% in May. 28% expect at least one cut.

🟡 **Brent crude drops below $80** — Brent fell 3% intraday to $80.45/bbl. Markets are pricing in the ceasefire, but shipping giant Mitsui O.S.K. warns "substantive security guarantees" are needed before resuming full navigation.

🟡 **G7 backs more energy sanctions on Russia** — France stated that G7 leaders support imposing additional energy sanctions on Russia.

🟡 **Poland to tax windfall fuel profits** — PM Tusk said the government will pass a draft bill Tuesday to impose taxes on windfall profits from fuel sales.
