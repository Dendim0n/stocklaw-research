## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. NVIDIA Scales Back OpenAI Data Center Guarantee**

Per WSJ, NVIDIA and OpenAI are nearing a financing deal for a 10GW data center campus in Ohio, but the deal structure has been revised: NVIDIA's guarantee reduced from $250B to under $120B, covering only 5GW, in response to investor concerns over financial risk.

**2. Trump Urges Americans to Accept Higher Oil Prices**

Trump stated in a New York speech that Americans should "accept higher gas prices" as the cost of stopping Iran from obtaining nuclear weapons, and again claimed he would declare the Strait of Hormuz as US territory after "defeating Iran."

**3. Iran Responds Forcefully to Trump**

Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister responded that "the Strait of Hormuz cannot be controlled by a tweet, an aircraft carrier, or an executive order"; IRGC Navy Commander stated the strait remains under full Iranian military control.

**4. UAE Vessel Attacked in Strait of Hormuz**

An Abu Dhabi National Oil Company vessel was attacked on the night of the 14th while transiting the Strait of Hormuz, with no casualties. This follows reports of two UAE tankers attacked by Iran on the night of the 13th.

**5. Middle East Situation Update**

US Central Command denies pushing for a new military strike on Iran; 62 merchant vessels diverted, 3 disabled. Israel launched a new round of airstrikes on Al-Mansouri in southern Lebanon.

**6. Russia-Ukraine Conflict Update**

Finland established temporary air and maritime traffic restrictions in the eastern Gulf of Finland; a drone was shot down in Latvian airspace with NATO fighters scrambled; Lavrov stated Russia will intensify efforts to destroy Western aid to Ukraine.

**7. Daily Tech Highlights**

Alibaba open-sources Qwen3.8 (over 3 billion global downloads); Apple partners with Alibaba to train a China-specific large model; Zhipu releases GLM-5.3 model with improved coding and long-duration task performance.

**8. Indonesia 7.7 Magnitude Earthquake**

A 7.7 magnitude earthquake struck near Flores Island; tsunami warning lifted; 1 death reported so far.
