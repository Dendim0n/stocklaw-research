## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Hormuz Strait Shipping Volumes Begin to Rise**
The Joint Maritime Information Centre (JMIC) issued multiple updates: vessel traffic in the Arabian Gulf is increasing, shipping volumes through the Strait of Hormuz via Oman and Iran routes are picking up, and the maritime security threat level has been downgraded to "Medium." This marks the first clear signal of shipping recovery following the US-Iran negotiations.

**2. VP Vance: Coordination Mechanism Established to Prevent Middle East Escalation**
US Vice President JD Vance stated in Switzerland that the US has established a coordination mechanism to prevent further conflict escalation in the Middle East, noting it has already shown results with the situation in Lebanon calming recently. Vance also revealed that once the US agrees to unfreeze Iranian assets, those funds could be used to purchase American soybeans, corn, and wheat.

**3. US-Iran Finalize Negotiation Roadmap**
Iran's foreign minister previously outlined multiple breakthroughs: establishment of a working group to address the Lebanese issue, exemptions for Iranian oil and petrochemical exports, lifting of blockades, and partial unfreezing of frozen assets. Both sides have now finalized a roadmap for subsequent negotiations.

**4. Saudi Central Bank Redeems Funds from Asset Managers**
Market reports indicate the Saudi central bank has redeemed funds from at least two asset management companies, with the redemptions reportedly beginning before the outbreak of hostilities. The bank has reallocated some funds to alternative investment strategies, suggesting a forward-looking portfolio adjustment to geopolitical risks.

**5. UK Reform Party Demands Snap Election**
Reform Party leader Nigel Farage has called for a general election, claiming readiness to "drive radical change." This comes after PM Keir Starmer announced his resignation, deepening political turmoil in the UK.

**6. UBTECH Unveils Walker C1 Commercial Robot**
UBTECH Robotics debuted the next-generation Walker C1 humanoid robot designed for commercial service scenarios at the China International Supply Chain Expo.

## 🧭 Situation Assessment

The increase in Hormuz Strait shipping volumes and the security downgrade signal that US-Iran negotiation outcomes are materializing, with geopolitical risk premiums beginning to unwind.

Crude oil's outsized decline reflects not only the easing of supply disruption fears from restored shipping, but also Saudi Arabia's preemptive portfolio rebalancing, which suggests oil producers themselves are not optimistic about the outlook.

Gold remains elevated on lingering safe-haven momentum, but if Middle East tensions continue to de-escalate, safe-haven demand may gradually fade, and the risk of a pullback from elevated levels is building.

UK political instability and the yen approaching a 38-year low keep uncertainty elevated in non-US markets, providing a degree of hedging support for gold.

Overall, geopolitical risk premiums are being repriced downward, which could open a relief window for risk assets, but volatility remains elevated.
