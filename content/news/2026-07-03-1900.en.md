**Gold Surges: Strategist Says Prices Return to Fair Value, Eyes $5,000 This Year**

Japan's largest labor union federation Rengo released final statistics showing 5,368 companies averaging 5.01% wage increases. Markets are pricing in a BOJ rate hike by year-end, with the wage-inflation virtuous cycle narrative continuing to strengthen.

Samsung will invest approximately 60 trillion won (~$43 billion USD) in South Korea's Yeongnam region to build production centers for humanoid robots, solid-state batteries, AI server packaging substrates, and high-value-added ships.
