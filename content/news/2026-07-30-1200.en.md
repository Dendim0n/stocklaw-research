**US Treasury Yields Surge to 2007 Highs as Warsh's "Silence" Backfires**

**Japan's Takaichi Pushes Food Tax Cut to 1%**
The Japanese PM decided to temporarily cut the food and soft drink sales tax from 8% to 1% starting April next year, while pledging not to rely on deficit-covering bonds to maintain market confidence. The same day saw weak demand at the 2-year JGB auction.
