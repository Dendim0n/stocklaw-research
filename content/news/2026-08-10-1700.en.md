🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Eurozone Sentix Investor Confidence Returns to Positive**
August Sentix investor confidence index came in at 0.9, well above the expected -0.5 and prior -3.1. The improvement was driven by significantly better assessment of current conditions, marking the fourth consecutive month of improvement and signaling European economic recovery.

**2. Citadel Securities Turns Bullish on Gold for First Time This Year**
Citadel Securities strategists listed five catalysts: Fed policy pivot expectations, central bank gold buying, short covering, options signals, and retail fund inflows — suggesting further upside for gold prices.

**3. JPMorgan Raises S&P 500 Target to 8,000**
JPMorgan is bullish on AI entering a new "profit monetization" phase, shifting from "spending to build AI" to earnings-driven growth. Also raised tech bond issuance forecast to over $500 billion this year.

**4. Low Rhine River Levels Force German Chemical Giants to Cut Output**
Covestro and BASF were forced to cut sales or production of some petrochemical products due to extremely low Rhine River water levels, and have issued force majeure notices to customers. European natural gas prices rose in tandem.

**5. Apple Seeks to Source Chips from CXMT**
According to the Wall Street Journal, under cost pressure from "chipflation" driven by the AI investment boom, Apple is looking at domestic memory semiconductors. CXMT responded that disclosures should be based on official announcements.

**6. US-Iran Tensions Continue to Escalate**
Iran's Army Commander made hardline statements: "If US forces dare set foot on Iranian soil, we will cut off their feet." The Foreign Ministry spokesperson spoke extensively on the Strait of Hormuz, Ukrainian attacks on Iranian cargo ships, and Iran's constant readiness enhancement.

**7. Alibaba Cloud Plans to More Than Triple Global Data Center Capacity**
Based on a fully modular design architecture, large AIDC data center delivery time has been reduced to 100 days, with construction costs down over 10%.
