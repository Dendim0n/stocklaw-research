🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. 🔴 WTI Crude Plunges 8.3% — Hormuz Strait Talks in "Final Stage"**

Iran claimed that Hormuz Strait negotiations are ongoing and have entered the "final stage." Crude prices tumbled, with WTI posting a single-day decline exceeding 8%. The geopolitical risk premium accumulated from Middle East tensions is rapidly unwinding. However, former Iranian FM Zarif warned: if no final agreement is reached within the 60-day negotiation window, Iran could face further economic hardship, domestic unrest, or renewed military strikes.

**2. 🇯🇵 Japan Likely Spent $34B on Friday to Stabilize Yen — Monthly Record**

Latest analysis suggests Japanese authorities may have deployed approximately $34 billion on Friday following Thursday's massive intervention. The US-Japan monetary policy coordination is now the tightest in 15 years.

Eli Lilly announced its pancreatic cancer therapy received FDA breakthrough therapy designation. Pancreatic cancer remains one of the largest unmet needs in oncology — a positive signal for LLY's pipeline value.

**4. 📈 Tesla Europe July Sales Sharply Diverged**

Tesla's July registrations surged 86% in France and 52% in Denmark, but collapsed 97% in Norway, 81% in Spain, and 60% in Sweden. Extreme market divergence — investors await Germany and UK data later this week.

Alibaba officially launched its new foundation model Qwen3.8-Max with 2.4 trillion total parameters, becoming the second Chinese model to surpass two trillion parameters after KimiK3. Available on the National Supercomputing Internet platform on launch day.

**6. 🏦 US Treasury Refuses to Adjust Bond Issuance Guidance — Wall Street Gives Up**

Treasury Secretary Bessent's debt management team has persistently rejected recommendations from Wall Street and TBAC to eliminate forward guidance from the Treasury issuance plan. Wall Street is gradually abandoning expectations for this adjustment.
