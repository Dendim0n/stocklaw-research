## 🔥 Key Overnight News

**Fed Rate Hike Expectations Surge as Warsh Hawkish Signals Rattle Markets**

Castle Securities issued a rare warning — markets may be underestimating Fed Chair Warsh's hawkishness, with a surprise 25bp rate hike possible this week. This stands in stark contrast to the Fed's prior "on hold, data-dependent" narrative. Citi strategists simultaneously warned that small-cap stocks would be the hardest hit if a hike materializes.

**Trump Attacks Fed "Rate-Cut Opponents," Demands Lowest Rates Globally**

Trump publicly commented on Warsh on Monday, calling him "excellent" but constrained by a group of "very political" board members with "bad intentions." He insisted: "America must have the lowest interest rates in the world." This clashes sharply with Warsh's potential hawkish leanings, sending Fed policy uncertainty soaring.

**Iran: Final Diplomatic Window Alongside Military Threats**

Trump stated he is holding off on striking Iran to allow diplomacy, but warned negotiations must "move fast" — if they fail, the US will resume even larger-scale military action. Netanyahu departed for the US on Monday and is scheduled to meet Trump at Mar-a-Lago today, with the Iran nuclear issue as the central agenda item.

**Nvidia's $750 Billion "Circular Financing" Model Raises Wall Street Concerns**

Nvidia's practice of lending to customers so they can buy its own chips is being criticized as amplifying systemic risk across the entire industry, triggering another round of debate on AI investment bubbles.

**FAA Warns: Hundreds of Boeing 737 MAX Aircraft Have Improperly Installed Seats**

The FAA stated that Boeing 737 MAX seats may loosen during emergency landings and proposed new rules requiring airlines to fix the issue.

## 🧭 Market Assessment

The core overnight tension lies in the sharp divergence of Fed policy expectations — Warsh's hawkish rate hike signals versus Trump's public demands for rate cuts — this is the single largest source of uncertainty for markets right now.

The Iran situation is at a critical diplomatic inflection point, and the outcome of the Netanyahu-Trump meeting will directly impact Middle East risk premiums.

The significant miss in US June durable goods orders points to weakening economic fundamentals that do not support aggressive rate hikes; a Warsh-led hike would risk being a policy error.

Nvidia's financing model controversy continues to simmer, and AI sector valuation pressure remains unrelieved — sentiment contagion bears watching.

Gold barely declined despite rising rate hike expectations, indicating strong safe-haven demand, supported by the dual uncertainties of the Middle East and Fed policy.
