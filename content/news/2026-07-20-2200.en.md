## 🔥 Key Developments This Hour

**⚔️ Iran Launches Three-Phase Strike on US Military Targets in Bahrain and Kuwait**
The IRGC announced "Victory 2" Wave 23 operations, striking US positions in Bahrain and Kuwait across three phases. Bahrain's Civil Aviation Authority confirmed navigation equipment was hit by Iranian drones. The Kuwaiti military is actively intercepting missiles and drones. This marks a substantive escalation in the US-Iran conflict.

**🛢️ Russia Accuses Ukraine of Attacking CPC Tankers; Kazakhstan Oil Hub Paralyzed**
Russia accused Ukraine of attacking oil tankers during loading at the Caspian Pipeline Consortium (CPC) terminal, claiming attempts to destabilize global oil markets. This follows the forced shutdown of Kazakhstan's critical oil export terminal due to Black Sea drone strikes, triggering a dual crisis in energy and grain supply.
