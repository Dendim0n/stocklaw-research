🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. US July Existing Home Sales: 4.06M Annualized, Slightly Better Than Expected but Still Declining**
July existing home sales annualized at 4.06M units vs. 4.05M expected, prior 4.09M; monthly rate -1.7% vs. -1% expected, prior -2.4%. Downward trend continues but pace of decline is narrowing.

**2. NVIDIA Launches Nemotron 4 Open-Source AI Model, Parameters Expected to Exceed 1 Trillion**
NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning open model, runnable locally on RTX PCs, DGX Spark, GB10 OEM systems, and Jetson platforms, partnering with vLLM, Ollama, llama.cpp, and others. According to The Information, engineers involved expect the largest Nemotron 4 model to have at least 1 trillion parameters. NVIDIA stock up 1.8% at open.

**3. Intel Expands Share Offering and Prices, Stock Down 1.1%**

**4. Rocket Lab Neutron Rocket First Flight Likely Delayed, Stock Down 3.9%**

**5. Abbott Partners with Google on AI-Powered Glucose Health Management**
Abbott and Google launched their first collaborative project, combining Lingo bios wearable glucose monitoring with Google health technology, including large-scale AI training research.

**6. Russian Crude Exports Drop to 3.71M Barrels/Day**
In the four weeks ending August 9, Russian crude exports fell to 3.71M bpd, the lowest since late May. The export surge from Ukrainian strikes on Russian refineries is fading. Meanwhile, SIBUR's ZapSibNeftekhim petrochemical plant suspended operations after a drone attack.

**7. EU Electricity Coordination Group: Supply Situation to Remain Tense This Week**
The EU electricity coordination group discussed supply security, stating no short-term adequacy risk but the situation will remain tense throughout the week.

**8. JPMorgan Downgrades Brazil to Neutral, Upgrades Peru to Overweight**
JPMorgan cut Brazilian equities from "overweight" to "neutral" while upgrading Peruvian equities to "overweight."

**9. IIF: EM Portfolio Flows Turn Positive in July, But Equity Outflows Reach $86B YTD**
IIF data shows non-resident investors injected $18.8B net into emerging markets in July, ending two consecutive months of outflows. However, YTD EM equity outflows reached $86B vs. $9B in the same period last year.
