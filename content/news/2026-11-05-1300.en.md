## 3. Key incremental items

**① Korea and Japan make new lows; KOSPI breaks the morning low | 13:28–13:29**
KOSPI at 5,385.13 (−5.25%) with an intraday low of 5,367.56, below the 5,386.29 low at 09:22; the Nikkei at 60,971.64 (−1.96%) is at its session low. At 09:18 the Korea Exchange had already triggered the sidecar mechanism on KOSPI to suspend programme selling, with SK Hynix down more than 9% and Samsung Electronics down 7% (Jin10 09:22); at 13:04–13:05 Korea's finance minister said the country "must prepare for the possibility of increased market volatility" and would watch US AI policy after the midterms (Jin10 13:05). **Impact**: the selling is concentrated in memory and semiconductor heavyweights, and it continued after circuit-breaker tools were deployed — this is a continuing re-rating of regulatory discount, not a one-off opening shock.

**② A-shares flip negative in the afternoon; STAR50 gives back nearly 4% from its high | 13:13–13:28**
Shanghai Composite 3,670.27 (−0.43%), down 0.53% from the 3,689.79 midday level; STAR50 1,276.53 (+0.87%), 3.96% below the 1,329.14 morning high; ChiNext down from +1.24% to +0.23%. The structure is intact: CSI 500 +1.38%, Shenzhen Composite +0.75%, CSI 300 just −0.06%. **Impact**: the midday read of "excellent breadth, lagging index" (stock rotation, not new money) was confirmed in the afternoon — high-flying themes are being distributed, while low-level small caps still find buyers.

**③ Toyota Q2 operating profit +34%, buyback raised to ¥1.3 trillion | 12:56–13:02**
Toyota's July–September operating profit rose 34% y/y, the first increase in six quarters; the full-year dividend was raised to ¥100 and the buyback expanded to ¥1.3 trillion; at the same time the company cut its full-year group retail sales guidance to 10.99 million units from 11.18 million and changed its USD/JPY assumption to 157 from 160. **⑤ Two side-evidences on AI: revenue mix and new orders | 12:02**

## 4. Situation assessment

On weights, SK Hynix −9% and Samsung −7% show the selling is entirely in memory. **Impact**: the memory chain, Korea-exposed export proxies, and A-share/Hong Kong memory and optical modules are near-term bearish; holders face widening rather than released risk. **Invalidation**: if KOSPI closes at 14:30 above 5,500 (about −3.2%), there was real afternoon absorption and this section downgrades to intraday noise; if it closes below 5,367.56, the catch-down risk for tomorrow's Asian open needs re-assessment.

Hong Kong moved the other way: Hang Seng +3.23% and Hang Seng Tech +5.05%, both above their midday closes. **Impact**: Hong Kong platforms and apps still have allocation support, but after this run they sit at session highs where the risk/reward of chasing is poor; A-share STAR and computing-power themes are near-term bearish, low-level small caps relatively favoured. **Invalidation**: if the Shanghai Composite closes at 15:00 above yesterday's 3,686.17 and STAR50 recovers 1,300, "distribution" should be reclassified as a shakeout; if Hang Seng Tech breaks 4,000 by the 16:00 close (about −3.3% from here), Hong Kong's repair rally is over too.

**C. Commodities: gold's momentum is fading, crude is finding bids at the low — neither justifies a directional bet.** Evidence: gold is up 1.58% on the day but down 0.31% this hour and 0.55% below 4,146.79, while silver's gain narrowed from +1.59% to +0.95%; crude is −1.10% but has bounced 0.50% from 86.629, and did not rise after Russia reported a drone strike on an industrial facility in Ryazan. **Trade-off**: gold shifts from "chase" to "wait" — 4,100 is a pullback watch level, not a buy signal; crude gets no directional bet, with 86.6 the low tested for a second time today. **Invalidation**: gold below 4,100 with silver below 52.2 would mark the end of this metals bounce; crude back above 88.12 (prior settle) would force a rethink of the "war premium is zero" pricing.
