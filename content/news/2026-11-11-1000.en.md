## 2. Key increments

**① Bowman puts AI financing into financial-stability risk, with an $800bn figure｜09:44, Jin10 ID 234888**
Her prepared remarks put AI-related financing through corporate bonds, private credit and project finance at **more than $800bn this year**. Hyperscalers and chip designers use their high ratings to carry **off-balance-sheet liabilities**, letting smaller firms pledge those for equipment financing while the same large firms take equity stakes in them — what she called "**the appearance of circular financing**". If returns disappoint, she expects "a sharp correction in equity valuations" and "a sharp rise in long-term rates in private markets". The same piece hints she could back a December hike; Logan said at least two more 25bp moves are needed.
What is new is the mechanism and the size. Implication: the AI-bubble question has moved from market chatter to a Fed governor's financial-stability agenda — bearish for richly valued AI hardware and data centres. **This is a speech, not policy**: she also conceded October's pause was meant to observe the effects of September's hike.

## 3. Read on the situation

**A｜The bounce is watchable, not chaseable.** Futures and Asian semis are up together while Chinese assets fall alone, and gold, silver and copper all firmed at the same time — that looks like a short breather in the dollar and discount rate, not new information on demand. The trade-off: sit out this bounce and wait for two verifiable points — whether Nasdaq-100 futures hold above the prior close after the US open, and whether the 10-year Treasury backs off 4.98%. **Invalidation**: if NVDA reclaims 178.67 and SOX reclaims 10,033, yesterday's breakdown was a one-day overreaction and the bearish call has to change with it.

**B｜Japan is the most easily missed variable.** Today's JGB move is not yet a hike being priced (the yen did not strengthen), so the immediate shock is smaller than the US CPI; but it opens a new tail risk: short ends rising faster than long ends (bear flattening), the currency falling instead, and the PM stressing in-session that fiscal policy will not overheat inflation. That hits global duration and carry-sensitive high-beta equity. **Confirmation**: if USD/JPY breaks 157 while the 10-year JGB keeps rising, this becomes hike pricing and volatility steps up a level.

**C｜Precious metals need confirmation, crude needs data.** Gold's 0.3% bounce did not reclaim 4,070–4,080 and came with no ETF accumulation — **the structure is still bearish, only the downside momentum is fading**. Crude: API showed **+8.968m bbl** for the week to 06 Nov, while the Jin10 calendar has EIA consensus at **−1.109m bbl** for the same week (due 11-13 01:00 Beijing). With two numbers that far apart, taking a directional position near $86 is a bet on the data source; wait for the official print before talking about trend.
