## 3. Key Increments

**① [13:08–13:09 | Meta completes equity pricing: common upsized to $25bn at $625/share, total raise capped at $95bn]**

- Company announcement (via Jin10): the common stock offering was **upsized from $22.5bn to $25.0bn**; Class A common tied to a forward sale agreement was **priced at $625/share**, a **0.88%** discount to the 10-26 close of 630.56; depositary shares of the Series A mandatory convertible preferred were **priced at $50 each**.
- Full structure (announced Monday 10-26): roughly **$35bn** of securities = $22.5bn common + **$12.5bn of 3-year mandatory convertible preferred**, plus an ATM of up to **$60bn** of non-voting Class C shares from **2027**, for a **$95bn** ceiling. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom indicated interest in subscribing to up to **$5bn** combined, **non-binding**.
- Pressure side: the market expects capex of about **$140bn**, exceeding operating cash flow; the 5-year CDS spread has roughly **doubled since August to 120bp** (media figures, not company disclosure). At today's price, the common portion alone adds 40.0m shares, about **1.57%** of the existing 2.548bn shares.
- Same-day sibling deals: Broadcom arranged **$63bn** of financing with BofA, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley through a newly formed SPV that buys chips and leases them to Anthropic (Bloomberg basis); SpaceX signed a roughly **$40bn** equipment lease with Apollo to buy Nvidia GPUs — **final pricing and rate not set, closing expected by year-end** — Musk had earlier said the deal was done, while the company's Monday filing shows it is still to be priced.
- Impact: two ends of one trade — buyers convert equity and structured debt into compute, sellers book orders and financing. For buyers, the valuation anchor shifts from "EPS growth" to "free cash flow after dilution and interest."

The same day brought Broadcom's $63bn SPV and SpaceX's $40bn off-balance-sheet lease; what all three share is that **equity and structured debt are now financing what operating cash flow used to**. Impact: mega-cap platforms must be revalued on "free cash flow after dilution and interest," not EPS growth; conversely the parties getting paid — packaging, memory, equipment, power — gain order visibility, with SK hynix's report tonight and this week's platform capex guidance as the two-end verification.
