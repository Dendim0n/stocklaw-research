🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**US-Iran Conflict Fully Escalates as Iran Strikes Multiple US Gulf Bases**

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps issued multiple statements this morning, announcing the third phase of retaliatory operations: IRGC aerospace forces destroyed the US carrier logistics support center and refueling platform at Oman's Duqm Port; simultaneously struck Qatar's Al Udeid Air Base, destroying fighter maintenance centers and command & control facilities; a US military radar station in Kuwait was also hit. Al Arabiya reported Iran launched fresh attacks against Bahrain, Qatar, UAE, and Kuwait simultaneously.

**US Completes Third Round of Strikes, Over 300 Iranian Targets Hit**

US CENTCOM stated it completed its third round of strikes against Iran this week on July 11, employing land-based and carrier-based fighter jets, drones, and naval vessels to strike approximately 140 Iranian military targets with precision munitions, including missile and drone bases, naval capabilities, ammunition storage facilities, communication networks, and coastal surveillance sites. Total targets struck now exceed 300.

**Fire at US Navy 5th Fleet Facility in Bahrain After Iranian Missile Strike**

Market sources report a fire at the US Navy 5th Fleet facility in Bahrain after Iranian missiles struck the area. Bahrain's Interior Ministry sounded alarms urging citizens to proceed to nearest safe locations, later announcing the danger alert was lifted. Kuwait's Armed Forces stated they are intercepting hostile aerial targets in Kuwaiti airspace.

**Iran: Era of Unilateral Agreements Is Over**

Iranian Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf posted on social media that "the era of unilateral agreements is over," demanding parties honor commitments and obligations or pay the price. An Iranian military spokesperson stated US attempts to open an "illegal shipping lane" in the Strait of Hormuz are worsening regional insecurity. US CENTCOM responded that commercial vessel transit through the Strait of Hormuz continues.
