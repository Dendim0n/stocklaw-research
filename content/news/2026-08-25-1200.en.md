🔥 **Top News This Hour**

**Strait of Hormuz transit hits new low; Pakistan-Iran hold talks** — Preliminary shipping data show only one bulk commodity vessel transited the Strait of Hormuz on Monday, the lowest since May 7. **UK authorizes local cruise missile production in Ukraine; Moscow threatens strikes** — Britain's new PM made Kyiv his first visit and authorized local cruise missile manufacturing in Ukraine; the Kremlin warned it would "systematically eliminate all missile production bases" in Ukraine, adding fresh geopolitical tension.
