## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**🕊️ Lebanon-Israel Ceasefire Details Still in Flux**

Although Israel and Hezbollah have agreed to a ceasefire, details remain unclear:

- Israeli Channel 12: An Israeli official confirmed the ceasefire but stated "we will remain in the security zone in southern Lebanon; if they attack us, we will retaliate"
- IDF spokesperson: The military will continue to be stationed in the "buffer zone" as long as Israeli citizens face Hezbollah threats
- Kann News: IDF said "the agreement was reached at the political level; we have not received any other instructions"
- White House senior official: Netanyahu "100% agreed" to the Lebanon ceasefire

While the ceasefire is in place, Israel retains the right to maintain troops and self-defense. Coordination gaps between military and political levels mean execution risks remain.

**🏦 Fed Rate Hike Bets Surge (Major)**

Market pricing shows rising bets on a Fed rate hike, **fully pricing in a 25bp hike in September**. This is another significant confirmation following Goldman Sachs' gold target cut and Waller's hawkish signals from the previous hour. Dollar bulls' momentum continues to build.

**🇮🇷 US-Iran Talks Postponed Over Islamic Calendar**

Pakistan's Foreign Minister Dar revealed that the next round of US-Iran negotiations in Switzerland has been postponed due to the arrival of the Islamic month of Muharram, with talks set to resume after the month ends. The delay reduces near-term expectations for a US-Iran breakthrough.

**🇷🇺 Russia Sells Gold Miner Stake Below Expectations**

Russia sold a seized top gold mining company stake for $1.3 billion, roughly half the initial expected price. The timing of Russia's divestiture is far from ideal amid gold's volatile downturn.

**🇮🇱 US Senator Criticizes Iran Policy as "Art of Catastrophe"**

Democratic Senate leader Schumer criticized the Trump administration's Iran strategy, saying Iran used "understandings" to fool the US, and accused the government of repeating mistakes from the 2015 nuclear deal negotiations.

**🇺🇦 Ukraine Energy Infrastructure Hit Again**

DTEK energy company reported that Russian attacks over the past two days severely damaged energy infrastructure in the Dnipropetrovsk region, further straining Ukraine's power supply.
