🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Turkey-Iraq Sign One-Year Oil Pipeline Agreement**
Turkey's Energy Minister announced a one-year agreement allowing use of the pipeline to Ceyhan, with a daily capacity of 750,000 barrels. Efforts continue for a long-term deal. This is the direct catalyst behind today's oil rally.

**2. Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Plant Loses External Power for 23rd Time**
IAEA confirmed the plant lost external power for the 23rd time during military conflict. Cause of the latest ~2-hour outage remains unclear. Nuclear safety remains under pressure.

**3. US EUCOM Warns: Insufficient Naval Forces to Protect Israel**
According to The Washington Post, US EUCOM commander Alexus Grynkewich privately warned the Pentagon this week that there are insufficient naval forces to continue protecting Israel from ballistic missile attacks. Military resources stretched thin.

**4. US Treasury Secretary Pursuing Iranian Assets Globally**
Bessent stated during a Camp David cabinet meeting that, under President Trump's direction, the Treasury is conducting an economic offensive against Iran and pursuing Iranian assets worldwide.

**5. Lebanon-Israel 7th Round of Negotiations to Begin Tuesday in Rome**
Lebanese sources say the talks will last three days. Lebanon will not accept Israeli verification of "Phase 1" implementation. Diplomatic process continues but significant disagreements remain.

**6. Hungary's Danube Water Level Drops, Paks Nuclear Plant Output Plummets**
Hungarian PM announced Paks nuclear plant output has dropped to 480 MW (normal: 2,000 MW). Danube water level decline raises regional energy crisis concerns.

**7. Rhine River Water Level Hits Record Low**
Cologne gauge station measured water level at 67-68 cm, breaking the 2018 record low. Inland shipping logistics under sustained pressure.

**8. China July New Energy Vehicle Sales Results**
BYD July sales exceeded 419,000 units (record high, nearly 180,000 overseas); Leapmotor surpassed 100,000; Xiaomi Motors sustained above 30,000; SAIC Group sold 339,000 units in July.
