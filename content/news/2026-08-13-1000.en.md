Tencent's Q2 total revenue reached 204.8 billion yuan, up 11% YoY. However, cash flow turned negative at -13.8 billion yuan; excluding computing prepayments, it would be 37.6 billion yuan. Stock fell 3.29% on open as markets worried about AI capex compressing margins.

The People's Bank of China stated it will "timely plan and introduce practical incremental policies" to strengthen counter-cyclical adjustment. **4. US July Fiscal Deficit Surges 48% YoY**

The US federal deficit in July surged 48% year-over-year. The cumulative deficit for the first 10 months reached $1.8 trillion, with the CBO projecting $2.1 trillion for the full year. Total national debt has reached $40 trillion, raising fiscal sustainability concerns.

**5. DeepSeek V4 Pro Official API Update**

DeepSeek released the official V4 Pro API update, with performance approaching Fable 5 in multiple benchmarks. Chinese LLMs continue to close the gap, intensifying the AI competition landscape.

**6. Korea KOSPI Enters Technical Bull Market**

Global AI trading resurgence drove Korea's KOSPI up over 4%, rebounding more than 20% from its July 30 low to enter a technical bull market. This follows a historic sell-off last month.
