🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Iran Supreme Leader Holds Talks with President; Rezaei Appointed to Security Council**

Iranian state TV reported that Supreme Leader Mojtaba met with President Pezeshkian as the latter begins his third term, discussing livelihood, the ongoing "third war of aggression," and military matters. Meanwhile, Mohsen Rezaei was appointed as the Supreme Leader's representative to the Supreme National Security Council. The deputy commander of Iran's Basij organization said videos of the Supreme Leader in public and meeting with commanders will be released. **2. Netanyahu Responds to US 15-Point Gaza Plan**

Israeli PM Netanyahu stated regarding the US-proposed 15-point Gaza plan: "Some ideas we can accept, some we cannot." Previously, Netanyahu had outright rejected the plan, insisting Israel would not withdraw from Gaza until Hamas is fully disarmed, and claiming no Palestinian state would exist in Gaza or the West Bank as long as he is PM. The latest remarks represent a slight rhetorical softening, though the core stance remains unchanged.

**3. Typhoon "White Dolphin" Weakens After Second Landfall in Zhejiang**

Typhoon "White Dolphin" made its second landfall in Yueqing, Zhejiang, and has weakened to typhoon intensity. It is moving westward at 15-20 km/h with intensity decreasing rapidly. Impact on coastal areas is gradually diminishing.
