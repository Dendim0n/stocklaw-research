## 4. Read on the situation

**Judgment 1: the afternoon stabilization is position repair, not narrative repair; neutral on the index, do not chase this bounce.**
Basis: the bounce is synchronized (A-shares, KOSPI, Nikkei, Nasdaq futures and VIX futures all in one direction), but the catalyst is bearish — the morning's HK$986.7bn half-day turnover was a decline on shrinking volume, and three minutes of the afternoon session cannot prove buyers have arrived; the three data points that can reprice everything (core PCE, Q3 GDP, jobless claims) only land at 20:30 tonight, with the ECB at 21:15. **A synchronized bounce before the data carries close to zero information.**
Impact: high-beta compute hardware and ChiNext are still not worth catching; cash-flow names that do not depend on capex (banks, insurers, utilities) need not be trimmed into a bounce — wait for 20:30.
Invalidation: if the afternoon half-day turnover exceeds the morning's HK$986.7bn and ChiNext closes within -2%, real buyers are present; if Nasdaq futures fall back below -1.5% after the 20:30 data, this paragraph is void.

**Judgment 2: the AI trade is not exiting, it is rotating internally — money moves from the payer to the payee, and two facts today reinforced that split.**
Basis: Marvell buying Lumentum (acquirer -10%, target +15%) and PIMCO reportedly halving Meta/Oracle are one in, one out, both inside the chain; the BOK revising the 2026 current-account surplus up by $50bn and pointing straight at AI dollar inflows is official confirmation that **the money really is being paid to suppliers**. The counter-evidence is equally hard — Samsung capex +62% yet chip profit still missed consensus, which says **expanding capacity does not guarantee excess returns**.
Impact: optical components and modules (LITE, COHR and peers) are tactically long, but the premium rests on an unconfirmed deal; data-centre operators that expand with debt and outside financing are the short side; memory inflation is a cost line for downstream hardware. The self-funding side (the Microsoft line) remains the most defensive spot in the chain.
Invalidation: if Marvell denies the deal or talks collapse (earliest resolution next week), the optical M&A premium unwinds the same day; if the PIMCO item is refuted or is far smaller than "half", the funding-side argument reduces to price alone and this paragraph downgrades to a purely structural call.

**Judgment 3: commodities have gone numb to catalysts — oil ignores geopolitics, gold ignores the equity repair; both are waiting for tonight's data and the central banks.**
Basis: after the drone shootdown USOIL's intraday low is still 100.541 with no new low; while equities repaired, gold rose only 0.088% and silver 0.282%, and gold printed a marginal new low of 4,035.77 (just $0.37 under the prior 4,036.14 — noise); the dollar index is up only 0.03% and 10Y has no new sample since 10:44, so there is no "stronger dollar / higher real yields" explanation. **Gold fell this morning when stocks fell and failed to rise this afternoon when stocks rose — both directions point to precious metals pricing their own positioning; with no positioning or flow data available, this is a price-based inference only.**
Impact: do not chase energy equities on geopolitical headlines and do not short them either (there is earnings support above $100); keep precious metals on bearish watch, but with price already pinned near the intraday low, the odds on chasing shorts are just as poor; hedging equity risk with gold has now failed for a third consecutive period today.
Invalidation: if gold reclaims 4,068.76 (today's platform base) and silver recovers 53.95, this paragraph flips back to neutral-to-bullish; only if gold closes an hourly bar below 4,035.77 while the dollar index breaks 102.2993 (today's high) does a trend decline become arguable. Oil's direction still rests on Iran's reply, which has no scheduled time.
