🔥 **Key News This Hour**

1. **US-Iran Technical Talks Officially Underway**: VP JD Vance has arrived in Bürgenstock, Switzerland. Pakistan PM Shehbaz Sharif and Army Chief Asim Munir have also arrived to participate in mediation. The talks are being held under the framework of the US-Iran ceasefire memorandum of understanding, with delegations from both sides and mediators continuing to arrive.

2. **US to Press Iran on UN Inspections, Offer Billions in Frozen Assets**: Reports indicate the US will pressure Iran during the talks to allow UN inspectors back into key nuclear facilities, with the core incentive being the unfreezing of tens of billions in frozen Iranian assets. This issue is expected to be the most critical focus of the first round of negotiations.

3. **Iran Names Ending Lebanon Conflict as Top Agenda Item**: An Iranian official stated that ending the Lebanon conflict is the most important item on the Iranian delegation's agenda. Iran's oil minister said that once an agreement is reached, Iran's petroleum industry will become the world's largest platform for investment, technology, and financial cooperation.

4. **Israel Confirms 6 Soldiers Killed in Lebanon**: Israel confirmed that 6 soldiers, including a senior officer, have been killed in Hezbollah attacks since last Friday, with over 20 wounded. Israeli Finance Minister Smotrich declared that a Palestinian state is "more dangerous than Iran" and must be prevented.

5. **MSCI Assigns SpaceX Lowest ESG Rating**: MSCI granted SpaceX its lowest ESG rating of "CCC" ahead of the company's record-setting $75 billion public offering this month.

The formal start of US-Iran talks is the biggest variable this weekend. VP Vance's personal presence signals high US engagement, but Israeli casualties in Lebanon and hawkish rhetoric from Israeli officials cast a shadow over ceasefire prospects.
