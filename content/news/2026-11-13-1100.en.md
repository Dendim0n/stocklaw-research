## 2. Key increments

**① Asia's AI hardware chain retraced in the second hour; money rotated within the chain | 09:39–11:05**
ChiNext went from flat at 10:04 to **2,470.91 (−1.54%)** this hour; STAR 50 from +1.56% at the open to −0.64%; in Hong Kong Victory Giant fell −8.63% and Zhipu −7.09% ([10:55](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113105528130800)). Flows point the same way: by 10:40 main-force net outflows were Zhongji Innolight **−1.650bn CNY**, TFC Optical −1.172bn, Eoptolink −0.697bn, while inflows were Changlian Technology +0.411bn and SMIC +0.304bn ([10:40](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113104024347800)). Impact: money moved from optical modules/assembly to equipment/foundry inside one chain — a net de-risking of the sector, not a rotation. The Shanghai Composite held +0.11% only because banks (Bank of Jiangsu, Bank of Nanjing at record intraday highs) offset the growth complex.

**② Iran claims a strike on a US early-warning radar in Bahrain; oil pulses for 20 minutes | 10:13–11:00**
Iran's Revolutionary Guard said it struck a "US early-warning radar" in Bahrain in response to an attack on Iran's Sirik communications tower ([10:13](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113101349424800)); as of 11:00 there was no US or Bahraini response, so **this remains a one-sided claim**. WTI rose from the 10:05 session low of 88.058 to 88.668 at 10:35 (+0.69%), then back to 88.21 at 11:00 — a net +0.10% for the whole hour. Jin10 at 10:25 quoted CMS Prime saying the market is "pricing diplomatic possibility as though it had already become physical supply" ([10:25](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113102512828800)).
Impact: the target escalated from oil facilities to a US military asset, yet the price only held it for about 20 minutes — crude's geopolitical beta is decaying unless shipping or production is actually disrupted.

**③ US formally confirms July's joint yen intervention: about $500m of euro | 09:51–10:42**
The New York Fed's quarterly FX report shows that on 31 July, as Treasury's agent, it bought **79.4bn yen** and sold euro held by the Exchange Stabilization Fund — about **$497m**; Japan spent a record $97.5bn (MoF, to 26 Aug). USD/JPY broke **160** on Thursday (first time since September) and traded 159.93 in Tokyo on Friday, with Finance Minister Katayama saying Japan will coordinate with the US and "take appropriate action as necessary" ([235119](https://xnews.jin10.com/details/235119)).
Impact: 160 is now a verified intervention zone, reopening a volatility source for yen carry and the long end of JGBs; for gold it is an implicit discount-rate variable.
