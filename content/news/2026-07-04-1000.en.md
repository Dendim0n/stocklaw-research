🧭 **Market Assessment**

Gold's break above the $4,170 level, combined with fading rate hike expectations after a soft NFP and the dollar's sharp weekly drop, provides strong support — the near-term bullish bias remains intact.

Samsung's 20% DRAM price hike is highly significant, confirming the memory upcycle. Watch for SK Hynix follow-through and Micron's relative benefit.

Alibaba's Claude Code ban reflects rising AI supply chain security awareness. Combined with Claude's own error incidents, this may provide a short-term boost to domestic alternatives and open-source model narratives.

Trump's Independence Day economic victory lap plus the stock donation policy is clearly political marketing, but the near-term market sentiment impact leans positive.

Dual geopolitical risk from the Middle East and Russia-Ukraine persists, yet oil's muted reaction (+0.45%) suggests the market is underpricing supply shock tail risks — worth monitoring.
