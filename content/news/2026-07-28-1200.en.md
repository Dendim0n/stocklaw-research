🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**Asian Stocks Enter Correction** — The MSCI Asia Pacific Index plunged 3.5% on Tuesday, with cumulative losses exceeding 10% from the June 22 high as the chip selloff intensified. **China A50 Midday Divergence** — Banking and liquor stocks gained while semiconductors and telecom equipment saw sharp declines. CSI 500 futures main contract fell 3.23%.

**SpaceX Market Cap Evaporates $1.2 Trillion** — The stock continues to retreat from its post-IPO highs, with some large option trades suggesting investors are still searching for a rebound opportunity.

**Fed Meeting Suspense Escalates** — Citadel Securities warns the market may be underestimating Warsh's hawkishness, with a surprise 25bp rate hike this week potentially becoming the key step to reinforce his inflation-fighting credibility. The Fed whisperer calls this meeting a critical test.

SpaceX's $1.2 trillion market cap evaporation is a warning — post-IPO euphoria valuation mean-reversion is becoming the most expensive lesson of 2026.
