## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Trump Slams Oil Companies "Ripping Off" Americans, Orders DOJ Investigation**
Trump publicly accused oil companies of insufficiently lowering gasoline prices and ordered the DOJ to investigate. This comes as the Republican-led Senate passed a resolution limiting presidential war powers against Iran, with Trump facing domestic pressure on both energy policy and the Iran conflict.

**2. Goldman Sachs: US Core PCE Expected to Return to 2% by 2027**
Goldman Sachs released its latest forecast, stating that while the "last mile" of US inflation decline has been slow, both PCE and CPI will ultimately converge toward the Fed's 2% target, with the timeline around 2027. This implies the Fed is unlikely to rush into rate cuts in the near term.

**3. ASML Partners with Dutch TNO on Photonic Chip Mass Production**
ASML and the Netherlands Organisation for Applied Scientific Research (TNO) announced a collaboration to develop and industrialize photonic chips, centered around TNO's photonic chip facility under construction in Eindhoven. Photonic chips are seen as a key technology pathway for next-generation data centers and AI compute.

**4. Dow Jones to Add Alphabet, Replace Verizon**
The Dow Jones Industrial Average is set for an adjustment, with Alphabet replacing Verizon as a new component. This marks the first time the Dow simultaneously holds Alphabet, Nvidia, and multiple tech giants, further increasing tech weighting.

**5. Samsung Electronics Reportedly Planning KRW 90 Trillion Share Buyback**
Samsung Electronics is reportedly planning a massive ~KRW 90 trillion (~$67 billion) share buyback to absorb new equity from employee compensation restructuring. Details are pending formal disclosure. If confirmed, it would be one of the largest buybacks in Korean corporate history.

## 🧭 Market Assessment

Gold bounced slightly to 4076 this hour but remains below 4100, with bulls and bears locked in a 4050-4100 range battle.
