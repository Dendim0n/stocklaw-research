## 🔥 Key News This Hour

### 1. US July Challenger Job Cuts Report Released

July layoff announcements fell to 33,429, down 27% month-over-month and 46.1% year-over-year — the lowest since July 2024. AI was the top cited reason for layoffs for the fifth consecutive month. Hiring plans rose 25% year-over-year, showing a labor market characterized by "slowing layoffs but structural transformation." Year-to-date layoffs totaled 477,033, down 41% from the same period last year.

### 2. China Launches Cybersecurity Review of Palo Alto Networks

China's Cybersecurity Review Office, citing the National Security Law and Cybersecurity Law, has launched a cybersecurity review of Palo Alto Networks (PANW) products sold in China. PANW may face pre-market pressure; cybersecurity sector warrants attention.

### 3. Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan Increase Stakes in Zhongji Innolight

Hong Kong Exchange disclosures show Goldman Sachs raised its long position in Zhongji Innolight (03308.HK) H-shares from 11.65% to 12.19% as of August 3; JPMorgan raised its stake to 13.72%. Top investment banks continue to accumulate positions in the optical module leader.

### 4. China-Argentina Central Banks Renew Currency Swap Agreement

The People's Bank of China and the Central Bank of Argentina have renewed their bilateral currency swap agreement, sized at 130 billion RMB / 28 trillion Argentine pesos, with a five-year term and option for renewal.

### 5. Vanke USD Bonds See Largest Gain in 2 Months

Vanke's 3.5% bond due November 2029 rose 1.9 cents per dollar to 50.2 cents; the 3.975% bond due November 2027 also gained. A signal of recovery in Chinese property dollar bonds.

### 6. Israel Launches New Round of Attacks on Southern Lebanon

Israel's new round of attacks on southern Lebanon injured at least four people, according to reports. Middle East tensions continue to support safe-haven gold buying.

## 🧭 Market Assessment
