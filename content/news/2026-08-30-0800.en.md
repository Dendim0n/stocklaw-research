🧭 **Outlook**

Gold's 3.2% one-day plunge is aggressive pricing of a monetary-policy shift, not mere risk-aversion — watch for further hawkish repricing pressuring precious-metals longs.

Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole tone has markets re-pricing a "higher for longer" rate path, a headwind for both richly valued growth stocks and gold.

The US-Iran standoff over Hormuz remains in a war-of-words phase, yet oil is barely moving — markets are not pricing supply disruption as the base case.

If Iranian threats turn into actual disruption or escort clashes, oil's upside risk would be repriced quickly — this is the tail risk to watch most closely.
