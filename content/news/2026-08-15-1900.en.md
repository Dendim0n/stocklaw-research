🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. UAE Strongly Condemns Iranian Attacks on ADNOC Vessels in Hormuz Strait**

The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs issued a statement condemning Iran's Revolutionary Guard attacks on Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz, calling the actions "piracy" and accusing Iran of using commercial shipping as a tool for coercion and economic extortion. The UAE emphasized that Iran's aggression poses a direct threat to regional stability and global energy security, demanding Iran fully cease hostile actions and unconditionally reopen the Strait of Hormuz.

**2. Iran and Tajikistan Sign Long-Term Oil Cooperation Agreement**

According to Iranian media Fars News, Iran and Tajikistan reached a long-term energy cooperation agreement in the latest round of oil talks, primarily covering Iran's export of petroleum products to Tajikistan and crude oil supply for Tajikistan's refineries.

Disney's new CEO Josh D'Amaro stated that Disney+ is the core of the company's future development, leveraging streaming and scale advantages to establish direct relationships with consumers.
