🔥 **Key News This Hour**

1. **Russia cuts 2026 oil output and export forecasts**: government documents show 2026 oil production forecast cut to 494.2 million tons, a 17-year low; oil product exports cut by 24.1 million tons to 98.5 million tons. Earlier LSEG data showed Russia imported nearly 460,000 tons of gasoline in August as Ukrainian drone strikes hit refineries — supply contraction and import substitution side by side.
2. **SB Energy files for US IPO**: the power-infrastructure company backed by SoftBank and OpenAI, with Nvidia as a strategic investor, filed for a US listing to capitalize on surging AI datacenter power demand — the latest firm to ride the "AI power" theme to the capital markets.
3. **NIO (NIO) Q2 earnings**: revenue of RMB 32.14B (est. RMB 33.49B) missed expectations; net loss of RMB 722M (vs. RMB 5.14B loss a year earlier) narrowed sharply and beat estimates; Q3 delivery guidance of 108,000–111,000 vehicles, Q3 revenue guidance up 52.7%–56.2% YoY.
4. **Israel arrests head of Hamas general security agency in Gaza City**: Defense Minister Katz announced a senior Hamas member was captured — Israeli media identify him as the head of Hamas's general security agency; Katz reiterated no withdrawal until demilitarization and Hamas disarmament. Gaza death toll has risen to 73,462.
5. **US–Iran war of words escalates**: Trump called Iran "officially a failed state — it's dead"; President Pezeshkian responded that US pressure weakens diplomatic efforts; Iranian MP Nabavian floated a preemptive strike on US interests in the Middle East; Iran's central bank says it has $2B in FX ready to stabilize markets.
6. **Markets add to BoE rate-hike bets**: pricing ~50bp of hikes by February; UK borrowing costs are at multi-year highs, adding pressure on public finances. France's election jitters linger with the France–Germany spread near euro-crisis-era highs.

🧭 **Situation Assessment**

Gold and silver are at two-week lows with the driver now global bond yields rather than geopolitical safe-haven demand — no sustainable precious-metals bounce until rates stop climbing.

Russia cutting output forecasts to a 17-year low, plus forced gasoline imports after refinery strikes, gives oil dual support from geopolitics and supply — WTI holds above $86.

SB Energy's filing is another capitalization signal for the "AI datacenter power" theme, which remains one of the strongest US narratives (compute → power → grid equipment).

NIO's loss narrowed sharply with solid delivery guidance, but revenue missed — "beat on losses, uncertain on growth" could split sentiment for HK/China EV names.

US–Iran rhetoric is hot but without direct military escalation; Hormuz-blockade expectations are partly priced in — oil is up but not out of control. The real variable remains tonight's US data.
