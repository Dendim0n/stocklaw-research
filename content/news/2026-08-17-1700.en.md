🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. China July Economic Data Misses Across the Board**
Retail sales +0.6% YoY (est. 1.5%), industrial output +4.5% (est. 4.8%), FAI -6.7% YTD, unemployment rose to 5.2%. Both consumption and output missed expectations, signaling weak domestic demand. NBS said "results of curbing irrational competition continue to show."

**2. PBOC July Financial Data Released**
Jan-Jul social financing totaled 22.25 trillion yuan; July M2 +7.7% YoY, M1 +4%. SAFE stated the FX market remained stable in July, with bank FX settlement surplus of 124.1 billion yuan.

**3. Oil & Gas "15th Five-Year Plan" Released**
NDRC and NEA issued the plan, proposing "replacement with reduction" for refining capacity, improving oil reserve systems, refining petroleum consumption tax, and developing high-pressure pure hydrogen pipeline technology.

**4. Middle East Tensions Escalating**
US-Iran ceasefire agreement expired Monday. Lebanon saw the worst clashes in months, Israeli far-right calls to occupy all of Gaza. Iran's foreign ministry stated US sanctions have "no impact" on Iran's position, Pakistan's mediation role undermined.

**5. LME Copper Squeeze Brewing**
LME 3-month copper near record highs, spot premium hits record, inventories drop to February lows. A squeeze may be underway.

**6. Institutional Views**
Goldman Sachs believes market is too hawkish on Fed rate hike expectations. BofA says Republican midterm wins would sustain the bull market.
