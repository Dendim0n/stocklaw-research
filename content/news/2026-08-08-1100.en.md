🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Yemen Launches Military Operation Against Houthis, Red Sea Tensions Rise**

Yemen's Ministry of Defense announced military operations against Houthi forces. The Yemeni military subsequently warned that further retaliatory measures would be taken if Houthi attacks continue. Red Sea shipping lane security faces renewed uncertainty, with potential impacts on crude oil transport and container routes.

**2. Apollo Chief Economist: Inflation Is Now a Fed Credibility Issue**

Torsten Sløk, Chief Economist at Apollo Global Management, stated that inflation is no longer just an economic issue but one of Federal Reserve credibility. With inflation persistently above target since 2021, the longer high inflation persists, the weaker market confidence in the Fed's ability to restore price stability. This suggests the rate cut path may be less aggressive than markets expect.

**3. NVIDIA Plans Up to $3B Investment in Lancium, Deepening AI Power Infrastructure**

According to The Information, NVIDIA (NVDA.O) has agreed to invest $2 billion in power infrastructure developer Lancium, with commitments to add up to $1 billion more as the company secures additional planned power. Following last month's investment in a Blackstone-backed power company, this marks NVIDIA's continued expansion in AI data center power — signaling that electricity has become a core bottleneck in the AI supply chain.

**4. Turkey Says Saudi-Turkey-Pakistan Defense Pact Does Not Conflict with NATO**

Turkey's Communications Office stated that the "Mecca Common Defense Agreement" signed with Saudi Arabia and Pakistan does not conflict with NATO's Article 5 collective defense clause. Leaders of the three nations signed the pact in Saudi Arabia, reflecting a reshaping of Middle East defense dynamics.
