🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🔴 OPEC+ to raise output target by ~188k bpd from July**

According to CNBC citing three OPEC+ sources, despite the US-Iran war hindering output increases by several members, OPEC+ will agree on Sunday to raise oil production targets for the fourth time in just a few months, by approximately 188,000 bpd. This is the most explicit near-term bearish signal for crude supply.

**🔴 Israel: Massive underground infrastructure discovered in southern Lebanon**

Israeli PM Netanyahu announced the discovery of massive underground infrastructure during operations in southern Lebanon. The Israeli military also stated it will not allow Hezbollah to fire on its territory and will take corresponding action. Middle East tensions escalating further.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated publicly that the memory shortage issue will persist for years, while also revealing plans to meet with SK Group Chairman Choi Tae-won on Monday and potentially announce some news. Clear signals of upstream AI computing supply chain bottlenecks.

**🟡 Armed clash in Iran border region kills 4**

Iranian security forces clashed with an armed group in Sistan-Baluchestan province in southeastern Iran, killing 4 people. Security conditions remain tense in Iran's southeast.
