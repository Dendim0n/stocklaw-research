## Key Additions

### 1. Japan 10Y Bond Auction Demand Strong, Bid-to-Cover 3.76 Highest Since May

- **Time**: Oct 6, 11:35–11:46 CST
- **Source**: Japan Ministry of Finance tender results (Jin10 Data 11:35:54, 11:37:29)
- **Content**: Japan's Tuesday 10Y bond auction bid-to-cover ratio was 3.76, above last auction's 3.29 and the 12-month average of 3.2; tail was only 0.02, lowest since Jun 2025. 20Y yield retraced 1bp to 3.98% following the auction.
- **Impact**: **Above-expectation bid-to-cover reflects healthy Japanese bond demand, but the minimal tail means extremely uniform allocation — market pricing on this tenor is highly consensus, lacking both divergence and excess demand.** This result does not support a "Japanese bond sell-off" panic narrative, but combined with the 30Y yield at 4.24% new high, it indicates **the long-end rate rise is systemic, not liquidity-driven**. Bearish for global bonds (rate center rise confirmed), continued headwind for high-valuation growth stocks.

### 2. Bridgewater's Dalio: Stock and Bond Expected Returns Converging, US Debt Crisis May Erupt Within 3 Years

- **Time**: Oct 6, 11:13–11:22 CST
- **Source**: Bridgewater founder Ray Dalio (Jin10 Data 11:13:16, 11:22:14)
- **Content**: Dalio stated stock and bond expected returns are now converging; equity market sensitivity to rate changes will increase; US debt crisis could erupt within 3 years; we are entering a new era of AI-driven investing.
- **Impact**: **This is a rare high-profile macro warning.** Dalio's core thesis — equity/bond return convergence — aligns with current data: US 10Y yield ~5.25%, S&P earnings yield ~4.5%. **This means the traditional 60/40 stock-bond portfolio diversification effect is disappearing, and rising rate sensitivity will press both asset classes.** The "3-year debt crisis" framing is an extreme scenario, but his point about increased rate sensitivity is worth heeding: **if the 10Y breaks 5.4%, Nasdaq valuations could face material repricing pressure.**
