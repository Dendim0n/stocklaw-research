🔥 **Key Headlines This Hour**

**1. OPEC+ Announces 188K b/d Production Increase for August**

A declaration from OPEC+ confirms that seven member states — Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, and Oman — will raise oil production quotas by 188,000 barrels per day in August. The group reaffirmed its commitment to market stability and scheduled its next meeting for August 2. The modest increase was met with a muted market response, with WTI holding near $68.7.

**2. Trump: No US-Iran Hostilities Until Khamenei Funeral Concludes**

Trump stated that Iran is "begging for a deal" but both sides agreed to pause negotiations for one week until the funeral of Iran's Supreme Leader Khamenei concludes. No mutual attacks will occur during the pause. Trump remarked, "We could take them all out with one strike, but we won't do that."

**3. Cargo Ship Attacked Off Yemen's Hodeidah Coast**

The UK Maritime Transport Office reported an incident 30 nautical miles southwest of Hodeidah, Yemen, where a cargo vessel triggered a distress alert, reporting it was under attack by unidentified armed assailants. Red Sea shipping security risks have escalated once again.

**4. Iraq Signs Oil Field Management Deal with Halliburton**

Iraq's Oil Minister announced a five-year agreement with US oilfield services giant Halliburton to manage the Bin Umar and Sinbad oil fields. Bin Umar's production target is 150,000 b/d, while Sinbad targets 80,000–100,000 b/d, with combined associated gas production increasing by approximately 560 million standard cubic feet per day.

**5. Germany Plans to Raise Net New Borrowing to €118 Billion in 2027**

An increase of approximately 7% from the April estimate, reflecting the continuation of Europe's fiscal expansion trend.
