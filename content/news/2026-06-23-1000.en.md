🧭 **Analysis**

KOSPI's single-day plunge of over 4% is the biggest variable in Asia-Pacific today, with FX jitters and exporter selling creating a self-reinforcing downward spiral.

Rising JGB yields imply the market is pricing in faster BOJ tightening — if Japan moves decisively on rates, it could trigger a reversal of carry trades with global spillover effects.
