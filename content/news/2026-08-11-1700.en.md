🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Fed's Hammack: Multiple Rate Hikes May Be Needed**

Fed official Hammack struck a hawkish tone, saying multiple rate hikes may be necessary to effectively curb inflation. Meanwhile, China's central bank emphasized improving its monetary policy framework. Goldman Sachs holds the opposite view, expecting no Fed rate hikes this year.

**2. Nomura: Japan Intervention May Reach 14.1 Trillion Yen**

Nomura Securities estimates Japan's intervention scale may have exceeded the April-May round. The US Treasury pledged "full support" for Japan's intervention, but the Fed has not contributed funds, leaving fiscal resources capped. Yen bears are regrouping.

**3. Gold Pulls Back from Two-Month High, CPI in Focus**

Gold hit $4,435 intraday, a two-month high, before retreating to around $4,369. Market focus shifts to Wednesday's CPI data — Comerica Bank believes July CPI will carry more weight for the Fed's next decision than the jobs report, expecting YoY CPI to ease from 3.5%.

**4. RBA Holds Rates Steady, As Expected**

The Reserve Bank of Australia maintained its cash rate at 4.35%. Commonwealth Bank expects rates to stay on hold for the rest of the year; Capital Economics sees a possible hike, but only under stringent conditions.

**5. China NEV Exports Surge 147.8% YoY in July**
