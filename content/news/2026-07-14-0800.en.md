🔥 **Overnight Headlines**

**1. Oil Tankers Attacked in Strait of Hormuz — 1 Dead, 8 Injured**

UAE Defense Ministry confirms two Emirati oil tankers were struck by two Iranian cruise missiles in the southern shipping lane of the Strait of Hormuz, killing one Indian crew member and injuring eight others (four critically). Iran has not responded. This marks the first direct attack on civilian tankers in the current US-Iran conflict — a severe escalation.

**2. Trump Orders Iran Port Blockade Starting Tomorrow, Threatens Underground Nuclear Site**

For the third consecutive night, US forces bombed Iran. Trump announced a blockade of Iranian ports effective 4:00 AM July 15, along with a 20% "protection fee" on Strait of Hormuz shipping. He also threatened to strike Iran's most fortified underground nuclear facility "Kuh-e Aseman," while simultaneously stating "a deal is still possible" and confirming Iran has re-engaged with Washington. Trump labeled the operation a "military conflict" but refused to set a timeline.

**3. WTI Crude Surges 9% Overnight, Gold Briefly Below $4,000**

Driven by the sharp escalation in the Middle East, WTI crude surged approximately 9% yesterday, touching $79/bbl this morning. Spot gold briefly fell below the $4,000 mark, with COMEX futures following suit. The divergence between safe-haven and inflation-expectation assets reflects the market's tug-of-war between "war premium" and "rate hike fears."

**4. Fed's Waller Goes Hawkish: Tonight's CPI Sets the Course**

On the eve of the CPI release, Fed Governor Waller issued a clear hawkish warning — if core inflation continues to run hot, the FOMC needs to consider near-term rate hikes. Market-implied probability of a July rate hike surged to 50% overnight. Tonight's US June CPI release at 20:30 Beijing time (expected 3.8% YoY, prior 4.20%) will be the decisive variable.

**5. Saudi Arabia Accused of Bombing Sanaa Airport, Yemen Crisis Escalates**

Houthi rebels accused Saudi Arabia of airstrikes on Sanaa International Airport, triggered by the first Iran-to-Sanaa passenger flight in nearly a decade. The 2022 UN-brokered ceasefire framework faces severe strain.

🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

Today's core market tension is "geopolitical war premium vs. inflation-driven rate hike fears" — two powerful forces pulling in opposite directions.

WTI crude's 9% overnight surge is the market pricing in a genuine Strait of Hormuz supply-chain disruption risk. If the Iran port blockade materializes, roughly 20% of global oil transit will be directly affected.

But Waller's hawkish stance has raised the stakes for tonight's CPI to an extreme level. If CPI surprises to the upside, the July rate hike probability will climb further from 50%, hitting risk assets with a double whammy (war + rate hikes).

Gold failing to rally and instead hovering near $4,000 suggests the market currently fears liquidity tightening more than it seeks safe havens — a warning signal worth noting.

The S&P 500's 0.79% decline is not extreme, but if tonight's CPI compounds with further Middle East deterioration, markets could shift into "stagflation trading" mode.

The prudent course is to stay defensive and await the CPI print before making directional calls.
