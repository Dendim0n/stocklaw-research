**Crude is precarious at $89; supply recovery narrative is dominating geopolitical risk premium near-term.** Saudi pipeline resumption plus G7 100M barrel release create clear supply-side pressure. Yemen offensive broadens conflict scope but Saudi military action objectively protects its own oil infrastructure, not constituting an near-term supply disruption threat. **If WTI effectively breaks $88, it tests this year's low region, where OPEC+'s November output decision (no increase) may be re-priced by the market as "empty talk."** Strategically, shorting below $89 has poor risk-reward — geopolitical tail risk remains; avoid large short positions below key support.

**Asian equities lead but Japanese bonds send warning.** Nikkei 225 broke 70,000, Taiwan weighted +1.99%, FTSE China A50 futures +0.2% — sentiment is optimistic. But Japan 30Y at 4.23% record — a global pricing anchor — rising with equities suggests capital chose "growth optimism" over "rate rise" for now. **Confirmation**: If today's US Services PMI holds high (prior 58.7), US yields may continue rising, testing equity optimism. **Disconfirmation**: If PMI shows services cooling, rate-rise narrative may reverse quickly, and equities/bonds could rally together.

**Euro breaking 1.12 signals Europe growth worries are being priced.** EUR at lowest since May 2025; Eurozone services PMI final (expected 53.0) at 16:00 CST. If Germany/France services PMI miss, euro may weaken further. **For US stocks, strong dollar is a double-edged sword — pressuring multinationals but reducing inflation expectations, positive for rate path.** Watch European PMI to decide dollar bias.
