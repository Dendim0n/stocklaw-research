🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Global Funds Accelerate Inflows into Chinese Equities**
In the month ending Aug 5, Chinese equity funds saw net inflows of $50.82B, accounting for nearly 60% of emerging market totals. Overseas-listed China ETFs are seeing significantly accelerated inflows as international capital positions in Chinese equities.

**2. France July CPI Final In Line with Expectations**
France's July CPI YoY final came in at 2.1%, MoM at 0.6%, both matching expectations and prior readings. Harmonized CPI YoY final at 2.4%, MoM at 0.6%, also in line. No new directional signals from the data.

**3. US CENTCOM Denies Pushing for New Iran Strikes**
US Central Command spokesperson Tim Hawkins denied that military leadership is pushing for a new round of military strikes against Iran, pushing back against earlier media reports. Geopolitical tensions remain worth monitoring.

**4. Oil Prices Strengthen, China Retail Fuel Prices Cut**
WTI rose above $81, Brent climbed to $86.72. China's domestic retail fuel prices will be cut tonight by 230 yuan/ton for gasoline and 220 yuan/ton for diesel, marking the 5th cut this year.

**5. South Korea's KOSPI Surges 11.5% for the Week**
KOSPI closed up 2.41% at 6,977 on Friday, with a weekly gain of 11.5%, ending a 7-week losing streak, indicating significant oversold bounce momentum.

**6. JPMorgan Raises Applied Materials Target Price**
JPMorgan raised Applied Materials (AMAT) target price from $515 to $660, reflecting optimism in the semiconductor equipment sector.

**7. Japan's Top FX Official to Meet Korean Counterpart on Aug 21**
Japan's Ministry of Finance announced that the top FX officials from Japan and South Korea will meet in Tokyo on Aug 21, worth watching for FX market coordination signals.
