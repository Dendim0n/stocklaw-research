🧭 **Assessment**

Gold broke above $4,600 as the expanded Treasury buyback, the $40 trillion debt narrative and Dalio's gold call converge, keeping short-term momentum strong.

But gold has rebounded ~10% from its low, and several analysts flag "too far, too fast," raising technical pullback risk around 4,600-4,650.

Trump's "economic war without limiting military options" stance means the US-Iran game shifts from military confrontation to economic strangulation, and the dispute over Hormuz volumes keeps geopolitical pricing alive.

With Brent above $94 and hedge funds turning bullish on European diesel, the fuel-supply crisis narrative remains intact and oil is prone to stay firm near-term.

US stocks rose Friday led by Tesla, but CFTC data shows S&P speculative shorts still building, reflecting unresolved disagreement over stretched valuations.

The $40 trillion debt and the "bonds or dollar" dilemma remain the biggest macro undercurrent; next week's Jackson Hole symposium and core PCE may serve as validation.
