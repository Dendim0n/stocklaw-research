🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Hormuz Strait Transit Volume Drops to 3-Month Low**
Iran publicly rejected Trump's claim of US "full control" over the Strait of Hormuz, stating it will not reopen until Iranian conditions are met. Average daily transit over the past five days fell to ~13 vessels, a 3-month low. Geopolitical risk premium persists, but oil prices remained subdued, suggesting the market has partially priced in the tension.

**2. Cerebras Plunges 16% Post-Earnings**
AI chip challenger Cerebras dropped 16% in after-hours trading following its earnings report. The company bets on large-chip architecture (reducing chip interconnects and HBM costs) to challenge Nvidia, but the market remains skeptical about its profitability path and competitive pressure.

**3. Cisco Beats on All Fronts but Still Falls Post-Market**
Cisco's Q4 revenue, profit, next-quarter guidance, and full-year forecast all exceeded expectations, yet the stock still declined after hours. Shares are up over 60% YTD and ~8% in August alone, creating significant profit-taking pressure after good news is absorbed. Analysts raised the price target to $160.

**4. Goldman: Japan Has Ample Intervention Ammo, Yen Nears 160**
Goldman noted that Japan holds over $1 trillion in foreign reserves, enough to support two rounds of intervention similar to last month's historic scale. The yen is again approaching the 160 level, with markets on high alert for potential authorities' action following July's intervention.

**5. Putin Visits Disputed Kuril Islands**
TASS reported Putin visited Iturup Island (part of the disputed "Northern Territories" claimed by Japan). This remains a core obstacle to a Japan-Russia peace treaty, and a strong reaction from the Japanese government is expected.

**6. Middle East: US Officials Criticize Israeli Defense Minister's "Long-term Occupation of Southern Lebanon" Remarks**
US State Department officials criticized Israeli Defense Minister Katz's statement about being "prepared for long-term occupation of southern Lebanon," noting it contradicts prior Israeli government commitments. US-Israel differences on Lebanon are now public.

**7. New Zealand Inflation Expectations Cool**
RBNZ survey showed Q3 one-year inflation expectations at 2.6% and two-year expectations at 2.34%, both near the central bank's target range, putting pressure on the NZD.

🧧 **Market Assessment**

Gold's pullback from $4,450 reflects short-term profit-taking, but geopolitical risks (Hormuz, Middle East, Japan-Russia) continue to provide support, limiting downside potential.

Cerebras' post-market plunge and Cisco's "sell the news" reaction highlight that AI concept stock valuations are highly stretched. The market's tolerance for misses is narrowing — any disappointment could trigger sharp corrections.

The yen approaching 160 is a key risk to watch this week. Japanese authorities could intervene again at any time, which would impact the dollar and ripple through risk assets.

The continued decline in Hormuz Strait transit volume warrants vigilance. If the situation escalates further, oil prices could spike.
