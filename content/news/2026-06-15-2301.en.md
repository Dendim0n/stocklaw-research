Marine tracking data shows only 29 vessels transited the strait between June 10–14, with 23 moving west-to-east (into the Gulf) versus just 6 east-to-west (outbound). The severe flow imbalance indicates recovery is far from complete.

June NAHB confidence index declined further as rising mortgage rates and building material costs weighed on sentiment. The southern region saw the sharpest deterioration.

**6. Senator Warren Questions BlackRock and Vanguard**
