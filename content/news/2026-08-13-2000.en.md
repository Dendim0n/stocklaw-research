🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Bank of England Warns of AI Bubble Risk**
The Bank of England estimates that an AI bubble burst could trigger simultaneous capital outflows from US stocks and Treasury bonds, with spillover effects on the UK financial markets. This marks one of the most explicit warnings from a major central bank regarding AI valuation bubbles.

**2. DeepSeek V4 Pro Officially Withdrawn**
DeepSeek's V4-Pro-0813 release notice and open platform announcement have been withdrawn, less than 24 hours after its silent launch on the evening of August 12. Meanwhile, DeepSeek announced a new pricing scheme effective August 17, adopting peak/off-peak pricing with off-peak rates at half of peak rates. The move highlights intensifying competition in the AI model space.

**3. Mild US CPI Eases Rate Hike Pressure, But Gold Falls**
US July CPI data came in softer than expected, prompting traders to trim Federal Reserve rate hike bets. The dollar came under pressure, but gold failed to benefit — falling from the intraday high of $4,449 to around $4,387, indicating profit-taking dominates the short-term picture.

**4. US Defense Secretary Issues Military Threat Against Cuba**
Defense Secretary Hegseth stated that if Cuba does not comply with Washington's demands, the US will take military action against the country, with all options including military ones on the table. Geopolitical risk extends to the Latin American theater.

**5. White House Trade Advisor Navarro: Customs Overwhelmed by Tariff Evasion**
Navarro indicated that US customs officials are overwhelmed by tariff evasion issues, exposing execution-level pressures in the current tariff system.

**6. Explosion at Rotterdam Port**
An explosion at the port of Rotterdam killed one person and injured several others. As Europe's largest port, the impact on logistics chains remains to be assessed.

**7. UK GDP Beats Expectations, Sterling Supported**
UK Q2 GDP data showed encouraging economic activity, with GBP/USD recovering early losses. Traders weighed Fed rate hike relief against UK economic resilience.
