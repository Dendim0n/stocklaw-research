🧭 **Situation Assessment**

The dollar breaking below 100 is the key variable this hour — gold surged $50, silver and palladium rallied, non-USD currencies firmed; the main market logic has shifted from "safe haven" to "weak dollar + precious metals re-rating", and the 4,330–4,345 band has broken to the upside, putting short-term momentum with the bulls.

The Bank of Canada's seventh straight hold, paired with Macklem's "energy prices remain elevated" remarks, contrasts with ECB September hike expectations — major central banks remain in wait-and-see mode between tariff inflation and slowing growth, with "hold" becoming the mainstream stance.

The catalyst-free yen spike and repeated tests of the 160 level keep intervention risk elevated — if official BOJ intervention is confirmed, carry-trade unwinds could hit global risk assets; this is the key secondary risk to watch after the dollar broke 100.

The "Dow strong, Nasdaq weak" divergence at the US open, with Dell's surge validating AI hardware strength, keeps the AI capex narrative supported — but Chinese ADRs and high-valuation growth names are under pressure; in a divergent tape, chasing highs is not advised.
