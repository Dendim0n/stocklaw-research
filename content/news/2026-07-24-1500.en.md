## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**🌐 Trump Tariff Shock: "Forced Labor" Tariffs Hit 60 Nations, EU Gets a Pass**

The Trump administration on Friday imposed new tariffs on 60 global trade partners citing "forced labor" concerns. Allies including Australia and Brazil strongly refuted the allegations. The EU escaped unscathed thanks to bilateral agreement protections, with the European Commission issuing a "cautious welcome." The UK also confirmed no negative tariff changes. Analysts view this as Trump leveraging a new legal instrument to bypass Supreme Court restrictions on tariff authority.

**🇰🇷🇯🇵 Asian Markets Bloodbath: KOSPI Collapses 5.73%, Nikkei Drops Nearly 3%**

KOSPI closed down 406.87 points (-5.73%), the hardest-hit Asian market in this global risk-off wave. Nikkei 225 fell 1811.45 points (-2.73%). Triple headwinds — escalating Middle East conflict, elevated oil prices, and hawkish Fed expectations — triggered accelerated capital flight from Asian risk assets.

**🇪🇺 ECB Hawkish Signal: September Meeting Is the Key Window**

ECB Governing Council member Nagel stated the central bank is in a "favorable position," with the September meeting emerging as a critical decision window. The ECB held rates steady as expected but kept the door open for a September hike. Middle East escalation-driven inflation pressures are becoming a key variable.

**🇮🇶 Middle East Escalation: Two Explosions in Erbil, Iraq**

Iran's Fars News Agency reported, citing Arab sources, two explosions in Erbil, Iraq. The same day, Houthi forces opened a "second front" at the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, threatening Saudi crude export routes. Russia's Defense Ministry confirmed strikes on a military cargo vessel at Ukraine's Mykolaiv port.

**🇩🇪 German Chancellor Merz to Address the Nation**

Der Spiegel reports Merz will deliver a statement in Berlin at 15:00 Beijing time (09:00 CET). Markets are on high alert.

**🇪🇺 EU's 21st Russia Sanctions Package Approved**

The EU formally approved its 21st sanctions package against Russia, targeting the financial system. Due to surging oil prices, the Russian oil price cap is frozen at $44.10/barrel for 12 months. The exemption for transshipment of Russian LNG to third countries is extended by one year.

**🤖 Industry: Xpeng Humanoid Robot Enters Pilot Production; Momenta Joins Robovan Race**

Xpeng's humanoid robot has begun small-batch trial production at its Guangzhou factory, with the mass production line in final commissioning. Autonomous driving leader Momenta confirmed its entry into unmanned freight (Robovan), already operating in Suzhou for several months.

## 🧭 Strategic Assessment

Panic selling in Asian markets — KOSPI's near-6% single-day crash is a systemic risk release signal, not a short-term correction.

The actual impact of Trump's new tariffs may be overstated by markets — the EU and UK are already exempt, and the "forced labor" legal instrument is far harder to enforce than traditional tariffs.

The Middle East is the biggest uncertainty source right now; the Erbil explosions signal conflict spillover into Iraq, raising proxy war risks.

The Brent-WTI divergence is worth watching — Brent above $100 reflects geopolitical supply premium, while WTI's sharp drop may signal emerging demand-side weakness.

Gold hovering near $4037 without a risk-off rally suggests USD strength is capping gold prices, with safe-haven flows rotating toward Treasuries and USD cash.

ECB September hike expectations combined with a Fed on hold mean narrowing EU-US rate spreads could become the core FX trading theme for H2.
