## 4. Assessment

**A. Asia-Pacific: the selloff is not over, but it has entered a "drop–bounce–drop" chop; don't take a directional position before the payrolls print.**
The evidence is the layered closes: the Nikkei and TAIEX closed above their lows while KOSPI made a second 1.5% leg down in the same window, meaning leverage structures and repair speeds differ across markets; A-shares' relative resilience came from banks while ChiNext sat at −3.62%, which is defensive rotation rather than inflows. **Lean**: short-term bearish on AI hardware and the semiconductor chain (TSMC −4.7% pressures SOX tonight); no directional longs before the 21:30 payrolls, and no chasing shorts in Asian indices here — closing above the day low is itself evidence that shorts are covering. **Confirmation**: KOSPI holds 5,000 at the close and HSI finishes above 24,344. **Invalidation**: KOSPI closes below 5,000 and Nikkei futures break 56,745 overnight, which would make today's bounce noise.

**B. Gold: sell-side targets are starting to move, the short-term bearish case is harder than yesterday's, but bids appeared near 4,035.**
Three layers of evidence: Goldman cut its target by $500 and pointed squarely at a December hike; BofA's 2y swap decomposition (+113bp YTD, +196bp on oil-up days) says the "energy inflation → hikes" chain is the main link; gold's minute-line low landed at 13:29, exactly when KOSPI made its second leg down and 30 minutes before the Nikkei close, with no haven bid. The counter-evidence is the bounce: after 14:05 gold recovered to 4,049, retracing about one-fifth of the day's $52.55 decline. **Lean**: neither chase the downside nor buy the bounce; 4,035.86 is today's low, not confirmed support, and only a move back above 4,090 (the 11-05 close) would count as repair. **Invalidation**: payrolls well below +65k with average hourly earnings under 0.3% would weaken hike pricing and void the bearish call for the day; a break of 4,000 (a psychological level, not a volume shelf) would signal deleveraging feeding on itself.

**C. Crude: still a short-covering rally, but today "oil up → rates up → gold and growth down" aligned for the first time.**
Evidence: Brent failed twice at 100 and its intraday range was 100.83–98.87; the direction of travel remains compression of the war premium (Saudi's December Asia OSP at a $6.50 discount, the lowest since May 2020). **Lean**: medium-term direction for crude is still lower premium and I would not chase geopolitical headlines; only a verifiable drop in Hormuz loadings would justify re-rating. **Invalidation**: Iran publicly rejects Qatar's two-phase draft, or a confirmed loading/export disruption appears.
