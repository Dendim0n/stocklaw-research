🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🔴 BREAKING: Chernobyl Spent Fuel Storage Facility Hit by Drone Attack**

The IAEA confirmed that Ukraine notified it this morning that the Central Spent Fuel Storage Facility within the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone was struck by a drone attack, causing major damage to the fuel reception building and affecting nearby structures from the blast wave. Ukraine reported radiation levels remain within regulatory limits, and an IAEA team will soon travel to the site for inspection. Zelensky called it a "despicably base" Russian attack on nuclear fuel storage facilities.

**🔴 Iran Drafts Hormuz Strait Environmental Service Fee Regulation**

Iranian Vice President and Head of the Environment Agency, Hina Ansari, announced that Iran has initiated drafting of the "Hormuz Strait Environmental Service Fee Regulation." The preliminary draft has been completed in recent days, though the fee structure and specific collection mechanism have yet to be finalized. This move could have far-reaching implications for global energy transport.

**🟡 France, UK, Germany Leaders Meeting with Ukraine's Zelensky**

French President Macron, UK Prime Minister Starmer, and German Chancellor Merz met with Ukrainian President Zelensky today to discuss continued support for Ukraine.

**🟡 Pakistan Conveys US Sanctions Easing Message to Iran**

According to Saudi outlet Alhadath, senior diplomatic sources revealed that Pakistan's Interior Minister conveyed to Iran that the US has agreed to ease sanctions.
