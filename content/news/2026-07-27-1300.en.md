🔥 **Key Developments This Hour**

**🛢️ Oil Plunges 6% as Middle East Ceasefire Dawns**
WTI crude tumbled over 6% intraday, breaking below $84. Simultaneously, Iran's navy intercepted six vessels attempting to transit illegal channels but deported them rather than seizing them, a notably restrained response. Markets are reading this as a major de-escalation signal.

Iranian officials reported that six vessels, "incited by the US," disabled navigation systems and attempted to cross dangerous southern channels in the Strait of Hormuz early this morning. One vessel suffered an accident; all were deported back to the Persian Gulf. The incident itself is tense, but Iran's restrained handling aligns with the broader ceasefire trajectory.

**🏦 Fed Meeting Uncertainty Intensifies**

Chair Warsh has broken with precedent by not signaling ahead of the meeting, leaving the bond market to force the Fed's hand. Analysts outline two scenarios: the committee collectively pushes for a hike, or Warsh temporarily holds steady.
