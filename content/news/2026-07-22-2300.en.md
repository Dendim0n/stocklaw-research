## 🧭 Strategic Assessment

Gold breaking above 4150 is not purely a safe-haven play — it reflects converging narratives: U.S.-Iran escalation + 30Y yields above 5% fueling debt anxiety + peak real rate expectations. Triple drivers keep gold bullish near-term, though 4160-4180 presents technical resistance.

Oil's strength is more fundamentally grounded than gold's: record EIA jet fuel demand + decade-low Midwest inventories represent genuine supply-demand improvement, compounded by Hormuz threats. WTI has momentum to test $90 short-term.

Russia's Black Sea port strikes and wheat's 4% surge signal that grain supply chain risk is being repriced. Watch for spillover into corn and soybeans.

The 30Y Treasury yield holding above 5% is the most underappreciated risk this week. If the ECB holds rates steady on Thursday while signaling hawkishness, the global rate anchor could shift higher, pressuring growth stocks and emerging markets.

Apple's AI Mac overhaul + OpenAI's $750B compute commitment + AMD's push reinforce the AI hardware narrative, but Amazon's AGI layoffs remind us that the bubble is simultaneously purging losers — winner takes all.
