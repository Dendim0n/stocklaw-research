🧭 **Situation Assessment**

The Chicago PMI miss muddies the "soft landing + disinflation" narrative, yet the market's muted open shows investors are deferring to tonight's Jackson Hole speech.

Warsh remains the biggest variable: with inflation off target for 65 straight months, markets are hypersensitive to any policy-framework shift (such as a new inflation gauge), and crowded low-volatility positioning could amplify any hawkish surprise.

Earnings dispersion is widening — PayPal's miss, Marvell's "beat but slow Google ramp," and BioNTech's clinical failure show stock-picking within tech and pharma is getting harder.

Crude keeps giving back its geopolitical premium; a Venezuelan investment deal would add to medium-term supply expectations, keeping oil biased lower though prone to sentiment swings.

Noise from Russia's missile test and Israel's Gaza strikes hasn't dented risk appetite — the open is calm, and all eyes are on monetary policy.
