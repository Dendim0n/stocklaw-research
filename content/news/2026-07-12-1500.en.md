🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🛢️ Strait of Hormuz Crisis Sharply Escalates**

Multiple locations in Oman's Musandam province (southern side of the Strait of Hormuz) were struck by drone attacks. Iran's Tasnim news agency reported that the Kondab region in central Iran was also attacked on Sunday. Qatar's Interior Ministry reported three injuries, including one child, from Iranian strikes. Jordan's military confirmed three Iranian missiles landed on its territory without casualties.

India's Foreign Ministry condemned the attack on the commercial vessel **GFS Galaxy** off the coast of Oman — 10 of 11 Indian crew members rescued, 1 missing. Bahrain has activated its alert status.

Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesperson stated that Iranian and Omani foreign ministers discussed Strait of Hormuz navigation and traffic management coordination mechanisms on July 11. Maritime analysts warn that with neither the US nor Iran backing down, the Strait of Hormuz could become a "long-term powder keg."

**🇺🇸 US Senator Lindsey Graham Passes Away**

Graham's office confirmed the veteran Republican senator died from a sudden illness. A key member of the Senate Armed Services Committee, his passing may affect defense budget and foreign policy trajectories.

**🇺🇦 Ukraine Strikes Russian Oil Refinery in Samara**

Ukraine's military claimed a strike on an oil refinery in Russia's Samara region, continuing pressure on Russian energy infrastructure.

**🌪️ Typhoon Bavi Moves North, Affecting Eastern China**

China's Ministry of Water Resources upgraded to a yellow flood warning. Heavy to torrential rain expected across eastern Jiangnan, Jianghuai, Huanghuai, North China, and Northeast China from July 12-14. Three government departments have dispatched 70,000 central relief supply items to Zhejiang, Tianjin, and Anhui. Beijing has lifted its city-wide flood emergency response.

🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

The Strait of Hormuz is the core market variable this week. Neither the US nor Iran shows signs of backing down, and the conflict has escalated from diplomatic war of words to substantive military exchanges — drone strikes on Oman, attacks on central Iran, commercial vessel hit — a spiral of deterioration.

Gold oscillates above $4,100 with an intraday $60+ swing signaling enormous long-short divergence. If the conflict further disrupts shipping, gold could test $4,200; but a weekend diplomatic breakthrough would unleash significant profit-taking pressure.

Crude oil remains relatively restrained, hovering around $71, as markets have not yet priced in a full Hormuz shipping halt. A tanker attack or surge in shipping insurance premiums could trigger a spike in crude.

Senator Graham's sudden passing is a black swan event. As a hawkish core figure on the Senate Armed Services Committee, his absence may briefly unsettle defense stocks but does not alter medium-to-long-term defense spending trends.

Typhoon Bavi poses short-term disruption to eastern China's logistics and agricultural production zones — watch for risk-off sentiment in A-share insurance and agricultural sectors.
