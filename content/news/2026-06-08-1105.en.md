The Middle East conflict is evolving from a bilateral Israel-Iran confrontation into a multi-party melee, with Houthi direct involvement meaning the risk premium will continue to climb.

Gold is caught between safe-haven demand and Fed rate hike expectations, with the 4300 level serving as the bull-bear dividing line; a breach would open short-term downside.

Key watchpoints today: whether the Saudi base attack claims hold up, whether the US escalates military response, and whether Korean authorities intervene in FX markets.
