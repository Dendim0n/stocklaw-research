## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**Middle East Escalates on Three Fronts: Iran Reveals Satellite Proof of US Base Damage, Trump Threatens Tougher Action**

Iran released satellite imagery showing the complete destruction of a US ammunition depot at King Faisal Air Base in Jordan. Trump responded by saying the US has "many exit strategies" on Iran, including military options, but does not believe Iran is ready to reach a deal yet — and would not rule out intensifying military strikes. Separately, Kuwait and Bahrain were reportedly involved in secret airstrikes on Iranian targets earlier this month, with Kuwait denying the claims. Houthi spokesman announced a "blockade for blockade" maritime operation against Saudi Arabia, while an incident involving an oil tanker and military forces was reported in the Gulf of Oman.

**Ukraine Opens a New Caspian Sea Front: Strikes Russian Oil Platform and Iranian Supply Vessels**

Ukraine's Security Service confirmed strikes on Russia's Filanovsky oil platform in the Caspian Sea and two cargo vessels. President Zelenskyy stated that Ukrainian long-range strikes achieved "very good results," targeting ships transporting Iranian military cargo and a military vessel, with additional strikes on a factory in Kirov Oblast.

**Trump Threatens Massive Tariffs on the EU**

Trump stated the US is negotiating with Iran while simultaneously threatening large-scale tariffs on the European Union. Germany's Chamber of Commerce and foreign trade association criticized the frequent tariff adjustments, warning they increase business and investment uncertainty.

**Typhoon "Hongxia" Triggers Flood Alerts Across 7 Chinese Provinces**

China's Ministry of Water Resources activated a Level IV flood emergency response for seven provinces across the Pearl River and Yangtze River basins. Zhuhai issued an orange alert for severe thunderstorms with gale-force winds of up to Beaufort scale 10.
