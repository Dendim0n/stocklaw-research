## 4. Judgments and counter-evidence

**Judgment 1: AI hardware's demand is confirmed, its gross margin is not; neutral-to-bullish on the complex, but do not chase AMD's after-hours move.**

For: AMD's data centre share of revenue went from 47% to 64% with revenue nearly doubling, and its Q4 guide is $770m above consensus; Super Micro lifted the top of its full-year range by $10bn — two different links in the chain (chip and system) pointing the same way, which is more credible than a single beat. Against: AMD guides Q4 gross margin to 55% versus 55.3% expected, gaming is −40%, client plus gaming is −8%, and the CEO himself expects 2027 PC units down double digits. **"Low-margin data centre growth has to carry high-margin PC decline" is what this report actually said**, and the 3-point fade from +9.6% to +6.6% in three hours is the market pricing exactly that. **Stance**: AI hardware **neutral-to-bullish**, but the after-hours high is not a buy reference. **Confirmation**: if tonight's 23:00 ISM services and the 05:00 Qualcomm print repeat "more volume, less price", the complex can keep going. **Invalidation**: SOX is already 1.91% below its 200-day (10,807); if it keeps sliding while NDX holds above its own 200-day (27,952), the bounce is index-level only, not semiconductor-level.

**Judgment 2: Crude's physical and price signals are diverging; pricing power remains with the geopolitical premium. Neutral-to-bearish short term, but $95 is not a fundamental floor.**

For: API crude −5.267m barrels and distillates −1.192m, both opposite to consensus, while WTI settled −0.38% and probed 95.73 in Asian hours (94.997 on the Jin10 series) — inventory bullishness was overrun by premium removal. Trump's three lines at 07:36 ("no immediate plan", "more oil through Hormuz than before the war", "energy prices will fall sharply after the midterms") are the only substantive premium-reducing input in this window. Against: none of the counter-evidence was withdrawn — the IRGC's 00:22 readiness statement, the 03:55 deadlock report, the 03:00 tanker hit by two projectiles, and the 06:17 seven-conditions demand; Qatar's 36-hour feedback window does not expire until tonight; and the official version of the inventory data (EIA) is not out until 23:30. **Stance**: crude **neutral-to-bearish short term**, but **do not chase it lower** — today's $95 area is a headline low, not a supply-demand low. **Confirmation**: if EIA confirms the draw tonight and crude still closes below 96, "inventories don't matter" is established. **Invalidation**: if Iran rejects the proposal inside 36 hours and Hormuz sees another attack, the premium returns in one move.

**Judgment 3: The most underpriced variable this issue is rates, not earnings — "stocks up, bonds down" says the bounce is low quality.**

For: 10-year 4.95% (+4bp), 30-year 5.32% (+4bp), 2-year 4.61% (+2bp) with the 2-year 61bp above the top of the fed funds range; CME at 78.9% for a December hike; and on the same day New Zealand unemployment at 5.7% with negative employment and China's warehousing index in contraction for three months — **the market is pricing hikes while growth decelerates**. That is not "the economy is strong so hike"; it is "energy inflation is forcing hikes". Against: the Nasdaq still rose 0.83% on a +4bp day and VIX fell 1.5% to 20.83, so equities are currently insensitive to rates; the 3-month bill did not move at all, so there is no front-end panic. **Stance**: rates alone cannot call direction, but they can call **quality** — this bounce runs on delivered narratives, not on easier liquidity, so the upside is capped and the pullbacks will be fast. **Confirmation**: if the 10-year clears 5.00% while NDX stays below its 20-day, the divergence has run its course. **Invalidation**: if the 10-year falls back under 4.85% and NDX reclaims 29,626 (20-day), the rates headwind is gone.

**Judgment 4: Gold's stabilisation is short covering plus election uncertainty, not allocation money returning; neutral-to-bearish stands, but do not short it here.**

For: spot is +0.20% at 3,938.52, still below the 11-02 close of 3,946.47; **SPDR Gold Trust holdings fell 2.851 tonnes to 1,037.662 tonnes** and iShares Silver fell 7.54 tonnes to 15,751.9 tonnes (07:04–07:19 flashes); the domestic night session had SHFE gold −0.11% and silver −1.31%, the opposite direction to the offshore tape. Against: silver is up 1.69% since 16:00 versus gold's 0.81%, taking the gold/silver ratio from 79.66 to **78.96** — if this were pure haven demand, silver should not outperform; and Jin10 spot traded a **3,926.66–3,940.52** range across this window (0.35%), without breaking down again. **Stance**: precious metals **neutral-to-bearish**; "don't chase it down, don't buy the dip". **Confirmation**: a reclaim of 3,946.47 is the first sign of repair. **Invalidation**: a break of the 11-02 low at 3,876.89 opens a new leg lower; three consecutive days of ETF inflows would void this call.
