🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**⚔️ Ukraine Claims Hitting 12 Russian Oil Tankers & Oil Terminal**

Ukraine's military claims striking 12 Russian oil tankers, 1 tugboat, and 1 cargo ship in the Azov Sea, along with a Russian oil terminal. The vessels were allegedly used to supply fuel to Russian forces and transport oil in circumvention of international sanctions. Oil prices fell despite the news, suggesting the market views this as a localized escalation with limited impact on global supply.

**🏦 London Asset Manager Dumps All US Treasuries — First Time in Nearly a Decade**

A major London asset manager's fund has reduced its US Treasury holdings to zero for the first time in nearly a decade. Fund managers cited an overheating US economy, greater Fed rate hike potential vs. Europe, and multiple risks including debt, inflation, and Fed independence — flagging a clear mispricing between US and European interest rates.

**🔋 BYD Energy Storage Signs 11.275 GWh Deal with UAE's Masdar**

BYD Energy Storage will provide round-the-clock energy storage solutions for Masdar's RTC project in Abu Dhabi, totaling 11.275 GWh. Middle East storage demand continues to surge as China's battery supply chain accelerates its global expansion.

**🇨🇳 Reuters Poll: RMB Only Asian Currency with Bullish Bets**

The latest Reuters FX survey shows short positions on the Korean won have eased but remain elevated, while the Chinese yuan is the only Asian currency receiving bullish bets in this round.

**🛢️ Goldman Warns: Hormuz Blockage Threatens Global Oil Supply Recovery**

Goldman Sachs notes that if negotiations over the next 60 days can proceed with security guarantees and new sanctions waivers, oil supply could recover by late July; otherwise, flows will tighten further.
