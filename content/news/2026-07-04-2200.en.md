🔥 **Key Headlines This Hour**

**US-Iran Talks: July 11 in Pakistan**
Saudi outlets Al-Hadath and Al-Arabiya report that the next round of US-Iran negotiations is scheduled for July 11 in Pakistan, covering sanctions, frozen Iranian funds, and nuclear issues. The composition of the Iranian delegation is yet to be announced.

**Iran Internal Developments**
- Iran's Intelligence Ministry announced the dismantling of four "terrorist and extremist" cells linked to US and Israeli intelligence, in a joint operation involving the IRGC and national police.
- Ali Azmai has been appointed commander of the IRGC Navy, having served in the force for an extended period.
- Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister warned non-regional countries against creating a crisis in the Strait of Hormuz, responding to a joint UK-France statement.

**Tesla, US Tech Firms Tighten AI Spending**
Tesla will restrict employee spending on AI tools starting July 6. Similar measures are being adopted by multiple US tech companies, signaling that AI ROI is coming under internal corporate scrutiny.

🧭 **Assessment**

Gold surged over 1% after oscillating in the 4122-4195 range, driven by safe-haven demand from Middle East geopolitics and US-Iran negotiation uncertainty. Watch whether 4200 resistance breaks in the near term.

ECB dovish signals are emerging. The oil price pullback has created room for a policy pivot, and the July meeting could become a window for a tone adjustment.
