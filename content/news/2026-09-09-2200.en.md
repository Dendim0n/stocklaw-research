## 🧭 Market Assessment

SC crude night-session surged over 4%, confirming domestic sensitivity to geopolitical premium. But WTI is approaching $94, near technical resistance, having already accumulated 1.2% gains during the day. **Absent further substantive supply disruption (e.g., strait blockade), chasing longs at current levels offers poor risk-reward; a more prudent approach is waiting for a pullback to the $91–92 zone before reassessing.** Positive for energy stocks, negative for transportation/high energy-cost sectors.

Do not chase longs at current levels; wait for clearer direction.
