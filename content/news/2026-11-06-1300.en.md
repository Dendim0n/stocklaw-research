## 4. Assessment

**A. Equities: the afternoon give-back is not new bad news, it is insufficient bid — the bounce is tactical, not durable.**
The evidence is directional agreement across four markets: STAR50 −2.54%, Nikkei −6.10%, KOSPI −4.87%, HSI −1.30%; and the A-share lift was concentrated in optical-module names that announced buybacks at midday (Luxshare, Zhongji Innolight, Eoptolink, TFC, Dongshan), triggered by the PBOC's 11-04 cut to the relending rate for buybacks and increased holdings (explicitly cited in several company filings) — corporate statements, not a change in demand. The more telling split is Dow futures **−0.96%** alongside Nasdaq futures **+0.90%**: if risk appetite were recovering, both would move together. **Stance**: do not chase the AI-hardware bounce; watch the real volume after the 22:30 open, and avoid directional longs before the 21:30 payrolls. **Confirmation**: KOSPI closes above 5,000, HSI closes above 24,533 (its midday level), Nasdaq futures hold +0.9%. **Invalidation**: Nikkei closes below −7%, or A-shares widen losses past −2% into the close — that would make the afternoon give-back the start of a new leg down rather than intraday churn.

**B. Gold: what is falling is the real-rate story, not demand for insurance; short-term bearish, but the room depends on the 21:30 payrolls.**
The evidence is timing: the 4,037 low came at 12:09, exactly as equities weakened into the afternoon — if safe-haven bids dominated, gold should have moved the other way. BofA's decomposition (2y swaps +113bp YTD, +196bp cumulative on oil-up days) shows the market treating "energy inflation → hikes" as the main chain, consistent with 11-05's structure where VIX closed 28.18 (+69.66%) and gold rose only 0.72%. Offsetting slow variables exist but do not change the short-term price: Indian physical demand is recovering as prices fall (12:01) and Poland's central bank added 28 tonnes in October. **Stance**: 4,037 is only a reference low, not confirmed support; until gold reclaims 4,090 (the 11-05 close) it is a repair, not a turn — do not front-run the data. **Confirmation**: payrolls ≤65k with average hourly earnings below 0.3% m/m, and gold back above 4,090. **Invalidation**: payrolls clearly above 65k with hourly earnings ≥0.3% m/m, and gold probes 4,000 (a psychological round number with no volume-based support evidence; use only as a magnitude reference).

**C. Crude: today's bounce is short covering, the war premium still points lower, but do not bet volatility on headlines.**
Evidence: Brent is −12% and WTI −12.1% on the week, so today's +1.1% retraces only a fraction; ANZ attributes it directly to traders judging Wednesday's selloff overdone; on the physical side the Saudi OSP discount is the lowest in six years and Middle East spot premiums are back to pre-war levels. The counter-evidence is equally specific: the Pentagon's strike plan includes energy infrastructure, the third carrier group only reaches the theatre in mid-November, and at 13:13 the FT reported Iran-linked vessels loitering above subsea cables (single source, unconfirmed). **Stance**: do not chase crude higher, and do not short volatility on Qatar-draft headlines; a verifiable drop in Hormuz loadings would invalidate the bearish-premium view immediately. **Confirmation**: whether Brent can close above 100 — it failed twice intraday today. **Invalidation**: Iran publicly rejects the two-day phased draft.
