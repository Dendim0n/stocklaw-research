**⚔️ Middle East Escalation**: Iran's Revolutionary Guard announced it has attacked U.S. military facilities at Al Jaber Base in Kuwait. **📱 Xiaomi Raises Shipment Target**: Full-year 2026 smartphone shipment target raised from ~90 million to 110 million units, a ~16% increase, driven primarily by entry-level models.

**🇨🇳 Ruijun Asset Management Self-Invests ¥100M**: The multi-billion-dollar private fund announced a ¥100 million self-investment into its own funds this month, the third such ¥100M+ commitment recently.

**🔌 Texas Heatwave Threatens Record Power Demand**: ERCOT's preliminary estimates show Monday evening electricity demand nearing the record 85.5 GW set in August 2023.

**🥇 Capital Economics Defends Gold**: Strategists argue gold retains value as a hedge against equity selloffs, despite its recent behavior resembling a risk asset more than a safe haven.

🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

The Middle East is the biggest variable this hour — Iran directly attacking a U.S. base in Kuwait marks a spillover beyond Iranian territory and the Red Sea.

The U.S. State Department global alert combined with Trump's hardline rhetoric signals that the diplomatic window is narrowing, making Qatar's 10-day ceasefire proposal extremely difficult to land.

Escalating geopolitical risk plus record hedge fund tech selling could further fuel risk-off sentiment, providing near-term support for gold and crude oil.

However, gold is already at elevated levels (4024) — any meaningful ceasefire progress would create significant pullback pressure.

On the A-share front, insurance fund inflows and private fund self-investments provide a near-term sentiment boost, but sustainability remains questionable.
