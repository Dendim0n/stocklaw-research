NVIDIA's continued strength as the index weight core is the primary engine. But risks: ① NASDAQ/DOW two-month gap near 10pp shows "narrow market" dynamics (Jin10 ID: 231763); history shows similar divergence preceded the 2000 bust; ② VIX at 15.01 — extremely low given high geopolitical and political uncertainty. **Low VIX in this context is not evidence of "market calm" but of under-priced risk.** Strategy: **do not chase longs at current levels, but also do not short — the trend remains strong, the magnitude of any pullback is unpredictable.**

**Gold $4,166 rebound + accelerating ETF inflows confirm medium-term allocation trend.** The bounce from $4,134 to $4,166 is not a brief technical repair — SPDR adding 3.7T in one day (vs 0.57T yesterday) shows institutional capital accelerating allocation. **Crude $89.19 stabilization faces conflicting forces.** API data (refined products +461K vs expected -2.341M) is near-term bearish; Hormuz danger premium and Venezuela refinery shutdown are medium-term bullish. **Key watch: today's EIA crude inventory (16:30 CST). **Japan data divergence puts BOJ hike path博弈 at a critical juncture.** Manufacturing near 5-year high + overtime pay +5.2% support hiking; services collapsing to 23 supports pausing. New审议委员 Sato's "rates need gradual adjustment" (with "no预设 pace") is dovish-leaning, meaning December hike is not a certainty. **If non-manufacturing data continues deteriorating, BOJ may push hike expectations into 2027.**
