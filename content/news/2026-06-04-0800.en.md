🧭 **Market Analysis**

The deepening Fed divergence is the core overnight signal.

Williams and Logan may sound contradictory, but they're two sides of the same coin: economic momentum holds, but inflation stickiness is far worse than expected.

Logan's hawkish stance as a voting member deserves serious attention — if upcoming CPI data doesn't cooperate, a rate hike this year is no longer a fantasy.

All three major US indices fell in unison, with the Dow dropping over 1.2%. Markets are repricing for a more hawkish Fed.

The Israel-Lebanon ceasefire is mildly bearish for oil, but the Hormuz risk premium won't vanish quickly — WTI has support around 97.

Gold bucked the trend, breaking above 4450, supported by dual tailwinds of geopolitical uncertainty and inflation expectations.

However, watch the 10+ billion yuan outflow from gold ETFs — institutions reducing positions at highs is a signal that can't be ignored.

Friday's NFP is the key variable. JPMorgan lays out five scenarios: an overheated jobs print would reinforce hike expectations and pressure both stocks and bonds.
