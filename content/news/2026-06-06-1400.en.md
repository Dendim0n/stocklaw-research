🧭 **Assessment**

Gold consolidating in a tight 4,310-4,330 range as bulls and bears deadlock. The first wave of post-NFP panic has subsided, but hawkish repricing limits near-term upside; next week's CPI is the key catalyst.

Analysts' warnings on gold holding costs are worth noting — when the market pivots from "rate cuts" to "rate hikes," non-yielding assets lose appeal, and gold's medium-term headwinds remain intact.

Weekend watchpoints: Russia's characterization of the St. Petersburg attack, Iran situation developments, and next week's CPI expectation management. Volatility likely to stay elevated.
