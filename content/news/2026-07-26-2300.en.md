## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**Iran-US-Israel Three-Way Standoff Intensifies**

Netanyahu issued a rapid-fire series of statements between 22:22-22:24 CST: Iran's nuclear program must be ended "whether by agreement or without"; the war will end only when the Iranian regime collapses or is weakened enough to terminate its nuclear ambitions; the Iran question ultimately depends on Trump's decision.

Saudi media reported at 21:27 that Iran told Pakistani officials it has "not withdrawn from negotiations" and is willing to continue talks with the US in Geneva and other locations. However, the Iranian Army spokesman at 22:02 maintained a hardline tone, claiming the US is "in a desperate situation" and may exit the war.
