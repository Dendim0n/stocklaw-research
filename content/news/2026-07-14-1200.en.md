🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

Tonight is the decisive moment of the month. CPI plus Warsh testimony represent a double-barreled event capable of repricing the entire July rate hike path.

If core CPI prints above 0.3% MoM, July hike odds will climb further from 50%, the dollar will strengthen, risk assets will come under pressure, and gold may see a short-term pullback.

But if core CPI meets or comes in below expectations, the currently overpriced hike narrative will rapidly unwind — bullish for equities and gold.

The Middle East situation is an independent risk vector. With Iran and the Houthis escalating in parallel, crude supply uncertainty is rising. Brent could retest $85.

The Korean chip ETF collapse is a classic case study in leverage risk and carries contagion risk for US semiconductor sentiment. Watch tonight's Philadelphia Semiconductor Index.

Baidu's single-day 10% drop reflects deep market pessimism about Chinese tech advertising revenue — the AI cloud narrative cannot offset this in the near term.
