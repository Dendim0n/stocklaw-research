## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. WSJ: Trump Has Ordered New Round of Attacks on Iran**
U.S. officials told the Wall Street Journal that Trump has ordered a new round of attacks against Iran, aimed at forcing Tehran's surrender. A major escalation signal following the Strait of Hormuz closure declaration.

Saudi media Al-Hadath reported an explosion in western Islamabad, Kermanshah province. Iran's Tasnim News Agency later said an explosion near Islamabad-e Gharb was caused by an old artillery shell. The timing is sensitive as markets closely watch Iran's domestic security situation.

A deputy governor of Iran's Hormozgan province stated that rumors about evacuating parts of the province are "complete nonsense." The official clarification comes after the Strait of Hormuz closure declaration sparked panic speculation.

**4. Russian Forces Capture Two Villages in Eastern Ukraine**
According to Interfax, Russian forces seized Lyubitske village in the Zaporizhzhia region and Olhivka village in the Kharkiv region. Front-line advances continue alongside the massive overnight strikes on Kyiv, showing multi-axis pressure.

**5. BYD July Sales Exceed 419K, Overseas Volume Hits Record**
BYD reported July sales of 419,211 units, with overseas sales nearing 180,000 — a new all-time high. January-July 2026 cumulative sales reached 2.227 million units. Chinese automakers' global expansion accelerates.

**6. Geely July Sales Hit Record High**
Geely Auto reported July sales of 250,161 units, a new record for the period. Overseas exports reached 107,000 units, a monthly record. NEV sales hit 160,165 units (+23% YoY), with NEV exports surging 616% YoY.

During his U.S. visit, Zelensky asked Trump and other officials to persuade Musk to allow broader military use of the Starlink system. Current Starlink usage for Ukrainian forces has strict restrictions; loosening them would significantly enhance Ukraine's battlefield capabilities.
