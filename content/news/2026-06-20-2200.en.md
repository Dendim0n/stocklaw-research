🧭 **Analysis**

The Hormuz Strait closure is one of the most significant geopolitical events in the past year.

Approximately 20% of global oil trade and 25% of LNG trade transits through the Strait of Hormuz; a blockade would have systemic implications for global energy supply chains.

Iran's "first step" language suggests the closure may be incremental — initially restricting specific vessels rather than a full shutdown — but escalation risk is extremely high.

Vance denying the Hormuz closure while Iran officially announces it represents a severe factual divergence between the two sides, clouding negotiation prospects.

Monday's global market open is the critical observation window — oil could gap higher, gold may bounce on safe-haven demand, and the dollar will also face headwinds.

Investors holding energy longs face extreme volatility risk this weekend; close monitoring of developments and preparation for Monday's open is essential.
