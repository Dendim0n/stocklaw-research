## 4. Assessment

**A House call is not a "blue wave sweep", so this is neither a moment to chase the downside nor to bottom-fish - what is priced is still half a ballot, not a full result.** Evidence: Senate control is undecided, and even the House call is one forecaster's projection; more importantly the **structure** of the decline is deteriorating - the prior hour was a Nasdaq-only move (-0.98% versus -0.05% for the Dow), while this hour it spread to the Dow (-0.35%) and A-shares, and S&P futures were still printing lows at the sample, so selling has not exhausted. **Affected assets**: Nasdaq-100 and the semis have the most beta, the Dow and value are relatively defensive, and long-duration Treasuries are the only long with clean logic (tariffs and fiscal expansion constrained, term premium down). **Confirmation/invalidation**: Nasdaq-100 futures reclaiming 28,149 (the 08:55 level) would count as repair; if they break today's low of 27,712.75 while S&P futures also lose today's low of 7,506, the selling has moved beyond an "election discount" and the right response is to cut risk budgets across the AI chain rather than wait for final results.

**Precious metals: neutral-to-bearish short term, do not buy the dip - same conclusion as last period, but the evidence is stronger.** Evidence: stocks, the dollar, gold and silver all fell together, which only "de-risking" explains; the gold/silver ratio widened 0.88 in an hour and silver's daily loss exceeds gold's by 1.35 percentage points; SPDR Gold Trust still shed 2.851 tonnes yesterday (reported last period, no fresh data this hour); the onshore Shanghai premium over international gold is only 0.6 yuan/gram, so there is no physical scramble. **Affected assets**: gold miners and silver's industrial demand chain (solar, electronics) under pressure; A-share precious metals bearish. **Confirmation/invalidation**: if gold reclaims **3,950** (the top of today's 3,945-3,953 quote band around 10:00) while the dollar keeps weakening, this is a dollar trade returning rather than liquidation, and this call is void; a break of the 3,919.80 session low opens the 11-02 low of 3,876.89 as the next reference.

**Crude: bearish short term, but what is being sold is the "peace expectation", not supply and demand - do not chase it lower.** Evidence: conflicting US-Iran signals (ANZ), attacks have not stopped (last period's WSJ tally: more than 35 Hormuz attacks in October, at least 5 vessels hit this month), yet price broke 95; an API draw of 5.267 million barrels failed to hold it up. On the supply side, Saudi Aramco's pledge to maximise output this quarter and recovering Gulf exports are the physical basis for the premium bleed. **Affected assets**: energy equities pressured near term, airline and transport costs improve, and a lower inflation impulse is mildly supportive for long-end Treasuries. **Confirmation/invalidation**: if tonight's 23:30 EIA confirms a draw while crude still settles below 95, "inventories do not move price" is confirmed a second time and the bearish call can stand; if Qatari mediation collapses or there is **an actual loading disruption** (not another attack report), the premium rebuilds in one move and shorts must exit.
