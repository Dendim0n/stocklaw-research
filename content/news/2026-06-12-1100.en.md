🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Trump Signals Peace, Halts Iran Strike — But Iran Cools the Enthusiasm**
Politico reports that after Trump warned Thursday morning of "very tough measures against Iran tonight," leaders from Qatar, UAE, and Pakistan made urgent calls urging restraint. Trump subsequently signaled that a US-Iran deal could be signed over the weekend. However, Iran responded coldly, saying it had received no formal proposal.

**2. Iran Names Musk's Entities as Military Targets**
Iran's Revolutionary Guard declared all of Musk's Middle Eastern commercial entities — including Starlink and SpaceX — legitimate military targets. SpaceX launched the largest IPO in history the same day, raising $75 billion at a $1.77 trillion valuation, surpassing Tesla as the seventh-largest US listed company.

**3. CME Eyes 24/7 Trading for Gold and Crude**
CME plans to launch round-the-clock, seven-day-a-week trading in new crude oil and gold contracts. The new WTI contract will be one-tenth the size of the existing Micro WTI futures, aimed at covering Asian and weekend "black swan" risk periods.

**4. Fed Meeting Next Week May Bring a Communication "Style Shift"**
Pimco believes Fed Governor Warrick is pushing to reduce forward guidance and downplay the dot plot, allowing markets to price the rate path themselves. Next week's FOMC meeting communication style may matter more than the rate decision itself.

**5. Global Markets Surge on Peace Signals**
South Korea's KOSPI spiked as much as 8%, Japan's Nikkei 225 and Australia's ASX 200 both rose over 2%. In A-shares, industrial metals stocks surged with limit-up moves in Tongling Nonferrous and Zinc Industry. Precious metals sector advanced, with Zhaojin Gold up over 6%.
