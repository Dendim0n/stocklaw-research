## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Silver "Short Squeeze" Returns — Goldman Warns It's Just Beginning**

However, the 3-month lease rate has actually declined rather than risen, suggesting physical demand hasn't caught up — raising questions about the sustainability of the squeeze. Silver gained 3% intraday to $63.37/oz, up over 8% on the week.

Europe's largest economy is weathering geopolitical headwinds. However, the workday-adjusted annual rate came in at -0.1%, indicating the recovery remains modest.
