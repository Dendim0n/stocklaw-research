## 4. Situation assessment

**Crude: the downside now has an industry basis, but the 100 handle has not broken today — bearish within the range, no chasing either side.** Three facts from this hour: the Saudi Foreign Ministry framed the meeting as de-escalation (official language capping the escalation narrative); BPCL says Middle East supply is improving, November purchases rising and Russian volumes cut sharply (physical supply-side evidence); USOIL's session range remains 100.050–101.736 with the low not refreshed since the morning. Implications: bearish upstream; the relative strength in refining and tankers comes from product markets and freight rates, not from crude direction; energy equities making new highs while crude is down 2.54% means buyers want cash flow and dividends, not oil beta. Invalidation: a verifiable loading disruption or mine incident would gap crude back above 102 and void this call, as would a US-session close above the 101.736 session high. Reverse confirmation: if US energy stocks keep outperforming while crude extends its decline tonight, the dividend logic is intact.

**Gold: neutral, do not chase; today's pullback looks like a liquidity event, not the death of the hedge case.** Evidence: gold touched 4,019.3 and failed to hold, back to 4,013 (-0.13% on the hour), while Nasdaq futures fell 0.24% in the same hour — the two moved together. Morgan Stanley's Thursday data point (real yields -14bp, gold -2.8%, rarest since December 2008) shows gold gets used as an ATM during deleveraging. Implications: the medium-term holding-cost direction (real yields) still supports gold, but short-term there is no bid underneath — two pushes higher today both failed; do not chase gold miners, with Zijin down 5% in Hong Kong this morning against a session high in bullion. Invalidation: gold reclaiming 4,019.3 while Nasdaq futures strengthen would mean allocation demand rather than liquidity demand, and this read would need revision; a break of the 3,978 session low turns the short-term trend down and forces a re-assessment of the 10-24 high at 4,034. Missing: gold ETF tonnage was not available this window, so redemptions cannot be separated from paper selling.

**AI compute chain and A/H shares: divergence is widening, this is not a broad recovery.** Three same-day data points: Shanghai turned positive but ChiNext gave back its entire move from -0.88% to -1.44%, so the rebound is weights and themes; SK Hynix's leveraged ETFs are liquidating three months after launch with the stock 56% below its year high; money simultaneously pushed PetroChina to a 2008-era high and Unitree up more than 56% in a month. Trade-off: do not chase a bounce in the compute chain — let this week's Asia-Pacific earnings and the SOX give direction. Do not chase robotics either; its crowding is the same problem that hit gold miners this morning. Confirmation and invalidation: if SK Hynix's Tuesday post-close report delivers upside HBM guidance and the SOX then outperforms the Nasdaq, deleveraging is over and the compute chain can be re-rated. If the SOX keeps lagging the Nasdaq while robotics rallies alone, this is a local theme rotation, not a global risk-appetite recovery.
