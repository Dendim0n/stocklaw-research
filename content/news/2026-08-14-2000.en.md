🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Iran Escalation: MQ-9 Drone Shot Down + "Foreign Military Aggression" Claims**

Iran's IRGC air defense system intercepted and destroyed a US MQ-9 Reaper drone over Hormozgan province. Subsequently, Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister claimed oil pollution along Qeshm Island's coast resulted from "foreign military aggression." The two reports compound Middle East geopolitical risk, warranting attention to Strait of Hormuz navigation safety and crude supply risks.

**2. Ukraine: Black Sea Ports Effectively Closed, Agricultural Exports Could Halve**

Ukraine's government reports no vessels have entered Odesa port since early August, with Black Sea ports effectively closed under Russian airstrikes. Even using all alternative routes, Q3 agricultural exports cap at 30 million tonnes, leaving another 30 million stranded. Meanwhile, Ukrainian forces struck Russia's Ust-Luga complex in the Leningrad region. Conflict escalation on dual fronts threatens both grain and energy supply chains.

**3. Goldman Sachs Raises Euro Stoxx 600 Target to 695 Points**

Goldman Sachs upgraded its 12-month target for the Euro Stoxx 600 from 660 to 695 points, reflecting optimism about European corporate earnings. European equity valuations remain below US markets, making capital reallocation trends worth monitoring.

**4. SK Hynix H1 Highlights: Nvidia Revenue at 13%, R&D Spending Up 70%+**

SK Hynix reported H1 revenue from Nvidia exceeding 17 trillion KRW (~13% of total revenue). Capex and R&D spending topped 18 trillion KRW, up over 70% YoY. Employee per-capita compensation reached 144 million KRW (~$104K), up 23% YoY. AI supply chain capex is still accelerating, with clear signals of HBM capacity expansion.
