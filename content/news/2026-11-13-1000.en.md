**① Gold reclaims 3,900, but the driver is not the discount rate | 09:00–09:24**
Spot gold +0.64% this hour, tagging a session high of 3,916.69 at 09:24 ([09:24](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113092431950800)); silver rose 1.2%, outpacing gold. **The discount-rate leg is not helping — it is pushing the other way**: EUR/USD flat at 1.1060, USD/JPY flat at 159.88, the 30-year JGB yield up 6.5bp to 3.985% at 09:52 ([09:52](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113095206462800)) and the 40-year up 4.5bp to 4.115% at 09:10 ([09:10](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113091035178800)). One research item worth recording: Metals Focus and BMO disagree on 2026 silver — the former sees a sixth consecutive annual deficit (35 Moz short, solar demand −21%), the latter argues the traditional deficit measure misleads and an expanding surplus is coming ([09:24](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113092439819800)). The report landed almost exactly with silver's high, but this issue cannot establish causation.
Impact: precious metals have a positioning base for a bounce but no discount-rate support, so upside is limited; for gold miners this is repair, not reversal.

**② Crude gives back the attack premium: Trump says Saudi Arabia asked for the status quo | 08:08 (post previous sample)–10:01**
WTI broke below yesterday's 88.74 settlement, falling 0.30% this hour to 88.13 with a session low of **88.04**, down 0.68% on the day. Trump said "I could have ended this war immediately, but at the request of the countries I am protecting we are taking a slower, more civilised approach" and "Saudi Arabia has asked me to keep the situation as it is for now", adding that Qatar, the UAE and Bahrain prefer a "waiting period" ([08:08](https://flash.jin10.com/detail/20261113080858200800)); separate reporting says he is no longer fixated on a deal and the war may settle into a long stalemate ([235100](https://xnews.jin10.com/details/235100)). That directly contradicts the "Saudi facilities hit → supply interruption" chain — **the official US line is non-escalation**. Add the already-confirmed EIA build of 4.657m barrels and record 14.013m b/d output, and yesterday's +2.55% has no fundamental backing.
Impact: the war premium in front-month crude and energy equities is now in retracement; one-way pass-through to inflation expectations weakens with it.

## 3. Situation assessment

**A | Gold: the bounce is real but not a trend; neutral-to-bullish, tactical only.**
Basis: this hour's +0.64% came with a flat dollar and JGB long-end yields up 4.5–6.5bp — the discount-rate leg offered no help at all; the previously verified 0.38-tonne drop in SPDR holdings shows the selling came from futures and leveraged accounts, and that kind of supply exits fast, so bounces are fast too, without becoming trends by themselves. Affected assets: gold, silver (50.65), gold miners and gold ETFs. Falsification and confirmation: the bar for a trend repair is reclaiming the 11-11 close of **3,987.93** (about 1.9% above spot) with the 10-year back below 5.00% — not reachable today; conversely, if tonight's PPI m/m prints above the 0.4% consensus (previous 1.30%) and the 2-year pushes back above 4.90%, this bounce is a pause in a downtrend.

**B | Crude: pricing power over the premium has shifted from "the attack" to "the US attitude" — bearish.**
Basis: yesterday's +2.55% rested on the attacking party's unilateral statement, and today Trump himself denied any appetite for escalation, undermining the premise of a supply interruption; the EIA added a hard 4.657m-barrel build with record output. Price has already broken yesterday's settlement and printed a session low of 88.04. But do not chase the short: the Brent–WTI spread at $7.08 is unchanged from yesterday, meaning the market has not pulled the risk premium out of the waterborne barrel wholesale — it has only deflated the spike. Affected assets: front-month crude, product cracks, tankers and war-risk insurance, energy equities. Falsification and confirmation: if Saudi Arabia or a third party confirms damage at Pumping Station 7 or the Yanbu facilities, or actual loading disruptions appear, this section is void immediately; if WTI returns below 87 without fresh news, yesterday's gain is confirmed as a one-off spike.

**C | China semis: SMIC separated "AI-adjacent plus localisation" from "weak handsets" — a structural signal, not an aggregate one.**
Basis: revenue above the top of guidance, ASP +1.8% q/q and full-year guidance above $12bn show utilisation and pricing improving together; but smartphones −8% and consumer −1% show this is not a broad recovery. Flows read it the same way: memory and compute chips strengthen while CPO optical modules opened high and slid (Yuanjie −10%). Affected assets: STAR 50, memory and specialty processes, Hang Seng Tech (SMIC +5.46%) — bullish; CPO / optical modules and AI server assembly — bearish. Falsification and confirmation: if Nvidia's 11-17 earnings cut guidance, SMIC's "AI-adjacent" logic gets interrupted from the demand side; if CPO recovers its losses in the afternoon, this was opening rotation rather than a trend change.
