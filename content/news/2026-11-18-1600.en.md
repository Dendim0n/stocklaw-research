## Key incremental items

**① Nvidia formally exits China's H200 market; the revenue estimate for the new rule comes from the sell side｜disclosed US post-close 11-17 (05:20 Beijing today), resurfaced at 15:20 this hour**
Interim CEO Colette Kress said on the Q3 call that the company is "no longer shipping licensed Hopper data-centre products to China" (H200 is a Hopper-architecture part); licensed Hopper shipments to China were about **$2.4bn** in the quarter, and forward guidance contains none of these products. In the same window the company set up a board-level committee overseeing national security, export-control compliance and global trade risk. The backdrop is Monday's US rule barring offshore data centres from providing advanced-chip remote compute to Chinese customers. Jefferies estimates that remote-access restriction could cut data-centre revenue by **$44–62bn** next year; Kress said Vera Rubin backlog is enough to offset the top-line impact. Some analysts note China had largely refused H200 deliveries already (a Georgetown CSET researcher cited by TechCrunch); if so, the revenue loss has already happened. At 15:20 China's foreign ministry was asked about it and only restated its principled position on US chip restrictions. (Jin10 flash 15:20:54; also BBC, TechCrunch 11-17)
Impact: for Nvidia this removes "China revenue" from the valuation entirely and leaves the bet on backlog; for China's compute chain it adds policy certainty to domestic substitution — today's A-share semiconductor strength points the same way. This brief had not covered this item before; it is a backfill and its event time is outside this hour.

Evidence: gold -2.01% at a new low for this leg of 3,764.74, silver -3.14% with 45.743 retested but held, LME tin down more than 6%, SGE gold T+D -2.46%, while the dollar sits at **103.6** (a 19-month high, Jin10 relay) and the 10-year is around **5.10%**; St. Louis Fed President Musalem says "several more rate increases" are needed before spring. In the same hour NQ was +0.53% and ES +0.21%, so equities did not fall in tandem — this is not a "sell everything" liquidity event. Bank Indonesia's surprise hike to defend the currency is the same dollar chain showing up directly in EM.
Impact: gold/silver miners and precious-metals ETFs, EM currencies and local rates are the first in line; A-share precious-metals names already led the decline.
Invalidation / confirmation: a recovery needs gold to reclaim **3,800** and then hold above **3,832.20** (the 11-16 low); if the 11-19 03:00 minutes lean dovish it will show up in the dollar first. Below today's low there is no historical reference, so position rules rather than price rules apply. FXStreet's RSI(14) is around 33, close to oversold, which raises short-term rebound risk and is another reason not to chase the short.

**B｜Crude: bearish, but with crowded shorts and casualties in the tail, don't chase; the 23:30 EIA print is the arbiter.**

Don't pre-position on the 11-19 China-Russia-US Shenzhen summit (the first trilateral ever; the agenda includes global nuclear arms reduction and Iran) before the outcome is known.
