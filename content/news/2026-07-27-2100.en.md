🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🛢️ Crude Plunges Nearly 7% as Black Sea Supply Fully Resumes**
WTI crude crashed 6.97% to $82.97, the largest single-day drop in recent memory. Russia's largest Black Sea crude terminal has officially resumed loading after days of shutdown from drone strikes and storms, with the CPC terminal also restarting simultaneously. Previously tight global supply pressure has abruptly eased, and prices have broken below the $84 key support.

**🇺🇸 US June Durable Goods Orders Miss by Wide Margin**
June durable goods orders rose just 0.3% MoM, significantly below the 2.5% consensus (prior revised to -4.0%). However, core capital goods — non-defense capital goods ex-aircraft — rose 0.9%, slightly beating the 0.8% estimate (prior revised to 1.9%), indicating resilient business investment. Ex-transportation orders rose 0.6%, below the 0.8% expected.

**🇰🇷 Korea-NVIDIA $950B AI Infrastructure Mega-Deal**
South Korean conglomerates have reached a series of cooperation agreements with global tech giants including NVIDIA, totaling $950 billion. Naver, NVIDIA, and Brookfield will invest in gigawatt-scale AI cloud infrastructure across South Korea and the US. The GAK Sejong AI Factory in Sejong City will expand to 200MW, targeting completion by 2028, utilizing NVIDIA Vera Rubin and Blackwell platforms.

**🇷🇴 Romania Expels Russian Diplomat**
Romania announced the expulsion of a Russian diplomat following a drone incursion incident, as Eastern European tensions continue to escalate.

**🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia Urges Iraq to Secure Borders**
Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry called on the Iraqi government to take necessary measures to ensure its territory is not used as a launchpad for aggression — Middle East geopolitical risks persist.

**📊 German IFO Business Climate Beats Expectations**
German July IFO Business Climate Index came in at 86.6 (vs. 86 expected, 85.6 prior), with expectations surging to 86.7 (vs. 84.8 expected), though current conditions at 86.5 slightly missed the 87.3 forecast — mixed signals for European recovery.
