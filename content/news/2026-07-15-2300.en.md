🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🎙️ Fed Chair Warsh Testifies Before Congress**

Warsh appeared at the semi-annual monetary policy hearing with several notable remarks:
- Recent inflation data does not perfectly reflect underlying inflation pressures; one-off price changes do not necessarily lead to inflation
- Expects AI to push up observable price levels over the next 12 months; AI's impact on demand is arriving faster than on supply
- Cannot guarantee no job disruption from AI in the short term, but believes AI is a long-term job creator
- Corporate capital investment contributes enormously to GDP; prefers to see companies invest rather than buy back stock
- Has fully divested pre-chair assets, converted to cash equivalents and short-term Treasuries

**💻 Tech Stocks Hit Hard**

Dell (DELL) dropped 13% intraday, the largest decline since April 2025; SK Hynix ADR fell 11%. AI hardware sector under broad pressure.

**🍎 Apple Seeking AI Chip Acquisitions**

Sources say Apple has been in discussions with bankers in recent months about potential AI chip acquisitions to strengthen its in-house server chip capabilities.

**⚔️ Iran Threatens Response to U.S. Strikes**

Iran's Army Ground Forces stated they will deliver a "clear and immediate" response at the appropriate time to the U.S. attack on a military camp in southeastern Iran. Earlier, U.S. Central Command announced it had completed a new round of strikes on Iran and resumed a maritime blockade.

EUR/GBP breaking below 0.85 amid dollar strength, with continued outflows from UK equities — sterling assets facing structural selling pressure.
