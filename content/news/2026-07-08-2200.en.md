🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**US-Iran Tensions Sharply Escalate: Trump Calls Talks "A Waste of Time," May Strike Again Tonight**

After concluding his NATO summit speech, Trump publicly stated that the previous strike was merely a "simple warning" and that the US could strike Iran again as soon as tonight. He accused Iran of always "playing games," suggesting the US may abandon the agreement and take direct military action. Trump also revealed that Iran had asked the US not to attack during Khamenei's funeral — the US agreed, but Iran subsequently attacked three vessels belonging to Saudi Arabia and Qatar.

Iran's Supreme Leader Foreign Affairs Advisor Velayati responded forcefully: any adventurous act will be met with an immediate response, and US actions will push the Middle East into flames. He also stated that the responsibility for verbally voiding the memorandum of understanding lies with President Trump.

**Hormuz Strait Tanker Traffic "Essentially Halted"**

CNN reports that as the 60-day US-Iran ceasefire teeters on the brink, tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has "essentially halted." Rystad Energy's head of geopolitical analysis noted that today's strait tanker activity was near zero. Energy stocks surged across the board: Occidental Petroleum +3%, Diamondback Energy +2.2%, Halliburton +2.1%, ConocoPhillips +1.9%.

**Apple Inks $30 Billion Deal for US-Made Broadcom Chips**

Apple and Broadcom reached an agreement for Apple to purchase US-manufactured chips worth up to $30 billion. Tim Cook publicly thanked the White House for its support, as Apple leverages long-term domestic procurement to secure tariff exemptions and stabilize its global high-end hardware supply chain. Broadcom shares rose 2.7%.

**Fed Minutes May Downplay Internal Debate, Rate Hike Expectations in Doubt**

Warsh previously compared the Fed's internal debate to a "family quarrel," signaling reluctance to let outsiders see the true policy deliberations. Historical patterns suggest market expectations of a single rate hike may be dashed.

**Nasdaq 100 Reverses to Positive**

The Nasdaq turned positive after being down 0.6%, demonstrating tech sector resilience. Broadcom led gains, with the energy sector also strengthening.
