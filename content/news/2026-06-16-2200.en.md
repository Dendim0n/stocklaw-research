🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Strait of Hormuz Expected to Fully Reopen by June 19**
Futures tracking indicates the Strait of Hormuz is expected to fully reopen by June 19. Trump's latest remarks triggered a sharp sell-off in night-session SC crude, which fell 3% intraday before narrowing losses. The strait had been closed for over 96 hours, and its reopening would significantly ease global oil transit pressures.

**2. SpaceX Surpasses Amazon to Become World's 5th Largest Company**
SpaceX (SPCX.O) surged over 10% intraday, reaching a market cap of approximately $2.8 trillion and overtaking Amazon (AMZN.O) as the world's fifth-largest company by market value. Stock options began trading the same day. Multiple institutions highlighted the AI and aerospace dual-premium narrative.

**3. ECB Chief Economist Lane: Inflation Far Above Target, Immediate Action Needed**
ECB Chief Economist Philip Lane stated that inflation remains far above target over the coming year, meaning "we need to take immediate action (monetary policy)." The hawkish remarks signal the ECB's policy trajectory heading into upcoming meetings.

**4. US Tech Stocks Broadly Lower, Chinese ADRs Under Pressure**
Major tech names declined: Tesla -1.9%, Intel -1.5%, Oracle -2.4%, ASML -1.3%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index fell 2%. SpaceX's surge created a siphoning effect, compounding profit-taking pressure across the sector.

**5. German Auto Industry Association Urges Immediate Passage of US-EU Tariff Deal**
German Auto Industry Association president Müller stated that the European Parliament has approved the US tariff deal and it must now be "passed formally without delay." A critical juncture for transatlantic trade relations.

**6. Russian Seaborne Oil Exports Hit Highest Since December**
In the four weeks to June 14, Russia's average daily seaborne crude exports reached 3.83 million barrels, the highest since last December. Russian oil supply resilience amid the Ukraine conflict is exceeding expectations, adding further supply pressure on crude prices.
