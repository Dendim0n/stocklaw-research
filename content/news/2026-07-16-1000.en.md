🧭 **Situation Assessment**

The Middle East is this hour's core variable. Trump's inclination toward expanded military action signals that the geopolitical risk premium will rise further, with crude supply-side uncertainty shifting from "potential risk" to "real threat."

Gold found support near 4033 before staging a modest rebound, reflecting the market's dual pricing of Middle East tensions and inflation uncertainty. If the Iran situation continues to deteriorate, gold could retest the 4065 previous high.

The Warsh-White House "hawk-dove divergence" deserves close attention. The Fed Chair is dissatisfied with inflation while the White House insists there is no case for rate hikes — this policy split raises doubts about Fed independence and could drive increased long-end rate volatility.

A-share pharma sector maintains its strength with Harbin Pharmaceutical hitting its 5th consecutive limit-up, but the broader market remains structurally divided. The pullback in CPO and lithium battery sectors indicates capital rotation from high-valuation tech into defensive sectors.
