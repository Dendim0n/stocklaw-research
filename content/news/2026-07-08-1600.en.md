🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🚨 US-Iran Conflict Fully Escalates**

Iran's Supreme Joint Military Command issued a stark warning: Tehran will treat any location supporting US attacks on Iran as a legitimate target. This follows US bombing of 80+ targets along Iran's coast and the revocation of oil trade waivers that cut off Tehran's core revenue source. Iran retaliated by striking 85 US military installations, with the fragile ceasefire on the verge of collapse.

The US airstrike on an Iranian school that killed nearly 200 civilians has been exposed — CNN exclusively revealed that senior commanders bypassed outdated intelligence warnings and used years-old data to make targeting decisions.

The US abruptly revoked Iran's oil sales waivers, with Vortexa data showing approximately 63 million barrels of Iranian crude stranded at sea and unsellable, further choking Tehran's export lifeline.

**📉 South Korea Stocks Plunge Toward Bear Market**

The KOSPI index plunged over 6% intraday on Wednesday, with cumulative losses exceeding 20% from its June all-time high, on the verge of entering a technical bear market. Memory chip giants SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics led the decline — the AI boom faces a "better earnings, worse price" paradox.

**🇯🇵 Japan Bond Market Sounds Alarm**

Japan's 10-year government bond yield hit a 30-year high amid a rare simultaneous rout in both yen and bonds. Institutions are fixated on the critical 3% threshold — once breached, doubts about Japan's debt servicing capacity could erupt.

**🇪🇺 Apple Loses EU Lawsuit**

Apple lost its legal challenge against the EU's designation of iOS as a "core platform service" for businesses to reach end users, further tightening Digital Markets Act regulation.

**🇫🇷 Le Pen Regains Presidential Eligibility**

Following the lifting of her ineligibility ban, Le Pen has officially launched her fourth bid for the French presidency, establishing a "dual leadership" model with party chairman Bardella.

**☁️ AWS Proposes Up to $5B Investment in Philippines**

Amazon AWS plans to invest up to $5 billion in the Philippines over the next 15 years to enhance digital infrastructure and create thousands of jobs.

**⚡ Global Nuclear Capacity Projected to Grow 44% This Decade**

A BNEF report shows global nuclear installed capacity could reach 535 GW by 2036, driven by rising power demand and aggressive reactor construction in multiple countries.

**🇺🇦 Ukraine Continues Strikes on Russian Energy Infrastructure**

Russia's Saratov refinery caught fire after a Ukrainian drone strike overnight, while two oil tankers were hit in the latest wave of attacks on Russian energy infrastructure.

The US-Iran conflict is the largest risk variable for global markets this hour — Iran's explicit inclusion of "third-party locations supporting the US" as targets signals the conflict could spill over into a broader geographic theater.

Gold holds above $4,100 with limited gains, suggesting the market has not yet fully priced in conflict expansion risk. If Iran actually strikes third-party targets, gold could quickly break above $4,150.
