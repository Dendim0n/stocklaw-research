🔥 **Key News This Hour**

1. **US-Jordan Diplomatic Confusion: Embassy Claims Aqaba Evacuation, Jordan Flatly Denies**
   The US Embassy in Jordan claimed Jordanian authorities evacuated Aqaba International Airport and seaport due to "specific and credible threats," urging US citizens to avoid the area. Jordan's government spokesperson then issued a forceful denial, stating the claim is false — both airport and port are operating normally, and no potential threats have been detected in recent hours. Jordan's Information Minister also stepped in to clarify.

2. **Bahrain Announces Interception of Iranian Missiles and Drones**
   Bahrain's Defense Force General Command stated that Iran continues to launch attacks against Bahrain, with air defense systems intercepting and destroying multiple incoming missiles and drones this morning. All Bahraini armed forces have been placed on maximum alert. This marks the first time the US-Iran conflict has publicly reached Bahraini soil.

3. **Iran Condemns US Airstrike on Darkhovin Nuclear Plant**
   Iran's Atomic Energy Organization, via Mehr News Agency, condemned the US airstrike on the under-construction Darkhovin nuclear power plant, calling it "a crime in violation of international law." The conflict is expanding from military targets to critical infrastructure.

4. **Russia Claims Control of Virne in Eastern Ukraine**
   According to RIA Novosti, Russia's Defense Ministry stated its forces have taken control of Virne in eastern Ukraine.

5. **Tianshu Zhixin Unveils Next-Gen GPU Flagship Tiangai 300**
   At WAIC 2026, Tianshu Zhixin officially launched the Tiangai 300, built on a SIMT general-purpose computing architecture targeting information, action, and exploration use cases.

6. **Hithink RoyalFlush H1 Profit Guidance: +75% to +95% YoY**
   Hithink RoyalFlush projects H1 net profit of ¥878M–¥979M, driven by active capital market trading fueling financial information services demand. Over 80% of Shenzhen-listed non-bank financial firms reported earnings growth exceeding 50%.
