## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**🛢️ US Pauses Iran Strikes, Oil Retreats**

For the first time in 14 consecutive nights, US Central Command did not announce new strikes on Iran. The pause comes after JPMorgan warned that each additional month of conflict could add $7–8 to oil prices. The signal sent WTI tumbling from above 93 to 90.89.

**🔌 Samsung-Broadcom Sign $200B AI Chip Cooperation MOU**

Samsung Electronics and Broadcom signed a memorandum of understanding covering memory chips, foundry services, and advanced packaging through 2030, with a potential value of up to $200 billion. Samsung will co-develop Broadcom's next-gen AI accelerators based on Samsung HBM technology and provide sub-2nm foundry processes and advanced packaging solutions.

**🤖 SK Hynix and Nvidia Establish Long-Term AI Memory Partnership**

SK Hynix officially announced a long-term AI memory partnership with Nvidia, locking down the HBM supply chain.

**🌪️ Typhoon Hongxia Makes Landfall: Dongguan "Four-Stop" Lockdown, Hong Kong T3 Signal**
