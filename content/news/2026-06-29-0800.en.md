🧭 **Analysis & Outlook**

The US-Iran ceasefire came fast and could evaporate just as quickly — markets have shifted from panic to "wait-and-see on Tuesday's Doha outcome," temporarily removing the biggest oil upside risk.

The Saudi Aramco crash, while not directly impacting production capacity, compounds concern around Ras Tanura as a global crude hub. Combined with Iran's "comprehensive management" declaration over the Strait of Hormuz, geopolitical premium hasn't truly evaporated.

Venezuela's Amuay shutdown adds another layer of supply-side uncertainty — if earthquake-related disruptions expand, Brent could find near-term support.

Fed's Barkin remains hawkish with 4.1% PCE elevated; market expectations for rate cuts this year are fading further. If Wosh's reform push continues, declining policy transparency could amplify market volatility.

This is a super data week — Friday's US nonfarm payrolls, plus central bank governors from the US, UK, Canada, and EU convening at the Sintra Forum. Volatility likely to ramp up in the second half of the week.

The Invesco survey signals a structural shift: global sovereign capital is losing confidence in the USD, increasing energy allocation — a leading indicator of long-term portfolio rebalancing.
