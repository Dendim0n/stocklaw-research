🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Trump says Iran requested delay of attack, agrees to cancel strikes in exchange for a deal** (10:06)

Trump posted on social media that the US is ready to confront Iran with military force unseen since WWII. However, he just received requests from Iran and other Middle Eastern countries to delay the attack. Trump agreed to cancel the strikes in exchange for a deal. This follows Iran's suspension of Hormuz Strait passage — a major de-escalation in US-Iran tensions.

**2. Japan officials: Finance Minister to announce Monday US-Japan coordinated intervention to curb yen depreciation** (09:13)

Two Japanese government officials told Reuters that Finance Minister Katayama will announce Monday that Tokyo and Washington have taken joint action in currency markets to stop yen depreciation. Sources indicated Katayama will likely emphasize that intervention is not over. Japan previously suspected of spending over $53 billion on Thursday — potentially a historic record.

**3. Visa plans to lay off 2,600 employees amid AI adoption and cost cuts** (09:45)

Visa (V.N) announced layoffs of 2,600 employees, marking another major corporate restructuring driven by AI replacing human labor. OpenAI President Brockman separately noted ChatGPT is a "great thought amplifier."

**4. Japan Kumamoto earthquake death toll rises to 38** (09:07)

Kumamoto Prefecture confirmed 38 deaths from the July 28 earthquake. A 5.0-magnitude quake struck near the east coast of Honshu this morning at 48.4km depth.

**5. Rubio: Era of "negotiation without consequences" for Iran is over** (09:48)

Secretary of State Rubio stated that under Trump, Iran faces a distinctly different US president and can no longer expect to negotiate without consequences. He also outlined a multi-step roadmap for Venezuela regarding post-Maduro reconstruction.
