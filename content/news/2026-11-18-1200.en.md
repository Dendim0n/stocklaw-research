## Assessment

**A｜Precious metals: this is the third leg down, not a new narrative — stay bearish; treat any bounce as repair only.**
Evidence: the 11:20 break of the prior low, the failed 11:45 bounce to 3,793.42, and a fresh low at 12:00 form a profile of persistent selling rather than an event gap; the ordering of platinum -3.92% / palladium -2.95% / Shanghai silver -2.24% / Shanghai gold -1.95% shows the entire metals book being reduced; over the same period EUR/USD was -0.07%, USD/JPY -0.00% and offshore CNY -0.00%, so the dollar is not participating and a "strong dollar" explanation falls short, while US futures are still higher, so a haven explanation fails too. A roughly 30% drawdown on the GLD measure marks this as a continuation of the trend since January.
Judgement: **short-term bearish; do not catch the knife.** 3,800 is only a psychological level; the only observation level with a real sample is today's low of **3,785.57**, and a recovery above **3,832.20** (the 11-16 low) would be needed to call it repaired. **Invalidation / confirmation**: if the 03:00 minutes reinforce "consecutive hikes" → next notch down; if gold recovers 3,832.20 while the dollar weakens in tandem → the deleveraging is over.

**B｜Crude: the inventory argument won, but Brent still carries a premium — neutral-to-bearish, no bet before EIA.**
Evidence: December WTI 84.909 (-1.28%) and January 83.682 (-1.14%), with the December-January spread narrowing from 1.36 to about 1.23, so near-term tightness is easing; Brent fell only 0.63% and the same-month spread actually widened to 8.87; API reported a +5.278 million barrel crude build against a Jin10 calendar EIA consensus of just +58,000 barrels.
Judgement: **bearish but with a floor; do not chase the short, do not bottom-fish** — narrowing time spreads alongside a widening Brent-WTI spread is a combination of "easy nearby supply plus an unresolved Brent risk premium", not a one-way bear turn. **Invalidation / confirmation**: a large same-direction build at 23:30 EIA → the spread gives back within a week; Cushing drawing again (API -1.691 million) → high-level range rather than a bear market; verifiable evidence of lower loadings or damaged facilities → the call is void.

**C｜AI: demand is fine; what is being priced is the discount rate and governance — neutral-to-bearish on NVDA, neutral on the compute chain.**
Evidence: earnings beat, yet the futures premium went from +1.93% to +0.09% within three and a half hours, showing the market will only pay for one day; Piper cut its target by 23%, and Muddy Waters plus the SEC probe stack up on the governance side; in Asia, Hang Seng made a new intraday low and the Shanghai Composite fell 0.40%, with only STAR 50 green and its gain narrowing from +2.4% to +0.48%.
Judgement: **neutral-to-bearish on NVDA (the governance discount outweighs the demand evidence), neutral on the compute chain (even the strongest branch is narrowing), bearish on Hong Kong and China ADRs.** **Invalidation / confirmation**: if the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index breaks **9,295.01** (the 11-17 low) tonight while NVDA trades below 155.46 → the turn weaker is confirmed; if NVDA holds 162.90 (after-hours close) and semis hold → the structure is intact.
