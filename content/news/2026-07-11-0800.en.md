🔥 **Key Overnight News**

**🛢️ US-Iran Hormuz Crisis Intensifies: Saturday Ultimatum Issued**

The dominant overnight theme. Per Axios, the Trump administration is demanding Iran publicly commit by Saturday (July 12) to keeping the Strait of Hormuz open and ceasing fire on commercial vessels. A US official bluntly warned: "If they don't make the relevant statement tomorrow, tomorrow will not be a good day for them."

CBS reports, citing senior US officials, that Iran privately told Washington the Hormuz vessel attacks were an "internal mistake" by hardline factions seeking to sabotage negotiations. Iran is expected to announce toll-free passage through the Strait.

The US simultaneously warned: no deal without clear commitments on Iran's nuclear program; the US possesses military means to disable Iran's nuclear facilities. Israel has restricted sensitive military operations in southern Lebanon at US request. Qatari representatives have flown to Iran to mediate.

**🍎 Apple Sues OpenAI for Trade Secret Theft**

Apple has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI in US court, alleging the AI company obtained confidential product information — including design blueprints, component specifications, and engineering files — through former Apple employees to develop AI hardware. Apple claims OpenAI and its hardware chief systematically stole trade secrets. This marks the first time the intensifying competition between AI giants has spilled into open litigation.

**⚡ Cuba Suffers Second Nationwide Blackout in a Week**

Cuba's power grid completely collapsed at 16:30 local time on July 10, the state power union announced. This is the second nationwide outage in a week, following a total blackout on July 6.

**🏛️ Fed July Rate Hike Probability Update**

CME FedWatch: 66.3% probability of holding rates in July, 33.7% probability of a 25bp hike. September hold probability at 31.0%.

**📊 Fitch Rating Actions**

Fitch affirmed the Netherlands at AAA (stable outlook) and Saudi Arabia at A+ (stable outlook). Fitch expects Saudi 2026 real GDP growth to slow to 0.6%.

**🌽 USDA July WASDE Report**

US 2026/27 corn ending stocks sharply reduced to 1.79 billion bushels (vs. 1.96B expected in June). Soybean exports raised by 30 million bushels. Argentina and Brazil soybean production estimates unchanged.
