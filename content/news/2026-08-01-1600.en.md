## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Indian Oil Corp: 85% of Crude Now from Spot Markets Due to Middle East Crisis**
Indian Oil Corp executives revealed that due to the Middle East crisis, approximately 85% of the company's crude oil now comes from spot markets, up from 50% previously. The company also raised August aviation fuel prices by 5 rupees per liter to 115 rupees and announced plans to invest approximately 1 trillion rupees in petrochemical capacity expansion over the next 5-6 years. Middle East supply disruptions are transmitting real cost pressures to Asian refiners.

**2. Ukraine Strikes Russian Naval Drone Depot in Crimea**
Ukraine's General Staff announced a strike on a Russian naval drone depot on the Crimean Peninsula. Zelensky earlier disclosed that Russia launched 35 missiles and 185 drones against Ukraine, admitting Ukraine lacks air defense resources and intercepted only one ballistic missile. Meanwhile, a Moscow source reiterated Russia's openness to negotiations, claiming Ukraine broke off talks.

**3. AMEC Targets 60% Coverage of High-End Semiconductor Equipment in Five Years**
AMEC Chairman and CEO Yin Zhiyao stated at the company's 22nd anniversary celebration that the goal is to cover at least 60% of high-end semiconductor equipment within five years, becoming the global leader in equipment coverage, and reaching international leadership in scale and product competitiveness by 2035. A clear signal of accelerating domestic semiconductor equipment substitution.

**4. DeepSeek-V4-Flash Official Release on Xfusion AI Lab**
Xfusion announced that DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 official version is now available on its AI Lab platform, accessible via PC for online experience. The domestic large model ecosystem continues to expand.

**5. Kazakhstan Rules Out Stopping CPC Pipeline**
Kazakhstan's Energy Ministry explicitly stated that completely stopping the Caspian Pipeline Consortium (CPC) is not under consideration. This partially alleviates market concerns about Central Asian crude export routes being affected by the Middle East situation.

## 🧭 Situation Assessment

Indian Oil Corp's surge in spot procurement from 50% to 85% is the most direct evidence yet that Middle East shipping disruptions are materially impacting global crude supply chains, with Asian buyers forced to purchase spot cargoes at premium prices.

Aviation fuel price increases passing through to consumers, combined with India's trillion-rupee petrochemical investment plan, reflect regional energy companies incorporating "crisis normalization" into long-term strategy.

The Ukraine battlefield situation is dire, with Zelensky publicly admitting air defense shortages and Russian attack intensity rising significantly. However, Moscow's simultaneous signaling of openness to negotiations suggests a complex "fight-and-talk" dynamic.

AMEC's five-year 60% coverage target for high-end semiconductor equipment, combined with Xfusion launching DeepSeek-V4-Flash, shows dual-track progress in domestic semiconductors and AI, with a clear trajectory toward self-reliance.

Kazakhstan ruling out a CPC shutdown partially offsets crude supply panic from the Hormuz Strait closure.
