## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**🛢️ Houthi Forces Strike Saudi Aramco Facilities**

Houthi forces attacked two Saudi Aramco oil facilities in the cities of Jizan and Yanbu. Saudi Aramco shares fell as much as 1.0% in early Sunday trading to 26.56 riyals. Yemen's Prime Minister stated that continued Houthi attacks on shipping and threats to Red Sea security prove the group is "executing Iran's directives."

**⚠️ Iran's IRGC Warns Israel of "Severe Consequences"**

IRGC spokesman Hossein Mokhbir warned in the early hours of July 26 that if Israel reignites war, Iran will make it "suffer severe consequences," emphasizing that any country supporting Israel in a US-Iran conflict would face the same outcome.

**🇬🇧 UK's New PM Burnham Willing to Publicly Criticize Trump**

In an interview released today, new UK Prime Minister Burnham stated he would be willing to publicly criticize President Trump if he believed it was right, stressing he will always put UK national interests first.

**🇯🇵 Takaichi Cabinet Approval Hits Record Low**

Japan's PM Takaichi cabinet approval rating has dropped to 53.7%, the lowest since the cabinet was formed, according to Kyodo News.

**🛩️ Romanian F-16 Shoots Down Unauthorized Drone**

Romania's Ministry of Defense confirmed an F-16 fighter jet shot down a drone that entered its airspace without authorization. Further details pending.

**🌍 African Development Bank: Super El Niño Threatens Multiple Nations**

The AfDB climate chief warned that super El Niño could cause $10–20 billion in economic losses for affected African countries and trigger mass migration from Sudan, Mali, Somalia, DRC, and even Nigeria. African nations need up to $50 billion in additional climate finance this year.

**🌀 Hurricane Fausto Weakens Entering Central Pacific**

The US National Hurricane Center reported Fausto has slightly weakened as it enters the Central Pacific basin.

Super El Niño's impact on Africa warrants monitoring, particularly soft commodity supply chains (cocoa, coffee), though the market's immediate focus remains on the Middle East and central bank week.
