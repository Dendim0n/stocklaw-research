🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🛢️ Houthi Forces Blockade Saudi Ports, Oil Surges**

The United Maritime Information Center warns: Houthi forces have completed preparations for attacking vessels, including deploying missiles and drones. No confirmed attacks on commercial ships in the prior 48 hours, but Iran-backed Houthis have declared a blockade of Saudi ports, directly threatening millions of barrels per day in global crude supply. WTI crude jumped over 2% intraday, China's SC crude futures surged over 5%.

**🇬🇧 UK June Inflation Slows More Than Expected**

UK June CPI fell to 2.6% (vs. 2.7% expected), core inflation held at 2.6%. The data reduces the likelihood of further BoE rate hikes, sending sterling lower.

**🇺🇸 Trump Poised to Impose New Tariffs on Dozens of Countries This Week**

Per the Financial Times, Trump may impose new tariffs on dozens of countries as soon as this week. The Wall Street Journal separately reports USTR Greer confirmed the US is preparing a new round of tariffs.

**🇺🇸 Trump Approves 30-Year Saudi Nuclear Deal**

The Trump administration has approved a 30-year nuclear cooperation agreement with Saudi Arabia, potentially paving the way for Riyadh to develop uranium enrichment capabilities. The arrangement raises controversy amid US demands for Iran to curtail its nuclear program.

**🇮🇷 US Airstrikes Fail to Suppress Iranian Missile Capability**

Despite sustained US airstrikes, Iran retains substantial ballistic missile stockpiles, and its underground weapons systems are being restored. Iran's interior minister will attend the BRICS meeting in India. Pakistan seeks $10B from the US for exchange rate stabilization after mediating Iran negotiations.

**📉 Hong Kong Tech Stocks Plunge in Afternoon Trading**

NetEase and Tencent both fell over 7%, Kuaishou -6%+, Bilibili -5%+, with Xiaomi and Alibaba also declining. SK Hynix-related storage ETF reversed from +14% at open to negative territory.

**💻 NVIDIA Vera CPU Delivered to OpenAI**

NVIDIA enters the CPU market — the Vera chip has entered mass production and been delivered to customers including OpenAI, directly challenging the Intel and AMD server ecosystem.
