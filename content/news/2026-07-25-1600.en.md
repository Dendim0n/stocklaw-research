🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🛢️ Yemeni Missile Attack on Saudi Refinery Intercepted; Oil Swings Wildly**

Greek security sources confirmed that a Greek-operated air defense system in Saudi Arabia intercepted two ballistic missiles launched from Yemen targeting the Yanbu refinery. Widespread fuel queuing was reported across Saudi Arabia following the attack. WTI briefly surged to 93.22 before tumbling back to 90.89, down over 2% on the day — signaling that markets remain cautious about pricing in actual Middle East supply disruption.

**🌪️ Typhoon Hongxia Intensifies; Dongguan Implements Full Shutdown**

Typhoon Hongxia has strengthened to typhoon level (Cat 12) and is expected to make landfall between Shenzhen and Huilai tonight or tomorrow morning at typhoon/strong typhoon strength. Dongguan has ordered a citywide shutdown (schools, work, transport, businesses). The Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge shipping channel is under temporary traffic control. China's Ministry of Natural Resources raised Guangdong's geological disaster response to Level III.

**⚔️ Middle East Heats Up: Israel Operates in Lebanon and West Bank Simultaneously**

Powerful explosions were reported in southern Lebanon's Tyre region, which Lebanese authorities attributed to Israeli military action. Meanwhile, Israeli forces conducted large-scale raids in Nablus and Tubas in the West Bank, arresting approximately 50 Palestinians in Nablus alone.

**🛡️ Russia-Ukraine Drone War Escalates; Romania Downs Second Intruding Drone**

Russia's Defense Ministry reported shooting down 328 Ukrainian drones overnight. Russian e-commerce giant Wildberries' warehouse in Yekaterinburg (~1,750 km from the border) was struck by a Ukrainian drone. Romania scrambled F-16s to shoot down a second drone violating its airspace. Russian attacks on Sumy killed three.

**🤖 Musk Backs Jensen Huang: Open-Source AI Models Should Not Be Restricted**

Jensen Huang made his debut on X with a tweet featuring NVIDIA's co-signed open letter supporting open-source AI models. Elon Musk retweeted: "Jensen is right." This follows a joint letter from 25 tech giants pressuring Trump against blanket restrictions on open-weight AI models.

**🏠 Dalian Pioneers Second-Home Provident Fund Interest Subsidy**

Dalian is offering up to 3 years of 15% interest subsidies on second-home provident fund loans. Nearly 90 Chinese cities have rolled out various housing subsidy policies this year.
