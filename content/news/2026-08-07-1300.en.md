🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. AI Server Demand Surge: 2026 Shipment Growth Revised Up to Nearly 31%**
TrendForce's latest research shows that under surging AI infrastructure demand, the combined capex of the world's top 9 cloud service providers (CSPs) is estimated to grow ~90% YoY in 2026. AI server shipment growth has been revised up to nearly 31%.

**2. CATL's Yichun Lithium Mine Confirmed Under Maintenance Shutdown**
Yifeng County Ecology and Environment Bureau officially responded: during on-site inspection, the facility was under maintenance shutdown with no ore loading, transport, or crushing operations. The company has been urged to complete relevant procedures promptly.

**3. Saudi Crude Exports to US Drop to Zero in July, Rebound Expected in August**
Saudi crude exports to the US fell to zero in July — the first zero-month since 1985. US refiners turned to Venezuela as an emergency alternative. However, US Middle East crude imports are expected to rebound in August.

**4. Goldman Sachs Questions Threats to Dollar Dominance**
Goldman Sachs believes that US support for Japan's yen-stabilizing measures is unlikely to undermine the dollar's status as the world's primary reserve currency. Japan is the largest foreign investor in the $31 trillion US Treasury market.

**5. Beijing Yizhuang Releases Token Economy Policy**
Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area released a token economy policy, distributing 100 million yuan annually in model and token vouchers, with increased support for inference-specific chip R&D.

**6. Hong Kong Stocks Edge Up at Midday**
Hang Seng Index rose 0.15%, Hang Seng Tech Index rose 0.34%, with half-day turnover reaching 141.185 billion HKD. PCB, commercial aerospace, pharma outsourcing, and storage concept sectors were active.

**7. Domestic Gold Jewelry Prices Cut by ~11 Yuan/Gram**
Multiple brands' pure gold jewelry prices fell about 11 yuan/gram from yesterday, with most brands quoting 1281-1286 yuan/gram.
