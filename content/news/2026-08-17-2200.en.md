## 🔥 Key News This Hour

### 1. Wells Fargo Investment Institute: Expects Fed to Hike 25bp This Year

Wells Fargo Investment Institute changed its previous forecast of "no rate change," now expecting the Fed to hike 25bp this year. Also expects another 25bp hike in 2027, bringing the benchmark rate to 4.00%-4.25%. This is the most significant macro expectation shift this hour.

### 2. Wells Fargo Lowers Gold Price Target but Remains Long-Term Bullish

Lowered 2026 year-end gold target from $5,300-$5,500 to $4,900-$5,100; 2027 target from $5,800-$6,000 to $5,400-$5,600. Simultaneously upgraded global energy sector from "unfavorable" to "neutral."

BofA Chief Strategist Hartnett's bullish gold thesis does not depend on Fed rate cuts, but is based on surging US fiscal pressure + continued global central bank gold accumulation as dual structural supports.
