🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**BOE Governor Bailey Issues Rare Consecutive Warnings: Stock Market Faces Sharp Correction Risk**
Following the FPC meeting, BOE Governor Bailey made intensive remarks, stating bluntly that "the expansion in equity valuations appears to go beyond AI optimism alone, and the risk of a sharp correction in equity markets remains high." He also noted that UK domestic bank leverage rules need "cleaning up," with the FPC having conducted "a particularly in-depth discussion" on leverage reform. Bailey further questioned whether issues related to market-based leverage should be addressed through banks. This string of statements reveals deep concern among UK regulators about current asset prices and financial leverage.

**Former Fed Official Bullard: Rate Hike Likely This Year, September Is Key Window**
Former St. Louis Fed President Bullard made it clear that core inflation remains persistently above the 3% internal warning line, and Fed policy credibility faces unprecedented challenges. His proposed policy path: hold steady in July, with September emerging as the critical window for a rate hike. This is among the most explicit hawkish signals recently, and combined with Bailey's warnings, expectations of a global central bank policy pivot are heating up.

**France Cuts 2026 GDP Forecast to 0.7%**
French Finance Minister Lescure stated that budget delays and the Middle East conflict are dragging on output, leading to the downward revision. "This correction takes into account a weaker-than-expected start to the year," while noting signs of improvement in inflation and consumption data.

**Trump: Russia-Ukraine Peace "Closer Than Expected"**
Following calls with leaders of both Russia and Ukraine, Trump struck an optimistic tone, claiming a peace plan is "just around the corner," while his view on Ukraine has also shifted. Israel also signaled it will hold negotiations on Lebanon in Rome next week.

**Strait of Hormuz Tensions Escalate: Saudi Plans Pipeline Bypass, Iran Seeks to Rewrite Passage Rules**
Two stories converge: Iran is attempting to rewrite Hormuz passage rules, while Saudi Arabia is secretly assessing with multiple nations an upgrade of its east-west crude pipeline to turn the Red Sea into a new global oil export gateway. WTI crude rose over 1% intraday — geopolitical risk premiums are returning.
