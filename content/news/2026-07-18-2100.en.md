## 🔥 Key Developments This Hour

**1. 🚨 Iran Officially Suspends Iran-US Memorandum of Understanding**

Iranian Deputy FM Gharibabadi confirmed Iran has ceased fulfilling the Iran-US MOU, citing US violations. Iran's Ambassador to Pakistan further condemned the US for "distorting the MOU and controlling parts of the Strait of Hormuz," stating Iran will not accept these actions. This marks the most severe diplomatic rupture since the US-Iran conflict began on June 27 — the only remaining de-escalation framework has now collapsed.

**2. 🥇 Gold Breaks $4,000 for the First Time in History**

Spot gold hit an intraday high of $4,023.72 before settling at $4,016.55, up 1.01%. Iran's MOU withdrawal was the direct catalyst, compounded by Strait of Hormuz tensions driving safe-haven inflows.

**3. 🛢️ WTI Crude Surges 3.6%**

WTI spiked from $78.47 to $82.59, closing at $82.33. Iran's statements regarding Strait of Hormuz control directly threaten the world's most critical oil transit chokepoint, with markets rapidly pricing in supply disruption premiums.

**4. 🔔 Bahrain Issues Nationwide Alarm**

Bahrain's Interior Ministry issued a nationwide alert instructing citizens to proceed to the nearest safe location. Military alert levels across Gulf states are visibly escalating as spillover from the Iran-US conflict spreads across the Middle East.

**5. ⚛️ Saudi Uranium Enrichment Deal Awaits Trump Approval**

CNN reports the Trump administration has preliminarily agreed to allow Saudi Arabia to conduct uranium enrichment without international non-proliferation safeguards. If finalized, the deal would fundamentally alter the Middle East nuclear balance; Israel has already voiced strong opposition.

**6. 🛸 Moscow Region Hit by Ukrainian Drone Strike**

A Ukrainian drone attack on a logistics center in the Moscow region caused at least 30 injuries, 7 in critical condition. This is among the largest strikes on Russian territory in the current conflict.

**7. ☢️ India's Largest Nuclear Plant Suffers Massive Data Breach**

Nearly 19,000 files — including engineering blueprints, supplier data, and personnel records — were leaked from the Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant. Experts warn this could compromise the facility's security.

US equities face a geopolitical shockwave when markets reopen Monday — VIX likely to spike, energy and defense sectors may rally counter-trend, tech under pressure.
