A verifiable de-escalation signal this week (e.g., resumed strait transit volumes, formal restart of Saudi Yanbu loading) could rapidly reprice oil and energy equities. Counter-evidence: VIX at 14.2, US equities closing essentially flat — equities are still digesting "higher for longer."
