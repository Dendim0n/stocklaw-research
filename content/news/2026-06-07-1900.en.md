🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🔴 Israel Military Issues Evacuation Warning for Tyre, Lebanon**

The Israeli military issued an evacuation warning to residents of Tyre and surrounding areas in southern Lebanon, signaling potential escalation on the Lebanon front.

**🟡 Jensen Huang Signals: Samsung Meeting Tomorrow, Possible Announcements**

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang confirmed a meeting with Samsung's vice chairman tomorrow, and hinted "we may have some announcements tomorrow." Markets watching closely for AI chip supply chain developments.

**🟡 Iran Hormuz Strait Transit Fees Exposed: $150K–$2M Per Ship**

Iranian media Fars News reported that each oil tanker transiting the Strait of Hormuz will be charged $150,000 to $2 million, with some vessels potentially paying in commodities or barter. Fees are significantly lower than at the start of hostilities but represent a major new cost for energy shipping. Iran has drafted the "Hormuz Strait Environmental Service Fee Regulations" but final rates and collection mechanisms are still pending.

**🟡 Iran War 100-Day Poll: Only 16% of Voters Say US Is Winning**

Sunday marks 100 days since the US-Israel war on Iran began. Polls show only 16% of voters believe the US is winning, with a majority (including 33% of Republicans) saying the war harms American interests. Trump reposted a White House statement saying "jobs data don't lie."

**🟡 Russia-Ukraine: Non-Public Contacts Maintained**

Russian presidential aide Ushakov stated that Russia and Ukraine maintain both public and non-public contacts. Moscow and Kyiv are in "close touch."

**⚪ CITIC Securities: Tech Stocks in Short-Term Correction, Watch New Energy Value**

CITIC Securities research note says the short-term US tech correction stems from monetary policy expectation recalibration and crowded positioning. The AI bull logic is unlikely to reverse. Recommends attention to value in new energy, chemicals, non-ferrous metals, and power equipment.

**⚪ Lingyi iTech Passes HKEX Listing Hearing**
