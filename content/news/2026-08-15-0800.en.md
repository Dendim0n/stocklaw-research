🔥 **Key Overnight News**

**1. Trump Threatens to Declare Strait of Hormuz as "US Territory"**

Trump called the blockade against Iran "unstoppable" and a "wall of steel," stating the US would soon declare the Strait of Hormuz as US territory. Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister responded that the opening or closing of the strait can only be decided by Iran and that Iran would not be intimidated by US threats.

**2. US-Iran Ceasefire Expires Monday, Negotiations Stalled**

According to Politico, the one-month US-Iran ceasefire agreement expires Monday. White House officials said as of Friday afternoon, negotiations remain "stalled" with no extension plans. Iran's Foreign Minister stated no decision has been made on resuming talks with the US.

**3. ADNOC Tanker Attacked in Strait of Hormuz**

UAE's ADNOC confirmed one of its vessels was attacked while transiting the Strait of Hormuz on August 14. No injuries reported, situation under control.

**4. Jane Street Lost ~$15B in July**

According to the Financial Times, quant giant Jane Street recorded approximately $15B in losses in July, its first monthly loss since 2016. The firm stated 2026 revenue has declined ~25% from its June peak, and it has closed most risk positions in areas that incurred July losses, adopting a more cautious approach to risk.

**5. Anthropic Q2 Revenue Exceeds $11.5B, Up 14x YoY**

According to Bloomberg, Anthropic's Q2 preliminary revenue exceeded $11.5B, up at least 14x from $787M in the same period last year, surpassing Q1's $4.73B, while achieving positive adjusted operating profit.

**6. 13F Filings Roundup**

**7. US July Retail Sales Post Largest Drop in Over a Year**

Just as inflation cooled, US consumer spending unexpectedly weakened with retail sales posting their largest decline in over a year, making the Fed's policy choices more delicate and reducing the probability of multiple rate hikes this year.

**8. BOJ May Hike Rates as Soon as September**

Sources indicate the Bank of Japan may hike rates as soon as September and is considering accelerating its tightening pace. The market has pushed the probability of a September hike to approximately 80%.
