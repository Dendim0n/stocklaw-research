🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Iranian Militia Claims Control of Strait of Hormuz**

Iran's Fars News Agency reported that Hossein Taeb, head of Iran's militia organization, stated the Strait of Hormuz is "under Iran's control and management." Brent crude dropped below 1% following the statement.

**2. Ukraine Grain Exports Plunge 76%**

Ukrainian Railways reported that due to the ongoing blockade of major Black Sea ports, grain export shipments in August fell 76% year-on-year to 201,700 metric tons. On the same day, a passenger train in Odesa Oblast was hit by a drone strike, killing two crew members.

**3. UOB: US Inflation May Have Peaked in Q2**

Alvin Liew, senior economist at UOB Singapore, stated that US inflation likely peaked in Q2, but energy price uncertainty remains and inflation could rebound. Lower energy prices help short-term, but geopolitical risks remain a variable.

**4. Foreign Investment in Japan's Chip Industry Reaches ¥6 Trillion**

Foreign companies have invested approximately ¥6 trillion (~$37B) in Japan's semiconductor industry. Sony Group and TSMC's recent announcement of an image sensor factory further expanded this figure.

**5. UK Economy Withstands Energy Shock**

UK Q2 GDP grew 0.4%. The National Institute of Economic and Social Research noted the economy has weathered recent energy price shocks better than many feared, though the outlook remains challenging.

**6. Extreme Heat Could Cost EU €180 Billion**

Triodos Bank, based in the Netherlands, assessed that this summer's extreme heat will cost EU nations approximately €180 billion in economic losses, roughly 1% of 2025 EU GDP.

**7. Turkey Central Bank Governor Speaks**

Turkey's central bank governor pledged to maintain a tight policy stance and use all available tools to fight inflation. Services inflation continues to trend down, but disinflation is slowing and domestic demand weakness is increasingly evident.
