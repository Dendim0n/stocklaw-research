🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. NVIDIA-OpenAI-SB Energy Mega AI Infrastructure Alliance**

NVIDIA announced a $1.5B investment in SoftBank's SB Energy, locking in the Ports-Pike tech campus in Ohio as a dedicated AI compute hub. Initial capacity 4.25 IT-GW with options for an additional 3.75 IT-GW, totaling 8 IT-GW. OpenAI committed to massive deployment of NVIDIA AI infrastructure through 2030, corresponding to approximately $600B in compute business, with SB Energy operating under a 20-year lease. Each generation of systems may be equipped with ~1.5M GPUs. Groq simultaneously raised $350M at a $3.5B valuation.

**2. Iran Shifts to "Full Offensive" Posture**

Per Reuters, senior Iranian officials announced a policy shift from defensive to "full offensive," setting a multi-week deadline for the US to fully implement a memorandum of understanding. If diplomacy fails, Iran threatens to escalate the Strait of Hormuz situation. The IRGC stated it will decisively strike against any threat or aggression. Trump separately threatened Oman, the mediator, with bombing if it obstructs the US-Iran peace deal. Middle East geopolitical risk has materially increased.

**3. US August NY Fed Manufacturing Beats Expectations**

NY Fed manufacturing index came in at 20.6, well above the expected 11 and prior 15.6. However, components showed divergence: new orders 17.3 (prior 22.2), employment 9.3 (prior 11.4), prices received 22.7 (prior 27.6). Still in expansion territory but with marginally decelerating momentum.

**4. Canada July CPI Beats Expectations**

CPI YoY 3.0% vs expected 2.9% and prior 2.8%; MoM 0.5% vs expected 0.4%. Core CPI YoY rose to 2.3%, MoM 0.2%. Inflation stickiness is evident, potentially slowing the BoC's rate cut pace.

**5. European Bond Yields Surge Collectively**

French 10Y yield rose to 4.05% (highest since June 2009), 30Y to 4.86% (highest since September 2008); German 10Y to 3.21% (highest since May 2011); Italian 30Y to 4.82% (highest since November 2023). Euro area bond sell-off pressure continues to build.

**6. Uber Partners with Zipline for Drone Delivery**

Uber CEO announced a partnership with drone startup Zipline. Uber Eats will begin delivery by year-end, targeting 1 million daily drone deliveries by end of 2029.

**7. Raytheon Secures 7-Year Tomahawk Contract with US Navy**

RTX signed a seven-year Tomahawk missile procurement contract with the US Navy, continuing the steady flow of defense orders.

🧭 **Market Assessment**

The NVIDIA-OpenAI-SB Energy mega alliance is the biggest variable this hour. A $600B compute deployment scale signals that AI capex is far from peaking, further deepening NVIDIA's competitive moat.

Iran's shift from defensive to full offensive posture is a serious signal — the Strait of Hormuz handles ~20% of global oil transport. If diplomacy collapses, oil prices could rapidly break out of the current range, and gold's risk premium would also strengthen further.

European bond yields hitting multi-year or even multi-decade highs reflect market repricing of the ECB rate cut path. Inflation stickiness combined with fiscal expansion expectations is pushing term premiums higher — watch for euro bond volatility spilling into risk assets.

The consensus for the Fed to hold in September is now shared by 90% of economists surveyed by Reuters. The combination of a weaker dollar and rising Treasury yields suggests the market is digesting "higher for longer" with delayed rate cuts — persistent pressure on growth stock valuations.

US stock futures are lower across the board, and with a dense calendar of data and earnings this week, short-term volatility risk is skewed to the upside.
