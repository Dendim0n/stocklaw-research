**Trump Sets Generic Drug Tariff Ladder: 100% by 2028 → 200% by 2029**

Trump announced a stepped tariff regime for companies that don't move generic drug production to the US — rising to 100% by 2028 and doubling to 200% a year later. This weaponizes tariffs to force pharmaceutical supply chain reshoring.

Despite Japan's authorities spending over ¥11 trillion on intervention, the yen has broken below 163 to a near 40-year low. The massive US-Japan rate gap and geopolitical headwinds are fueling doubts about the effectiveness of further intervention.
