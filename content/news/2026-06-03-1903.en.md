🧭 **Situation Assessment**

The Middle East situation is evolving from bilateral conflict to a regional crisis — Iran's strikes on Bahrain and Kuwait represent a critical inflection point as hostilities spread to the heart of Gulf oil-producing nations.

Gulf refining capacity has plunged 3M bpd, and with Hormuz Strait reopening estimated at a minimum of 6 months, crude supply disruption risks are transitioning from "event-driven" to "structurally tighter."

The IEA's signal that it may coordinate another strategic reserve release is itself an endorsement of the severity of the supply situation.

Gold surpassing US Treasuries as the world's top reserve asset marks "de-dollarization" shifting from narrative to data-supported reality, providing long-term support for gold prices.

Trump oscillates between aggression and diplomacy — berating Netanyahu while floating a meeting with Iran's leader. Markets may price in short-term peace-talk optimism, but substantive progress remains the key variable.

The ECB and BOJ turning hawkish in tandem is reshaping global rate differentials, warranting vigilance on carry trade unwinding risks.
