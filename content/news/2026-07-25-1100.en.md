🔥 **Key Headlines This Hour**

**Middle East Powder Keg: Saudi Oil Facilities Targeted, US Navy Opens Fire in Gulf of Oman**

Yemen's Houthi forces struck Saudi Arabia's Jizan and Yanbu with missiles and drones, targeting Aramco oil facilities. Yanbu briefly triggered emergency alerts. Meanwhile, the US Navy fired on a merchant vessel attempting to breach the Iranian port blockade in the Gulf of Oman, disabling the ship. Both the Strait of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb are now under simultaneous pressure.

**Trump Sends Mixed Signals: Iran's Drones "Largely Gone," But Not Ready for a Deal**

Trump stated the Iran situation is "going very well," claimed Iran's drones are "largely gone," while revealing Iran is in contact but "not ready to make a deal yet." He also announced Israeli PM Netanyahu will visit Washington next week.

**WTI Wild Ride: Spikes to 93, Then Crashes to 88**

Crude briefly surged to 93.22 on the Saudi attack news, then plunged to 88.11 before settling at 90.89, down over 2%. The market's reaction to strikes on Saudi oil infrastructure follows a classic "buy the rumor, sell the fact" pattern, amplified by Trump's conciliatory signals prompting long liquidation.

**Hurricane Genevieve to Strengthen into Major Hurricane**

The US National Hurricane Center warns Genevieve will intensify into a major hurricane in the coming days. Watch for potential impacts on Gulf of Mexico crude production and refining operations.

**South Korean President Lee Meets AI's Big Four**

Lee Jae-myung held separate meetings with the CEOs of Anthropic, OpenAI, NVIDIA, and Broadcom to discuss AI-era cooperation. All four leaders view South Korea as a critical partner.

**Trip.com Fined by China's Market Regulator**

Trip.com issued a statement accepting the penalty from the State Administration for Market Regulation, pledging full compliance with regulatory requirements.
