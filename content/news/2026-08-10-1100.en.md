🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. BOJ Hawkish Signals Intensify**
The Bank of Japan's July summary of opinions released strong hawkish signals. Committee members noted that inflation upside risks can no longer be ignored, proposing to break from the established pace and adopt flexible, dynamic rate hikes. The pace of future rate hikes may exceed expectations, putting pressure on yen carry trades.

**2. Gold Bullish Bets Warming Up**
Weak NFP, falling 10-year Treasury yields, a weaker dollar — plus an unexpected catalyst: Chinese retail investors are scooping up domestic gold ETFs. Gold bullish bets are rising. Spot gold hit an intraday high of 4352 before pulling back to around 4320, with short-term long-short battles intensifying.

**3. Sony and TSMC Invest ¥1 Trillion in Kumamoto Image Sensors**
Sony Group and TSMC plan to begin mass production of next-generation image sensor semiconductors in Kumamoto, Japan as early as 2029. The joint venture will be majority-owned by Sony, as semiconductor capacity expansion continues to accelerate.

**4. DDR5 Memory Prices Surge Back to 2007 Levels**
Driven by sustained AI demand, memory prices on a per-unit-capacity basis have risen back to near-2007 levels, effectively erasing nearly 20 years of price declines. The upstream storage price trend puts pressure on semiconductor supply chain costs.

**5. DeepSeek-V4-Flash Tops Global AI Model Usage**
Chinese AI model usage has led for 15 consecutive weeks. Last week, total global AI model calls reached 69 trillion tokens, up 21.48% week-over-week, with DeepSeek-V4-Flash formal release taking the top spot.

**6. Qwen Open Platform Launched**
The platform is now open to ecosystem partners and developers, supporting service integration across mobile, PC, and AI glasses. The first batch covers logistics, real estate, local services, finance, automotive, and more than a dozen other domains.
