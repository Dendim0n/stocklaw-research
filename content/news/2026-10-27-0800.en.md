## 4. Judgements and counter-evidence

**Judgement 1: The AI question has shifted from "is there revenue?" to "can cash flow cover committed spending?" — and every new piece of evidence this week points to the latter.** This is the most important narrative switch of the past three days.

Evidence: (1) Anthropic's operating side has turned — Q2 operating profit $974m (+8%), H1 operating cash flow **+$3.9bn**, compute cost/revenue from 251% to 48%, net revenue retention 900%, **no customer above 10% of revenue**; of the $80.7bn net loss, $72.4bn is fair-value adjustment, **not cash outflow**. (2) But its long-term compute commitments are about **$637bn ($780–810bn including estimates)** — Broadcom lease $161.2bn, SpaceXAI $120.5bn, Google $114.5bn, Amazon $110bn. (3) The funding mix is changing: Meta raising $35bn of equity plus a $60bn ATM, Broadcom/Blackstone/BofA/Citi/MS structuring a $63bn SPV, SpaceX's $40bn Apollo lease (bonds up to 8.75%), and **US high-grade issuance heading for a record $1.76tn**. (4) Price dispersion matches: names with existing cash flow rose (NVDA +2%, MU +3%, AVGO +2.8%, SK Hynix +4%) while those dependent on outside financing fell (CRWV −4%, NBIS −4.7%, IREN −2.8%).

Counter-evidence (must be stated): (1) Microsoft offered buyouts to 60,000 staff and Meta raised equity while *raising* the capex floor by another $5bn — **the buildout has not stopped**; (2) HSBC cutting equities to neutral and Morgan Stanley's 10–15% semi downside are **opinions, not data**; (3) the real leverage inside SPV structures sits off balance sheet, and **how much of the $63bn is equity versus debt was not disclosed**; (4) Anthropic's $780–810bn is a total that *includes estimates*, not signed cash obligations — usable to size the number, not to compute solvency.

Implications and invalidation: keep pricing heavy-capex AI on **commitment delivery**, not P/E. Watch the updated Anthropic filing (~two weeks, 21:37) for a price range, SK Hynix's 10-28 results and shareholder-return plan, and the **tranching** of the next SPV. If a second and third similar SPV appear within November with widening spreads, the financing market is pricing AI capacity rather than companies expanding their own balance sheets — that would be the precursor to a credit event. If this SPV remains a one-off, it is a structural exception.

**Judgement 2: Marginal pricing power in crude has shifted back to the "de-escalation" side, but not one physical indicator has improved.** Bias: range-bound with downside, do not chase rallies.
