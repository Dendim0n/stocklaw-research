## 🔥 Key Headlines This Hour

**🚨 Trump Announces Resumption of Iran Blockade; Oil Surges 5%**

Trump declared the resumption of blockade measures against Iran, sending crude prices sharply higher. WTI broke above $75/bbl with intraday gains exceeding 5%; Brent surged 5% to $79.74/bbl. Chinese crude futures followed suit, with SC crude +2.21%, bitumen +3.13%, and fuel oil +2.51%.

**🛥️ US Military Strikes Iran Port with Maritime Drones in Historic First**

US Central Command confirmed that forces used multiple one-way attack unmanned surface vessels to successfully strike a submarine and vessel repair facility at Iran's Bandar Abbas naval base. Three "Pirate" USVs struck the port — the first combat use of maritime drones by US forces — aimed at degrading Iran's ability to continue attacking commercial shipping.

**🛡️ Ten European Nations Form Anti-Ballistic Missile Coalition with Ukraine**

Leaders from Denmark, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Ukraine, and the UK reached agreement to establish an integrated anti-ballistic missile coalition with Ukraine. Germany's Foreign Minister also called for recalibrating security, defense, and deterrence measures on NATO's eastern and northern fronts against Russia.

**🛢️ Russian Refinery Halts After Drone Strike; Europe Rushes to Buy Russian Gas**

A Ukrainian drone attack forced Russia's Syzran refinery to suspend production. Simultaneously, European companies are accelerating purchases of Russian natural gas ahead of an impending ban, pushing European gas prices to their highest since May 20.

**🇮🇶 Iraqi PM Arrives in Washington; Gulf Tensions Escalate**

Iraq's Prime Minister has arrived in Washington for talks with US officials. DP World plans to build a new port on the UAE's eastern coast to bypass the Strait of Hormuz. This follows Trump's earlier threat to take control of the Strait and demand massive compensation, which Iran's military forcefully rejected.

**📉 Gold Crashes 2.5%, Briefly Breaks Below $4,000**

Spot gold plunged $105 intraday, hitting a low of $4,000.36/oz. Precious metals were under broad pressure, with Shanghai gold futures down 2% and silver down 2.11%. A stronger dollar and rotation of safe-haven flows into energy markets were the primary drags.

**⚖️ ~12 States to Sue to Block Paramount-Warner Bros Merger**

According to sources, approximately a dozen states plan to file a federal antitrust lawsuit on Monday to block Paramount-SkyDance's acquisition of Warner Bros Discovery.
