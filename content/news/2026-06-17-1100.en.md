🔥 **Key News This Hour**

【1】Pan Gongsheng delivers sweeping policy agenda at Lujiazui Forum
PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng announced multiple policy measures: creating an offshore central bank repo facility allowing foreign central banks to obtain liquidity via Chinese government bond repos; launching an onshore-offshore RMB forex trading pilot in Shanghai FTZ with authorization for six major banks; researching a macro-prudential tool for non-bank liquidity support under specific stress scenarios; pushing for increased long-term capital allocation to equities and bonds; and refining the short-end interest rate mechanism by adjusting the overnight repo corridor to ±25bp around the 7-day reverse repo rate. The "Shanghai International Financial Center Offshore Finance Action Plan" was officially released simultaneously.

【2】NFRA Chairman Ding Xiangqun speaks at Lujiazui
National Financial Regulatory Administration Chairman Ding Xiangqun stated that financial resources should be channeled toward emerging industries and future industries, with strict enforcement against disorderly competition and financial illegal activities, and firm implementation of the insurance industry's "reported-to-executed" consistency policy.

【3】SpaceX surges 49% in three days post-IPO, surpasses Amazon
SpaceX continued its meteoric rally on the third day of trading, with market cap breaking into the global top five. Limited float amplified gains while the Cursor acquisition deal shifted market focus toward Musk's AI ambitions.

【4】Third Iranian oil tanker departs blockade line
According to Tanker Trackers, a third NITC (National Iranian Tanker Company) tanker has left the blockade line carrying 1 million barrels of crude. Combined with the 680 million barrels awaiting deployment, signals of Iran's oil export unblocking continue to strengthen.

【5】WTI crude breaks below $76
Under pressure from returning Iranian supply expectations, WTI crude fell to $76/barrel, down 0.92% intraday.
