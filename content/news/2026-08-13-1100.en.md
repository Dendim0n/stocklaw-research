## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. US Dollar Index DXY Breaks Above 100**

The dollar index broke above 100, up 0.03% intraday. This marks continued stabilization of the USD recently, pressuring dollar-denominated commodities like gold and crude. Combined with rising JGB yields, Asia-Pacific FX market dynamics warrant attention.

**2. Japan 30Y Bond Yield Hits 4.000%**

Japan's 30-year bond yield rose 1bp to 4.000%, with the 10Y climbing to 2.855%. The persistent rise in JGB yields reflects market expectations for BOJ policy normalization, potentially triggering capital flows back to Japan from emerging markets.

**3. China Carbon Market Cumulative Volume Exceeds 9.3 Billion Tons**

The Ministry of Ecology and Environment announced at the "15th Five-Year Plan" briefing that the national carbon emissions trading market has surpassed 9.3 billion tons in cumulative volume as of end-July. A new air quality improvement action plan is being formulated, with plans for a more precise and efficient modern ecological supervision system.

**4. Shanghai Issues Computing Vouchers to Support Private Enterprise**

Shanghai released an action plan to promote private investment, introducing "computing vouchers," "model vouchers," and "data vouchers" to reduce costs of digital factors including computing power, models, and datasets. The plan also supports private enterprises participating in national-level projects including railways, nuclear power, and hydropower.

**5. Capital Economics: Korea Semiconductor Boom May Cool Within Two Years**

Capital Economics economist Marcel Thieliant noted that while Korea's GDP growth faces upside risk in the near term, the semiconductor-driven economic boom could lose momentum within two years. A slowdown in US AI investment growth would be the primary drag factor.
