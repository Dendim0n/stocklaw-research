**1. US Senate Passes Continuing Resolution to Avoid Government Shutdown**

The Senate passed a continuing resolution with an overwhelming 90-vote majority, funding federal agencies through December 11 and averting a shutdown before the midterm elections.

Ukrainian forces attacked Russia's Ilsky and Syzran oil refineries, causing fires. The fire at the Ilsky refinery in Krasnodar region has been extinguished.

Treasury Secretary Bessent stated in an August 6 NBC interview that the Strait of Hormuz will never return to its former state, but emphasized this is not the favorable outcome for Iran that Tehran portrays, suggesting a shift in US strategic assessment.
