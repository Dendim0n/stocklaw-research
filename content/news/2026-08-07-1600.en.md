## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. SK Hynix Commits $38.4B to Chip Expansion in South Korea**

SK Hynix announced a $38.4 billion investment to expand chip operations in South Korea. The plan includes 19.1 trillion KRW ($13.47B) for the M17 fab in Cheongju and 35.2 trillion KRW for phase-two fab construction in Yongin, with completion targeted by 2031. The Yongin facility will produce HBM and next-generation DRAM. The company also declared a quarterly cash dividend of 375 KRW per share. This marks one of the largest single semiconductor investments in recent memory, signaling strong HBM capacity expansion.

**3. UAE Hits Record 4.1M Barrels/Day After Exiting OPEC**

Just three months after leaving OPEC, Abu Dhabi National Oil Company is expanding capacity at a stunning pace, with the 5 million bpd production target moved up to 2027. Saudi Arabia has been forced to cut prices in response. Meanwhile, Kpler data shows only 6 oil tankers passed through the Strait of Hormuz this week, indicating severe shipping disruption. Supply-side expansion and transport-side bottlenecks are creating a tug-of-war.
