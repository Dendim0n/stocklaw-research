## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**Gold Bulls Hit Hard: WGC Sharply Revises Down Q1 Central Bank Purchases**

The World Gold Association has significantly revised down Q1 global central bank gold purchases, throwing cold water on the "ultimate moat" narrative that gold bulls have been leaning on. Combined with technical rejection at the $4,100 level, gold has tumbled over $60 from its intraday high.

**Deutsche Bank: Nasdaq Enters Correction, Fed's Vague Signals Trigger Sell-Off**

Deutsche Bank notes that the Fed's decision to hold rates steady, coupled with Chairman Warsh's limited forward guidance, triggered a sharp steepening of the Treasury yield curve, pushing the 30-year yield to 5.20%. Both the S&P 500 and Nasdaq are under pressure, with the Nasdaq officially entering correction territory.

**BOE Decision Tonight, US PCE and GDP to Follow**

The Bank of England announces its rate decision at 19:00 CST, with markets expecting a fifth consecutive hold. The key focus is on whether dissenting votes emerge. US Q2 GDP advance and June core PCE follow shortly after, providing critical inputs for the Fed's next move.

Despite ongoing tensions, shipping activity through the Strait of Hormuz has increased in recent days, with the US Navy reportedly escorting tankers through the waterway. A Qatari LNG carrier transited the strait for the first time in three weeks, signaling a potential easing of the energy transport deadlock.

**India's Central Bank Sells ~$7 Billion to Defend the Rupee**

The Reserve Bank of India reportedly sold approximately $7 billion on Friday, its largest direct intervention in months, operating simultaneously in both onshore and offshore markets.

## 🧭 Situation Assessment

Gold faces dual pressure in the near term from the central bank buying narrative revision and technical rejection at $4,100, though geopolitical risks (Russia-Ukraine escalation, Middle East) still provide a floor — watch the $4,000 psychological level.

The Fed's "let the market speak for itself" strategy is being met with a vote of no confidence — spiking bond yields and the equity sell-off are themselves a warning signal, and a hotter-than-expected PCE tonight would amplify this feedback loop.

The BOE decision itself is a non-event on the surface, but the real focus is on whether any members shift toward supporting rate hikes or signal quantitative tightening — a hawkish surprise could ripple through GBP/USD and indirectly impact gold and risk assets.

The resumption of Strait of Hormuz shipping is a positive signal, but the Trump administration's strategic dilemma in the Middle East remains far from resolved — the Iran situation continues to pose upside risk for oil prices.

On the China front, the Party symposium signals policy direction, and combined with the AI hardware price surge, the tech self-sufficiency theme may find new catalysts.
