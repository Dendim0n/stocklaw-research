## 4. Assessment

**Crude: marginal pricing has shifted from geopolitical premium to product tightness plus demand destruction; do not chase the short.** The evidence is three same-source observations this hour: USOIL is down 2.38% on the day but only +0.06% this hour, having dipped to 100.050 and recovered; crude did not react to the coalition's 11:46 offensive strikes; and Chinese bitumen, fuel oil and methanol rose 3-5% on the same day crude fell. Together they say the tight link is in refined products and transport, not in crude itself. Implication: neutral on upstream energy (neither short nor long), constructive on refining and tanker rates. Falsification and confirmation: if bitumen and fuel oil give back 5% and crude breaks 100.05 again, today's product strength was restocking and the refining thesis downgrades; if verifiable loading disruption appears at Hormuz, crude returns above 103 and this view is void.

**Gold: mildly constructive, but this move is safe-haven pricing, not miner earnings pricing.** Spot gold 4,007.38 (+0.24%), intraday high 4,016.59 a new high for the session, with COMEX December 4,022.98 (+0.38%) and silver 54.103 (+0.36%) moving in step. Direction is the point: crude fell 2.4% and USD/JPY was flat, yet gold made a new high - that requires independent haven buying. But A-share gold miners fell (Chifeng Gold leading losses, Zijin with 718m of main-force outflow), so the new high did not transmit to miners - a crowding signal, not a fundamental one. Implication: the holding case stands, but "gold new high therefore chase miners" is the wrong path. The genuine gap is the 10-year real yield and gold ETF flows, neither available in this window, hence only a weak signal. Falsification: if gold falls back below 3,978 (the intraday low) with crude falling too, today's haven bid was a head fake.

**Asian equities: the selloff is over and the rotation continues, but breadth reversal on shrinking volume is not new money.** ChiNext narrowed from -3.09% (10:16) to -1.65%, and more than 3,700 decliners turned into more than 2,400 advancers, while turnover shrank by 61.9bn - selling pressure has genuinely exhausted, but no incremental buying is visible. At the index level, weights and dividend names are holding it up while small caps and AI hardware de-rate. Two readings: first, the pressure on AI hardware comes from discount rates and crowding, not a demand collapse (Cambricon's Q3 net profit +147.74%, and it still saw 333m of main-force inflows today); second, whether money moving from optical modules into oil and banks is a global rotation or a local one can only be tested tonight. Strategy: do not chase an AI hardware bounce; wait for the semis-versus-Nasdaq relative move after the 21:30 open. Falsification: if semis fall clearly more than the Nasdaq, this is global deleveraging and the local-rotation call must be withdrawn; if the Nasdaq and semis rise together while energy follows crude lower, the rotation is over and risk appetite is repairing.
