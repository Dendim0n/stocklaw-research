**1. Houthi Forces Claim Attack on Saudi Aramco Refinery**

Yemen's Houthi rebels announced a drone strike on Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery, claiming it "successfully hit the target" in retaliation for Saudi drone operations over Yemen's Saada province. Middle East supply-side risk resurfaces, potentially supporting short-term oil prices.

Chinese GPU maker Moore Threads released its semi-annual report: H1 2026 revenue reached 1.736 billion yuan, up 147.42% YoY, already exceeding full-year 2025 figures. The S5000 intelligent computing cluster achieved scaled sales, signaling accelerated domestic compute substitution.
