**🌪️ Typhoon Bavi Approaches, Multiple SE Coast Provinces Activate Emergency Response**

Typhoon Bavi (Category 9, severe typhoon level, max sustained winds of 14 on the Beaufort scale) is expected to make landfall between Sanmen and Cangnan in Zhejiang Province early on July 12. China's State Flood Control Headquarters has activated Level 4 emergency response for flood and typhoon prevention across Shanghai, Jiangsu, Anhui, Jiangxi, and Sichuan. The Ministry of Emergency Management has also activated Level 4 geological disaster emergency response for Tianjin, Liaoning, and Jilin.
