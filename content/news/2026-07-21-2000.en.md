🔥 **Key Headlines This Hour**

**Middle East Tensions Continue to Escalate**

Kuwait's power and desalination plants were struck by Iran for the fourth consecutive day, causing fires. Meanwhile, another tanker was attacked in the Strait of Hormuz, and Kazakhstan announced it will suspend crude shipments to the Black Sea — tanker companies are refusing to sail due to safety concerns. With both major energy arteries under pressure simultaneously, WTI surged over 1.7% intraday.

**US-Iran Standoff: Ceasefire vs. Freedom of Navigation**

Iran has proactively proposed a 10-day ceasefire, but the US insists on finalizing freedom of navigation terms first. US troop reinforcements, Houthi Red Sea blockade, and ceasefire talks all emerged on the same day — bulls and bears are shouting bids on every headline, but the underlying trend has yet to shift.

Despite the Middle East conflict highlighting supply vulnerability, Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) is pushing forward with its $6.2 billion Umm Shaif gas cap development project, betting that long-term natural gas demand will remain robust.

🧭 **Market Assessment**
