Basis: three independent strands point the same way — turnover CNY 26.9bn lighter rather than heavier; a 2.6 percentage point gap between IH −0.25% and IC/IM −2.9%; and a financial split where insurers rise, brokers fall and banks make record highs. Implication: the trade-off is "no knife-catching, no shorting, only switching" — high-capex, high-multiple compute hardware without pricing power stays under pressure near term; segments with long-term contracts and order visibility belong on a watch list rather than in the bin; high-dividend banks are a tactical position only, because their record highs came from money switching duration, not from earnings upgrades.
