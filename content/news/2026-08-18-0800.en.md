## 🧭 Assessment

The US-Iran conflict is the biggest risk variable. With the ceasefire expired, neither side shows signs of backing down. Iran has shifted to a "full offensive" posture, the Strait of Hormuz remains closed, and oil is biased to the upside.

Rising oil prices plus sticky inflation have pushed the probability of the Fed holding rates in September to 65%, with even a 35% chance of a 25bps hike. Extreme rate expectations are pressuring growth stock valuations.

Surging Treasury yields are another concern. The 30-year is near 20-year highs, top three holders are all reducing, and financing pressure is mounting under $2 trillion deficit expectations. Gold ETFs are seeing contrarian inflows, with Bank of America recommending long gold as a fiscal risk hedge.

China's July data was weak across the board, with domestic demand softening notably. The 0.6% retail sales growth is concerning, raising expectations for additional policy support.

On the AI front, Anthropic's annualized revenue exceeding $65B and Nasdaq's trading hours expansion show the AI industry is still accelerating. However, the ~$3 trillion in off-balance-sheet AI commitments from 9 tech giants means the true scale of capex warrants attention.

Short-term focus: whether the US-Iran situation deteriorates further, today's UK employment data and Eurozone ZEW sentiment index.
