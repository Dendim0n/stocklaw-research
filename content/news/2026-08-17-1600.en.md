## 🔥 Key News This Hour

### 1. China's July Economic Data Broadly Misses Expectations

July key data released at 15:00 came in weak across the board:
- Retail sales YoY +0.6% (expected 1.5%, prior 1.0%) — consumption sharply slowing
- Industrial production YoY +4.5% (expected 4.8%, prior 5.3%)
- Fixed asset investment YTD YoY -6.7% (expected -6.0%) — investment decline deepening
- Urban surveyed unemployment 5.2% (prior 5.0%) — employment pressure rising

NBS stated the next phase requires stronger counter-cyclical adjustments and efforts to expand domestic demand. Lower-tier market consumption is improving, but the "strong supply, weak demand" structural mismatch remains prominent.

### 2. Iran Makes Intensive Statements on Hormuz Strait

Foreign Ministry spokesperson Baghaei reaffirmed Iran's "sovereign rights" over the Hormuz Strait, stating the US violated the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding and no negotiations have commenced. IRGC political advisor said the strait will reopen only after the US fulfills its commitments. Mediators are Pakistan and Qatar; negotiations with Oman are ongoing.

### 3. Ukrainian Drone Strikes Russian Astrakhan Industrial Facilities

According to Interfax, Ukrainian drones struck industrial facilities in Russia's Astrakhan region, near the Caspian Sea.

### 4. Ant Group CEO: Smart Agent Commerce to Explode in 6-12 Months

At the Alipay AI Ecosystem Conference, Ant Group CEO Han Xinyi said smart agent commerce is on the verge of explosive growth. Huawei's Xiaoyi, vivo's Blue Heart, and Honor's YOYO are integrating with Alipay's Abao, enabling smartphone AI assistants to access Alipay's service agent capabilities.

### 5. Trump Directs Pentagon to Cut US-South Korea Military Exercises

Citing high costs and his good relationship with Kim Jong-un, Trump directed the Pentagon to significantly reduce US-South Korea joint military exercises. China's Foreign Ministry responded that maintaining peace and stability on the peninsula serves all parties' interests.
