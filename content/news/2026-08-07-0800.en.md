🔥 **Key Overnight News**

**1. Middle East Escalation**
Saudi Arabia sounds alarm — Iran is directing Iraqi militias and Houthi forces to plan "multi-directional attacks" on Saudi Arabia, with energy facilities and ports as potential targets. Iran's parliament is reviewing a Hormuz Strait management bill that would ban US and Israeli vessels. Iran warned Gulf states: if the US strikes again, it will attack their oil infrastructure and power grids.

**2. Fed's Musalem Turns Hawkish** (2028 FOMC Voter)
Inflation remains well above the 2% target, core inflation at 2.5%-3%. Leaned toward hiking at the last FOMC meeting. Gradual hikes are less costly than abrupt shifts. CME FedWatch shows 55% probability of a 25bp hike in September.

**3. Walsh Sticks to "Minimalist" Communication**
Fed Chair Walsh acknowledged communication missteps but refused "spoon-feeding" the market, retaining the September hike option. JPMorgan CEO Dimon publicly backed Walsh, calling the approach "visionary."

**4. Trump Imposes Polysilicon Tariffs**
Invoking Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962, Trump imposed minimum import prices and additional tariffs on polysilicon and derivative products. Effective December 4, 2026.

**5. Weak Japan Data**

🧭 **Assessment**

Middle East geopolitical risk is escalating again. The Iran-Saudi confrontation now involves energy infrastructure, and the Hormuz Strait navigation issue has become a new flashpoint.

Oil's overnight jump already prices in geopolitical risk premium. If the conflict materially escalates, crude has further upside risk, which would reignite inflation concerns.

Today's US employment report is the key event to close the week. A stronger-than-expected print would further reinforce hike expectations, pressuring both stocks and bonds.

Gold benefits from the geopolitical safe-haven bid versus the hawkish rate expectation — maintaining a high range. Continued ETF inflows indicate allocation demand remains intact.
