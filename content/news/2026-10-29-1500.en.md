Evidence: overnight the Fed held but upgraded its growth assessment to "strong", the long end sold off and 2s10s widened to 34.1bp; a 25bp December hike is priced at 83% (LSEG). Today DXY rose only 0.10%, while gold fell 0.84% and silver 2.04%, with silver breaking its prior low to 52.595. Invalidation: gold recovers today's basis of 4,068.76 and silver recovers 53.95, which would return this call to neutral; if 10Y pushes above 5.10% from 5.05%, this call strengthens rather than weakens.

**Call 3: The oil market has already "eventised" Hormuz — a single attack is no longer a trading reason, which means no catalyst for energy equities and relief for cost-side sectors (airlines, chemicals, refiners).**
Evidence: after the 14:33 fire report, oil moved only from 100.561 to 100.706, and the session low remains the pre-report 100.286. Since October the strait has seen multiple vessels hit by unknown projectiles, commercial transits far below the 2025 baseline, and continued US blockade enforcement (UKMTO 8 October weekly). Today's two "escalation" headlines — South Korea considering deployment (combat deployment ruled out) and Iran downing a drone — drew no price response.
Impact: do not chase long on "escalation" headlines, and do not chase short on new lows either ($100 is a level tested repeatedly this week, not the start of a trend); what would genuinely reprice is quantifiable supply loss, not another fire.
Invalidation: USOIL holds above today's high of 101.528, or hard evidence appears — loading suspensions, insurance-rate spikes, a single-day drop in transits to zero — which would be a real supply shock rather than a demand story.
