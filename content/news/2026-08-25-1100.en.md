**Jackson Hole opens Friday, Warsh under "show your cards" pressure** — Inflation has exceeded target for over five years, with both markets and the Fed internally calling for hikes; the "global savings glut" has become a "global savings squeeze," driving Treasury yields higher while Treasury Secretary Bessent's intervention in the long end raises overreach concerns; Warsh's calls with Trump face congressional scrutiny. **Citi lifts near-term gold target to $4,800; JPMorgan bets on big swings** — Citi raised its 0-3 month gold target to $4,800/oz while keeping its 6-12 month target at $5,000; JPMorgan sees gold trading in a $4,500-5,000 range, with Wednesday's PCE and Friday's Jackson Hole signals as the near-term drivers.

**Hormuz traffic hits new low; US-Iran tensions support oil** — Ship transits through the Strait of Hormuz fell to the lowest in over three months; the US threatens to expand economic pressure on Iran via secondary sanctions, while Japan's Finance Minister hopes the strait reopens soon. **BofA: BoJ may raise rates to stabilize the yen** — BofA believes the Bank of Japan may use rate hikes as the primary tool to stabilize the yen and needs to adjust its policy mix; Japan's Finance Minister is also studying tax incentives for retail investors buying JGBs.
