🔥 **Key News This Hour**

1. **Alphabet (GOOG) Cloud Backlog Nearly Doubles YoY to Over $460 Billion** — Google Cloud delivered a stunning quarter, expecting to recognize over 50% of backlog within 24 months. Daily token processing exceeds 3 trillion, AI Overview MAU surpasses 2.5 billion, and AI subscription plans are performing "exceptionally well." This is one of the most important tech earnings data points of the quarter.

2. **US Indices Sell Off Across the Board** — S&P 500 and Dow fell ~0.6%, Nasdaq dropped 0.8%, Russell 2000 declined 1.1%. Nvidia (NVDA) led tech lower with a 3% drop at the open.

3. **Trump Signals Willingness to Meet Iran's Supreme Leader** — Trump stated "Iran has agreed not to possess nuclear weapons" and emphasized the US does not need ground troops to achieve its strategic objectives. This is a significant signal of a potential diplomatic opening in US-Iran relations.

4. **S&P Global PMI Finals Revised Lower** — US Services PMI final: 50.7 (vs 50.9 flash), Composite PMI final: 51.5 (vs 51.7 flash). S&P Global noted rising prices are constraining business activity and sales, employment falling at the fastest pace in six years, and business optimism at its lowest since October 2022.

5. **Russia's Druzhba Pipeline Oil Exports Resume** — After maintenance, exports to Hungary and Slovakia via the Druzhba pipeline returned to a normal 165K barrels/day in May, easing European supply pressure at the margin.

6. **Kuwait Plans Overseas Oil Storage Expansion** — A Kuwait Petroleum executive stated that Strait of Hormuz transit is no longer safe. The country is accelerating storage and pipeline investments to diversify away from single-strait dependence. Middle East energy infrastructure thinking is undergoing a fundamental shift.

7. **Russia Discloses Gold Mining Output for First Time in Four Years** — Official estimates put last year's mined gold output at ~480 tonnes, which if accurate would make Russia the world's top gold producer. The figure far exceeds industry association and third-party estimates, raising data credibility concerns.

🧭 **Situation Analysis**

Alphabet's earnings unleashed a powerful AI monetization acceleration signal — $460B in backlog and 3 trillion daily tokens processed show enterprise AI spending is not slowing but re-accelerating.

Yet amid Middle East war and rising inflation expectations, the market chose to "sell the news." Nvidia's 3% drop signals AI upside is fully priced in, with short-term risk-off sentiment dominating.

Trump's willingness to meet Iran's leader is a delicate diplomatic window. If substantive contact follows, oil and gold geopolitical premiums could unwind rapidly.

S&P Global PMI data shows the US economy is experiencing stagflation-like slowdown — rapid employment contraction coupled with persistent price increases. The Fed holding in June is a near-certainty.

Kuwait's proactive overseas storage buildout is a vote of no confidence in Hormuz Strait security. This infrastructure pivot signals accelerated global energy supply chain restructuring, bullish long-term for pipeline and storage assets.
