🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. US Initial Jobless Claims + Q2 Productivity**

Initial jobless claims for week ending Aug 1: 199K (expected 202K, prior revised to 198K). 4-week moving average 198.75K. Continuing claims 1.801M (expected 1.79M). Q2 nonfarm productivity preliminary 1.4% (expected 0.6%), unit labor costs preliminary 1.3% (expected 2.1%, prior revised to 1.3%).

Labor market remains resilient with layoffs at historically healthy levels. Productivity exceeded expectations while labor costs came in below — wage inflation pressure is contained, supporting the Fed's rate-cut path.

**2. Iran Warns Gulf States: Strike Energy Infrastructure if US Attacks Again**

Iran's foreign minister reportedly made urgent calls to Gulf states, warning that if Washington proceeds with military action, Tehran will target core energy infrastructure in Gulf nations. Oman and Iran to issue a joint statement on the Hormuz Strait transit corridor.

Middle East geopolitical risk has escalated significantly. Hormuz Strait passage security is now a focal point.

**3. World Gold Council: Global Gold ETF Inflows $3B in July**

July reversed two consecutive months of outflows, attracting $3B in inflows. Total AUM rose to $530B, holdings increased 23 tonnes to 4,068 tonnes. Europe led the recovery ($2B), Asia added $616M. However, daily average trading volume declined 3.5% to $356B. Gold price was flat in July; WGC warns a second wave of high inflation similar to the late 1970s cannot be ruled out.

**4. Alphabet Plans $25B Bond Issuance, Testing AI Capex Appetite**

GOOG has begun marketing a US investment-grade bond issuance after raising 2026 capex guidance, which triggered AI-related bond selling. Different maturities reflect a refined financing strategy. This tests investor appetite for AI spending at scale.

**5. Oklo Small Modular Reactor Achieves Criticality**

The US Department of Energy announced that Oklo's small modular reactor has achieved "critical" status. SMR commercialization is accelerating, reinforcing the nuclear-as-AI-data-center-power narrative.

**6. Alibaba Cloud Launches Wan3.0 Video Generation Model**
