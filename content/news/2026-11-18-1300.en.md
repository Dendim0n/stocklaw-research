View: **bearish near term, but the risk/reward is poor for shorts — no adds, no bottom-fishing.** Crowded shorts plus a widening stream of statements means any verifiable drop in loadings or facility damage would amplify a covering rally. **Invalidation / confirmation**: a large same-direction EIA build at 23:30 (consensus already at -500k) hardens the inventory case; a US or Kuwaiti confirmation of base damage, or renewed mine strikes or a closure in Hormuz, means repricing immediately as a supply disruption.

Evidence: the hourly decline went to zero (gold -0.02%, silver +0.01%), but the new low of 3,781.11 is only $4.5 below the prior low, and the 12:26 bounce to 3,790.63 faded all the way back to 3,784 — "stopped falling, not recovering"; platinum -1.97% and palladium -2.57% show the whole metals book is still being reduced; retail longs above 80% mean the long side has not cleared, so rallies lack fuel. View: **bearish, stay out of the way.** 3,800 is only a psychological level; a repair requires reclaiming **3,832.20** (the 11-16 low). Evidence: Hang Seng, Shanghai Composite, CSI 300 and ChiNext all made intraday lows at 13:02–13:03, in the first minute of the afternoon session; the CSRC had already denied the AI exposure reporting requirement at 12:26, so policy rumor was not the driver; the Nikkei at +0.14% shows this is not a global risk-off, the pressure is concentrated in China assets; A-shares still had computing-power and components names rising in the morning (11:33 midday wrap), while Hong Kong was hit on both earnings and precious metals.
View: **bearish on Hong Kong, neutral but not constructive on the A-share index — the real divergence is between "compute/components" and "resources/precious metals", not at the index level.** Both confirmation conditions set in the previous issue (Hang Seng breaking 23,248.50, Shanghai breaking 3,568.82) triggered this hour, so the first gate toward a broad sell-off from a structural rotation has opened. **Invalidation / confirmation**: if Shanghai reclaims **3,568.82** (the prior-issue low it broke today) after 14:00, treat it as a false break; Tencent's Q3 call at 20:00 is Hong Kong's next decision point.
