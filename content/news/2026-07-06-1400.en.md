🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**Ukrainian Drones Strike Russian Oil Ports, Russia Faces Worst Fuel Crisis in Decades**

The local governor confirmed infrastructure damage at Russia's Ust-Luga and Vysotsk oil port areas following drone attacks. This follows earlier reports of intensified Ukrainian strikes on Russian energy facilities, with drone capabilities surging under US intelligence support. Russia is now facing its most severe fuel crisis in decades. WTI crude has shown a muted response so far, hovering near $69.

**Japan Bond Yields Surge Across the Curve, Goldman Sachs Targets USD/JPY 165**

Japan's 10Y yield jumped 5.5bp to 2.825%, 20Y hit 3.800%, and 30Y reached 4.065%. Goldman Sachs raised its 12-month USD/JPY target to 165, citing the US-Japan rate differential, fiscal pressures, and the BOJ's slow pace of rate hikes as drivers of continued yen weakness.

**SK Hynix Files for $29B US IPO — Largest Foreign Company Listing Ever**

The South Korean chip giant will list on Nasdaq on July 10, raising $29 billion. Meanwhile, analog chip leader ADI warned customers of extended lead times up to six months, urging advance ordering — signaling renewed semiconductor supply tightness.

**BNY Mellon: Fed Tightening Urgency Has Diminished**

A senior macro strategist at BNY Mellon noted that softening US labor data and improving inflation prints have reduced the urgency for further Fed tightening, though uncertainty remains over whether the growth slowdown is manageable.

**HSBC Reshuffles Healthcare Ratings**

HSBC upgraded Gilead Sciences (GILD) from Hold to Buy, raising its target from $133 to $155; raised targets on UnitedHealth (UNH) $300→$380, Merck (MRK) $135→$150, Johnson & Johnson (JNJ) $280→$290, Biogen (BIIB) $150→$157; cut Pfizer (PFE) target from $32 to $28. Jefferies lowered Newmont Mining (NEM) target from $158 to $146.

**Trump Approval Drops to 36%, US-Iran Conflict Seen as "Not Worth It"**

A new poll shows 58% of voters believe the Iran operation was not worth the cost, with Trump's approval dragged down by $67B in new military spending and high oil prices.

**NVIDIA Kyber NVL144 Reportedly Delayed to 2028**

PCB manufacturing bottlenecks are stalling AI rack deployment, with delivery pushed back by over 12 months to 2028.
