## 🔥 Key News This Hour

### 1. Fed's Collins Turns Hawkish: Would Support September Rate Hike If Inflation Persists

Boston Fed President Collins told the Financial Times that the Iran war has exacerbated cost-of-living pressures, with America's poor struggling to make ends meet. She warned the Fed may need to raise interest rates to curb inflation, saying she would support a September hike if inflation remains elevated. This is the most explicit hawkish signal from a Fed official regarding the inflationary spillover of the Iran war to date.

### 2. US VP Vance Asked Ukraine to Stop Attacking Tankers at Russian Ports

According to the Financial Times, Ukrainian officials revealed that after US Vice President Vance made the request last month, Ukraine has halted its intensive drone attacks on oil tankers using key Black Sea ports. Washington was reportedly alarmed by Ukraine's earlier attacks. The intervention highlights US concerns over global energy supply chain stability.

### 3. Japan PM: Canadian Crude Oil Arrives in Japan for First Time

Japanese Prime Minister Takaichi Sanae announced that Canadian crude oil arrived in Japan today, marking the first such shipment since the Middle East situation deteriorated. This signals substantive progress in Japan's efforts to diversify energy import sources amid the risk of Middle East crude supply disruption.

Omdia published a report stating that AI demand continues to exceed global supply capacity, driving strong growth in the DRAM and NAND markets. The firm raised its 2026 global semiconductor revenue growth forecast to 94.1% year-over-year. The AI-driven semiconductor boom cycle continues to accelerate.
