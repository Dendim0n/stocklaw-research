🧭 **Market Assessment**

The Fed released a strongly hawkish signal overnight, with both Governor Cook and Kashkari signaling readiness to hike, pushing the September probability above 50%.

However, ADP's meager 44K — the lowest this year — reveals a labor market cooling alongside stubborn inflation, creating a stagflation narrative risk.

On the Middle East front, Iran and Oman nearing a Hormuz shipping deal has reduced geopolitical risk premium, but Houthi attacks on Saudi tankers remind us the threat isn't gone.

The US-Japan joint intervention sent the yen surging, pushing the dollar index below 100 to 99.7, with gold benefiting to reclaim $4,270.

Walsh's "silence" and the resulting communication crisis warrant attention — declining policy transparency amplifies market volatility.

Friday's nonfarm payrolls will be the next key catalyst; a weak-jobs-plus-strong-inflation combination could accelerate rate hike expectation pricing.
