🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**JPMorgan Slashes Gold Target, Weighing on Bullion**
JPMorgan cut its Q4 gold price target from $6,000 to $4,500, becoming the most cautious among major investment banks. Spot gold fell 0.74% to $4,144, dropping over $50 from the intraday high of $4,202. JPM sees limited upside for gold in H2 but made clear that current prices still have support.

**SK Hynix $29B Nasdaq IPO on Deck**
On July 10, Korean chip giant SK Hynix will list on Nasdaq, raising $29 billion in what could be the largest foreign company IPO in history. With a market cap already exceeding $200 billion, IPO pricing will be a key market focus this week.
