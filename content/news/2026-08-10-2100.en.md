## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Fed Governor Cook Charged**

White House National Economic Council Director Hassett said he hopes Cook is right to plead not guilty, declined to comment on the charges, and made clear he would not fill her Fed governor seat. If Cook departs, it would affect the Fed's board composition and FOMC voting balance.

**2. Japan-US Joint Yen Intervention — First in 28 Years**

According to Kyodo News, BOJ Governor Ueda's hint at a September rate hike was the decisive factor behind the joint Japan-US yen-buying intervention on July 31. This marks the first such coordinated action in 28 years. USD/JPY dropped about 40 pips to 158.41 before recovering to 158.67.

**3. Boeing to Acquire Stake in Archer Aviation**

Boeing plans to acquire a stake in eVTOL company Archer Aviation, sending Archer shares up 15% in premarket trading. A traditional aerospace giant enters the flying car space.

**4. Anthropic Partners with Macquarie and GIC for AI Data Centers**

Anthropic has established a strategic partnership with Macquarie and Singapore sovereign wealth fund GIC. The new entity, Theseus I, will build data centers for Claude developers, further expanding US AI infrastructure investment.

**5. Colombia Hit by 6.7 Magnitude Earthquake**

Colombia's Geological Service reported the epicenter near Chocó province, depth 82km. Strong tremors were felt in the capital Bogotá. No major casualties reported so far.
