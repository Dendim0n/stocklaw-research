## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**NATO Ankara Summit: Iran Must Not Have Nuclear Weapons**

A draft NATO Ankara summit statement obtained by Reuters shows NATO leaders will explicitly demand Iran fully respect freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz and declare that Iran must never possess nuclear weapons. The statement comes against the backdrop of the post-conflict regional reordering following the February Israeli-US military operation against Iran.

**Iran Initiates First Crude Trade Talks with Japan Since 2019**

Multiple sources reveal that under the 60-day temporary US sanctions waiver, Iran has begun crude procurement evaluations with three Japanese firms. However, the waiver extension outlook and Strait of Hormuz transit safety remain key sticking points. Meanwhile, Saudi Arabia's Deputy Foreign Minister paid tribute to Iran's late Supreme Leader Khamenei, signaling a potential Sunni-Shia diplomatic thaw.

**Ukraine Tensions Escalate: Guided Bombs Launched Toward Kherson**

Guided aerial bombs have reportedly been launched toward Kherson, Ukraine. Poland's Foreign Minister warned the same day that it would be madness for Putin to test NATO unity. Eastern front dynamics may intensify further during the NATO summit.

**Gulf June Oil Exports Surpass 10M bpd, UAE Hits Record High**

Data shows Gulf region June oil exports rose by over 3 million bpd month-on-month, breaching the 10 million bpd mark, led by the UAE. However, volumes remain 40% below pre-conflict levels, with millions of barrels still stranded in the Gulf.

**Japan Spring Wage Talks Conclude: 3rd Consecutive Year Above 5%**

Japan's largest labor union federation Rengo published final statistics showing 5,368 companies with an average wage increase of 5.01%. Markets are further cementing expectations for a BOJ rate hike by year-end.

## 🧭 Strategic Assessment

Gold is supported by a triple geopolitical layer: NATO's stance on Iran's nuclear program, escalation in the Kherson direction, and Strait of Hormuz navigation disputes. Any deterioration in these factors could push gold to test the 4200 round-number resistance.

Crude fundamentals are increasingly contradictory — Gulf export volumes recovering above 10M bpd clash with uncertainties around Iranian crude re-entering the market (Japan talks) and Middle East transit safety, likely keeping prices in a 67-72 range in the near term.

Japan's wage-inflation virtuous cycle continues to validate, with the 5.01% spring wage outcome making a BOJ year-end rate hike nearly certain. The medium-term yen strength thesis remains intact, exerting sustained pressure on export-oriented Japanese equities.

Iran's diplomatic trajectory warrants close attention — the Saudi Deputy FM's tribute to Khamenei combined with Iran-Japan crude negotiations suggests rapid regional order restructuring under the US-led 60-day waiver framework, with Strait of Hormuz security as the pivotal variable.
