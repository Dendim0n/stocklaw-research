🔥 **Key Overnight News**

**1. CoreWeave Q2 Revenue Doubles, Stock Up 12% After Hours**
AI compute leader CoreWeave (CRWV) reported Q2 revenue of $2.58B (+112% YoY), beating estimates of $2.56B; net loss of $626M better than expected $757M. Backlog reached $104B. Raised 2026 full-year revenue guidance to $12.4-13.2B, year-end compute capacity to exceed 1.85GW. 2026 capex projected at $35-39B.

**2. API Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Surge 9.07M Barrels**
For the week ending Aug 7, API crude stocks rose 9.072M barrels vs an expected draw of 0.5M and prior build of 2.69M. Cushing +1.57M, gasoline -1.53M, distillates -0.596M. Build far exceeded expectations — bearish for oil near-term.

**3. Fed September Rate Hike Probability at 48%**
CME FedWatch: 52% probability of holding rates steady in September, 48% for a 25bp hike. October hold probability 38.7%. Former NY Fed President Dudley criticized Wash's policy confusion, warning that the Fed "copying answers from the market" would trigger a credibility collapse.

**4. Houthi First Fatal Attack on Saudi Vessel Kills 3 Crew Members**
A commercial ship was attacked in the Mandeb Strait resulting in 3 deaths — the first fatality from Houthi ship attacks since the US-Iran conflict. Both critical Middle East waterways (Hormuz + Mandeb) under pressure. Panama Canal congestion also worsening due to route rerouting; one shipowner paid $4M to jump the queue.

**5. Trump Considers Capital Gains Tax Cut**
According to a senior economic advisor, Trump is seeking new policy promises ahead of midterms, including potential capital gains tax cuts and tax exemptions for certain home sales.

**6. US Unit Labor Costs Drop to 1.4%**
Easing wage pressure signals that core inflation may continue to decline, providing room for the Fed to maintain a wait-and-see stance.

**7. US Equity Sentiment Flashing Strongest Warning Since 2018**
Wells Fargo warns: if CPI exceeds expectations combined with soft employment, stagflation narrative would hit markets. Advises adding hedges before CPI. Retail bearish sentiment at multi-year highs; institutional positioning severely lagging.

**8. Japan Manufacturing Sentiment Hits Highest Since March**

**9. European Stocks Enter Bull Market, Capital Returns En Masse**

**10. CME to Launch Compute Futures in October**
