🧭 **Outlook**

The Doha agreement to release $3 billion is a positive signal but falls short of a fundamental shift in US-Iran relations.

Iran's simultaneous signal about initiating final-stage talks appears more like testing US willingness to negotiate ahead of the November midterm elections.

USD strength combined with falling oil prices creates a double headwind for precious metals short-term; the 4020 support level for gold needs close monitoring.

EM currency drawdowns reflect market repricing of Fed policy path; interest rate differentials remain the core driver.

US equities had a stellar Q2 but Wall Street bulls are already hedging against pullbacks; volatility risk in this leverage-driven rally cannot be ignored.
