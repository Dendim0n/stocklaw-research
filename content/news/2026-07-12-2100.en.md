🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**Iranian Drone Strikes Hit Omani Territory, Middle East Tensions Spike**
Iranian drones struck locations in Oman's Musandam province and central provinces. Oman's Foreign Ministry summoned the Iranian ambassador to deliver a formal protest. This marks the first time a third-party Gulf state's territory has been directly hit in this round of US-Iran conflict, signaling a significant escalation in spillover risk.

**Commercial Vessel in Distress Near Strait of Hormuz, 23 Rescued, 1 Missing**
The Cyprus-flagged vessel "GFS Galaxy" suffered an incident approximately 4.4 nautical miles off Oman's Musandam coast near the Strait of Hormuz. Twenty-three crew members were rescued; one remains missing with search operations ongoing. The cause of the incident has not yet been disclosed.

**US and Iran Wage War of Words Over Strait of Hormuz**
US Central Command stated the Strait of Hormuz "is open to all vessels seeking legitimate passage," rejecting Iranian claims of control. Iran's military spokesperson cited the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding, asserting that Iran bears responsibility for maritime traffic management in the Strait and accusing the US of attempting to impose shipping routes.

**Iran Reports Intercepting Enemy Cruise Missile in Western Iran**
Iran's Revolutionary Guard air defense systems intercepted an enemy cruise missile near Khorramabad in Lorestan province, western Iran. The missile's origin remains unspecified.

**Jordan Protests Iranian Violation of Sovereignty**
Jordan's government issued a statement condemning Iranian drone and missile attacks as a flagrant violation of Jordanian sovereignty and a dangerous escalation, citing breaches of international law and the UN Charter. A US official denied claims of significant damage to US military facilities in Jordan.

**Iraq PM to Visit Washington Monday, Oil & Gas Deal Expected**
Iraq's Prime Minister will visit Washington on Monday to deepen strategic ties, with an oil and gas cooperation agreement expected to be signed. Iraq seeks to balance relations with both the US and Iran amid ongoing military tensions.

**Ukraine Launches Political Strategy Overhaul, Zelensky Proposes PM Replacement**
President Zelensky announced a political strategy adjustment, assigning dedicated personnel to each foreign policy priority. He proposed replacing Prime Minister Svyrydenko, with a cabinet reshuffle to follow.

🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

The Middle East situation is evolving from a "US-Iran standoff" into a "regional crisis." The strike on Omani territory means the conflict is no longer confined to the bilateral US-Iran framework — the involvement of a third-party Gulf state will force a collective response from the Gulf Cooperation Council.

The Strait of Hormuz contest has extended from the military domain into legal and public-opinion battles. Both the US and Iran cite different international instruments to assert their claims, making a near-term resolution unlikely. Strait navigation safety remains the market's most sensitive variable.

If Iraq signs an oil and gas deal during Monday's Washington visit, it could partially ease market concerns over Middle East supply disruptions, but actual strait transit conditions will be the decisive factor.

Gold has found support above 4100 but has yet to breach this week's high of 4135. If the situation deteriorates further by Monday's Asian open, gold could challenge the 4150-4200 range.

Crude's relatively muted response (oscillating in the 70-73 range) suggests the market is pricing in limited Middle East risk premium for now. A spike could occur if actual strait transit disruptions materialize.

Ukraine's political reshuffle is a routine diplomatic adjustment with limited market impact.
