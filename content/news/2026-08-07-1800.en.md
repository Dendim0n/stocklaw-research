🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. Precious Metals Surge — Safe Haven Demand Rises**

Gold breaks above $4,316, up 1.79% on the day, extending its rally to new highs since June 18. Silver surges 5% to $64.60/oz. The synchronized precious metals rally signals a significant increase in safe-haven demand.

**2. BofA: Market Sentiment at Most Extreme Bullish Level Since 2021**

Bank of America strategists led by Michael Hartnett report that investor bullishness has climbed to its most extreme level since 2021, recommending reduced exposure to risk assets. This is a warning sign for US equity investors — extreme bullishness often precedes market tops.

**3. Trump on AI and the Fed**

Trump stated that "AI is many times bigger than the internet" and "may be more important than oil — whoever wins AI wins everything." On the Fed, he said rate decisions are "not entirely up to Warsh," noting Warsh faces a "very political committee." Trump also claimed he "may be the last Republican president" and expressed optimism about midterm elections.

**4. Turkey-Saudi-Pakistan Sign "Mecca Agreement" Defense Pact**

According to Iran's Tasnim news agency, the trilateral defense agreement among Turkey, Saudi Arabia, and Pakistan has been officially named the "Mecca Agreement." A new variable in the Middle East-South Asia geopolitical landscape.

**5. Russia-Ukraine Conflict Continues**

Russia's Defense Ministry reported hitting 34 Ukrainian military vessels over the past week, along with two large-scale strikes and 12 cluster strikes on Ukrainian defense industry and logistics centers.

**6. Japan's GPIF Records Largest Quarterly Profit Ever**

The world's largest pension fund, GPIF, posted a record quarterly profit of 24.1 trillion yen in Q2, benefiting from the global stock bull market. However, Japanese bond yields spiked on expectations of fiscal expansion under the Takaichi government.

**7. Investment Banks Adjust Chinese Stock Holdings**

Goldman Sachs increased its long position in Luxshare Precision (02475.HK) to 20.27% while cutting Zhongji Innolight (03308.HK) to 11.6%. JPMorgan moved in the opposite direction, raising its Zhongji Innolight stake to 15.02%. Institutional divergence emerges on optical module leaders.

**8. Analysts Raise Energy Stock Price Targets**

TD Cowen raised ExxonMobil (XOM) target from $155 to $168; JPMorgan raised ConocoPhillips (COP) target from $124 to $134. Despite oil falling on the day, analysts remain constructive on the energy sector's long-term value.

**9. South-Bound Capital Net Sells HK Stocks by HK$778M**

Tencent and Alibaba-W saw net selling via Stock Connect. Meanwhile, China and Malaysia renewed their currency swap agreement, expanding it to CNY 220bn / MYR 130bn.
