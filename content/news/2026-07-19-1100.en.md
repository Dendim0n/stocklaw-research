**US-Iran Conflict Escalates Sharply — Iran Strikes US Bases in Kuwait**

The State Department issued a worldwide caution advising American citizens globally to exercise increased vigilance, citing heightened tensions in the Middle East and the potential for unintended escalation.

**Pentagon Rushes Fighter Jets to Middle East**

According to the Wall Street Journal and Air & Space Forces Magazine, the Pentagon is accelerating the deployment of F-16 and F-35 fighter jets to the Middle East amid the intensifying US-Iran conflict.

🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

The US-Iran conflict has entered a new phase — shifting from unilateral US airstrikes to direct two-way engagement, with Iran extending strikes to US military assets inside Kuwait for the first time, shattering the previous geographic boundaries of the conflict.

Kuwait being drawn into the crossfire is a pivotal turning point. Gulf Arab states have thus far sought to maintain neutrality, but Iran's attack on Kuwaiti oil facilities will force the entire Gulf Cooperation Council to reassess its security posture.

Gold breaking above $4,000 and crude surging 3.6% in a single session signals that markets are pricing in a prolonged and widening conflict. If shipping through the Strait of Hormuz faces material disruption, crude has significantly more upside.

The State Department's global security alert is a rare diplomatic signal, typically indicating that the US assesses the risk of terrorist attacks or retaliatory strikes has spread beyond the Middle East.

Key watchpoints this week: whether the US and Iran engage through third-party diplomatic channels, and the ECB rate decision's indirect impact on the dollar and gold.
