## 🔥 Key Overnight Headlines

**1. Qualcomm Surges 14%+ After Hours — AI Ambitions Expanded**
Qualcomm's CFO dramatically raised 2029 fiscal year targets: non-handset revenue doubled to $40B; AI data center revenue target at $15B (with $5B by FY2027); automotive at $10B; IoT exceeding $14B. Two hyperscaler custom chip clients will contribute $1B by FY2027. Handset share will drop to ~⅓ of core semiconductor revenue. Qualcomm is going all-in on AI chip diversification.

**2. Fed Stress Test: All 32 Banks Pass**
The Fed's annual stress test showed 32 large banks can collectively absorb over $708 billion in losses under a hypothetical deep global recession scenario, all above regulatory thresholds. A Wall Street dividend and buyback wave is expected to follow.

**3. Gold Dips Below 4000: $12 Billion in Outflows**
Gold briefly fell below $4000 as JPMorgan warned that hawkish Fed Chair nominee Wunsch could reshape rate expectations. Markets are pricing in two Fed rate hikes before early 2027. Silver crashed as much as 8%. Bessent's strong-dollar rhetoric added further pressure on precious metals.

**4. Iran Situation: Easing Tensions, But Divisions Persist**
