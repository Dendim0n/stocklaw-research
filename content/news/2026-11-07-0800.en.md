## 3. Assessment and counter-evidence

**A. Rates: the direction is credible, the magnitude is not citable; the next test is CPI on 11-10.**
Three things point the same way: 2Y +5bp versus 30Y +1bp (bear flattening), the dollar +0.74%, and gold losing $28 in the single minute of the release. The counter-evidence is just as clear — **wage growth is decelerating**, U-6 rose and part-time work increased, so this is a report that is strong on headcount and not strong on pay. **Lean**: the front end already treats a December hike as the base case; if CPI on 11-10 prints below 0.3% m/m, a retreat in 2-year yields below 4.46% (the 11-05 level) would not be surprising, and gold plus long-duration growth would benefit first. **Invalidation**: CPI at 0.4% m/m or higher with core y/y not falling, in which case 2-year yields could retest 4.58–4.61% (the 11-03/04 closes) and the hike trade spreads from the front end to the long end.

**B. US equities: Friday was a bounce, not a bottom.**
The magnitudes make the case: SOX fell 12.20% on Thursday and rose 4.21% on Friday, recovering only a third; the two-day change is −8.51% for SOX and −4.49% for the Nasdaq, while the Dow fell 3.88% over two days and was the only index down on Friday — money rotated inside semis rather than returning to risk overall. Software's absence (ADBE −2.90%, MSFT −0.99%) is consistent with the AI-capex narrative still being damaged. **Lean**: do not chase this bounce, and do not add shorts here either — SOX is still −35.93% from its 52-week high, so this is no longer a good-odds short at the highs. **Confirmation**: SOX closes above 9,627 (Friday's intraday high) on Monday, meaning the covering continued. **Invalidation**: a break below 9,011 (Thursday's close), which would make Friday a dead-cat bounce.

**C. Gold: resilience is real, repair is incomplete, and the evidence does not support "haven inflows".**
Three things support resilience: the dollar rose 0.74% while gold fell only 0.43% (no amplification of the offsetting pressure); the low came within 30 minutes of payrolls and was followed by a 1.09% recovery; and the 23:00 drop in inflation expectations gave a fundamental reason via lower real rates. **What argues against "inflows"**: 4,090 (the 11-05 close) has not been reclaimed (0.47% away), silver is still −0.71%, and CFTC gold net length was still falling in the week to 11-03 — no positioning or flow data supports "new money arriving". Price action can show selling was absorbed; it cannot identify the buyer. **Confirmation**: reclaim 4,090 with silver turning positive. **Invalidation**: a break of 4,026.86, which would make the post-23:00 recovery nothing but short covering.

**D. Crude: the geopolitical premium is largely gone, but do not conflate two different things.**
Settlements barely moved (+0.34% / +0.03%), the post-settlement tape weakened, and CFTC net length fell to 86,469 — all point to premium compression. The two headlines have different magnitudes: **easing sanctioned-tanker restrictions** affects effective tonnage and freight (small, reversible); **progress in Iran nuclear talks** affects the return of barrels (large, irreversible). Treating the first as the second overstates the bear case; treating the second as the first understates it. **Lean**: short-term bearish, but do not chase headlines; what actually reprices this market is measured Hormuz loadings. **The reverse transmission is worth watching**: if crude keeps sliding, energy inflation pressure eases, the case for hikes weakens, and that turns supportive for gold and growth. It is the cleanest cross-asset chain available, but it needs two consecutive weeks of lower crude to be valid — one day does not count.

**E. Primary market: funding has not stopped, pricing is tiering.**
OpenAI receiving $11bn at a $1.25trn valuation and Anthropic's range moving down to 1.2–1.3trn are happening at the same time. The first proves the leader can still raise; the second proves the runner-up must discount. **Neither** proves "there is no AI bubble" nor "AI funding is frozen". The verifiable checkpoint is whether Anthropic still starts its roadshow within the next two weeks — already flagged in the previous edition, with no new confirmation today.
