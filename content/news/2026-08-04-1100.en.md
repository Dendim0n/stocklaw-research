🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. JPMorgan Brings Forward Fed Rate Hike Expectation to December 2026**

Warsh's communication with markets has triggered a trust crisis. JPMorgan's economics team overturned its previous forecast for the timing of rate hikes, moving it from H2 2026 to December 2026, and acknowledged that a move as early as September is possible. The core logic is that the urgency of policy tightening has increased sharply after Fed credibility was damaged.

**2. Amazon Market Cap First Breaks $3 Trillion**

Amazon shares surged over 20% in two days following earnings, with market cap exceeding $3 trillion for the first time. The key driver is market confirmation that AI demand is translating into actual revenue, with Wall Street重新 awarding a high valuation.

**3. Bessent Publicly Pressures Fed to Expand FIMA Facility**

Treasury Secretary Bessent publicly called on the Fed to expand the FIMA (Foreign and International Monetary Authorities) repo facility, described by former Treasury officials as "very unusual" — such matters are typically discussed privately. Japan holds $1.1 trillion in U.S. Treasuries; Bessent's public pressure raises market concerns about Fed independence. Warsh's response contained hidden implications.

**4. Japan's Economy Minister Makoto Ito Delivers Series of Remarks**

Ito stated: June overall CPI shows year-over-year price gains remain moderate; cost pass-through from goods price increases to consumers remains limited so far; but vigilance is needed on food cost pass-through that could persist from summer into autumn. He emphasized he would not comment on specific FX levels but would closely monitor the impact of FX volatility on the economy and prices, and expressed hope that the Bank of Japan would appropriately implement monetary policy to stably achieve the 2% inflation target.

**5. Korea KOSDAQ Triggers Buy-Side Circuit Breaker**

Korea's KOSDAQ index surged strongly, with futures rising over 6% to trigger a five-minute trading halt. Institutional net buying reached 354 billion Korean won. KOSPI performed relatively weakly by comparison.

**6. A-Share Market Rallies Strongly**

ChiNext (创业板指) rose over 4%, and the Shenzhen Composite gained over 2%. CPO, lithography, and PCB sectors led gains, with over 3,500 stocks advancing across the market. Liquid cooling servers, storage chips, and Pinduoduo concept sectors also saw broad strength.
