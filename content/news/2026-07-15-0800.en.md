🔥 **Overnight Headlines**

**Geopolitics — Strait of Hormuz Firefight**
Iran's Hormozgan provincial government confirmed an exchange of fire in the Strait, with explosions reported in Bandar Abbas and Persian Gulf islands. US CENTCOM stated Iran has attacked seven commercial vessels over the past seven days, causing nearly ten civilian casualties. The US has resumed a maritime blockade against Iran.

**Trump's 24-Hour Reversal**
Trump's 20% Strait of Hormuz toll plan lasted only one day. Under collective pressure from Gulf allies, he hastily withdrew it, substituting "investment in America," while simultaneously restarting the blockade and threatening to destroy Iran's power plants and bridges. Trump also pressured Netanyahu to withdraw Israeli forces from Syria and Lebanon.

**Houthis Warn: Oil Could Hit $200**
Yemen's Houthi leadership warned that if conflict spreads to the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, oil prices could surge to $200. Both the Persian Gulf and Red Sea — two critical maritime chokepoints — are now simultaneously in the risk spotlight.

**US June CPI Turns Negative for First Time in Six Years**
June CPI fell 0.4% MoM, the first monthly decline in six years. However, the data does not reflect recent energy price movements, and analysts view energy as the biggest wildcard ahead.

**Fed Chair Warsh: Ending the High-Inflation Era**
Warsh's congressional testimony emphasized restoring price stability as the top priority, with zero tolerance for persistent high inflation, while pledging to assess AI's impact on productivity, employment, and inflation.

**White House Launches "Golden Eagle" AI Cybersecurity Initiative**
The White House, together with Treasury, DHS, and DOD, launched the "Golden Eagle" platform to coordinate cybersecurity vulnerability response and advance AI innovation.

**CoreWeave Hedges Against Memory Chip Price Declines**
CRWV is exploring financial derivatives to hedge against future declines in memory chip prices, signaling cost management pressure amid the AI infrastructure investment boom.

**CXMT IPO Priced at 8.66 Yuan**
ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) disclosed an IPO price of 8.66 yuan per share, drawing attention to China's memory chip self-sufficiency drive.

**Lithium Battery Giants Post Explosive Earnings**
Upstream lithium battery leaders reported H1 net profit growth of up to nearly 50x, a clear signal of cyclical bottom reversal in the sector.

**Japan Manufacturing Confidence Holds Steady**
July Reuters Tankan manufacturing index held at 13, supported by semiconductor demand; non-manufacturing fell to 25 from 32, weighed down by costs.
