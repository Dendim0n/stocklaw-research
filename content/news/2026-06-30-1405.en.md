🧭 **Outlook**

Germany's positive stance on the US-Iran ceasefire marks a marginal easing signal for Middle East geopolitical risk.

Yet oil prices fall rather than rise, confirming the market has switched from geopolitical premium to oversupply pricing — Morgan Stanley's 4.8 million bpd surplus forecast for 2027 is the core headwind.

Lagarde's "no need for aggressive hikes" dovish signal, combined with falling oil prices, is boosting expectations of an ECB policy shift, supportive for European assets.

BofA's "Wave 3 correction" warning warrants attention — while S&P 500 net margins hit a record 14.8%, stretched valuations and seasonal risks cannot be ignored.

The split between China's strong STAR50 and weakening banking sector signals accelerating style rotation from defensives toward growth.
