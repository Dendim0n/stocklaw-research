🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. OpenAI Completes $7B Employee Stock Buyback at $852B Valuation**

OpenAI completed a ~$7 billion employee stock buyback, maintaining its $852 billion valuation. No external buyers were involved — the transaction was conducted by the company itself. Seen as a liquidity arrangement ahead of its IPO.

**2. Nvidia Partners with Six Wall Street Giants, Commits $500B AI Infrastructure Financing**

Nvidia has partnered with six major Wall Street institutions to commit $500 billion toward AI infrastructure buildout. Jensen Huang stated none of the six declined, marking one of the largest capital expenditure programs globally.

**3. Anthropic IPO Imminent This Fall, Courting Investors**

Anthropic, valued at $965 billion, is meeting with potential investors ahead of what could be the largest IPO in history. The company faces multiple challenges and is actively working to boost market confidence.

**4. BofA: CPI May Confirm Inflation Persistence, Fed Could Still Hike in September**

Bank of America warns that upcoming CPI data may confirm sticky inflation. Under Fed Chair Warsh's new approach of de-emphasizing forward guidance, elevated borrowing costs could persist beyond market expectations.

**5. Gulf States Forced to Accept Hormuz "New Normal"**

After five-plus months of war, Gulf oil producers have concluded that Iran's control over the Strait of Hormuz may be permanent. This reshapes Middle East geopolitics and oil supply security dynamics.

**6. RTX 50 GPU Prices Surge, Mid-Range Models Up 39%**

NVIDIA RTX 50 series retail prices have surged sharply in the US market, with mid-range products hit hardest — some models up as much as 39%. Supply chain tightness and AI demand diversion are the main drivers.
