## 🔥 Key News This Hour

**🛢️ Iraq-Kuwait Border Crossing Hit by Iranian Missile — WTI Surges 4.8%**
Iraqi security sources report the Abdali border crossing on the Iraq-Kuwait border was struck by an Iranian missile on July 23 local time, forcing its temporary closure. Combined with existing concerns over simultaneous pressure on the Strait of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb, WTI crude broke above $91 with a near-5% single-day surge.

**📈 US 10-Year Yield Hits 4.689%, Highest in 18 Months**
The US 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.689%, its highest since January 2025. JPMorgan simultaneously warned that markets are replaying the late-1990s divergence pattern — AI hardware names rallying while cloud giants are punished for capex spending, with the coming weeks described as crucial.

**🚀 Google Discloses $94B SpaceX Stake**
Google revealed in its 10-Q filing that its SpaceX holdings are valued at approximately $94 billion post-IPO, with $80 billion subject to short-term lockup restrictions. ORTEX data shows SpaceX short sellers have accumulated an estimated $15.5 billion in paper profits since the IPO.

**🇺🇦 Zelensky Announces Major Personnel Reshuffle**
Ukraine's president proposed former PM Svyrydenko as envoy to the US, former Defense Minister Fedorov as deputy PM for defense innovation, and a new defense minister to improve conscription. Zelensky also indicated US-Ukraine representatives may meet in the coming days.

**🇬🇧 UBS: UK Inflation May Peak at 3.3% in November**
UBS economists forecast UK inflation will reaccelerate, potentially hitting 3.3% in November driven by July utility bill hikes. However, the likelihood of further BOE rate hikes is diminishing due to sluggish growth and labor market weakness.

**📉 D.A. Davidson Cuts Alphabet Target**
The investment bank lowered its Alphabet price target from $375 to $350. Google shares have pulled back notably from recent highs.

## 🧭 Market Assessment

WTI crude's 4.8% surge today represents a concentrated release of geopolitical risk — the Iranian missile strike on the border crossing directly threatens crude supply security, and with both the Strait of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb already under pressure, energy risk premiums are being priced in rapidly.

The 10-year yield's climb to 4.689% is the most critical macro signal this hour, not only pressuring gold (down over 1% today) but also stress-testing high-valuation tech stocks.

JPMorgan's warning about "1990-style bubble divergence" deserves serious attention — the structural split between AI hardware and cloud giants is often a precursor to broader market fragility.

The "rare simultaneous rise" pattern of gold-dollar-oil has broken today, with gold and oil moving in completely opposite directions, suggesting Treasury yields are reclaiming their pricing power over gold.

Key watchpoints for the rest of this week: whether WTI can hold above $90, and whether the 10-year yield challenges the 4.7% threshold.
