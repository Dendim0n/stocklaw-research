## Assessment

**TSMC's earnings confirmed AI demand's authenticity, but the market reaction was "sell the fact," not "buy the prosperity."** 50% YoY growth, $46.7B quarterly revenue — by any company's standards, these are explosive numbers. Yet A-share semi sectors accelerated lower and the TAIEX closed -0.99%. **This isn't a fundamental problem; it's valuation digestion.** When AI supply chain growth has been fully priced in (even over-priced), "in-line" or even "beat" won't push prices higher — only "significantly beat" will. **Strategy: If US AI names gap down Monday with Asian tech, it's a short-term trading opportunity, not a sufficient reason for long-term position building — wait for November NVIDIA/Broadcom earnings to confirm the breadth and depth of the supply chain before deciding.**

**A-share widening decline shows risk-off spreading from HK to mainland.** From Shanghai Comp -0.27% at 13:00 to -0.91% at 14:00, ChiNext -3.07% — the decline pace doubled within an hour. SciTech 50 -4%, index futures -2.9% — small caps and leveraged products hit hardest, a classic "deleveraging selloff." **But A-share volume above ¥1 trillion means money is still inside the market, just rotating from tech to energy/financials.** For US stocks: if this style rotation repeats in the US (growth out, energy in), XLE vs. QQQ outperformance could persist through the week.

**Middle East is in a "fighting without collapsing" stalemate.** Houthis attacking Saudi soil + Iran restoring limited flights + Hormuz throughput at lows — this isn't one-sided escalation or one-sided de-escalation. It's a "sustained disruption without large-scale interruption" state. **Crude at $89.7 prices in exactly this expectation: there's a premium but no bubble.** If Houthis expand attacks to Saudi oil fields (not just airports/barracks) or the US launches military action against Iran, crude could surge to $93–95. If IEA releases strategic reserves or diplomacy produces a breakthrough, $85 below isn't excluded. At current levels, the risk/reward for going long is average — wait and watch beats acting.

**Gold's retreat from $4,143 to $4,120 deserves attention.** From intraday high $4,143 to $4,120, a drop of ~$23 (0.56%), showing that with high rates (Fed hawkish minutes + NY Fed inflation expectation at 3.9%), gold's upward momentum is decaying. **$4,100 is strong support (intraday low, psychological level), $4,140 is strong resistance (intraday high).** With US 10Y yield above 5.25%, gold will likely oscillate in $4,100–$4,140. A breakout catalyst is needed (Middle East escalation or 10Y yield decline).
