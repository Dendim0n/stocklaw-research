🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**⚔️ Ukraine Launches Massive Drone Strike on Russian Oil Tankers in Black Sea**

Ukrainian drones struck 20 Russian vessels overnight in the Black Sea, including 17 oil tankers. This marks one of the most direct attacks on Russian energy shipping since the conflict began. Shipping insurance premiums may spike again, posing a tangible threat to crude supply.

**🇺🇸🇮🇷 Trump War Room Discusses Expanded Strikes on Iran**

According to Axios, Trump convened senior security officials to discuss larger-scale strikes on strategic targets inside Iran. The US hopes military escalation will force Iranian concessions, but the Strait of Hormuz remains locked in a war of attrition. Middle East geopolitical premium is unlikely to fade in the near term.

**🇺🇸 US Treasury Yields Capped — CPI Miss Continues to Ripple**

US June CPI came in below market expectations, keeping Treasury yields in check during Asian trading despite a modest rebound. Fed rate hike expectations have plummeted. Overnight, SK Hynix ADR surged 27%, Korea's KOSPI triggered a circuit breaker, and today Taiwan's Weighted Index closed up 2.0% at 45,631.59.

**🇸🇦 Middle East Producers Invite Japan to Join Hormuz Bypass Pipeline**

Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and other Middle Eastern oil producers have proposed that Japan participate in expanding oil pipelines that bypass the Strait of Hormuz. This reflects growing producer concern over long-term strait security and signals a potential structural shift in global oil transit routes.

**🇩🇪 Rhine River Water Levels Threaten German Manufacturing**

Persistent drought is driving Rhine River water levels lower, threatening supply chains for German manufacturers. The national inland shipping association warns that transport costs are climbing, clouding the recovery outlook for Europe's largest economy.

**💰 Buffett Launches $140 Billion Wealth Transfer**

The 95-year-old Warren Buffett unveiled his latest charitable arrangements, planning to dispose of all remaining Berkshire Hathaway shares by the end of 2034 — a roughly $140 billion transfer. The Gates Foundation was notably absent from the recipient list.

**🇯🇵 BOJ Negative Rate Internal Split Exposed — Rate Hike Debate Reignited**

Newly released records reveal deep internal divisions when the BOJ adopted negative rates a decade ago, with officials warning it would trigger a "currency war." As a new rate hike debate unfolds, history's fault lines are resurfacing.
