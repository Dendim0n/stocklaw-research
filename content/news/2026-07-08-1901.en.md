🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🛢️ Trump Declares US-Iran Ceasefire Void, Oil Surges**

Trump publicly announced that the US-Iran temporary ceasefire is "over," derailing peace talks originally scheduled after Khamenei's funeral. Both WTI and Brent surged over 2%, with WTI briefly touching $74.89. At the NATO summit, Trump launched direct verbal attacks on Iran, while Iran's Navy Admiral countered that "US rhetoric is psychological warfare against the Iranian people" and insisted the US military "lacks the courage" to act.

**⚔️ Strait of Hormuz Tensions Escalate**

Slovenia's Foreign Minister called for respect of navigation freedom in the Strait. Belgium's Defense Minister stated NATO's Hormuz operations are defensive and the alliance will not join a war. Jin10 reports that Hormuz shipping disruptions are amplifying already-tight fertilizer supply, with elevated prices expected to persist. Buyers shifting to alternative sources have already cost US agricultural exports approximately $15 billion.

**🇱🇧 Lebanese President Heads to Washington for Talks**

The Lebanese President vowed to take action to stop Israel's "destruction and slaughter" and ultimately end the occupation, emphasizing "I chose negotiation so the country does not go into the abyss." He expects positive outcomes from his meeting with Trump, noting the initiative is supported by most Lebanese, including Shia groups who have borne the heaviest cost of war.

**📈 Alibaba Surges 14%**

BABA recorded its largest single-day gain since September 2025. The market had been most worried about Alibaba falling into a "subsidize for share, margins get squeezed" cycle. Now, improving instant-retail losses combined with renewed Alibaba Cloud growth expectations have triggered a massive short squeeze.

**🍎 Apple Closes In on Nvidia, Market Cap Gap Narrows to ~$200B**

In less than a year, Apple has shrunk its market cap gap with Nvidia from approximately $1.37 trillion to roughly $200 billion. The race for the world's most valuable company has suddenly intensified.

**🏦 Warsh Comments Cool Rate Hike Bets**

Former Fed Governor Warsh's remarks shifted market expectations, with traders increasing hedges against overly hawkish Fed pricing. Bets on additional rate hikes have rapidly receded.

**🤖 AUD 166B Australian Pension Fund: AI Has No Bubble, Will Buy Dips**

One of Australia's top pension funds flagged two key risks — above-expectation inflation forcing multiple rate hikes, and cooling financing for top AI companies — but stated it will continue adding to AI positions as long as shocks remain manageable.

**🏭 Goldman Sachs: "HALO Trade" Enters New Phase**

Goldman's report argues investors are underestimating the strategic importance of real assets. As the HALO investment framework enters a new phase, the core factors driving stock performance are shifting toward five heavy-asset sectors.

🧭 **Strategic Assessment**

The dominant variable this hour is Trump tearing up the US-Iran ceasefire — not a surprise, but an inevitable escalation following mutual strikes. The 2.5% oil spike reflects the market repricing the supply disruption risk through the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical crude chokepoint.

The gold-oil divergence is notable. Normally, escalating geopolitical conflict should boost gold's safe-haven appeal, yet gold dropped 1.3% today. This suggests the market is repricing "stagflation" odds — cooling rate hike expectations (Warsh) outweighing haven demand, with gold's rate sensitivity dominating.

Alibaba's 14% surge is not a technical bounce. The core logic: narrowing instant-retail losses + recovering Alibaba Cloud growth expectations = the market is starting to believe BABA's earnings bottom is in. The short squeeze may have further room to run.

The Apple vs. Nvidia market cap race is fundamentally a contest between two AI narratives — "AI-powered devices" vs. "AI infrastructure." Apple is closing the gap on the certainty of on-device AI, but Nvidia still has the Blackwell card to play.

Tightening global edible oil supply combined with El Niño risk has driven sharp upward revisions in palm oil price forecasts — one of the inflation transmission chains worth tracking in the months ahead.
