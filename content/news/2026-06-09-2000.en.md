### 🔥 Key News This Hour

**1. Trump Announces Full Blockade of Iran Oil Exports**
Core change: Escalated from "threat" to "declared destruction" — Trump claims the US military has destroyed Iran's oil export capacity and will destroy any ship docking at Iranian ports. This is a "zero tolerance" stance far exceeding previous sanctions. Oil spiked then pulled back as markets digest the "supply shock already partially realized" narrative.

**2. US F-35s Deployed to UAE, 350km from Iran**
New info: US F-35 fighters have been deployed to Al Dhafra Air Base in the UAE, just 350km from the Iranian border. This dramatically enhances US forward strike capability in the region, placing Iran under more direct military pressure.

**3. Iran Claims Uranium Enrichment Continues**
New info: Despite US claims of destroyed nuclear capability, Iran says its nuclear program is operating normally. This contradiction adds uncertainty — if Iran is indeed still enriching, further US military action may follow.

**4. USS Makin Island Amphibious Ready Group Enters Persian Gulf**
New info: The USS Makin Island ARG (amphibious assault ship + dock landing ship + Marines) has entered the Persian Gulf. This is a landing-capable formation with strong signaling — the US is positioning for potential larger-scale operations.

**5. Japan PM Taro Kono Rejects Trump's 3.5% Defense Spending Demand**
New info: Trump demanded Japan raise defense spending to 3.5% of GDP; Kono explicitly said "impossible to accept" and hinted at potentially calling a snap election. US-Japan alliance shows clear cracks on burden-sharing.

**6. Iran Rejects US Meeting, Situation Hardens Further**
New info: Iran's FM explicitly refused to meet with the Trump administration, saying Washington's "maximum pressure" has lasted 40+ years and "has no effect" on Tehran. Diplomatic channels are essentially closed, lowering the threshold for further escalation.

**7. Trump Hints Iran War "Could End Soon"**
Contradictory signal: Amid full-scale escalation, Trump said "I hope the war ends soon" and suggested negotiations may be arranged. This starkly contrasts with his military deployments — markets read it as a "maximum pressure then seek exit" strategy.

**8. US Cuts Japan Auto Tariffs from 25% to 10%**
New info: US-Japan negotiations produce a tangible breakthrough — auto tariffs slashed from 25% to 10%. Major positive for Japanese auto exports and eases some trade tensions.

**9. China Rare Earth Exports to US Plunge 93%**
New info: May exports to US were just 1.2 tons, down 93% YoY. MOFCOM says it will implement "one country, one policy" precision targeting. Rare earths have moved from theory to active weapon — a critical negotiating chip with no short-term substitutes.
