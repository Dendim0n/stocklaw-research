🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**🇮🇷 US-Iran Deal Confirmed; IEA Breathes Sigh of Relief**
IEA Director Birol confirmed the US-Iran agreement, calling it "a great relief for me and everyone." He had previously warned of a global red alert if Hormuz remained closed past June. However, he cautioned that "Pandora's box has been opened" — all parties now know the strait has been shut once and could close again. First-round technical talks are set for tomorrow in Switzerland, covering sanctions relief, frozen assets, Hezbollah, and nuclear issues. IAEA Chief Grossi noted contacts with Iran are at "lowest levels" but multiple options exist for handling uranium stockpiles.

**🇱🇧 Israel and US in Tough Negotiations Over Southern Lebanon Troops**
Israel continues military operations in Lebanon. A senior official close to Netanyahu said Israel and the US are in "tough negotiations" over troop presence in southern Lebanon, with Israel showing no willingness to concede. China also weighed in, calling for ceasefire and peace.

**🇺🇸 Fed Rate Hike Risk Sharply Higher**
Fed dot plot shows 9 officials favor rate hikes this year, 6 supporting two increases. Analysts note this will disappoint Trump and reflects deepening concern about persistent inflation. Dollar strength pushes yen to a near two-year low.

**🇺🇦 Russia-Ukraine Attacks Escalate**
Ukraine targeted Moscow refineries and surrounding facilities; Russia launched ballistic missiles at Kyiv. Zelensky seeks breakthrough at G7 summit.
