## 🔥 Key Overnight News

**1. Japan Q2 GDP Misses Estimates**
Japan Q2 real GDP quarterly preliminary +0.3% vs +0.5% expected; annualized +1.1% vs +2% expected. Corporate spending -1.2% vs +0.4% expected; private consumption 0% vs +0.5% expected. Domestic demand weakened across the board, pressuring the Nikkei.

**2. Middle East Tensions Continue**
- Hormuz Strait ship transits slowed over the weekend due to tanker attacks and lack of progress in US-Iran talks
- Iranian president says the US-Iran memorandum of understanding "maintains dignity and shows strength," denying submission to enemies
- IRGC deputy commander: actions have been defensive so far, but may turn offensive in the future
- US is assessing "unprecedented" sanctions on Iran, expected as soon as this week
- Israeli attacks on Lebanon have killed 4,346 people

**3. Hamas Agrees to Trump Ceasefire Plan Phase 2**
Hamas political bureau chief Haniyeh met with mediators in Egypt, conveying agreement to phase 2 of the Trump plan — conditioned on permanent ceasefire and full Israeli withdrawal. Hamas accused Israel of failing to honor phase 1 commitments. Egyptian President Sisi met with Kushner, urging implementation of the Gaza ceasefire.

**4. Trump Demands Reduction of US-South Korea Military Drills**
Trump posted on social media expressing dissatisfaction with US-South Korea joint military exercises, citing high costs and inappropriate "hostile signals" to North Korea. He has directed Defense Secretary Hegseth to significantly scale back the drills.

**5. Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Pakistan Sign Joint Defense Pact**
Trump welcomed the signing of the "Mecca Joint Defense Agreement" by the three nations, saying the Middle East is strengthening unity.

**6. Fed September Hold Probability at 66.9%**
According to CME FedWatch: probability of maintaining rates in September at 66.9%, with 33.1% for a 25bp hike. October hold probability at 53.6%.

**7. Stripe to Acquire OpenRouter for Over $7 Billion**
Stripe is set to acquire AI company OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, accelerating its AI infrastructure push.

**8. Anthropic CEO: Best Way to Defeat AI Skeptics Is to Deliver on Promises**
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei posted, acknowledging public distrust in AI and stating the best response is to deliver on hype promises.

## 🧭 Market Assessment

Middle East geopolitical risk remains the core variable for oil prices. Slowed Hormuz transit, Iran's tough rhetoric, and incoming "unprecedented" US sanctions create short-term upside risk for oil — but the actual 9 million barrels/day still transiting the strait indicates supply has not been truly disrupted.

Japan's GDP data broadly missed expectations, with both domestic demand and corporate spending weakening. This reinforces expectations for the Bank of Japan to cautiously exit easing, potentially pressuring the yen.

Hamas agreeing to phase 2 of the Trump ceasefire plan is a positive signal, but Israel's cooperation is the key variable. The Middle East remains in a "fragile game" phase.

The 33% probability of a Fed rate hike in September cannot be ignored. Markets are currently priced dovish — if this week's FOMC minutes come across as hawkish, rising Treasury yields could pressure risk assets.

Key events this week: FOMC minutes (Thursday), China's July economic data (today 15:00 CST), major economy PMI data. Further weakness in China's data could amplify policy stimulus expectations.
