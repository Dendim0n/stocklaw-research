🔥 **Key Overnight News**

**1. Japan Q2 GDP Misses Expectations**
Real GDP quarterly initial 0.3% (expected 0.5%), annualized 1.1% (expected 2%), corporate spending -1.2% (expected +0.4%), private consumption 0% (expected +0.5%) — broad miss across the board. Domestic demand remains weak under yen depreciation pressure.

**2. US Prepares "Unprecedented" Sanctions on Iran**
Treasury Secretary Bessent says measures could come as soon as this week with "unprecedented" intensity. Maritime blockade, energy sanctions, and financial restrictions already in place; further directions under assessment. Iranian president responds that the memorandum of understanding "preserves dignity and demonstrates strength." Hormuz Strait vessel transits slowed over the weekend due to tanker attacks and lack of progress in US-Iran peace talks.

**3. Trump Demands Major Cuts to US-South Korea Military Drills**
Trump posted on social media that he is dissatisfied with the cost of joint military exercises and their "inappropriate hostile signal" to North Korea, and has instructed Defense Secretary Hegseth to significantly reduce drill scale.

**4. Hamas Agrees to Second Phase of Trump Plan**
Hamas political bureau chief met with mediators in Egypt and agreed to the second phase of the Trump Gaza plan, conditioned on permanent ceasefire and Israeli withdrawal. Hamas also praised Trump's role in facilitating the ceasefire.

**5. Fed September Rate-Hold Probability at 66.9%**
CME FedWatch: 66.9% probability of holding rates steady in September, 33.1% probability of a 25bp hike. October hold probability at 53.6%.

**6. Stripe Acquires OpenRouter**
Stripe is set to acquire AI company OpenRouter for over $7 billion. Anthropic CEO Amodei stated the best way to defeat AI skeptics is to deliver on hype promises.
