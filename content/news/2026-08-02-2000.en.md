🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**1. OPEC+ Agrees to Raise September Output by 188,000 bpd**

OPEC+ statement confirms Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, and Oman agreed to adjust production quotas, raising September output by 188,000 bpd. The statement did not address post-September policy, with the next meeting scheduled for September 6. This marks OPEC+'s first explicit production increase signal after months of sustained cuts. Oil price reaction was limited (weekend market close), but Monday's open warrants close attention.

**2. Trump Claims Cancelled Iran Strike, Iran Dismisses as "Fake News"**

Trump posted on social media that he received requests from Iran and other Middle Eastern nations to pause attacks, claiming a framework deal including reopening the Strait of Hormuz was reached, and agreed to cancel the strike. Iran's Fars News Agency immediately denied this, calling reports about reopening the Strait "pure rumors," with informed sources emphasizing no agreement has been reached. Both sides are engaged in an active information war, leaving the situation highly uncertain.

**3. Iran Says Bahrain Amazon Data Center Strike Was Response to US Actions**

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) spokesman detailed "Operation Victory-2," stating the Amazon data processing center in Bahrain was targeted because it provided intelligence support, cloud computing, and services linked to US military command systems. This marks Iran's first explicit targeting of tech infrastructure as a military objective, signaling continued escalation of Middle East conflict spillover.

**4. Israeli Attack on Gaza Kills at Least 11**

According to Al Jazeera, Sunday's Israeli attack on Gaza has killed at least 11 people. Israel's N12 channel reports Israel does not plan to halt attacks on Gaza unless Hamas disarms. Ceasefire prospects remain dim.

**5. Five Lebanese Soldiers Injured in Israeli Airstrike**

The Lebanese military reported that five soldiers were injured in an Israeli airstrike in the town of Kafr Kila in the Bint Jbeil district of southern Lebanon. Israel's military operations in southern Lebanon continue.

🧭 **Situation Assessment**

The Middle East has entered a phase of high uncertainty — Trump claims to have cancelled strikes on Iran citing a framework deal, but Iran explicitly denies this, creating an active information war where the market cannot confirm the true progress of any ceasefire.

OPEC+'s decision to raise September output by 188,000 bpd, combined with the uncertainty around Strait of Hormuz navigation, creates a dual game on the crude supply side — bearish production increase vs. geopolitical risk premium, with direction dependent on whether the Strait can resume navigation.

Iran designating the Bahrain Amazon data center as a military target marks an expansion of Middle East conflict from traditional military confrontation to tech infrastructure, with conflict spillover continuing to widen.

Gold dropped nearly $60 from above 4,100 to 4,043 — despite no substantive de-escalation in geopolitical conflict, the pullback likely reflects short-term market optimism around Trump's "cancelled strike" statement, but if Iran's denial holds, correction risk exists on Monday.
