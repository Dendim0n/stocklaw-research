🔥 **Key Overnight News**

1. **US-Iran conflict escalates — second clash in three days**: CENTCOM confirmed strikes on IRGC targets (air defenses, radars, naval assets) including the key energy hub Asaluyeh port; Iran hit a US base in Jordan with heavy missiles, claiming mass US casualties, and attacked Jordan's Prince Hassan Air Base destroying drones; Bahrain intercepted drones with at least 4 explosions near the capital; US embassy in Qatar issued a security alert. Trump called the Iran deal "worthless" and said "the final blow is brewing"; Peru severed diplomatic ties with Iran; Treasury Secretary Bessent said the Strait of Hormuz will be bypassable within two years and the US will keep ramping sanctions.

2. **Fed hawkish signals pile up, Sept hike odds rise**: After Warsh's Jackson Hole hawkishness, Fed Governor Barr said the Fed should hike decisively if inflation doesn't cool enough; the "Fed whisperer" (WSJ) wrote that the energy crisis won't fade and the September meeting is getting harder, with the 10Y yield surging to 4.80%, a high for Trump's term.

3. **Putin vows massive strikes on Ukrainian energy infrastructure**: He said Ukraine peace talks are frozen and Russia will hit Ukraine's energy facilities at scale; Russia has cut its oil production forecast to a 17-year low, and Black Sea grain-route worries pushed wheat to a 3.5-year high and CBOT soybeans to a 2.5-year high.

4. **Trump pressures refiners to boost output; cheap Venezuelan crude to hit market in November**: White House revealed details of the closed-door meeting with Chevron, Marathon Petroleum and other executives; EPA issued its largest biofuel waivers since 2017; Valero's Port Arthur refinery suffered a partial power outage.

5. **Korea core inflation at 3-year high**: August CPI rose 3.1% YoY, below the 3.2% forecast, but core inflation hit its highest in over three years, keeping the BOK hawkish and raising hike pressure.

6. **Japan policy signals**: BOJ's Ueda said the market already reflects a strong chance of a September hike; Finance Minister Katayama was busy at the G20; Australia's 10Y yield hit 5.198%, the highest since mid-2011.

7. **Stocks see September opening loss**: All three indices fell — Oracle -5%, Micron -2%+, Dell -7%, Apple +2%; BofA strategists warn high yields threaten the breakout.

🧭 **Assessment**

The US-Iran conflict has seen two clashes in three days, now spilling into Jordan and Bahrain, and Trump's "final blow" rhetoric signals escalation rather than de-escalation — geopolitical risk premium won't fade soon.

Two Fed officials in a row turned hawkish, lifting September hike bets; high yields are trumping safe-haven demand, so gold finds little support even with Middle East risk — rate pricing dominates precious metals for now.

Putin's threat to strike Ukraine's energy infrastructure plus Russia's cut oil output forecast keep the supply narrative tight — crude is biased higher short-term, though cheap Venezuelan crude arriving in November is a medium-term cap.

BOJ's September hike expectations and Australia's record-high yields show global yields rising in sync, and external bond-market pressure could keep weighing on US equity valuations.

Today's RBNZ decision, the expected seventh straight hold from the Bank of Canada, and US ADP data will be the key tests of whether the hike trade continues.
