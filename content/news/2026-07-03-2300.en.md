🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**DRC Cobalt Exports Hit by System Failure, Up to 20,000 Tons at Risk**
A customs platform system failure in the Democratic Republic of Congo may prevent major cobalt producers from fulfilling H1 export quotas. Up to ~20,000 tons of cobalt—worth approximately $1.1 billion at current prices—could be stranded, posing a near-term disruption to the global battery metal supply chain.

**US Antitrust Regulators Scrutinize Oil Markets**
US antitrust authorities stated they are closely monitoring oil markets for potential price manipulation or collusion and urged state attorneys general to assist in investigations. This comes amid reports that Q2 profits for US oil majors may hit multi-year highs, potentially triggering political backlash.

**Germany Unveils 2027 Draft Budget**
Total expenditure set at €555.4 billion (up from €543.3 billion in the prior draft), with core net borrowing of €118.7 billion and total investment of €117.5 billion. Total borrowing of €203.7 billion marks a significant expansion from the previous €196.5 billion—a clear fiscal expansion signal.

**Iran Leverages Sanctions Waiver Window for Japan Crude Trade**
Under a 60-day temporary US sanctions waiver, Iran has begun crude procurement evaluations with three Japanese firms—the first such engagement since 2019. Waiver renewal and Hormuz Strait security remain the two key sticking points.

**Argentina Completes $6 Billion Repo Financing**
Priced at SOFR + 400 bps with maturity extended to 2028, underscoring that emerging markets continue to borrow at elevated costs.

**UK Approves Semaglutide for Liver Disease**
The MHRA approved the GLP-1 drug for a new indication, handing Novo Nordisk another win.
