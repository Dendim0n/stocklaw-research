🔥 **Key News This Hour**

**Iran's Supreme Leader Khamenei funeral begins, Middle East power transition underway.** A public farewell ceremony was held in Tehran on July 4 local time, with funeral rites spanning five cities across Iran and Iraq over the course of a week. Outside attention is focused on whether newly appointed Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei will make a public appearance. Trump stated the same day that "Iran very much wants to make a deal" and said he gave Iran "a week off" due to the funeral.

**Oman maintains strategic ambiguity on Strait of Hormuz tolls.** Analysts note Oman is simultaneously engaging Iran on a joint toll framework while facing US sanctions deterrence. Significant unknowns remain around the proposal details, keeping the Hormuz toll suspense alive — the strait carries roughly one-fifth of global oil shipments.

**Putin says large-scale strikes on Ukraine military facilities must continue.** The statement was released by the Russian presidential website on the night of July 3, following Putin's visit to a joint forces command post. No signs of near-term de-escalation in the Russia-Ukraine conflict.

**Micron Technology invests $9 billion to expand chip plant in western Japan.** The project totals 1.5 trillion yen (~$9B) for advanced memory chip production, with the Japanese government providing up to 500 billion yen in subsidies. It is a key part of Micron's global AI-driven capacity expansion.

**Huawei's He Tingbo publishes V2 of "Tao's Law" paper.** Supplemented with engineering details and measured data, following the V1 that sparked broad discussion in the semiconductor academic community. The theory addresses temporal miniaturization in multi-level electronic systems.

**World's first neuromorphic dynamics chip unveiled.** A team led by PKU's Yang Yuchao, in collaboration with CAS Shanghai Institute of Microsystems, successfully developed the chip based on phase-change memristors — the first to integrate neuromorphic dynamics computation onto a single chip, aiding brain-computer interface technology development.

**US Independence Day parade canceled due to extreme heat.** Washington D.C.'s scheduled celebration was called off, as extreme weather becomes an emerging variable for US infrastructure and public events.

**Qianwen and Doubao announce discontinuation of agent features.** Alibaba's Qianwen will sunset anthropomorphic interactive agents on July 10; ByteDance's Doubao agent features end July 15. China's AI application layer is undergoing a feature consolidation phase.

🧭 **Market Assessment**

Gold accelerated past 4160, with safe-haven demand stemming from three fronts: uncertainty around Iran's leadership transition, the unresolved Strait of Hormuz toll situation, and continued escalation in the Russia-Ukraine conflict. Near-term technicals favor the upside, but thin holiday liquidity warrants caution against excessive swings.

Crude oil is significantly lagging gold in pricing Middle East risk. If the Hormuz toll materializes, it would represent the most consequential shift in global shipping cost structure since the 1970s, with potentially non-linear and abrupt transmission to oil prices.

During the Khamenei funeral period, Iran's external negotiations are likely on hold. Trump's "week off" remark implies a near-term vacuum in US-Iran nuclear talks. Any escalation in proxy conflicts or Hormuz friction during this window will be amplified.

Micron's Japan expansion confirms the upward cycle in memory chip demand, but conflicting signals coexist — the big short warning of an "AI party ending" alongside Samsung and SK Hynix ramping investment. The semiconductor sector is entering a period of intensified long-short tug-of-war.

US oil majors' Q2 profits may hit recent peaks. Combined with elevated gasoline prices and the approaching midterm elections, the energy sector faces mounting political pressure. Trump's tolerance for high oil prices bears watching.
