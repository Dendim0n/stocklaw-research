## Overview

The Trade Desk is the largest independent programmatic advertising platform, focused on digital ad delivery across the open internet.

## Investment Thesis

- Long-term beneficiary of CTV ad budget migration from traditional TV
- Retail media networks as new growth vector
- Kokai AI engine improves ad delivery efficiency

## Risks

- Persistently high valuation, Forward PE at historical highs
- Google competition in ad tech
- Ad budgets highly sensitive to macroeconomic conditions
