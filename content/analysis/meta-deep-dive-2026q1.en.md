### AI Monetization Logic (Core Thesis)

This is what fundamentally differentiates META from other capex-heavy hyperscalers:

1. **No compute resale, purely internal consumption** — No customer acquisition problem, no pricing pressure
2. **AI-driven ad ranking** — Click-through rate +3% (FB Feed), conversion rate +5% (IG), ad price +12%, impressions +19%
3. **4x leverage** — AI ranking generates 4x the revenue increment of simply increasing ad load
4. **Cash ROIC 52%** — Not paper profits, real cash returns

## Conclusion

**META at 20.3x forward PE is the cheapest name in MAG7** (excluding TSLA's extreme outlier). Given the 52% Cash ROIC, the certainty of AI ad monetization, and the $31.34 full-year EPS estimate, this price reflects a "Zuckerberg discount" — the market hasn't priced in its AI monetization capability.

**Upside catalyst**: Q2/Q3 earnings continue to beat → market re-rates AI monetization efficiency → PE returns to 25x

**Downside risk**: capex continues to inflate but ROI deteriorates → FCF worsens → PE compresses to 15-18x
