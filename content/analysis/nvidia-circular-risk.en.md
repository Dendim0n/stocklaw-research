## Core Risk

NVIDIA's AI datacenter revenue has circular financing risk: clients raise equity/debt capital to fund GPU purchases for datacenter construction, and the revenue expectations from these datacenters support NVIDIA's lofty valuation.

## Key Data

- NVIDIA FY2027 Q1 revenue at record levels
- Datacenter revenue share continuously expanding
- Client capex growth far outpacing revenue growth
- If financing environment tightens, GPU demand could collapse

## Analogy

Similar to the 2000 telecom bubble: equipment vendors' revenue came from clients' aggressive network buildout funded by continuous financing. When financing dried up, equipment revenue collapsed.

## Risk Monitoring

Track: major client capex guidance, GPU delivery lead times, secondary GPU prices, financing market rates.
