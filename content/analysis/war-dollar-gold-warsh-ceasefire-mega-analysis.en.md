## I. Can War Eliminate Debt?

### The Traditional Logic

High debt → transfer contradictions → eliminate debt → in extreme cases, through war. Historically valid:

- Post-WWII US debt-to-GDP: 106%. Won the war, rebuilt global order, digested debt through growth + financial repression.
- Post-Napoleonic Wars Britain: debt-to-GDP 200%. Digested through the British Empire's global expansion.

**Core logic: Win the war → reset the order → winner makes the rules → debt is diluted in the new order.**

### But the US-Iran War Is Not WWII

| Condition | WWII (Debt Eliminated) | US-Iran War (Pre-Ceasefire) |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| Quick victory? | ✅ 4 years | ❌ 3.5 months, no end in sight |
| War profits? | ✅ Global hegemony | ❌ Hormuz semi-blockaded, oil surging |
| Creditors? | Eliminated (Germany, Japan) | China, Japan, Korea — can't eliminate |
| Competitors? | None | China, India free-riding |
| USD credit? | Rebuilt post-war | Weakening with every day of war |

**The war's core battlefield (Strait of Hormuz) directly controls 20% of global oil supply.** Oil price surges are themselves the source of inflation, forcing the Fed to hike, which worsens interest costs on $39 trillion in debt. War is not eliminating debt — it's adding to it.

### Vietnam Is the Correct Analogy

Vietnam era: military spending surged → inflation rose → USD credit declined → 1971 Nixon forced to abandon gold standard. The US-Iran war may follow the same path.

## III. What Happens If USD Credit Collapses?

Not a binary event — it's a spectrum.

### Slow Erosion (Already Happening)

Central banks quietly reducing US Treasury holdings, increasing gold reserves. De-dollarization slowly推进. USD's share of global forex reserves declining from 70%. America's "seigniorage" income decreases, import costs rise, living standards slowly decline.

### Accelerated Collapse

Trigger: Fed forced to大规模 print money to repay debt, or a lost war causes global confidence to动摇. Consequences: Treasury yields spike → interest costs explode → USD depreciates → imported inflation → Fed dilemma → EM contagion → global financial crisis.

### System Reset (Extreme Scenario)

Happened once: 1971 Nixon关闭 gold window. World predicted美元完蛋. Dollar survived — no alternative exists.

**RMB has capital controls, Euro has分裂 risk, gold不适合 modern trade, digital currencies need global consensus. Dollar's moat is not credit — it's monopoly.**

Most likely outcome: not "dollar collapse" but "dollar privilege shrinking." 30-50 year slow decline, following the pound's path.

## IV. Can the Dollar Recover?

It has recovered before (1971, 2008). But "can recover" ≠ "will recover." Recovery requires: inflation back to 2% (❌ 4.2%), fiscal deficit narrowing (❌ $1.5T+), debt-to-GDP declining (❌ 124%), a war winnable without printing money (❌ not in sight). **None of these conditions are met.**

## VIII. Warsh's "Balance Sheet Reduction + Rate Cuts" Framework

Confirmed via multiple sources: Warsh did advocate for "缩表降息" (balance sheet reduction + rate cuts) at the Senate hearing.

**Sources:** CICC/Sina Finance (Apr 22), CMB Research/21st Century Business Herald (Feb 14), Investopedia (Apr 21), Morningstar (Jan 30).

**Framework logic:** Shrink balance sheet → withdraw liquidity → asset prices fall → inflation eases → room to cut rates → support real economy.

### The Impact

**Short-term (0-6 months):** Stocks under pressure (liquidity withdrawn), USD weakens, EMs get short-term relief, Treasury yields fall.

**Core contradiction:** Balance sheet reduction = tightening financial conditions; rate cuts = loosening them. Doing both simultaneously confuses the market.

**Fatal flaw in current environment:** CPI 4.2%, war pushing oil higher — this is supply-side inflation. Balance sheet reduction can't fight oil inflation. You can withdraw money from markets, but you can't reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
