## Why This Stock

Southern Company's Georgia Power is the primary electricity provider for one of America's fastest-growing data center markets — Georgia. The company raised its capex plan to $81B, planning to expand Georgia's generation capacity by 70%, with ~95% dedicated to data centers. On June 3, 2026, $SO closed at **$92.04** with a market cap of **$104.1 billion**.

## Company Overview

Southern Company is one of America's largest regulated utilities:

## Investment Thesis

### 🟢 Bull Case

**1. Direct Georgia Data Center Exposure**

Atlanta/Georgia is one of the fastest-growing US data center markets. SO benefits directly through Georgia Power.

**2. $81B Capex Plan**

Largest investment plan in history, mostly for data center-related capacity expansion.

**3. Highest Dividend Yield**

3.36% yield is highest among data center utility plays, providing strong cash flow income.

**4. Lowest Forward PE**

18.77x Forward PE is lowest among peers, with revaluation potential.

**5. Real Revenue Growth**

Data center electricity sales +42% is real revenue growth, not narrative.

### 🔴 Risk Factors

**1. "Stranded Asset" Risk**

Investor advocacy groups have filed proxy proposals requiring management to disclose safeguards against AI bubble collapse stranding massive infrastructure costs.

**2. Political Backlash from Rate Increases**

Georgia residential electricity bills surged 43% from 2023-2025. Further increases could trigger stricter regulatory limits.

**3. Nuclear Asset Operational Risk**

SO's Vogtle nuclear project historically suffered massive cost overruns and delays.

**4. Limited Upside**

Analyst target $101 implies only +10% upside. For bearing utility risk, returns are modest.

---

## Investment Recommendation

**Rating: Hold for Income**

SO offers the highest dividend yield (3.36%) and lowest Forward PE (18.77x) among data center utilities. Suitable for income investors. But limited upside (+10%) and political/regulatory risks exist.

**Action Plan:**
- If seeking dividend income, current $92 is a reasonable entry
- If seeking growth, CEG or NEE are better choices
- Medium-term target: $101 (analyst consensus)
- Stop loss reference: $78

**Suitable for:** Income investors. SO works as a "bond substitute" within the data center theme.
