## Overview

PayPal, as a global digital payment leader, faces competitive pressure from Apple Pay, Google Pay, and emerging fintech companies.

## Investment Thesis

- Valuation has entered reasonable range after prolonged correction
- Venmo user growth and monetization still has room
- AI-driven personalization and fraud detection can improve margins

## Risks

- Intensifying payment market competition
- Interest rate environment impact on consumer credit business
