## Why These 11 Stocks

Following a deep analysis of the US data center infrastructure bottleneck (half of planned builds delayed/cancelled due to power shortages and Chinese component restrictions), we identified 11 stocks across three conviction tiers that benefit from this structural theme — many overlooked by the original research report.

## Tier 1: True Undervaluation

### EME — EMCOR Group (PE 28x, MktCap $38B)
**Thesis**: Largest US electrical/mechanical construction contractor. Direct beneficiary of data center construction boom. At PE 28x, cheapest in the sector — half the valuation of PWR (98x) or STRL (84x).
- Forward PE: 25.87x | Analyst target: $1,000 (+18%)
- YTD: +38% | Recent: William Blair conference presentation
- **Verdict**: Buy on pullback. Best risk/reward in the space.

### HUBB — Hubbell (PE 29x, MktCap $26B)
**Thesis**: Electrical equipment manufacturer for data center power distribution. Forward PE 22.48x is lowest among peers. 1.18% dividend yield provides income buffer.
- Just completed $1.9B senior notes offering
- **Verdict**: Hold/Small position. Steady, not exciting.

### CMI — Cummins (PE 36x, MktCap $95B)
**Thesis**: Largest data center backup generator supplier. "Behind-the-meter" trend shifts demand from backup to primary power. Forward PE 20.55x is extremely attractive for a century-old industrial.
- Data center demand + truck recovery = dual growth driver
- **Verdict**: Buy on pullback to $620-650. Best value play.

### GEV — GE Vernova (PE 28x, MktCap $264B)
**Thesis**: Gas turbine monopoly. $1,630B backlog. 90% capacity locked through 2030 — high certainty but limited upside surprise.
- Q1 2026: $18.3B new orders, +71% organic growth
- **Verdict**: Hold. Most certain play, but priced in.

### CEG — Constellation Energy (PE 26x, MktCap $97B)
**Thesis**: Nuclear renaissance leader. 20-year PPA with Microsoft to restart TMI Unit 1 (835MW). FERC waiver granted. $1B DOE loan. Analyst target implies +37% upside.
- YTD: -23% (pullback creates opportunity)
- **Verdict**: Buy on pullback. Best upside in utilities.

### NEE — NextEra Energy (PE 22x, MktCap $179B)
**Thesis**: $67B Dominion merger targets Virginia's data center cluster. 2.91% dividend yield. Battery storage leadership.
- **Verdict**: Hold for income. Merger uncertainty pending.

### SO — Southern Company (PE 24x, MktCap $104B)
**Thesis**: Georgia Power serves one of the fastest-growing data center markets. $81B capex plan. 3.36% dividend yield (highest in the group).
- Data center electricity sales +42%
- **Verdict**: Hold for income. Bond substitute in data center theme.

## Tier 2: Right Logic, Valuation Needs Digestion

### ETN — Eaton (PE 41x, MktCap $164B)
**Thesis**: Only company covering the full data center power stack — from grid connection to rack-level liquid cooling. Written into Nvidia Rubin reference architecture. Q1 data center orders +240%.
- Acquired Boyd Thermal for $9.55B
- Forward PE 26.86x | Analyst target: $452 (+6%)
- **Verdict**: Hold. Wait for pullback to $380-400.

### EQIX — Equinix (PE 76x, MktCap $108B)
**Thesis**: World's largest data center REIT. Unassailable interconnection density moat. But 76x PE for a REIT is extreme.
- YTD: +45% | Analyst target: $1,197 (+9%)
- **Verdict**: Do not chase. Wait for $850-900.

## Tier 3: Smaller & Interesting

### ITRI — Itron (PE 13x, MktCap $3.6B)
**Thesis**: Smart grid metering and load management. Data center power dispatch is a structural need. PE 13x is the lowest in the entire group. Analyst target implies +54% upside.
- YTD: -12% (creates entry opportunity)
- **Verdict**: Speculative buy. Low-cost lottery ticket.

### NVT — nVent Electric (PE 57x, MktCap $28B)
**Thesis**: Liquid cooling connection solutions. Record backlog. 2026 revenue guidance +26-28%. First $1B sales quarter.
- YTD: +72% (strongest performer)
- **Verdict**: Wait for pullback to $140-150.

## Key Takeaway

The data center infrastructure theme is real, but the market has already priced in much of the optimism. The best opportunities are in **overlooked corners**: EMCOR (28x PE), Cummins (Forward PE 21x), and Itron (13x PE). Avoid chasing VRT (83x), EQIX (76x), or PWR (98x) — the market has run too far ahead.
